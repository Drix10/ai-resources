### 🤖 Microsoft diagnostic AI solved 83% of 304 cases

Microsoft diagnostic AI solved 83% of 304 complex New England Journal of Medicine cases. Generalist physicians with reference materials solved 36%. The post calls for rapid, safe clinic deployment.

Key Points:

- **Diagnostic AI accuracy**: 83% on 304 NEJM cases.

- **Physician accuracy with references**: 36%.

### 🤖 Anthropic IPO outlook – open source weakening and compute diversity claim

Anthropic’s IPO is seen as favorable because open source momentum has slowed and the company uses a diverse set of chips, with about two thirds of its compute not from Nvidia. Dylan Patel links a weaker open source ecosystem to a potential monopoly or duopoly for Anthropic.

Key Points:

- Anthropic plus OpenAI added about $33 billion of revenue ARR in Q2, then less in Q3 due to open source growth.

- Two thirds of Anthropic’s compute is not Nvidia; they also use TPUs, Amazon Trainium, AMD GPUs, and are building their own chip.
