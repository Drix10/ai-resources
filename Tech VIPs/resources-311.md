### 🤖 UberEats Zipline launch orders lowered into yards in Spring, Texas

UberEats partnered with Zipline to lower orders from Jersey Mike’s, Zaxby’s and local restaurants into customers’ yards in Spring, Texas. The service uses tethered pods and does not require tipping.

Key Points:

- **Launch location**: Spring, Texas

- **Restaurants included**: Jersey Mike’s, Zaxby’s, and local eateries

### 🤖 macOS 27.3 Warns Against Giving AI Apps Full Mac Control

macOS 27.3 introduces warnings when AI applications request full desktop access. The alerts aim to reduce permission prompts for agents on macOS.

Key Points:

- New warning appears in macOS 27.3 for AI apps requesting full control.

- Alerts are described as “scary” by Apple.

### 🚨 FCC Bad Labs initiative blocks Shenzhen lab

FCC blocked a Shenzhen-based lab from testing electronic devices bound for the U.S. under the Bad Labs initiative. The agency said the lab had impermissible ties to a prohibited government entity.

Key Points:

- Blocked a Shenzhen based lab from testing electronic devices bound for the U.S.

- Determined the lab had impermissible ties to a prohibited government entity
