### 🤖 North Korea Responsible for $6 Billion+ Binance Attack

North Korea was responsible for the theft of more than $6 billion USD in virtual assets from Binance, dubbed “TraderTraitor”. The FBI says actors converted the stolen assets primarily into Ethereum and plan further laundering via DeFi and instant swap services. Private-sector operators are urged to block transactions linked to listed addresses.

Key Points:

- **Theft amount**: more than $6 billion USD stolen from Binance.

- **Conversion focus**: stolen assets primarily converted into Ethereum (ETH).
