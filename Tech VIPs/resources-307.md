### 🤖 China 100MW datacenter build cost under $300M

China 100MW datacenter build cost under $300M, about a fifth of US cost, with a 25% cost drop since 2021. Cost excludes IT equipment.

Key Points:

- **Cost**: 100MW datacenter in China now costs under $300M to build, excluding IT equipment.

- **Reduction**: China's build cost has fallen about 25% since 2021.

### 🤖 Ploid — The Search Engine, Built for People launches digital footprint tool

Ploid Deep Research lets users retrieve a full digital footprint of any person in minutes, including photos, videos, and obscure social accounts. The service operates inside agents like Claude, Codex, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Cursor. The underlying platform indexes over 3 billion people in real time and maintains a living index of more than two billion profiles. It also offers a "Superprofile" that merges social, professional, and personal data into a single view.

Key Points:

- **Launch**: Ask about anyone and get their full digital footprint in minutes.

- **Coverage**: Index of over 3 billion people; living index of over two billion profiles.
