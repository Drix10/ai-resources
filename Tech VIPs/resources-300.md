### 🤖 Texas Election Impact on Data Centers – 474 GW Queue, 90% Data Centers

Texas election is described as the most consequential for data centers, utilities and power this cycle. The incoming governor inherits a 474 GW interconnection queue, about 90% of which is for data centers and over five times the all-time system peak.

Key Points:

- **Interconnection queue size**: 474 GW

- **Data center share of queue**: roughly 90%
