### 🤖 AI and Engineering - Misleading Metrics and Valuations

- **Trade-offs and Failure Modes**: The use of misleading metrics can lead to a lack of transparency and trust in the industry. It can also make it difficult for investors and customers to make informed decisions.

- **Actionable Takeaway**: When evaluating companies, it's essential to look beyond the surface-level metrics and dig deeper to understand the underlying business model and revenue streams.
