### 🚀 Fortnite coming to Linux, Omarchy day-one readiness

Fortnite is coming to Linux, with Omarchy being prepared for day-one launch. The team is working with Epic Games to meet security requirements.

Key Points:

- **Announcement**: “Fortnite is coming to Linux!!”

- **Collaboration**: “working with the folks at @EpicGames to ensure that @Omarchy is ready on day one with all the security requirements.”

### 🤖 Epic Games EAC v2 pilot on Fortnite enables Linux support

Epic Games announced a pilot of EAC v2 on Fortnite and plans to let all EAC customers opt in to the new module to bring games to Linux. They are also discussing Steam Deck support with Valve.

Key Points:

- **Pilot**: EAC v2 on Fortnite

- **Plan**: enable all EAC customers to opt in and bring games to Linux

- **Discussion**: talking with Valve about Steam Deck support

🔗 Resources:

- [Original post](https://x.com/MarkRein/status/2110577111232643455)

### 🚀 EAC Linux v2 pilot with Fortnite announced

EAC Linux v2 is being rebuilt to give Linux players protection similar to Windows. The first pilot will be with Fortnite.

Key Points:

- Rebuilding anti-cheat into EAC Linux v2.

- Pilot EAC Linux v2 with Fortnite first.
