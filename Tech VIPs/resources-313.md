### 🤖 Positron Titan deploys 5 MW to cut cost of trillion-parameter models

Positron Titan will deploy 5 MW of capacity to help customers run trillion-parameter open-weight models at lower cost. The deployment builds on Atlas already in production with Parasail.

Key Points:

- **Deployment**: 5 MW of Positron Titan

- Builds on Atlas already in production with Parasail

### 🤖 Muse email rollout to supported markets, claim address

Muse email is now rolling out to everyone in supported markets, letting users claim a @muse email address. The Muse can send and receive emails on the user’s behalf. Access is via the avatar mail tab or a voice request.

Key Points:

- Email rolling out to everyone in supported markets.

- Muse can send and receive emails on your behalf.
