### 📊 STRCx becomes largest tokenized equity in DeFi

STRCx now holds the status of the largest tokenized equity in DeFi. It represents Strategy’s STRC preferred stock. The token is issued by xStocks and is used in yield rate markets on Ethereum and Solana via Pendle and ExponentFinance.

Key Points:

- Largest tokenized equity in DeFi, representing Strategy’s STRC preferred stock.

- Powered in yield rate markets on Ethereum and Solana through Pendle and ExponentFinance.

### 🤖 New electric Smart car unveiled at Paris motor show

The Smart brand introduced a new electric car at the Paris motor show. It is a 2‑seater with a 60 kW battery and an 80 hp rear electric motor. The vehicle claims a 180 mile range and a starting price around €20,000.

Key Points:

- **Battery**: 60 kW capacity

- **Motor**: 80 hp rear electric motor

- **Range**: 180 mile claimed

- **Price**: starting around €20,000
