### 🤖 The Power of Pull framework – buying decision conditions and outreach metrics

The Power of Pull framework defines four buyer conditions that trigger purchase and provides cold outreach benchmarks: 20 LinkedIn requests daily, 30-40% acceptance, 30-50% reply rates. It also outlines how to identify real pull in meetings.

Key Points:

- **Pull framework**: buyer has a Project, an Unavoidable reason, a List of options, and each option has Limitations.

- **Cold outreach benchmark**: 20 LinkedIn requests per day, 30-40% accepted, 30-50% of those reply.
