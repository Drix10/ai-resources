### 🤖 AI Model - BERT

BERT is a pre-trained language model developed by Google that has revolutionized the field of natural language processing (NLP). It has achieved state-of-the-art results in various NLP tasks, including question answering, sentiment analysis, and language translation. BERT's success can be attributed to its ability to capture complex contextual relationships between words, which is a significant improvement over traditional NLP models.

Key Points:

- **BERT Architecture**: BERT is based on a multi-layer bidirectional transformer encoder, which allows it to capture both left and right contextual relationships between words. This architecture is significantly different from traditional NLP models, which typically rely on recurrent neural networks (RNNs) or convolutional neural networks (CNNs).

- **Pre-training and Fine-tuning**: BERT is pre-trained on a large corpus of text data, which allows it to learn general language patterns and relationships. It can then be fine-tuned on specific NLP tasks, such as question answering or sentiment analysis, to achieve state-of-the-art results.

- **Advantages and Limitations**: BERT has several advantages, including its ability to capture complex contextual relationships and its pre-trained weights, which can be fine-tuned for specific tasks. However, it also has some limitations, including its large size and computational requirements.
