### 🤖 Zipline raises $1.5B, signs Walmart contract for 300 supercenters

Zipline raised $1.5 billion at a $21.5 billion valuation and signed a contract with Walmart to expand autonomous delivery to 300 supercenters. The company reports over 150m commercial autonomous miles and delivery noise that is six times quieter than the closest competitor.

Key Points:

- **Funding**: $1.5 billion raised, valuation $21.5 billion.

- **Contract**: Walmart partnership to scale delivery to 300 supercenters.

### 🤖 VFlash delivers 12x faster decoding, 5.1x robot speedup

VFlash delivers 12x faster decoding, 5.1x robot inference speedup on edge GPUs, and up to 61% lower 3D trajectory error. It outputs fixed-length geometric vectors in parallel instead of autoregressive token decoding.

Key Points:

- 12x faster action decoding

- 5.1x overall robot inference speedup on edge GPUs like the Jetson Thor and DGX Spark

- up to 61% lower 3D trajectory error
