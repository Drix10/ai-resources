### 📊 TAC-Sponsored Johns Hopkins Study – SAT Vocabulary to 10

TAC-Sponsored Johns Hopkins study finds SAT vocabulary questions fell from 60 to 10 per test. The study also documents shorter reading passages and removal of argumentative texts. It compares 1980, 1987, and 2026 practice tests. Authors call for a common-scale study to equate difficulty.

Key Points:

- **Vocabulary reduction**: SAT vocabulary questions dropped from 60 per test to 10.

- **Passage length**: Reading passages are now less than one-third of their former length.

### 🚨 Ledger hardware wallets tampered in Singapore, $3.5M loss

Ledger cryptocurrency hardware wallets sold in Singapore were found to contain malicious hardware implants, causing about $3.5 million in losses across at least two cases since 10 September 2026. Police advise users to avoid using suspected devices and to transfer assets to a new wallet.

Key Points:

- At least two incidents reported since 10 September 2026, with total losses of about $3.5 million.

- Malicious hardware components can capture recovery phrases or private keys, enabling unauthorized access to wallets.
