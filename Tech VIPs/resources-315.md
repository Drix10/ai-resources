### 🚨 MiniMax – 492 requests exposed, credentials rotated

MiniMax exposure involved 492 requests from one customer on November 21, prompting OpenRouter to remove the Zero Data Retention label and off-board three providers. The notice advises rotating exposed credentials and testing confidential routes before sending sensitive traffic.

Key Points:

- 492 requests routed through MiniMax were identified as potentially read.

- OpenRouter off-boarded three inference providers and removed many endpoints from ZDR eligibility.
