### 🚨 Polymarket supply chain attack cancels trades

Polymarket experienced a supply chain attack that executed arbitrary code on the frontend to cancel all trades and exfiltrate funds. The attack used a compromised third-party provider and was reported to law enforcement.

Key Points:

- Supply chain attack executed arbitrary code on the frontend.

- Cancelled/exit all trades, inflating gamblers’ balances and exfiltrating them to attacker-controlled wallets.
