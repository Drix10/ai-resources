### 🤖 L3Harris modernizes NAS communications, 70% done

L3Harris modernizes NAS communications, 70% of the planned conversion to fiber is complete. The company is supporting the FAA investigation of the conditions that led to the Nov. 13 ground stop.

Key Points:

- **Conversion**: more than 70% of the planned airspace communications conversion from legacy copper wires to a fiber optic system is complete.

- **Investigation**: supporting the FAA in its investigation to understand the conditions that led to the Nov. 13 ground stop.
