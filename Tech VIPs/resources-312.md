### 🚨 Nvidia chip export approvals rushed by US Commerce

Nvidia chip export approvals were rushed by US Commerce staff after Trump approved a sale with a 25% government cut last December. BIS chief Jeffrey Kessler sometimes bypassed the normal interagency process, and approvals were granted to at least two companies tied to the Chinese military or involved in smuggling Blackwell chips, while Nvidia leaders said they stopped some sales to China.

Key Points:

- Commerce staff felt pressured by leadership to expedite Nvidia chip export approvals after a Trump-approved sale with a 25% government cut in December.

- BIS chief Jeffrey Kessler said the normal interagency process for approval wasn’t necessary in some cases.

- BIS granted approval to at least two companies with ties to the Chinese military or state-owned enterprises, or involved in smuggling Nvidia Blackwell chips into China.

- Nvidia’s current leaders said they pulled the plug on some chip sales to China.
