### 🤖 MedChemBench release – GPT-6.2 Astra scores 84.5%

MedChemBench released with 99 real-world medicinal chemistry problems from 117 papers and patents. GPT-6.2 Astra achieved an 84.5% score, while GPT-5.6 Sol achieved 58.2%. Choosing the next molecule to make remains a challenge.

Key Points:

- MedChemBench contains 99 problems drawn from 117 papers and patents

- GPT-6.2 Astra scored 84.5% on MedChemBench

- GPT-5.6 Sol scored 58.2% on MedChemBench
