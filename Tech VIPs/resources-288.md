### 🤖 H-JEPA paper announced for hierarchical visual planning

H-JEPA: End-to-End Learning of Hierarchical World Models for Visual Planning is a new paper advised by Yann LeCun. The paper notes that most world models plan in a single latent space and at one timescale. This design makes long‑horizon planning expensive and forces one representation to handle both tasks.

Key Points:

- **Paper title**: H-JEPA: End-to-End Learning of Hierarchical World Models for Visual Planning

- **Claim**: Existing world models use a single latent space and timescale, limiting long‑horizon planning.
