### 🤖 Dell revenue up, AI backlog hits $132B

Dell reported Q3 FY27 revenue of $54.9 billion, beating estimates, and a record $132 billion AI server backlog. Operating income rose to $7.4 billion and FY27 revenue guidance increased to $202 billion.

Key Points:

- **Revenue**: $54.9 billion, $5.7 billion beat estimates.

- **AI backlog**: $132 billion, up from $95 billion in Q2.

### 📊 LLM trading papers audit finds 51% deduct no costs

Henry Zhang audited 91 papers on LLM trading agents and alpha mining. Of the 75 papers with a trading test, 51% deduct no costs and 9% model costs from data.

Key Points:

- 75 papers include a trading test.

- Median test window is 12 months.

- 51% of those papers deduct no costs.

- 9% of those papers model costs from data.

### 🤖 Gather Customer Intelligence platform cuts study cost 1/20th and time to days

Gather is a full-stack AI native Customer Intelligence platform that can replace a six-month, $400k market research study with results in a few days at about one-twentieth the cost. Fortune 500 firms can launch studies via Slack or Teams and receive research plus pre-tested marketing collateral.

Key Points:

- Six-month, $400k grocery store study reduced to days and ~1/20th price using Gather.

- Real panels feed AI until confidence allows simulated results to augment human data.
