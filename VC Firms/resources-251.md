### 🚀 Tech - Alcobev Industry Regulation

The Alcobev industry is not centrally regulated in India, resulting in varying regulations across states. This leads to a complex and challenging environment for businesses operating in this sector. Ankur Sachdeva, Co-founder and CEO of Uppal Brewers and Distillers, explains the implications of this lack of regulation.

Key Points:
• The Alcobev industry is not centrally regulated in India, leading to varying regulations across states.
• This lack of regulation creates a complex and challenging environment for businesses operating in this sector.
• States decide their own regulations, including excise, label registration, and other requirements.
