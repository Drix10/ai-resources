### 🤖 Token jar handles multitoken fees converts to burned uni

Token jar enables collection of fees in many tokens and conversion to burned UNI. The technology is now offered for any crypto project to convert fees into a chosen asset such as a stablecoin, project token, or Uniswap-type burn.

Key Points:

- Built token jar to handle the problem of collecting fees in a million different tokens across a ton of different deployments and needing to convert to just one thing as efficiently as possible: burned UNI.

- Offering that tech to all of crypto, but it can be converted to whatever you want – a stablecoin in an exchange wallet, your project token in a multi-sig, Uniswap type burn, etc.
