### 🤖 Southeast Asia Startup Funding H1 2026 raises $7.25B

Southeast Asia Startup Funding H1 2026 raises $7.25B, driven by DayOne’s $4.5B Series C. Venture-backed companies completed 217 deals, the fewest since 2018.

Key Points:

- Funding total $7.25 billion across 217 transactions.

- DayOne’s $4.5 billion Series C round lifted overall funding.

### 🚀 Ripplr builds automated distribution for kirana stores

Ripplr built an automated distribution backbone that connects enterprise consumer brands directly with India's kirana stores. The system replaces manual trade practices with a proprietary data platform covering order routing, real-time corporate office tracking, credit collections and warehouse operations.

Key Points:

- 200x growth phase during early COVID-19 lockdowns.

- Operates across over 10 million General Trade retail outlets.

### 🤖 Hyundai Motor Group NVDA NemoClaw, OpenShell

Hyundai Motor Group explores NVDA NemoClaw blueprints and OpenShell runtime for agentic AI development. AIM Intelligence integrates Starfort with OpenShell, and LG AI Research opens API for K-EXAONE-2.5 trained on Nemotron data.

Key Points:

- AIM Intelligence integrates Starfort platform with NVDA OpenShell to combine runtime controls and malicious instruction detection.

- LG AI Research opens API access for K-EXAONE-2.5 model trained on NVDA Nemotron datasets including Korean persona data.

### 🤖 Swiss National Cleantech Startup Team heads to Munich roadshow

Swiss National Cleantech Startup Team is traveling to Munich for the Venture Leaders Cleantech 2026 roadshow, bringing ten Swiss startups. The week includes pitching, networking and investor meetings.

Key Points:

- **Ten startups**: Divea, GraphEnergyTech, kuafu, New Generation Tanks, PeelPack, ProSeed Ingredients, Sixteen44, Thermal Transformer, Winduction, Windworks.

- Venture Leaders Cleantech organized by Venturelab; supported by Axpo, EPFL, ETH, Helbling Technik, Rentsch Partner, Swiss Champions Fund, EtatdeVaud, VISCHER Law.
