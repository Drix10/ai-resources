### 🚀 Lumentum acquisition gains UHP lasers and InP capacity 2028

Lumentum acquisition gives Marvell UHP lasers for CPO and access to InP substrate deposits totaling $87M, with a U.S. 6-inch fab ramp in mid-2028. The deal adds ultra-high-power CW lasers, InP substrate capacity, and internal InP manufacturing expertise.

Key Points:

- **UHP lasers**: ultra-high-power CW lasers supply light through external laser modules (ELSFPs) for silicon photonics engines.

- **InP substrate agreement**: two contractual deposits with AXTI total $87M, providing reserved substrate capacity.

- **InP fab ramp**: planned U.S. 6-inch InP production ramp scheduled for mid-2028.
