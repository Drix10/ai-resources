### 🤖 Maryland Requires Dispensaries to Report Sales Tax

Maryland will require marijuana dispensaries to include a sales tax field on every transaction record in Metrc starting November 18. The field is mandatory for receipts and deliveries and transactions lacking a value will be rejected.

Key Points:

- Sales tax field mandatory for sales receipts and delivery transactions in Metrc.

- Transactions submitted without a sales tax value are automatically rejected.
