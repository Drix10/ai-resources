### 🤖 Erebor bank built, chartered, $1B deposits in 2025

Erebor bank was built in May 2025, received a national charter nine months later and held over $1B in deposits within seven weeks of opening. It is promoted as the real bank for real Americans doing real things.

Key Points:

- Built in May 2025 out of Palmer Luckey’s garage.

- Held over $1B in deposits within seven weeks of opening.
