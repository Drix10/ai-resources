### 🤖 Venture Capital and Customer Capital – VC targets customer-capital firms

Venture Capital and Customer Capital reports that venture capital funds firms with high sales and marketing spend relative to revenue, large sales payrolls, and many trademark filings. Investors using a “spray and pray” approach invest in these customer-capital industries, while larger-check investors focus on knowledge-capital industries.

Key Points:

- VC specializes in financing firms with higher sales-and-marketing expense-to-revenue ratios, higher sales-and-marketing wage bills, and more trademark filings.

- “Spray and pray” investors target customer-capital industries; investors writing larger checks target knowledge-capital industries.

- [Original post](https://x.com/ADINonline/status/2115929053379047462)
