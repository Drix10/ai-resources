### 📊 AMD Venice SP7 2nm EPYC 9006 server processors ship November 10 2026

AMD ships 2nm Venice SP7 EPYC 9006 server processors on November 10 2026. Venice offers up to 256 cores, 512 threads, 1.6TB/s memory bandwidth and PCIe 6.0. Data center revenue grew 97% YoY to $8.5B in Q3 2026.

Key Points:

- **Shipping date**: November 10 2026 for Venice SP7 EPYC 9006.

- **Venice specs**: up to 256 cores, 512 threads, 1.6TB/s memory bandwidth, PCIe 6.0.

- Enterprise SP8 socket scheduled for H1 2027 with over 240 server platforms committed.

- **Q3 2026 data center revenue**: $8.5B, 97% YoY growth.
