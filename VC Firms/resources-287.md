### 📊 LITE optical-component demand outlook through 2029

LITE’s CEO says AI optical-component capacity is effectively sold out through early 2029. He expects roughly 70% of demand for some products to remain unmet through 2027 and about 30% unmet for other products through 2028.

Key Points:

- Capacity sold out through early 2029.

- 70% of demand for some products unmet through 2027.

- 30% of demand for other products unmet through 2028.
