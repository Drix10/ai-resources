### 🚀 Starlink Bolivia – 4,700 antennas for schools nationwide

Starlink will provide connectivity across Bolivia, including remote ravines, with 4,700 antennas slated for schools nationwide. President Rodrigo Paz highlighted the network’s goal of open information access.

Key Points:

- 4,700 antennas to be installed in schools across Bolivia

- President Rodrigo Paz said the network will reach “the deepest ravine” and make information “open to everyone”
