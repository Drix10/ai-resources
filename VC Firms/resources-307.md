### 📊 CoinZoom Cash adds cash to account with flat 5% fee

CoinZoom Cash lets users load cash at retail stores into their CoinZoom account with a flat 5% fee and funds appear in minutes. The service works through the CoinZoom app by selecting a nearby store, generating a barcode, and handing cash to the cashier.

Key Points:

- Flat 5% fee for cash loads, advertised as up to 5× less than some Bitcoin ATMs.

- Load up to $999 cash at participating retailers, with a minimum load amount of $20.

### 🤖 Omnigraph live on Product Hunt launch announced

Omnigraph is live on Product Hunt as an open source graph database where agents write on separate branches and the graph lives in an S3 bucket. The author rebuilt their internal operating system around OmnigraphDB and reports insane improvements in retrieval speed and accuracy.

Key Points:

- **Launch**: Omnigraph is live on Product Hunt.

- **Architecture**: each agent writes on its own branch, and the graph lives in your own S3 bucket.
