- **LLM Reliability Issues**: LLMs have faced a number of reliability issues, including errors and inconsistencies in their output.

- **Context Treatment**: Bharadwaj argues that these issues are due to the way context is treated, with LLMs often relying on an unlimited buffer of context to generate output.
