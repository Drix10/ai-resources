### 🤖 AI economy becomes agent economy, enterprise AI spend $177B in 2026

Enterprise AI deployment spend jumped from $33B to $177B in one year, a $144B increase. 69 cents of every AI dollar went to frontier labs, and frontier models rose to 63% of model-layer spend.

Key Points:

- Enterprise AI spend increased by $144B, from $33B to $177B in one year.

- 69 cents of every AI dollar was spent on frontier labs.

- Frontier models grew from 35% to 63% of model-layer spend.

- Coding agents grew from $1B to $24B, while the broader coding market reached $34B.
