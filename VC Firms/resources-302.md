### 🤖 Vibe Vibers Battlegrounds bans 1000+ accounts and 70 aimbot users

Vibe Vibers Battlegrounds reports that over 1000 accounts have been banned and more than 70 aimbot users have been removed to improve fairness. The cleanup follows the detection of botfarm and script hunters.

Key Points:

- 1000+ accounts have been banned.

- Over 70 accounts caught using aimbots have been banned.

### 📊 TSM clarifies no DRAM entry, reaffirms foundry focus

TSM says it has no plans to enter DRAM manufacturing and reaffirms its strategic focus on foundry services as its core business. The statement reduces uncertainty about capital allocation and competitive positioning.

Key Points:

- No plans to enter DRAM manufacturing.

- Reaffirms strategic focus on foundry services as core business.
