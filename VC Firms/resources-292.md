### 📊 S&P 500 October median rest-of-month gain +1.73%

S&P 500 October median rest-of-month performance was +1.73% when down MTD in the final week, based on 21 prior Octobers over 50 years. Gains occurred 71% of the time.

Key Points:

- **Median rest-of-month gain**: +1.73% in those 21 Octobers.

- Gains happened 71% of the time under the same condition.

### 📊 Anthropic revenue per employee $6.6M, NDR ~900%

Anthropic reports revenue per employee of over $6.6M and a net dollar retention of ~900%. Revenue grew from $41M three years ago to $16.3B in the first half of 2026, and quarterly revenue rose from $4.7B in Q1 to $11.6B in Q2.

Key Points:

- **NDR**: ~900%

- **Revenue per employee**: over $6.6M, up from about $1.4M a year earlier

### 📊 Neviswealth raises $70M Series B, total $110M funding

Neviswealth announced a $70 million Series B round led by EQT Ventures, bringing total funding to $110 million in two years. The company says advisors spend 80% of time on admin and 20% with clients, and they aim to reverse that.

Key Points:

- **Advisor time split**: 80% admin, 20% client interaction.

- **Goal**: shift advisor time back to client work.

### 🤖 Canva Craft launches AI image model, ranks #4 globally

Canva Craft is a new AI image generation model that ranked #4 globally on DesignArena. It is built for design and can create images up to 2K resolution with editable composition, typography, and elements via Magic Layers. The model is available to Canva’s 250M+ users and includes a public API for developers.

Key Points:

- **Rank**: 4 globally on DesignArena, making Canva Research the world’s second-highest-ranked AI lab for image generation.

- **Capability**: Generates images at up to 2K resolution with precise control over composition, typography, and elements editable through Magic Layers.
