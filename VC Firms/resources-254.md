### 🤖 Business - Resource Allocation

Resource allocation decisions should be CEO-led, not delegated to others. This approach is advocated by William N. Thorndike Jr. in his book "The Outsiders: Eight Unconventional CEOs and Their Radically Rational Blueprint for Success".

Key Points:
• CEO-led allocation process is more effective than delegation.
• Delegation can lead to misaligned priorities and inefficient resource use.
• CEO involvement ensures strategic alignment and resource optimization.

### 📈 Finance - Fund Evaluation

Traditional fund evaluation often relies on paper valuations and unrealised markups. Managing a sustainable investment cycle requires returning capital consistently within committed fund tenures. Releasing realised capital shapes conversations with institutional investors far more effectively.

Key Points:
• Traditional fund evaluation relies on paper valuations and unrealised markups.
• Sustainable investment cycles require consistent capital returns.
