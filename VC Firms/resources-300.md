### 🤖 Tractian raises $130M Series D at $1.4B valuation

Key Points:

- $100M ARR, 90% year-over-year growth.

- $130M Series D funding at a $1.4B valuation.

- Monitors hundreds of thousands of assets in real time with sensors, software, and AI.
