### 🤖 Clostridium perfringens Delta toxin targets CD9 to drive NEC and brain injury

Clostridium perfringens Delta toxin is identified as a virulence factor that drives intestinal necrosis and acute cerebral injury in necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC). A genome-wide CRISPR screen reveals CD9 as the host receptor, and anti-Delta toxin antibody protects against disease progression.

Key Points:

- Delta toxin preferentially targets macrophages, activating inflammasome signalling and pyroptosis, leading to blood-brain barrier disruption.

- Systemic and myeloid-specific Cd9 knockout mice are protected from toxin-induced lethality, blood-brain barrier dysfunction and multiorgan injury.
