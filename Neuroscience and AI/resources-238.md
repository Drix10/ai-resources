### 🤖 Updates in MSA – CSF assay distinguishes MSA with type 2 fluorescence

Updates in MSA reports a CSF seeding assay that uses thioflavin T fluorescence to differentiate MSA from controls, showing a type 2 response pattern. The assay reports high specificity and sensitivity. Prevalence estimates cite MSA at 2–5 per 100,000 and Parkinson’s disease at 130–170 per 100,000.

Key Points:

- CSF assay produces a type 2 fluorescence response linked to MSA.

- **Reported prevalence**: MSA 2–5/100,000, Parkinson’s disease 130–170/100,000.
