### 📊 Brown fat study links active brown fat to mental health

Brown fat study links active brown fat to lower rates of type 2 diabetes, depression and anxiety. Researchers used an AI model on PET/CT scans of over 100,000 people to quantify active brown fat.

Key Points:

- **Sample size**: more than 100,000 people.

- AI model quantified active brown fat from PET/CT scans.

- Active brown fat correlated with reduced type 2 diabetes, depression and anxiety.

### 📊 lonvo-z phase 3 trial shows lower attack rate

lonvo-z in a phase 3 randomized trial lowered hereditary angioedema attack rate versus placebo. The trial compared lonvo-z to placebo in patients with hereditary angioedema.

Key Points:

- **Trial**: phase 3, randomized trial of lonvo-z.

- **Result**: lower attack rate for patients receiving lonvo-z than for placebo.
