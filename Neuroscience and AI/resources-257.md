### 🤖 Math Puzzle: The longest shuffle – 30 shuffles required

Math Puzzle: The longest shuffle explains that the maximum number of shuffles needed for a 10-card deck to return to its original order is 30. The answer comes from selecting cycle lengths 5, 3, 2 whose least common multiple is 30.

Key Points:

- **Maximum shuffles for a 10-card deck**: 30

- **Cycle lengths achieving this maximum**: 5 + 3 + 2, giving LCM = 5 × 3 × 2 = 30
