### 🤖 IEEE bulk retraction of 1,417 conference papers

IEEE bulk retraction of 1,417 conference papers announced on 28 October, the largest single bulk retraction by any publisher. The papers were linked to paper-mill advertisements and represent less than 0.06% of conference publications over six years.

Key Points:

- **Announcement date**: 28 October statement by IEEE in New York City.

- **Retraction count**: 1,417 conference papers withdrawn in one batch.

### 🤖 PROGRESS trial transcatheter aortic-valve replacement reduces 2-year events

PROGRESS trial presented at TCT2026 shows transcatheter aortic-valve replacement lowered the 2-year composite of death or heart-failure events to 16.7% versus 23.9% with surveillance. Results are from patients with moderate aortic stenosis and high-risk features.

Key Points:

- **2-year death or heart-failure event rate**: 16.7% with transcatheter aortic-valve replacement, 23.9% with surveillance.

- Presented at #TCT2026.

### 📊 MARRS enables up to 100% in vitro and 84% in vivo editing

MARRS is a multivalent ADAR1-recruiting RNA editing platform that achieves up to 100% adenosine-to-inosine editing in vitro and 84% in vivo. It uses engineered RNA aptamers targeting the Zα domain of ADAR1 p150 embedded in a circular RNA scaffold to recruit endogenous ADAR1.

Key Points:

- Editing efficiency reaches 100% in cultured cells.

- In mouse models, editing efficiency reaches 84% with high specificity.

### 🤖 Bispecific CCR8-CD3 antibody combats tumors

Bispecific CCR8-CD3 antibody depletes regulatory T cells and revives anti-tumor T cells, combating multiple tumor types in mice. It also potentiated immunotherapy in mice and was safe in large animals.

Key Points:

- **Target**: CCR8 to deplete regulatory T cells

- **Safety**: Safe in large animals

### 🤖 ASPIRED trial: 14-day ECG monitoring no benefit

ASPIRED trial found immediate 14-day ECG monitoring did not reduce patient-reported syncope episodes at 1 year compared with standard care. The findings are detailed in the full trial summary.

Key Points:

- **Result**: Immediate initiation of 14-day ECG monitoring did not result in significantly fewer patient-reported syncope episodes at 1 year than standard care.

- **Population**: Patients with syncope that remained unexplained after evaluation in the ED.

### 🤖 JEEPERS tool announced for detecting and correcting jagged-end errors in cfDNA methylation

JEEPERS is a tool to detect and correct jagged-end-induced errors in cell-free DNA methylation analysis. It is introduced as a new capability for handling these specific errors.

Key Points:

- **Tool**: JEEPERS detects jagged-end-induced errors in cfDNA methylation analysis.
