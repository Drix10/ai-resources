### 📊 Tenecteplase approved for acute ischemic stroke in 2025

Tenecteplase received FDA approval in 2025 for acute ischemic stroke. It can be given as a single bolus and is considered noninferior to alteplase in benefit and safety.

Key Points:

- FDA approved tenecteplase for acute ischemic stroke in 2025.

- Tenecteplase dosing uses a tiered system approved by the FDA.

### 🤖 Foodome project uses AI to catalog food molecules

Foodome uses AI to identify and catalog molecules in everyday foods, creating a resource for exploring their effects on human health and potential medical applications. The initiative aims to make invisible food chemistry visible.

Key Points:
