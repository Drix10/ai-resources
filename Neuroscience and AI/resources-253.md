### 🤖 PEPPER trial VTE prophylaxis after arthroplasty results

PEPPER trial found aspirin was not noninferior to rivaroxaban or warfarin for symptomatic VTE or death. Warfarin and rivaroxaban were not noninferior to aspirin for safety.

Key Points:

- Aspirin was not noninferior to rivaroxaban or warfarin for symptomatic VTE or death.

- Warfarin and rivaroxaban were not noninferior to aspirin for safety.

### 🤖 Neandertal babies were a lot like ours – newborn size same, faster growth

Neandertal babies were about the same size as modern human newborns but grew much faster through infancy, according to two studies of Neandertal remains. The research indicates comparable birth size and accelerated developmental rate.

Key Points:

- **Newborn size**: Neandertal newborns were about the same size as those of modern humans.

- **Development rate**: Neandertal infants developed faster through infancy.
