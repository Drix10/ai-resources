### 🤖 Castration extends male mouse lifespan by >30%

Castration extends male life span across many mammalian species, including humans, and neutering older male mice adds over 30% to lifespan. The preprint reports the effect occurs even when the procedure is done later in life.

Key Points:

- Removing the testicles extends male life span across many mammalian species, including humans.

- Neutering older male mice lengthens their lives by more than 30%.

### 🤖 Imaging exoplanets below the telescope stability limit – 2–15× sensitivity gain

Imaging exoplanets below the telescope stability limit introduces a physics-based starlight subtraction method that achieves 2–15 times better sensitivity than standard techniques. The approach uses automatic differentiation to optimize millions of model parameters on JWST NIRCam coronagraph data and enables the first detection of 51 Eridani b at 4.3 µm.

Key Points:

- Sensitivity 2–15 times superior to standard techniques.

- First detection of 51 Eridani b at 4.3 µm.

- Automatic differentiation optimizes millions of model parameters.
