### 🤖 HSV encephalitis corticosteroid therapy not linked to lower mortality

HSV encephalitis adjunctive corticosteroid therapy was not associated with lower in-hospital mortality. It also did not improve functional outcomes. Authors said further research is needed.

Key Points:

- Adjunctive corticosteroid therapy not associated with lower in-hospital mortality in HSV encephalitis patients

- Adjunctive corticosteroid therapy not associated with better functional outcomes in HSV encephalitis patients

### 📊 APOE4 carriers up to 15-fold higher Alzheimer’s risk

APOE4 carriers can be up to 15 times more likely to develop Alzheimer’s disease compared to those with the most common gene form. The linked article explains the genetic basis and risk levels for single and double APOE4 copies.

Key Points:

- **Risk increase**: two copies of APOE4 raise risk about 15-fold.

- **Single copy risk**: about two to three times higher than two APOE3 copies.
