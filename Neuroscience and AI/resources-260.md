### 🤖 Conservation of the tumour microenvironment across organs and species

Conservation of the tumour microenvironment across organs and species describes a pan-cancer single-cell analysis of the tumour microenvironment across 42 cancer types and two species. The study integrates 3,381 human and mouse scRNA-seq samples, covering more than 15 million cells, and provides an interactive web atlas (tmeatlas.stanford.edu) and an R package (scTME).

Key Points:

- Integrated 3,381 scRNA-seq samples from 42 cancer types in human and mouse.

- Analyzed over 15 million cells, creating a web atlas and the scTME software package.

### 🤖 Brain keeps the score: trauma leaves scars in 300 brains

Study of over 300 post-mortem brains finds epigenetic DNA methylation changes linked to PTSD and depression. Researchers examined methylation in amygdala, hippocampus and prefrontal cortex. PTSD brains show reduced oligodendrocyte levels in the amygdala.

Key Points:

- 101 PTSD, 101 depression, 100 control donors analyzed

- DNA methylation examined across >850,000 genomic regions

- Amygdala oligodendrocytes significantly reduced in PTSD cases

🔗 Resources:

- [Original post](https://x.com/Nature/status/2126691584527724652)

### 🤖 short-latency afferent inhibition as functional marker

short-latency afferent inhibition could serve as a functional marker of cholinergic dysfunction linked to basal forebrain atrophy and cognitive impairment. Martín-Rodríguez et al. report the association between cholinergic basal forebrain atrophy and cognitive impairment in Parkinson’s disease.

Key Points:

- **Link**: cholinergic basal forebrain atrophy associated with cognitive impairment in Parkinson’s disease.

- **Suggestion**: short-latency afferent inhibition may serve as functional marker of cholinergic dysfunction.
