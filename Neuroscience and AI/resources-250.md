### 🤖 OnScene study survival with prehospital extracorporeal

OnScene study reports a higher percentage surviving to hospital discharge for patients 50 years or younger with refractory out-of-hospital cardiac arrest using prehospital extracorporeal versus conventional care. Editorial titled “Bringing Rapid Reperfusion to More Patients with Out-of-Hospital Cardiac Arrest” is linked.

Key Points:

- Patients 50 years of age or younger with refractory out-of-hospital cardiac arrest.

- Higher percentage surviving to hospital discharge with prehospital extracorporeal than with conventional care.

### 🚨 Pelacarsen trial fails to reduce cardiovascular events

Pelacarsen, an Lp(a)-lowering drug, failed in the Lp(a)HORIZON trial to cut heart problems or stroke. The drug can lower lipoprotein(a) levels by 80% or more.

Key Points:

- Lp(a)HORIZON trial showed no reduction in risk of heart problems and stroke.

- Pelacarsen lowers lipoprotein(a) levels by 80% or more.

### 🚀 PROMISE rover launch to moon in 2028 announced

PROMISE rover will launch to the moon in late 2028 on a Blue Origin lander for an 18-month primary mission. The rover is modeled after Mars rovers and will drill and study lunar regolith and radiation near the south pole.

Key Points:

- **Launch date**: late 2028, 18-month primary mission.

- **Launch vehicle**: Blue Origin lander.

- **Mission role**: scout Moon Base operations and prospect resources near the lunar south pole.

- **Project lead**: James Keane, planetary scientist at JPL.
