### 🤖 Medical Education - EEG Skills for Medical Students

Medical students can benefit from a 2-week elective to build EEG skills. A structured workplace-based assessment showed that the share of EEG encounters where faculty reported "I helped a lot" dropped from 48.7% in week 1 to 21.1% in week 2.

Key Points:

- **Structured Workplace-Based Assessment**: A 2-week elective with a structured assessment helped medical students improve their EEG skills.

- **EEG Encounter Feedback**: Faculty feedback on EEG encounters showed a significant decrease in the percentage of encounters where faculty reported "I helped a lot" from week 1 to week 2.

- **Actionable Takeaway**: Medical schools can consider implementing a similar elective program to improve students' EEG skills.
