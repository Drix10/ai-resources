### 🤖 tarlatamab–durvalumab therapy shows 21.1-month OS

tarlatamab–durvalumab maintenance therapy achieved a median overall survival of 21.1 months in a prespecified interim analysis of a phase 3 trial. Durvalumab alone resulted in a median overall survival of 12.7 months. The data are from the DeLLphi-305 trial presented at ESMO 26.

Key Points:

- **Median overall survival**: 21.1 months with tarlatamab–durvalumab maintenance.

- **Median overall survival**: 12.7 months with durvalumab alone.
