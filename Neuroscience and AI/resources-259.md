### 📊 AR1001 phase III trial shows 31% symptom slowdown in subgroup

AR1001, an erectile dysfunction drug, slowed symptom worsening by 31% over a year in a subgroup of early Alzheimer’s patients not on other Alzheimer’s medicines. The phase III trial involved 1,535 participants across 13 countries, with daily 30 mg dosing.

Key Points:

- **Trial size**: 1,535 early Alzheimer’s participants in 13 countries.

- **Subgroup result**: 31% reduction in symptom worsening for 604 participants not taking other Alzheimer’s drugs.

### 🤖 Echidna integrates genomic and transcriptomic data to reveal CNA impact on tumor evolution

Echidna is a Bayesian hierarchical model that combines single-cell RNA-seq and bulk whole-genome sequencing to infer copy number alterations across samples and time points. It quantifies gene dosage effects (GDX) and improves clonal reconstruction in patient tumor specimens.

Key Points:

- Integrates scRNA-seq and bulk WGS data for joint CNA inference.

- Computes a GDX metric to link phenotypic shifts to genetic alterations.

🔗 Resources:

- [Original post](https://x.com/naturemethods/status/2124960736006844422)
