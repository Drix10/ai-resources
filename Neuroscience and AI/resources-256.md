### 🚨 Lead azide detonation in Iowa – 68 drums planned

Lead azide detonation in Iowa – 68 drums planned to be exploded on Friday at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant. Health officials warn of lead-containing particulate matter and advise residents to stay indoors and seal ventilation.

Key Points:

- Detonation of 68 drums of lead azide scheduled for Friday at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant.

- EPA emergency permit requires daily environmental monitoring and cleanup of high-touch surfaces where lead is detected.

### 🤖 FAA ground stop shows risky air traffic control upgrades

FAA ground stop shows risky air traffic control upgrades, a four-hour nationwide halt canceled over 18,000 flights. The FAA is swapping old copper links for fiber, now 69% complete, and has asked Congress to raise upgrade funding to $50 billion.

Key Points:

- Nationwide ground stop on November 13 lasted more than four hours

- Over 18,000 flights canceled and over 28,000 delayed

- 69% of old copper wire replaced with fiber-optic cable

- Funding request raised from $30 billion to $50 billion

### 📊 Diabetes may change how Parkinson's looks – 40 patients

Diabetes may change how Parkinson's looks, showing worse gait and balance and more autonomic and urinary symptoms in a study of 40 patients. The research is cross-sectional and asks if metabolic health belongs in PD care.

Key Points:

- 40 patients with diabetes showed worse gait and balance.

- Patients also reported more autonomic and urinary symptoms.

### 🤖 Butterfield et al. study over 200 individuals linking D4Z4 methylation to FSHD severity

Butterfield et al. bring together genetic and epigenetic analyses of over 200 individuals from a historic Utah kindred with FSHD. They link stable, individual differences in D4Z4 methylation to disease severity.

Key Points:

- **Sample size**: over 200 individuals from a historic Utah kindred with FSHD

- **Finding**: stable, individual differences in D4Z4 methylation correlate with disease severity
