### 🤖 Nigrosome-1 neuromelanin and iron coupling in Parkinson disease

Nigrosome-1 neuromelanin and iron coupling in idiopathic and GBA-associated Parkinson disease reports that multimodal MRI captures PD-related nigral degeneration most strongly in nigrosome-1. The study quantified neuromelanin- and iron-sensitive MRI in 30 iPD, 26 GBA-PD and 30 healthy controls.

Key Points:

- NM-sensitive nigrosome-1 markers gave the strongest patient-control discrimination, especially between GBA-PD and healthy controls.

- The normal neuromelanin-iron coupling pattern was reversed in Parkinson disease subjects.

### 🚀 Microglial NUS1 deficiency drives dopaminergic loss

Microglial NUS1 deficiency drives dopaminergic neurodegeneration through intercellular iron dysregulation. Ferroptosis inhibitor liproxstatin-1 alleviated neuron loss and PD-like symptoms in mouse models.

Key Points:

- NUS1 deficiency in microglia disrupts intercellular iron homeostasis, causing neuronal iron overload.

- Liproxstatin-1 treatment reduced dopaminergic neuron loss and improved motor deficits in mice.
