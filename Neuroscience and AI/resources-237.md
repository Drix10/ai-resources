### 📊 Cortical Disarray Measurement reflects FTLD pathology

Cortical Disarray Measurement, a diffusion MRI metric, corresponded with autopsy-confirmed pathology in 36 FTLD cases. Associations differed by molecular subtype and were strongest in FTLD-TDP cases.

Key Points:

- **Sample size**: 36 autopsy-confirmed cases (19 FTLD-tau, 17 FTLD-TDP).

- Strongest MRI-pathology association observed in FTLD-TDP (TDP-43 pathology).

### 📊 icotrokinra phase 2 trial shows higher week 12 response

icotrokinra in a phase 2 trial showed a clinical response at week 12 that was more common than placebo. The trial tested three oral doses once daily in moderately to severely active ulcerative colitis.

Key Points:

- Clinical response at week 12 more common with icotrokinra than placebo.

- Three tested doses of once-daily oral icotrokinra were used.
