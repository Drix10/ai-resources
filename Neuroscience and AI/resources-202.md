### Transthyretin Amyloid Cardiomyopathy

A secondary analysis of the CARDIOTTRansform trial revealed that eplontersen benefited patients with transthyretin amyloid cardiomyopathy who were not on stabilizers, but not those who were.

Key Points:
• Eplontersen benefited patients with transthyretin amyloid cardiomyopathy who were not on stabilizers.
• Patients on stabilizers did not benefit from eplontersen.
• The CARDIOTTRansform trial provided insights into the treatment of transthyretin amyloid cardiomyopathy.
