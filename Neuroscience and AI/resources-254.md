### 🚀 Gene therapy inserts receptors to dampen seizures in three patients

Gene therapy inserted receptors into the human brain to manipulate neuron activity with a drug, targeting drug-resistant epilepsy. The trial treated three people, showed no major side effects and appeared to reduce seizure incidence.

Key Points:

- Therapy inserted receptors that allow drug-controlled neuron activity.

- Trial involved three patients with drug-resistant epilepsy and reported reduced seizures.
