### 🚀 Medical Research - Randomized Trials

Randomized trials have become a cornerstone of medical research, providing high-quality evidence to inform clinical decision-making. In this article, we will explore two recent randomized trials that have shed light on the effectiveness of different treatments.

### Key Points:

- **Noninferiority of 5-year surveillance colonoscopy**: A randomized trial found that first surveillance colonoscopy at 5 years after the removal of high-risk adenomas was noninferior to first colonoscopy at 3 years for the incidence of colorectal cancer.
- **Short duration of systemic antibiotics**: Another randomized trial demonstrated that a short duration of systemic antibiotics was noninferior to a long duration regarding treatment failure at 12 months in adults who received local antibiotics during surgery for orthopedic infection.
- **Treatment de-escalation and discontinuation**: A review of the current evidence on treatment de-escalation and discontinuation in MOGAD and AQP4-Ab NMOSD proposes future directions for clinical consideration.
