### 🤖 Google TPU 8tx and 8i Max launch specialized chips

Google introduced TPU 8tx for secure AI and TPU 8i Max for double compute and memory, both new variants of its eighth-gen TPUs. The 8tx uses the same silicon as the 8t and pairs with AMD’s fifth-generation EPYC CPUs. The 8i Max combines two 8i chips in a single package to double compute, SRAM and HBM bandwidth.

Key Points:

- TPU 8tx delivers up to half the latency of the 8t with near-zero performance penalty for security.

- TPU 8i Max doubles compute, on-chip SRAM and HBM bandwidth by packaging two 8i chips together.
