### 🤖 Anthropic Claude chip design and OpenAI Jalapeño chip news

Anthropic’s Claude AI models are being used to design custom AI chips, with Claude Fable 5.1 topping a recent benchmark and Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 creating three electronic devices from a paragraph prompt. OpenAI’s Jalapeño chip, designed with its own models and Broadcom, was built in nine months and will ship in volume next year.

Key Points:

- Claude Fable 5.1 scored the best on a chip design benchmark released by ChipAgents and Nvidia, achieving 16.6% on the metric.

- OpenAI’s Jalapeño chip design used its frontier AI models, took nine months to develop, and is slated for significant volume use next year.

### 🤖 Nvidia – board prepares to oust Jensen Huang, stock down 8% after hours

Nvidia’s board is preparing to remove CEO Jensen Huang and consider interim CEO options, with shares falling about 8% after hours to a 52-week low. The board will meet on Wednesday to discuss Huang’s future and has hired an outside firm to identify permanent CEO candidates.

Key Points:

- Board plans interim CEO, possibly CFO Colette Kress, and is reviewing external candidates including Apple hardware chief Johny Srouji.

- Nvidia shares rose ~3% on Wednesday, then dropped ~8% after hours, down >11% over five days, reaching a 52-week low.
