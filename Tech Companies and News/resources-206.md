The AI jobs boom is here, and it's creating over 1M new positions in the US, offsetting back-office layoffs. Key Points:


- **AI Job Creation**: AI is generating over 1M new positions in the US, primarily in AI engineering, data center construction, and other AI-related roles.

### The Future of Debt and Public Finance

Wars used to be the major driver of public debt. Now, we have World War II levels of debt without any major war, leaving us unprepared if one were to happen. This shift highlights the importance of managing public debt and preparing for future economic challenges.

- **Public Debt**: Public debt has increased significantly, driven by factors other than war.
