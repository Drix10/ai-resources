### 🤖 Europe grid dip every 60 s tied to battery checks

Europe’s power grid dips every 60 seconds, traced to large-scale battery storage systems that check state of charge each minute. Researchers report the one-minute signal grew globally, with the amplitude in Ireland quadrupling between 2016 and 2021, and propose randomising the checks.

Key Points:

- **Frequency**: dip every 60 seconds.

- **Cause**: battery storage systems perform a power check at the start of every minute.
