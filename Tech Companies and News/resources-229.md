### 🤖 ProjectDiscovery fine‑tunes model with LoRA for under $50

ProjectDiscovery demonstrated hiding a backdoor in a downloadable open model for less than $50. Their researchers used a small LoRA adapter and a rented L4 GPU for about 2.5 hours. They altered 125 parameters. The experiment shows low cost for such modifications.

Key Points:

- **Cost**: under $50 total expense

- **Compute**: 2.5 hours on a rented L4 GPU with a LoRA adapter

### 📊 AI agents rarely select WordPress when platform omitted

Seven popular AI agents were tested for website platform choice when no platform was specified. The test covered nearly 300 runs. The agents chose WordPress for 0% of the generated sites. When asked later, 50–74% said WordPress would be a good fit.

Key Points:

- **Zero selection**: 0% of sites used WordPress.

- **Later endorsement**: 50–74% named WordPress as a good choice.
