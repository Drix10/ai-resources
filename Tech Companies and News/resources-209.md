### 🚨 AI Won't Reduce Clinician Jobs: 3 Reasons Why

Eric Topol, a renowned expert in AI and healthcare, explains why AI won't reduce clinician jobs. He highlights three reasons: Jevon's paradox, "lump of labor" fallacy, and tasks ≠ skills.

Key Points:

- **Jevon's Paradox**: More efficiency in AI can lead to increased use, rather than reduced use.

- **"Lump of Labor" Fallacy**: The type of work changes, rather than reducing the amount of work.

- **Tasks ≠ Skills**: AI can perform tasks, but it may not possess the same skills as clinicians.
