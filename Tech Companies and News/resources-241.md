### 🤖 TSMC considers 3D DRAM production at Nvidia's request

TSMC is weighing entry into advanced memory by planning 3D DRAM manufacturing after Nvidia asked for more supply. The move could involve new fabs or acquiring an existing plant, similar to Micron’s recent $1.8 billion fab purchase. TSMC already holds patents on vertical DRAM designs.

Key Points:

- TSMC is drawing up plans for 3D DRAM at Nvidia’s request.

- Options include building new fabs or buying an existing plant; Micron bought a fab from PSMC for $1.8 billion.
