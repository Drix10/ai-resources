### 🤖 Inkling 2 Mini 125 tok/sec decode on M5 Max MacBook Pro

Inkling 2 Mini runs at 125 tokens per second decode speed on a 48GB M5 Max MacBook Pro. A quoted tweet lists 210 tok/sec decode and 3620 tok/sec prefill on a 96GB M5 Ultra Mac Studio, and 125 tok/sec decode and 1750 tok/sec prefill on the M5 Max MacBook Pro.

Key Points:

- 125 tok/sec decode speed on 48GB M5 Max MacBook Pro

- 210 tok/sec decode, 3620 tok/sec prefill on 96GB M5 Ultra Mac Studio
