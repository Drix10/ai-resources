### 📊 Tech jobs are concentrating in the Bay Area – 15% share

Tech jobs are concentrating in the Bay Area with 15% of all open tech roles now located there, a record high. Product roles are especially dense, with one in four PM and design openings and one in five engineering openings in the Bay Area. Remote tech roles have fallen to 14% of openings, the lowest on record.

Key Points:

- 15% of all open tech roles are now in the Bay Area, about one in seven.

- Remote tech roles offer a remote option in only 14% of openings, down from 21% in late 2022.

### 🚨 FAA telecom network update caused reboot, L3Harris operates system

FAA private communications network update caused the system to reboot, leading to a communications outage. The network is operated by L3Harris.

Key Points:

- Update to private communications network that connects America's air traffic control system caused reboot.

- System is operated by L3Harris.

### 🤖 Tofu-1 speech-to-speech model launch preview

Tofu-1 is Bland’s speech-to-speech model for high-stakes calls that can listen while speaking and reason while talking. A research preview is open to a limited number of enterprises.

Key Points:

- Listens while it speaks, reasons while it talks.

- Can distinguish the caller from other sounds such as a TV or a crying baby.
