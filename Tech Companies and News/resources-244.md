### 🤖 XERJ Reader-1 launches neurosymbolic model with 20-90× speed boost

XERJ Reader-1 is a neurosymbolic model that points to exact words in a document, writes a small program over them, and computes answers. It claims 20-90× faster execution and 10-1,000× cheaper operation because output tokens are free. Each answer is delivered as a replayable program.

Key Points:

- 20-90× faster performance claim.

- 10-1,000× cheaper cost claim (output tokens free).

- Answers provided as verifiable programs that can be replayed.
