### 🤝 Social Networks - In-Between Networks

We have Twitter for everyone and we have group chats for 10-20 people, but we need more social networks for the in-between - for the 200-500 people you want to chat and sense-make with more regularly, async & sync. Early Clubhouse was this, which is partly what made it so unique.

Key Points:

- **In-Between Networks**: In-between networks are needed to fill the gap between large-scale social networks and small group chats.

- **Features**: In-between networks could include features such as async & sync communication, sense-making, and more.

- **Opportunity**: The opportunity for in-between networks is significant, particularly for those that can provide a more targeted and relevant experience for users.
