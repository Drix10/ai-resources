### 📊 AMD models improve flagship GPU IP 14% PPA

AMD models identified up to 14% PPA improvement on flagship GPU IP in less than five months. The same approach helped improve Jalapeño, the first custom inference chip, with gains verified on real silicon. New evaluations show the models getting better at certain chip design tasks. Collaboration with Synopsys will bring model capabilities and their EDA tools to more chip designers.

Key Points:

- 14% PPA improvement on flagship GPU IP in under five months

- Jalapeño custom inference chip gains verified on real silicon

- Working with Synopsys to integrate model capabilities with EDA tools
