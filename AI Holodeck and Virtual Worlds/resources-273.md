### 🤖 Tavus PAL Kiosk mode pilot launch with 134 ms rendering

Tavus PAL Kiosk mode lets a walk-up kiosk check in guests without menus. The pilot is open to a limited number of companies. Tavus PALs render video from audio in 134 ms, the fastest on the market.

Key Points:

- Kiosk mode places a Tavus PAL on a walk-up kiosk for guest check-in.

- Rendering latency is 134 ms from audio to video.

### 🤖 Beam model launch with open weights and partner ecosystem

Beam by Reflection AI is now available on Design Arena with open-weight releases under Apache 2.0. The release includes multiple precision weight formats and a drafter component.

Key Points:

- **Release**: Weights, tokenizer, and reference implementation

- **Formats**: BF16, FP8 and NVFP4 weights and a DFlash2 drafter
