### 🚀 AI Security - Know Your Agent (KYA) Standard

The Know Your Agent (KYA) standard is a crucial aspect of AI security, ensuring that AI systems can verify the identity of their users. However, most teams trying to implement a KYA standard lack a moat, simply aggregating existing credentials, which creates the same shortcomings as Know Your Customer (KYC). This approach is no longer viable, and it's clear that traditional KYC is not the future.

Key Points:

- **KYA vs KYC**: KYA is a more advanced approach that focuses on verifying the identity of AI agents, whereas KYC is primarily used for human identity verification.

- **Limitations of KYC**: Traditional KYC methods are not designed to handle the complexities of AI systems, leading to security vulnerabilities and shortcomings.

- **KYA Standard**: A KYA standard would require AI systems to verify the identity of their users, ensuring that only authorized agents can interact with the system.
