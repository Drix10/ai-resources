### 🤖 MiniMax H3 2K video generator announced with 4-15 s render

MiniMax H3 is an online tool that generates 2K AI videos in 4-15 seconds from text, images and multimodal references. The post also highlights Idol, a free open-source model that creates 3D assets from a single image in about 45 seconds on 8GB VRAM.

Key Points:

- Idol runs on 8GB VRAM.

- MiniMax H3 produces 2K video in 4-15 seconds.
