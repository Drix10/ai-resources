### 🤖 MultiSet AI VPS achieves 79.74% within 10 cm & 1°

MultiSet AI's VPS can position location and orientation in AR space from a single smartphone photo, reaching 79.74% of cases with position error under 10 cm and angle error under 1°. It exceeds the prior best of ImLoc's 74.24% by 5.50 points.

Key Points:

- 79.74% achieved position error within 10 cm and angle error within 1°.

- Surpasses ImLoc's previous best of 74.24% by 5.50 points.
