### 🤖 Idol – open image-to-3D model with 600M params

Idol is an open image-to-3D model that generates full 3D objects from a single image, using 600M parameters and under 7.2GB VRAM. It can produce 1M Gaussian splats in about 3 seconds on consumer GPUs.

Key Points:

- **Parameters**: 600M

- **Speed**: 1M splats in 3 s
