### 🤖 GridCraft, DeckCraft, WordCraft released on GitHub

GridCraft, DeckCraft and WordCraft are free tools built with Anthropic’s Claude and available on GitHub. Brandon Thomas, creator of ArtCraft, built them as alternatives to Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint.

Key Points:

- **Developer**: Brandon Thomas created GridCraft, DeckCraft and WordCraft with Claude.

- **Distribution**: All three are free to download on GitHub.

### 🤖 MiniMax H3 generates 15-second cinematic AI video

MiniMax H3 produces a 15 second photorealistic action-comedy video of a corgi spy in an office, demonstrating consistent storytelling. The online tool creates 2K AI videos of 4-15 seconds from text prompts, images, and multimodal references.

Key Points:

- **MiniMax H3 test**: 15 s, 16:9 photorealistic cinematic action-comedy with a single corgi character.

- **MiniMax H3 online**: generates 2K AI videos 4-15 seconds from text, images, and multimodal references.
