### 🤖 Fluid AI announces multi‑industry agentic AI platform

Fluid AI introduced a single platform that adapts to six regulated industries and supports 27 use cases. The platform runs AI agents across voice, chat, email and WhatsApp and is SOC2 certified, on‑premise ready, and supports over 150 languages.

Key Points:

- **Industries**: banking, insurance, oil & gas, manufacturing, telecom, IT & HR (six total).

- **Capabilities**: deploy AI agents that execute multi‑step workflows, retain full context, and log every action for auditability.

### 🤖 Infinite image grid for portfolios and photo galleries

An infinite image grid enables portfolios and photo galleries. It supports drag and scroll in any direction, clicking images to expand, and smooth open and close animations with GSAP Flip. The component works with mouse, trackpad and touch and is fully responsive. Code and an AI setup prompt are available in the Vault.

Key Points:

- **Feature**: Drag + scroll in any direction

- **Feature**: Smooth open + close with GSAP Flip
