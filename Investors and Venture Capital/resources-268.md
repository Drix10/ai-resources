### 🚀 Praxis Practical Witness Encryption for NP announced with 7.88 GiB ciphertext

Praxis is a practical witness-encryption scheme for NP statements with a 7.88 GiB ciphertext at 100-bit security. It encrypts to a Groth16 verifier on BN254 and reduces size and cost by over ten orders of magnitude compared to prior constructions. The research is experimental and may enable trust-minimized Bitcoin applications.

Key Points:

- Ciphertext size is 7.88 GiB at 100-bit security.

- Encryption requires 2^34.2 base-field multiplications; decryption averages 2^33.0 operations.
