### 📊 BitcoinTalk Satoshi registration 17 years ago, hash rate growth

BitcoinTalk Satoshi registration 17 years ago shows hash rate then ~3.5 MH/s and now ~1,051 EH/s. Supply mined then was 6.6% and now 95.8%. Founder’s last post was December 2010 and rules remain unchanged.

Key Points:

- **Hashrate then**: ~3.5 MH/s

- **Hashrate now**: ~1,051 EH/s

- **Supply mined then**: 6.6%

- **Supply mined now**: 95.8%
