### 🚀 Ueno Bank collapse, $146 million loan to owner firms

Ueno Bank collapsed after lending $146 million to three companies linked to its owner without real collateral. At the end of June, 29% of its deposits were government money, much from the state pension fund, and all eight executives are now in custody.

Key Points:

- **Loan**: $146 million to three companies linked to owner Groupupo Vquez with no real collateral

- **Deposits**: 29% were government money, much from the state pension fund

- **Executives**: All eight executives involved are now in custody

### 🚀 Russia barrage balloon nets claim first drone catch

Russia's new barrage balloon system uses nets and Kevlar cords and reports a first drone capture. The developer says the system has achieved its first catch.

Key Points:

- Nets and Kevlar cords deployed from balloons.

- Developer reports first drone caught.
