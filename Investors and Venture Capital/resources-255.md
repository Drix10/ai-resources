### 📊 daytonaio 37.5x growth to $100M run rate this year

daytonaio reports a 37.5× increase this year, reaching a $100M run rate. Four quarters ago the run rate was $1M. The company pivoted, handed old customers to competitors, rebuilt from scratch, and relaunched with a few months of runway left.

Key Points:

- Run rate crossed $100M.

- Run rate was $1M four quarters ago.

### 🤖 Tana Bring Your Own Agents launch adds live AI agents to meetings

Tana introduces Bring Your Own Agents, letting Claude, ChatGPT, arithecfo or Lovable join Zoom, Meet, Teams or Tana calls. Agents hear conversation, see screen, keep connectors, skills and memory, and suggest actions during the meeting. Users approve suggestions and the work is completed while the discussion continues.

Key Points:

- **Launch**: Bring Your Own Agents is now available in Tana.

- **Supported agents**: Claude, ChatGPT, arithecfo and Lovable can operate in Zoom, Meet, Teams or Tana meetings.
