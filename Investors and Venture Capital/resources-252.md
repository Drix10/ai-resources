### 📊 Grok Bot daily active users grow to over 600K in two months

Grok Bot daily active users rose from under 100K in early September to over 600K by October 26, a more than 6× increase in less than two months. The growth streak reached 16 consecutive days, the longest since launch.

Key Points:

- Daily active users under 100K in early September.

- Daily active users over 600K on October 26.
