### 📊 Anthropic compute efficiency $2.08 revenue per $1

Anthropic generated $2.08 of revenue for every $1 spent on compute in Q2'26. Revenue per $1 of compute rose from $0.40 in Q1'25 to $2.08 in Q2'26, a 5.2× efficiency increase. Q2'26 revenue grew 1,368% YoY while compute costs grew 300%.

Key Points:

- **Revenue per $1 of compute Q2'26**: $2.08

- **Compute efficiency jump since Q1 2025**: 5.2×
