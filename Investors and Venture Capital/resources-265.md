### 🚀 Real Ice refreezes Arctic patch 50 cm thicker

Real Ice refreezes Arctic patch, achieving about 50 cm thicker ice on a test site. They drill through winter ice, pump seawater up, and the cold air freezes it into a fresh layer. Thickening an area twice the size of California is estimated at $5-6B per year.

Key Points:

- **Method**: drill through ice and pump seawater up on top, freezing air turns it into a fresh layer.

- **Result**: test patch came out about 50 cm thicker than the ice around it.
