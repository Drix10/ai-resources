### 🤖 Starshield speed limited to 161 km/h, affecting interceptions

Starshield is being limited to 161 km/h (100 mph) across Ukraine’s front line and occupied territories. The restriction hampers interception of Russian jet-powered drones, leading to missed strikes. Ukrainian officials are investigating and contacting the American side.

Key Points:

- **Speed restriction**: 161 km/h (100 mph) limit on Starshield.

- **Impact**: interception systems cannot reach sufficient speeds to stop Russian jet-powered drones.

### 🤖 OpenEvidence collaboration with ACP announced, licensing content

OpenEvidence and ACP announced a collaboration to bring trusted, evidence-based clinical content to physicians at the point of care. OpenEvidence will license ACP’s content.
