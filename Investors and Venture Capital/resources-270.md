### 🚀 SpaceX Starmind constellation FCC approval and launch timeline

SpaceX's Starmind constellation received FCC approval for up to a million orbital data center satellites, with the first launch expected next year. The company aims for 100,000 satellites by 2035 and a million by 2040.

Key Points:

- FCC approval for up to a million Starmind satellites.

- Target of 100,000 satellites by 2035 and a million by 2040.

### 🤖 State pension and Universal Credit portals remain offline after confirmed DWP cyber attack

State pension, Universal Credit and Personal Independence Payment portals are still offline after a confirmed DWP cyber attack. The DWP is working with the National Cyber Security Centre and Government Cyber Co-ordination Centre to investigate an attempt to gain unauthorised access. Payments continue as normal and no personal data breach has been identified.

Key Points:

- Online services for state pensions, Universal Credit and PIP taken offline on Thursday as a precaution.

- No evidence that personal information has been compromised; regular benefit and pension payments remain unaffected.
