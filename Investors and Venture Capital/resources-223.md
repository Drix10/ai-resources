Technocore Chat experiment, which involves sending random messages into busy rooms and measuring the response.

- **Technocore Chat**: Technocore Chat is an experiment that involves sending random messages into busy rooms and measuring the response.

- **Signed by one key**: Each probe post is signed by one key, which helps to identify the source of the message.

- **Causal influence**: The experiment aims to measure the causal influence of the probe posts on the response.
