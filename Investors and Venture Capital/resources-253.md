### 📊 Sensex best performing index in October with +2.73% gain

Sensex was the top-performing global index in October, posting a +2.73% return. Other major indices showed mixed results, with the DAX up 2.46% and the S&P 500 down 0.93%.

Key Points:

- **Sensex**: +2.73% in October 2026

- **S&P 500**: -0.93% in October 2026

### 🤖 agent-desktop launch – a computer-wide coding agent tool

agent-desktop is introduced as a new agent-browser that runs on macOS, Windows and Linux. It operates in the background while you work and lets multiple agents use the computer in parallel, each with its own cursor.

Key Points:
