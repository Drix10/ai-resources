### 🤖 Apple Speech framework STT and on-device Phonon TTS in voice assistant UX

Apple Speech framework provides speech-to-text and an on-device Phonon model supplies text-to-speech for a 3rd-party voice assistant that launches via an action button without opening the app. The UI is generated in the background and an app intent snippet displays a visual response.

Key Points:

- Action button triggers the assistant and entire flow happens without opening the app

### 📊 Merger - political risk clarified after midterms, no formal block

Merger remains possible despite the November midterms, which increase political risk but do not create a formal blocking mechanism. Key hurdles include shareholder vote, special-committee processes, antitrust review, SEC rules, and potential CFIUS review.

Key Points:

- Midterm outcome validates political risk and makes a low-scrutiny path less likely.

- Next concrete markers are special-committee work, disclosures before Tesla’s annual meeting on Dec 3, and formal terms if/when they emerge.
