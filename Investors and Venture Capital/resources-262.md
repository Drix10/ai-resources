### 🚨 Outlook reports 130,000 outages as many platforms fail

Outlook reports 130,000 outages as many platforms fail, with spikes across Outlook, Microsoft 365, Teams, Azure, Gmail, AWS, Cloudflare, Zoom, Steam, X, Facebook. Microsoft confirmed widespread problems and is restarting affected servers and redirecting traffic.

Key Points:

- 130,000 reports for Outlook alone.

- Microsoft is restarting affected servers and redirecting traffic.

### 📊 U.S. housing market October sales drop 44% from peak

U.S. housing market October sales fell to 3.75M annualized, down 44% from the pandemic peak. Existing sales hit the lowest level since 2010 and demand is described as the biggest depression ever when adjusted for housing stock size.

Key Points:

- **Sales**: 3.75M annualized units in Oct 2026, down 44% from pandemic peak.

- **Existing home sales**: lowest level since 2010.
