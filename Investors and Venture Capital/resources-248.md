### 📊 Diesel sanctions and export pledge – volume details

Sanctions were placed on diesel originating from Russia. Moscow pledged to export the same volume it is currently managing. A tweet notes that 500 kt in November equals 125 kbpd of diesel. Russia normally exports about ten times that amount when its refineries are not damaged.

Key Points:

- Sanctions on diesel origin and pledged export volume are equal.

- 500 kt in November equals 125 kbpd of diesel.
