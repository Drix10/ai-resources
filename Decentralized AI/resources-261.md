### 🤖 GEODNET network enables cm-level RTK for parking spaces

GEODNET's network of 22K+ stations across 176 countries provides RTK accuracy of a few centimeters, enough to identify a specific parking space. A parking space is about 2.5 meters (8 to 9 feet) wide, and regular GPS can be off by several meters.

Key Points:

- **Parking space width**: about 2.5 meters (8 to 9 feet)

- **RTK accuracy**: a few centimeters, identifies space, row, and side of line
