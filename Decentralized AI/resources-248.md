### 📊 Skill Store 33 slide deck scores and quality metrics

Skill Store 33 evaluated 150 slide decks with an LLM judge. The average score improved from 24 to 94 out of 100. Hard-to-read slides dropped from 86% to 2% and made‑up numbers fell from 13% to 3%. The store opens later this month and each skill is verified and measured.
