### 📊 Stablecoin supply shrinking 17B in 30 days

Stablecoin supply is shrinking at a pace not seen in over a year, down about $17B in the last 30 days. Leveraged positions on Ethereum fell about $14B and redemptions now exceed new mints.

Key Points:

- **Supply drop**: $17B in 30 days.

- **Ethereum leveraged positions unwind**: stablecoins fell $14B.

### 🤖 Keki – Mac menu-bar app connects coding agents

Keki is a Mac menu-bar app that connects to Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Grok agents and shows cards when they need input. The app displays question or approval cards next to the cursor and passes answers back without typing. It runs on Apple Silicon Macs and includes a node that sends a signed note to Vistara’s points server about once a minute.

Key Points:

- Works with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Grok on Apple Silicon Macs.

- Sends a signed note to Vistara’s points server about once a minute when an agent finishes work.
