### 🚀 Gaussian Light Transport

A new method for global illumination just dropped: “Gaussian Light Transport” skips neural networks entirely and solves the full rendering equation as a 13D Gaussian mixture over space, direction, normals, and materials. The result? 10–23× faster training (7–12 min on RTX 4080).

Key Points:


- **


- **Gaussian Light Transport:** This new method uses a 13D Gaussian mixture to solve the full rendering equation.

- **Faster Training:** The result is 10–23× faster training times.

- **RTX 4080:** The method can be trained on a RTX 4080 in 7–12 minutes.


**Actionable Takeaway:**

- **Explore Gaussian Light Transport:** Consider exploring this new method for global illumination.

### 🚀 InfiniBand and Ethernet

If your all-reduce step takes 30 seconds, InfiniBand isn't optional. If it takes 600 seconds, Ethernet is fine. That's the real decision rule. 8x H100 NCCL all-reduce, effective busbw: - InfiniBand NDR: about 350 GB/s - Tuned RoCEv2: 270-290 GB/s - Untuned RoCEv2: under 200 GB/s

Key Points:


- **


- **InfiniBand and Ethernet:** InfiniBand is necessary for all-reduce steps that take less than 30 seconds.

- **Decision Rule:** The decision rule is based on the time it takes for the all-reduce step.

- **Bus Bandwidth:** The effective bus bandwidth is different for InfiniBand and Ethernet.


**Actionable Takeaway:**

- **Consider the Decision Rule:** Take the time to consider the decision rule for InfiniBand and Ethernet.
