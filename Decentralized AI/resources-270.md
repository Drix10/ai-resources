### 📊 Nvidia down 40% from record after CEO ousted

Nvidia is down 40% from its October 6 record after former CEO Jensen Huang was ousted, while still up 360% over five years.

Key Points:

- Closed at $145.98, 40% below the $243.37 record from October 6.

- Up $114.30, a 360% gain over the past five years.
