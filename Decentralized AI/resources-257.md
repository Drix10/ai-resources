### 🤖 FAssets – trustless over-collateralized bridge description

FAssets is a trustless, over-collateralized bridge that connects non-smart-contract networks to Flare and creates wrapped tokens for assets like BTC, DOGE and XRP. It uses Flare’s Data Connector to verify off-chain transactions and the Time Series Oracle for price feeds. Minting and redemption are handled by agents and a Core Vault.

Key Points:

- FAssets creates wrapped tokens (FAssets) for BTC, DOGE, XRP and other assets.

- Flare Data Connector verifies off-chain actions; Flare Time Series Oracle provides decentralized price feeds.
