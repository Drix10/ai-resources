### 🤖 Inkling 2 Mini launch with 1M context and 20GB size

Inkling 2 Mini offers 1M context length, multimodal support, 20GB footprint and matches Qwen 3.8 27B performance. It uses a looped transformer with 26B parameters, runs on a laptop and scores 20.1 on the AA Index.

Key Points:

- **Context length**: 1M context length

- **Parameters**: 26B parameters

### 🤖 CactusXR adds fixture geometry, product locations, labeling

CactusXR will soon provide fixture geometry, product locations, labeled products from image recognition, and facing counts from reconstructions. It claims scanning barcodes and counting facings will drop from hours to a few minutes of filming.

Key Points:

### 🤖 Chainbase builds internet for traceable AI research

Chainbase is creating an internet where agents can do research and people can trace and verify results. OpenAI reports AI use in research rising exponentially, agents completing longer tasks while human involvement stays central and safety work increases.

Key Points:

- **Longer tasks**: agents take on longer research tasks and each finding needs a trail back to its sources.

- **OpenAI data**: researchers' AI use continues to rise exponentially, agents are completing longer tasks, human involvement remains central, and researchers are spending more time on safety.
