### 📊 Syndrome, Synergy, and Safety improves TCM prescription

Syndrome, Synergy, and Safety introduces a four-stage framework (SFT → PG-CoT → Dynamic → K-RL) for TCM prescription generation. The framework adds auditable reasoning, patient-trajectory modeling, and rule-based safety signals. Experiments on 12 fine-tuned models and 6 zero-shot baselines show a 7B Mistral-7B model beating zero-shot GPT-5 on all three TCM evaluation metrics.

Key Points:

- **Four-stage framework**: SFT, PG-CoT, Dynamic, K-RL.

- 12 fine-tuned models, 6 zero-shot baselines; Mistral-7B surpasses GPT-5 on all metrics.
