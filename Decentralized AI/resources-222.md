### 🤖 AI Hardware - L40 and L40S Comparison

L40 and L40S are two high-end AI accelerators from AMD, designed for demanding AI workloads. The key difference between them lies in their compute capabilities and power consumption.

Key Points:
• L40S has 733 TFLOPS dense FP8 compute capability, while L40 has 362 TFLOPS.
• L40S features a Transformer Engine that automatically recasts layers to FP8, whereas L40 does not.
• L40S consumes 350W of power, whereas L40 consumes 300W.

### 🤖 AI Hardware - MI300X Memory Capacity

The MI300X AI accelerator from AMD boasts an impressive 192 GB of HBM3 memory capacity, which is sufficient to fit large AI models that require two H100s to hold in memory.

Key Points:
