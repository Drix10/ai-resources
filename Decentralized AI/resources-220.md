### 🤖 AI Systems - LLM Architectures and GPU Compute

LLMs have made tremendous progress in recent years, but their training and deployment still rely heavily on massive GPU clusters. Meanwhile, most developers struggle to access or afford even a handful of GPUs.

Key Points:
• LLMs require massive GPU clusters for training, but most developers lack access to even a handful of GPUs.
• The current GPU compute landscape is characterized by clusters with effectively unlimited compute at one end and startups, researchers, and individual developers struggling to access or afford GPUs at the other.
• HarnessDev, a project that puts six frontier models to the test across 2,207 tasks, found that LLM-built harnesses reach human parity in writing and ML, but lag up to 40% in other areas.
