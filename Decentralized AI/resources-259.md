### 🚀 Hialeah Park Casino joins Helium Network, routes carrier traffic to Wi-Fi

Hialeah Park Casino joined the Helium Network and now automatically routes carrier traffic onto its own Wi-Fi, giving guests better signal and avoiding a new antenna system. The change is described as people-built coverage serving the people.

Key Points:

- **Helium Network integration**: 100-year-old racetrack becomes part of Helium Network.

- **Traffic routing**: casino automatically routes carrier traffic onto its own Wi-Fi, eliminating need for a new antenna system.
