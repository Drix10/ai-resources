### 🚨 LiteFold Dupilumab manufacturing recovery drop to 75%

LiteFold reports that recovery during Protein A purification fell from 92% to 75% across three consecutive batches in its Dupilumab manufacturing example. The drop was observed batch-by-batch, indicating a gradual loss of efficiency.

Key Points:

- **Recovery rate**: 92% initially, then 75% after three batches.

- **Process**: Protein A purification step in Dupilumab production.
