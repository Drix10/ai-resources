### 🤖 Inkling 2 Mini fine-tuned for trial adverse event arithmetic, 98.3% completeness

Inkling 2 Mini was fine-tuned to compute adverse event rates for two trial groups and output fractions, percentages and the higher or lower group in one line, achieving complete worked answers on 118 of 120 unseen trials (98.3%). The model runs on a single CUDA GPU, is 26B parameters, 19.5GB checkpoint size and released under Apache-2.0.

Key Points:

- Complete worked answer on 118 of 120 unseen trials (98.3%).

- Original Inkling 2 Mini gave complete answers on 37 of 120 trials (30.8%).

### 🤖 Living wound patch kills resistant Pseudomonas in 24 hours

Living wound patch pairs phages and probiotic lactobacilli and eliminates resistant Pseudomonas aeruginosa within 24 hours in lab tests. The patch uses a dual-hydrogel system that releases phages first and probiotic microbeads later.

Key Points:

- Phage-probiotic cocktail destroyed Pseudomonas aeruginosa bacteria in 24 hours in vitro and ex vivo skin model.

- Patch hydrogel combines a phage matrix with alginate microbeads containing lactobacilli, degrading only when pathogens are present.
