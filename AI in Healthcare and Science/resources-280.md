### 🤖 Genmab Petosentamab meets primary endpoints in HNSCC

Genmab announced that Petosentamab, a bispecific anti EGFRxLGR5 plus pembrolizumab, met both primary endpoints in R/M PDL1+ HNSCC. The trial reported overall survival hazard ratio 0.50 with 95% CI 0.37-0.67 (p<0.0001) and overall response rate 3.17 with 95% CI 2.06-4.89 (p<0.0001).

Key Points:

- **OS**: HR 0.50 (95% CI, 0.37, 0.67); p<0.0001

- **ORR**: 3.17 (95% CI, 2.06, 4.89); p<0.0001

### 🤖 CheckMate 8HW final overall survival results

CheckMate 8HW reported final overall survival (OS) data. NIVO combined with IPI significantly improved OS versus NIVO alone across all treatment lines. In first-line therapy, a clinically relevant trend toward improved OS was observed but did not reach statistical significance.

Key Points:

- **OS**: NIVO plus IPI significantly improved overall survival versus NIVO across all lines

- **1L**: trend toward improved OS observed, not statistically significant
