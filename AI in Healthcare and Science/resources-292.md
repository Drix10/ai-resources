### 🤖 Bivalirudin FDA approval 2000 as anticoagulant

Bivalirudin received FDA approval in 2000 as an anticoagulant for use with aspirin in unstable angina patients undergoing balloon angioplasty. The peptide is a synthetic 20-amino-acid derivative of hirudin, the leech anticoagulant identified in the 1950s.

Key Points:
