### 📊 Flare Up Forecast introduced in Nolla Derm app

Flare Up Forecast is a new feature in Nolla Derm. It uses three consecutive daily face scans to estimate a user’s risk of an acne flare‑up for the following three days. The announcement presents the feature and its intended function.

Key Points:

- **Feature**: Flare Up Forecast in Nolla Derm

- **Method**: Scan face three days in a row to estimate risk for next three days
