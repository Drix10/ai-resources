### 🤖 Blood pressure drugs prevent eight events per 1000 patients

Blood pressure drugs are estimated to prevent eight serious cardiovascular events for every two serious falls caused per 1,000 patients over 10 years. The balance is also favorable in older adults with frailty.

Key Points:

- Prevent eight serious cardiovascular events per 1,000 patients over 10 years.

- Cause two serious falls per 1,000 patients over the same period.

### 🤖 Sacituzumab tirumotecan improves OS in TroFuse-015 phase III gastroesophageal cancer trial

Sacituzumab tirumotecan (sac-TMT) met the primary overall survival endpoint versus physician’s choice in the TroFuse-015 phase III study of 473 patients with ≥3 line gastroesophageal adenocarcinoma. The trial did not meet the secondary progression-free survival endpoint. No hazard ratios or median survival times were disclosed.

Key Points:

- 473 patients enrolled, randomized to sac-TMT or physician’s choice.

- Primary endpoint overall survival achieved; secondary endpoint progression-free survival missed.
