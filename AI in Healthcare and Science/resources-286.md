### 🤖 lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan FDA approved for prostate cancer

FDA approved lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan (Pluvicto) with androgen receptor pathway inhibitor therapy for PSMA-positive metastatic androgen-pathway-modulation-naïve or-sensitive prostate cancer. The recommended dose is 7.4 GBq every six weeks for up to six doses.

Key Points:

### 🤖 lirafugratinib FDA approval for FGFR2-positive cholangiocarcinoma

lirafugratinib received FDA approval for adults with previously treated, unresectable, locally advanced or metastatic cholangiocarcinoma harboring FGFR2 fusions or rearrangements. The approval follows the REFOCUS trial (NCT04526106) showing a 46% objective response rate and median duration of response of 11.8 months. The recommended dose is 70 mg orally once daily. The drug carries warnings for ocular toxicity, hyperphosphatemia, soft tissue mineralization, and embryo-fetal toxicity.

Key Points:

- FDA approval for lirafugratinib in FGFR2-positive cholangiocarcinoma.

- REFOCUS trial reported ORR 46% (95% CI 36-55) and median DOR 11.8 months (95% CI 7.5-13.0).

### 🚀 sevabertinib accelerated approval for HER2-mutated NSCLC

sevabertinib (Hyrnuo) received FDA accelerated approval on September 9 2026 for adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic non-squamous NSCLC harboring HER2 (ERBB2) TKD activating mutations. In the SOHO-01 trial, 69 treatment-naïve patients showed a 75% confirmed objective response rate, with 73% of responders maintaining response ≥6 months.

Key Points:

- FDA granted accelerated approval to sevabertinib for HER2-mutated non-squamous NSCLC on September 9 2026.

### 📊 nogapendekin alfa inbakicept-pmln FDA expanded indication

FDA approved nogapendekin alfa inbakicept-pmln with BCG for BCG-unresponsive non-muscle invasive bladder cancer including high-grade papillary-only tumors. The indication expands from prior approval that covered CIS with or without papillary disease. The approval is based on data from Cohort A of QUILT-3.032 (NCT03022825).

Key Points:

- **Approval date**: October 14 2026.

- **Complete response rate in trial**: 62% (95% CI 51-73).

- **Recommended induction dose**: 400 µg intravesical weekly for 6 weeks with BCG.

- **Maintenance schedule**: 400 µg weekly for 3 weeks at months 4, 7, 10, 13, 19, 25, 31, 37 (up to 37 months).
