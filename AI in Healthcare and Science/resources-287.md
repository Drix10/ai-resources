### 🤖 OpenMed 3.1 split_overlap prevents scoring held-out pairs

OpenMed 3.1 cannot score a split where 3 of the 12 held-out pairs in a clinical NLI evaluation also appear in the dev set. The split is labeled split_overlap, the model is never loaded, and any change to the weights after sign-off causes the run to be refused.

Key Points:

- 3 of the 12 held-out pairs also sit in the dev set.

- OpenMed 3.1 won’t score split_overlap and the model is never loaded.
