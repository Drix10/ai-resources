### 🤖 tarlatamab 2L standard after platinum in SCLC – OS 13.6 mo

tarlatamab is reported as the second-line standard after platinum in small-cell lung cancer with overall survival of 13.6 months versus 8.4 months for chemotherapy. Extended follow-up shows 30.3% of patients alive at 2 years and no new long-term safety signals.

Key Points:

- OS 13.6 mo vs 8.4 mo (HR 0.62) in Dellphi-304 extended follow-up.

- 2-year OS 30.3% vs 14.9%; ORR 35.4% vs 20.4% versus chemotherapy.

### 📊 Overtraining may disrupt the brain's dopamine regulation

Overtraining may disrupt the brain's dopamine regulation, leaving levels elevated during rest. Mice that underwent chronic intensive training showed hippocampal dopamine remaining high for 48 hours after an exercise test during their rest phase. The same mice also had elevated tyrosine hydroxylase levels for the same period.

Key Points:

- Dopamine in the hippocampus stayed elevated 48 hours after the performance test during the mice’s rest phase.

- Tyrosine hydroxylase levels remained elevated for 48 hours in the chronic training group.
