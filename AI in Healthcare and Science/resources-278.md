### 🤖 KRAS drug plus immunotherapy trial 97% reach surgery

KRAS drug and immunotherapy trial in pancreatic cancer reported 97% of patients reached surgery with clear margins. The trial enrolled about 30 patients per group and had no control arm or survival data yet.

Key Points:

- 97% reached surgery in the immunotherapy group.

- Every surgery removed the tumour completely with clear margins.

- Trial size was roughly 30 patients per group.
