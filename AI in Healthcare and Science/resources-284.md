### 🤖 OpenMed GLiNER2.5 ClinicalNER Disease Spanish fine-tuned, F1 39.1-73.3

OpenMed GLiNER2.5 ClinicalNER Disease Spanish v1 is an open model for disease extraction in Spanish clinical text. After fine-tuning it reaches an F1 range of 39.1 to 73.3. It is released under the Apache-2.0 license.

Key Points:

- **F1 score after fine-tuning**: 39.1 – 73.3

- **License**: Apache-2.0

- Detects four disease mentions (HTA, DM2, EPOC, IC) at exact character offsets

- **Model size**: 0.3B parameters, runs on CPU.
