### 🚀 Clinical Trials - Recent Developments

Recent clinical trials have shown promising results in various fields of medicine. This article summarizes key findings from recent trials.

Key Points:
• Teclistamab + daratumumab (TRIMM-2, phase 1b) in RRMM showed an ORR of 68.9% with a median PFS of 26.3mo.
• The study also reported a high rate of grade 3/4 TEAEs, leading to the stopping of a fixed-dose cohort.
• Harmoni 2 OS Press release showed an OS benefit, challenging Keytruda's dominance in the market.
• Daraxonrasib's NSCLC data showed an ORR of 35% overall, with a median PFS of 8.3mo and a median OS of 16mo.
• The study also reported a high rate of rash and diarrhea, with 25% of patients experiencing grade 3/4 TRAEs.
