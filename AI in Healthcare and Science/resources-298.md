### 📊 IPS and SMART Health Links: Kill the Clipboard standards

IPS and SMART Health Links are the two standards behind Kill the Clipboard, using a QR code that holds a link and a 32-byte decryption key. The IPS US version reached 1.0 in September and CMS now publishes a guide for providers to scan these codes at check-in.

Key Points:

- QR code contains a link and a decryption key of 32 random bytes.

- SMART Health Links payload includes a manifest URL with at least 256 bits of entropy and a 43-character base64url-encoded key.
