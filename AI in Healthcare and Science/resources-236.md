### 🤖 AI/ML - ADCs and Therapeutic Index

ADCs (Antibody-Drug Conjugates) are a type of cancer treatment that combines an antibody with a cytotoxic drug. The therapeutic index (TI) is a measure of the ratio of the toxic dose to the effective dose of a drug. Companies are working to widen the TI of ADCs to improve their safety and efficacy.

Key Points:
• ADCs have a narrow therapeutic index, making them challenging to develop.
• Companies are exploring new technologies to widen the TI of ADCs.
• The use of ADCs is increasing in cancer treatment.
