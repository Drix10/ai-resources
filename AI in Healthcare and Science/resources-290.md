### 📊 INTerpath-001 trial shows 58% risk reduction with personalized vaccine plus pembrolizumab

INTerpath-001 trial results published in NEJM show that adding a personalized therapeutic cancer vaccine to pembrolizumab reduced the risk of melanoma recurrence or death by 58% compared with pembrolizumab alone, measured from 6 months after therapy start. The vaccine is customized to each patient’s tumor mutations.

Key Points:

- **Risk reduction**: 58% lower recurrence or death versus pembrolizumab alone.

- **Patient group**: melanoma patients post-surgery at high risk of recurrence.
