### 📊 Intensive breathing cuts brain blood flow up to 45%

Intensive breathing exercises reduce cerebral blood flow by up to 45%. Stronger altered states were linked to greater changes in the brain's default mode network, which supports self‑reflection.

Key Points:

- Global gray‑matter cerebral blood flow fell by around 45% during the session, on average from 16.24 to 9.81 ml/100g/min.

- Those who reported stronger altered states also showed greater changes in the brain's default mode network, which is involved in self‑reflection.

### 📊 Anodal M1 tDCS reduces thermal pain in triple‑blind trial

Anodal M1 transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS) reduced thermal pain compared with sham in a pre‑registered, triple‑blind trial of 50 participants. Cathodal stimulation increased pain compared with sham. The polarity‑dependent effects were stable across 35 minutes post‑stimulation.

Key Points:

- **Anodal M1 #tDCS effect size**: Cohen's d = 0.85 versus sham.

- **Cathodal stimulation effect size**: Cohen's d = 0.34 versus sham; effects stable for 35 min.
