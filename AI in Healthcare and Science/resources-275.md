### 🤖 GSK showcases pipeline momentum across lung

GSK will present more than 20 abstracts from its solid tumour portfolio at ESMO 2026, covering lung, gynaecological and gastrointestinal cancers. The first global Ris-Rez data will show positive anti-tumour activity in relapsed extensive-stage small-cell lung cancer. Late-breaking Mo-Rez data will demonstrate clinically meaningful and durable activity in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer.

Key Points:

- Over 20 abstracts from GSK’s solid tumour portfolio will be presented at ESMO 2026.

- Ris-Rez data from the EMBOLD PanTumor-101 trial will show positive anti-tumour activity in relapsed extensive-stage small-cell lung cancer (Abstracts #1071P, #3841P).
