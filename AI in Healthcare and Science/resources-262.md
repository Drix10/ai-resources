### 🚀 Medical Imaging - CERVINO Study Results

The CERVINO study, presented at the #IMS26 plenary session, reported efficacy and safety results from phase 3 in relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (RRMM). The study compared etentamig to investigator-choice SAT in triple-class exposed, BCMA-naive RRMM patients. The results showed an ORR of 74% vs 46% (P<0.0001) and a PFS HR of 0.40, with median NR vs 6.2mo.

Key Points:

- **CERVINO Study Design**: The CERVINO study was a phase 3 trial that compared etentamig to investigator-choice SAT in triple-class exposed, BCMA-naive RRMM patients.

- **Efficacy Results**: The study reported an ORR of 74% vs 46% (P<0.0001) in favor of etentamig.

- **Safety Results**: The study reported a PFS HR of 0.40, with median NR vs 6.2mo, and a 12-mo OS of 87.9% vs 72% (early signal).

- **Adverse Events**: The study reported a CRS of 39.5%, mostly low-grade.
