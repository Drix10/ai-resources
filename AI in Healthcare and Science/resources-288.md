### 📊 Qwen3.5-0.8B weight-space programming improves MedQA accuracy to 32.9%

Key Points:

- Accuracy on 1,273 MedQA questions increases from 24.4% to 32.9% after weight-space programming.

- Answers cut at the token limit drop from 18.3% to 0.1% after the edit.
