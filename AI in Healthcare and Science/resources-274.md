### 🚀 Gefitinib plus deworming drug slows lung tumors, 25-day doubling

Gefitinib plus a deworming drug slowed drug-resistant lung tumors in mice, extending median tumor-doubling time from 6.2 to 25 days. The result was reported on PLOS ONE.

Key Points:

- Pairing a deworming drug with gefitinib slowed drug-resistant lung tumors in mice.

- Median tumor-doubling time stretched from 6.2 to 25 days.
