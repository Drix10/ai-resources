### 🤖 Menin inhibitors trial results at ASH26 show response rates and safety

ziftomenib, revumenib, enzomenib and bleximenib presented early-phase data at ASH26 with specific overall response rates, CR/CRh rates and differentiation-syndrome incidences. Results cover R/R AML, frontline combos, MRD-directed therapy and post-transplant maintenance. Follow-up is limited and cohorts are small.

Key Points:

- **KOMET-008**: ziftomenib + gilteritinib in R/R NPM1/FLT3-mutated AML, ≥200 mg (n=41) ORR 68%, CRc 41%, CR/CRh 34%, grade 3 differentiation syndrome 10% (n=48).

- **INTERCEPT**: pre-emptive revumenib for MRD relapse, MRD clearance 45% in NPM1m (9/20) and 75% in KMT2Ar (6/8), no differentiation syndrome.

- **cAMeLot-1**: bleximenib phase 2 in R/R AML, CR/CRh 51.2% in KMT2Ar (n=43) and 40.4% in NPM1m (n=57), differentiation syndrome 28% and 11% respectively.

- **Beat AML**: frontline azacitidine/venetoclax/revumenib in NPM1m/KMT2Ar AML >60 y (n=82), ORR 90%, CR 68%, median OS 24.3 months, differentiation syndrome 16%.
