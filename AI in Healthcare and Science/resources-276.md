### 📊 Ris-Rez first global data shows 61% ORR in ES-SCLC

Ris-Rez (risvutatug rezetecan) achieved a confirmed overall response rate of 61% at 8 mg/kg in a phase Ib dose-optimisation study of relapsed extensive-stage small-cell lung cancer. The data were presented at ESMO 2026 and support the 8 mg/kg dose in the ongoing global phase III EMBOLD SCLC-301 trial.

Key Points:

- ORR 61% (11/18 patients) at 8 mg/kg, 95% CI 35.7-82.7.

- Grade 3+ treatment-related adverse events in 42% of 8 mg/kg patients; interstitial lung disease observed in 2% of all patients.

### 📊 Kidney cancer vein trial 71.4% recurrence-free at one year

Kidney cancer vein trial reports 71.4% of patients were recurrence-free at one year after radiation and surgery, exceeding a 55.7% historical benchmark. The result comes from a small trial.

Key Points:

- **Recurrence-free rate**: 71.4% at one year after radiation and surgery.
