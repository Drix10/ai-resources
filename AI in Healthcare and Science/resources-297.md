### 🤖 giredestrant approved as adjuvant, 30% risk reduction

giredestrant received FDA approval as adjuvant therapy after surgery for adults with estrogen receptor-positive, HER2-negative, stage I–III breast cancer at intermediate to high risk of recurrence. The lidERA trial showed a 30% reduction in invasive breast cancer recurrence or death versus standard endocrine therapy and a 3-year invasive disease-free survival of 92.4% versus 89.6%.

Key Points:

- 30% reduction in risk of invasive breast cancer recurrence or death vs standard endocrine therapy

- **3-year invasive disease-free survival**: 92.4% with giredestrant vs 89.6% with standard of care
