### 🤖 Oncology - I-SPY2 Trial Update on Breast Cancer Treatment

An I-SPY2 trial update indicates a new therapeutic combination for early-stage breast cancer. This treatment strategy involves combining immunotherapy drugs with neoadjuvant paclitaxel.

Key Points:

• The I-SPY2 trial investigated a combination therapy for early breast cancer.

• Cemiplimab (PD-1) and fianlimab (LAG3) were combined with neoadjuvant paclitaxel.

• This combination improved pCR (pathologic complete response) rates.

• Improvements were observed in triple-negative and hormone receptor-positive (HR+) cases.
