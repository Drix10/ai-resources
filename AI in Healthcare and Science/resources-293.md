### 🤖 HIPAA Privacy Rule 2026 patient access request deadline 15 days

HIPAA Privacy Rule 2026 reduces the response window for patient access requests to 15 calendar days, with a possible 30-day extension. Covered entities may not charge for access via internet-based methods such as personal health apps. The rule becomes effective 60 days after publication, and compliance is required 240 days after publication.

Key Points:

- **Access request deadline**: 15 calendar days, one 30-day extension allowed.

- No fees for internet-based access, including personal health applications.
