### 🤖 OpenMed 3.0 patient matcher integration

OpenMed 3.0 accepts any patient matcher for scoring candidates but does not perform linking. The resolver returns ambiguous results, sets review_required=True, and provides no canonical key until a person decides. Exact links that have already been reviewed pass through directly.

Key Points:

- Patient matcher can score candidates in OpenMed 3.0 but cannot link them.

- Resolver returns ambiguous, review_required=True, and no canonical key until manual decision; reviewed exact links go straight through.

### 🚨 OpenMed 3.0 probabilistic match does not auto‑merge records

OpenMed 3.0 uses a probabilistic match that never merges two records automatically. A candidate with 99% confidence or two candidates with 94% and 91% confidence are sent to a human reviewer. A wrong merge could place one patient’s allergy list in another patient’s chart.

Key Points:

- Probabilistic match never merges records on its own in OpenMed 3.0.

- Matches at 99% or at 94% and 91% are routed to a human reviewer.

### 🤖 Olive oil preload lowers early glucose rise in mice

Olive oil given 15 minutes before oral glucose reduces the early increase in blood sugar in mice. The effect remains in mice with severe insulin deficiency. The study also links the effect to slower gastric emptying and hormone changes.

Key Points:

- Olive oil before glucose blunted the early blood sugar rise versus oil given after glucose.

- The benefit was retained in mice with severe insulin deficiency.
