### 🚀 Datapoint Streams – online RL with live human feedback for multimodal models

Datapoint Streams provides online reinforcement learning that incorporates real‑time human preferences for image, audio, and video models. The system sends rollouts to a pool of over one billion raters, receives structured preferences as a continuous reward stream, and updates the policy while training. Low‑trust responses are filtered before influencing updates.

Key Points:

- 1B+ raters judge rollouts in real time, delivering preferences as a continuous reward stream.

- Feedback latency is measured in seconds per judgment, with low‑trust answers dropped in flight.
