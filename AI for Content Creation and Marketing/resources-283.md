### 🤖 Lean verification of Riemann Hypothesis proof released, 17 million lines

Lean verification of the Riemann Hypothesis proof was released as a Lean library of 17 million lines of code. The joint statement came from the Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative. Levent Alpöge announced the release on November 20.

Key Points:

- **Release**: Lean library of 17 million lines of code for the Riemann Hypothesis proof.

- **Announcement date**: November 20, by Levent Alpöge.

### 🤖 Blackstone AI uses speed gains across portfolio companies

Blackstone’s Jon Gray says AI cuts processing time for rental housing applications by 90%, for Ancestry data by 30×, and for European classifieds service by 80%. AI also powers a digital doorman for Chamberlain that reached $50M run-rate in under two years.

Key Points:

- **Rental housing company**: applications processed 90% faster.

- **Ancestry.com**: data and family histories processed 30 times faster.

- **European classifieds business**: customer service answers 80% faster.

### 🤖 Circleboom Cross Posting one-click X to Bluesky and Threads

Circleboom adds a toggle in its X Post Planner that lets a single scheduled post publish to X, Bluesky, and Threads at the same moment. The workflow connects the two networks once, writes the announcement under 300 characters, and schedules it in about three minutes per issue.

Key Points:

- One-click cross post enabled by a toggle in the scheduling panel.

- Post must be under 300 characters and image under 2MB to meet Bluesky limits.
