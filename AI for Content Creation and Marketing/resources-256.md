### 🤖 OpenAI shares progress on ChatGPT for Teens and College Planner preview

OpenAI announced updates to ChatGPT for Teens, a version of ChatGPT for users under 18. The post also previewed a new College Planner tool, additional study tools, and support features for college advisers and teen voices. ChatGPT for Teens will be applied automatically to accounts identified as belonging to under‑18 users.

Key Points:

- **Feature**: ChatGPT for Teens applies automatically to accounts identified as belonging to users under 18.

- **Announcement**: A preview of College Planner and new study tools was shared.

### 📊 Claude Sonnet 5.5 cache read price halved to $0.10 per million tokens

Claude Sonnet 5.5 now charges $0.10 per million tokens for cache reads, half the previous rate. This reduction makes running Sonnet 5.5 on most long‑running workloads about 20% cheaper.

Key Points:

- **Cache read price**: $0.10 per million tokens.

- **Cost reduction**: approximately 20% cheaper for long‑running work.

### 📊 Gemini 4 Argon pricing announcement by Google

Gemini 4 Argon is a Google model. The launch price is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. The price will double to $4 and $20 after the introductory period, with no end date specified.

Key Points:

- **Current price**: $2 per million input tokens, $10 per million output tokens

- **Future price**: $4 per million input tokens, $20 per million output tokens after introductory period
