### 📊 Solo founder website adoption stats from Hostinger survey

Hostinger surveyed 640 U.S. solo founders about online tools. The average founder waits 7 months after the first sale to build a website. 34% of respondents still have not built a website.

Key Points:

- **Average wait**: 7 months after first sale before building a website.

- 36% of founders with a website say they could not last a month without it.
