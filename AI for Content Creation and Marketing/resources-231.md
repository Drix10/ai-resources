### 🚀 Email Deliverability - Essential Checks for Cold Email Success

Before rewriting your cold email 10 times, do this. Check your deliverability by verifying SPF, DKIM, and DMARC settings, running an inbox placement test, and ensuring a bounce rate below 2%.

Key Points:

- **Run an inbox placement test**: This test checks if your emails are landing in the inbox or spam folder, helping you optimize your email content.

- **Monitor bounce rate**: A bounce rate below 2% indicates healthy email sending practices, reducing the risk of deliverability issues.
