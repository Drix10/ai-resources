### 🤖 Claude generate images – 3 ways tested, template got every word right

Claude cannot generate images by itself, but can create them via three methods. Using an Orshot template produced every word correctly and rendered the next week’s post in 0.5 seconds. The other two methods missed words or misspelled them.

Key Points:

- **Orshot template**: 1 credit per image, 100 free credits a month, first PNG returned in 92 seconds, subsequent renders took 0.8-2.2 seconds.

- **FLUX on Hugging Face**: misspelled the text in every take.
