### 🚀 Yarn Playbooks launch 12 hand-written agent skills

Yarn introduced Playbooks, a set of 12 hand-written agent skills for major video formats. The skills were derived from analysis of over 20,000 videos created with Yarn and 3,500 hand-picked videos from teams such as Anthropic, Linear, and Ramp.

Key Points:

- **Launch**: Playbooks, 12 hand-written agent skills covering major video formats.

### 🤖 Grammarly Universal Revision Model handles 24 languages

Grammarly became Superhuman one year ago and now uses the Universal Revision Model to handle revision tasks in 24 languages. The product has evolved over 17 years, moving from rule-based models to purpose-built LLMs and finally to the new model.

Key Points:

- One year ago, Grammarly became Superhuman.

- Started with rule-based models, then moved to purpose-built LLMs.

- Universal Revision Model handles revision tasks in 24 languages.
