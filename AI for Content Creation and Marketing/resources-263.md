### 🤖 Pictory – mobile video creation in browser, desktop edit

Pictory lets you create videos on a phone browser and later fine-tune and download them on desktop. No app download is required; the mobile flow produces a preview in about a minute.

Key Points:

- Mobile works in any browser, no app download needed.

- Quick start from idea to preview takes about a minute on a decent connection.

- Opening the same project on desktop provides the full studio and a final watermark-free download.
