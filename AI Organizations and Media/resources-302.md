### 📊 Scientists create the first whole-body single-cell atlas of an adult vertebrate

Scientists create the first whole-body single-cell atlas of an adult vertebrate, profiling over 600,000 cells from a 1 cm transparent fish. The atlas maps gene activity across brain, spinal cord, eyes, internal organs, fins, head, and trunk and is publicly available via DigiFish.

Key Points:

- Over 600,000 cells profiled to build the whole-body single-cell transcriptome atlas.

- Study published in Science on Oct. 29, 2026.

### 📊 On the Applicability of p-adic Numbers in Computational Modules for Neural Networks

On the Applicability of p-adic Numbers in Computational Modules for Neural Networks reports a one-feature linear regression test on the scikit-learn Diabetes dataset. Coefficients are represented in ℚₚ, predictions computed in that field, and rational reconstruction applied. Under the tested settings none of the test predictions is reconstructed correctly.

Key Points:

- Experiment uses a one-feature linear regression on the scikit-learn Diabetes dataset.

- In both pyAdic and SageMath experiments rational reconstruction fails to match baseline predictions.
