### 📊 MCTS Is Counting the Wrong Thing: Learning Exploration Counts for LLM Reasoning

MCTS Is Counting the Wrong Thing: Learning Exploration Counts for LLM Reasoning introduces Learned Pseudo-Count MCTS (LPC-MCTS) that shares exploration evidence across related branches. Replacing standard MCTS counts at inference raises mean@16 accuracy by 3.79 pts on Qwen2.5-7B and 2.71 pts on Qwen3-4B; training and inference together add 4.96 pts and 4.37 pts respectively.

Key Points:

- LPC-MCTS adds a Coin-Flipping Network head to estimate representation coverage and modify the UCT exploration statistic.

- Across 5 mathematical-reasoning and 7 search benchmarks, LPC-MCTS consistently outperforms strong MCTS baselines.

### 🤖 SoduWork self-hosted AI workflow with review and approval

SoduWork is a self-hosted open-source AI engine that adds built-in review and approval workflows to work requests.
