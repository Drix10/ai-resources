### 🤖 Label-Free Role-Aware Segmentation – F1 0.87

Label-Free Role-Aware Segmentation of Classroom Recordings achieves teacher F1 0.87 on German corpus. The pipeline runs on-device, requires no labels at inference, and runs 4.8× faster than pyannote.

Key Points:

- Pipeline chains VAD, pretrained speaker embeddings, agglomerative clustering, and logistic regression.

- **Teacher F1 scores**: 0.81 on English, 0.87 on German recordings.

- Real-time factor ~0.18; 4.8× faster than pyannote with comparable memory.

### 🤖 Searching the Pareto Front multi-objective retrieval improves galaxy search

Searching the Pareto Front introduces a multi-objective retrieval method for heterogeneous galaxy data and reports an 8% improvement in raw-data fidelity over a foundation-model baseline. The approach keeps modality-specific distance structure while optimizing via Pareto optimality. Evaluation uses star-formation history, spectra, and multi-wavelength observations from two telescopes.

Key Points:

- FM baseline achieves strong embedding-space proximity.

- Nearest neighbor is 8% closer to query (0.128 vs 0.139) across six properties.

🔗 Resources:

- [Original post](https://x.com/SciFi/status/2124127011232727052)
