### 🤖 Qwen, GPT robustness drops; Claude gains from docs-as-tool

Qwen and GPT lose robustness when reasoning is disabled, even on simple single-hop questions. Using retrieved documents as tool calls improves Claude models but harms Qwen and GPT with the same documents.

Key Points:

- Disabling reasoning tanked Qwen & GPT robustness on simple single-hop questions.

- Treating retrieved docs as tool calls helped Claude models but hurt Qwen & GPT.

### 📊 VAPS – Viability‑Aware Policy Selection for Safe Humanoid Acrobatics

VAPS is a decision framework that selects between a tracking, abort, and fall policy for humanoid robots. It predicts short‑horizon viability of the tracking and abort policies at each control step and chooses the least‑sacrificial viable option. Experiments show reduced head and hand contacts on Unitree G1 and LimX Oli robots during flips.

Key Points:

- VAPS uses learned predictors to estimate viability of nominal tracking and abort policies over a short horizon.

- In simulation with randomized disturbances, VAPS sharply reduces head and hand contacts compared to single‑network baselines.
