### 🤖 Scaling public input on AI policy with pluralistic agents – study results

Scaling public input on AI policy with pluralistic agents reports a preregistered study of 746 participants. Pluralistic agents shifted policies toward represented personas and recovered 68% of the baseline-direct satisfaction gap and 82% of the fairness gap. Participants viewed agents as useful and fair, preferring them over a single-model baseline.

Key Points:

- 746 humans participated in the pluralistic agents process.

- Agents recovered 68% of the baseline-direct gap in reported satisfaction and 82% in fairness.

### 🤖 Beyond Flat Walks: Compositional Abstraction for Autoregressive Graph Generation announced

Beyond Flat Walks: Compositional Abstraction for Autoregressive Graph Generation introduces a hierarchical graph abstraction and coarse-to-fine generation pipeline. The authors report consistent distributional and structural gains on five molecular benchmarks and analyze fidelity-V.U.N. trade-offs.

Key Points:

- Hierarchical graph abstraction separates coarsening, tokenization, and decoding.

- Traversal-based hierarchy improves distributional and structural fidelity across benchmarks.
