### 🤖 MicroLens: Interactive viewer for residual stream analysis released

MicroLens introduces an interactive viewer that decodes residual stream cells with logit and Jacobian lenses and allows on-the-fly editing. The tool shows that the logit lens’s word direction sets a token’s output while the J-lens’s direction changes what later tokens read.

Key Points:

- **Two lenses**: logit lens sets what a token outputs; J-lens changes what later tokens read.

- Swapping state or country token at layer 7 redirects answer 92-98% of the time; the information is not used at the answer position.

### 📊 Simulating Vein Compressibility for Ultrasound-Guided Femoral Access

### 🤖 Controlling Unknown Unknowns – anytime-valid bound

Controlling the Probability of Unknown Unknowns in Pre-Deployment Testing of AI Systems introduces an algorithm that provides an anytime-valid upper confidence sequence for the probability of unseen failure modes. The method yields tighter upper bounds than a multiple-testing corrected Good-Turing baseline and outperforms a fixed-horizon Good-Turing bound that is not anytime valid.

Key Points:

- Algorithm certifies when the probability of observing an unseen failure mode is sufficiently small.

### 🤖 Robust Flow Matching under Target Corruption and Label Noise accepted at NeurIPS 2026

Robust Flow Matching under Target Corruption and Label Noise is a new paper accepted at NeurIPS 2026 that treats Flow Matching training as a time-dependent regression problem over conditional vector fields. It introduces two corruption-specific scores—Temporal Residual Score and Comparative Conditional Score—to weight samples based on residual structure.

Key Points:

- Formulates Flow Matching training as a time-dependent regression problem over conditional vector fields.

- Proposes Temporal Residual Score for target corruption and Comparative Conditional Score for label noise to map residuals to sample weights.
