### 🤖 TMT runtime backdoor detector for VLA policies announced

TMT is a runtime backdoor detector for vision‑language‑action policies. It uses a Token Manifold branch and a latent Transition branch trained on benign rollouts. The authors also propose policy purification through self‑distillation using a frozen copy of the backdoored policy. In evaluation TMT outperforms ten baselines on unseen tasks across three VLA backdoor attacks.

Key Points:

- **Detector**: two branches assess input‑token structure and prediction errors in adjacent‑layer latent dynamics

- **Purification**: frozen copy provides benign‑input actions to supervise a student on paired benign and triggered observations without a separate clean reference policy

- **Evaluation**: compared with ten baselines, achieves state‑of‑the‑art backdoor detection performance on unseen tasks across three VLA backdoor attacks
