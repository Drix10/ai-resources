### 🤖 CORA O-RAN adaptation raises recall to 0.771

CORA is a teacher-guided reinforcement-learning framework that improves causal jamming detection recall to 0.771. It trains a masked controller offline using a retrospective KPI detector and deploys it causally without labels.

Key Points:

### 🤖 Central pivot point control of a body-attached spring-mass runner

Central pivot point (CPP) control restores central-force structure in trunk-ed SLIP models and yields an analytic return map that predicts liftoff velocity with 1.6% mean error and apex position with 0.9% error. The paper also provides a scalar equation that predicts natural gaits within 0.8% and a deadbeat stride controller based on the analytic map. Results are demonstrated on a linear radial template with closed-form stance radius and constant-momentum sweep.

Key Points:

- Analytic return map predicts liftoff velocity with mean error 1.6% and apex position with mean error 0.9%.

- Scalar equation predicts natural gaits within 0.8% error.

### 🤖 Energy-Based Policy Orchestration paper announced at CoRL 2026

Energy-Based Policy Orchestration introduces EPO, an energy-based orchestrator for modular goal-conditioned policies. It learns a composite energy per policy to refine goals and select the lowest-energy policy-goal pair at runtime. Simulations show improved precision-robustness trade-off, and the same frozen policies and energy models are deployed on real humanoid loco-manipulation tasks.

Key Points:

- **Authors**: Weirui Ye, Yen-Jen Wang, Elvis Hsieh, Zhiyang Dou, Shibo Zhao, Pieter Abbeel, Yilun Du, Leslie Pack Kaelbling, Tomás Lozano-Pérez.

- Presented at CoRL 2026 with arXiv preprint arXiv:2611.02546.
