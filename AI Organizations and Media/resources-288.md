### 🤖 We replaced Redis with MySQL for inventory reservations – scaled to $5.1M/min

We replaced Redis with MySQL for inventory reservations and scaled to handle $5.1 million in sales per minute on Black Friday 2025. The design uses one row per sellable unit, MySQL 8 SKIP LOCKED, and a bounded pool of 1,000 rows per item/location. This kept ACID guarantees and met peak throughput targets.

Key Points:

- One row per inventory unit, reserving by selecting and moving rows in a single transaction.

- Pool of available rows limited to 1,000 per item/location to keep scans fast.

- Handled $5.1 million in sales per minute during Black Friday 2025 peak.
