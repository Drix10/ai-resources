### 🤖 Accelerated naive CD8 T cell loss limits cancer antigen recognition in male mice

Accelerated loss of naive CD8 T cells in male mice leads to lymph node contraction and limited cancer antigen recognition. Restoring thymic output improves responses to immunotherapy in middle‑aged male mice.

Key Points:

- Accelerated loss of naive CD8 T cells causes lymph node contraction.

- Restoring thymic output improves immunotherapy responses in middle‑aged male mice.

### 🤖 STING drives NF-κB signaling via IKK recruitment

STING drives NF-κB signaling. It does so by recruiting the IKK complex. The recruitment is M1-linked ubiquitin-dependent and IRF3-independent.

Key Points:

- **Signaling**: STING drives NF-κB signaling.

- **Recruitment**: STING recruits the IKK complex in an M1-linked ubiquitin-dependent but IRF3-independent manner.
