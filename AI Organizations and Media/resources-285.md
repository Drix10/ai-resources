### 🤖 Hy-Nomas video model released with 8-step inference

Hy-Nomas video world generation model released with 8-step distilled inference at 720×1280 @ 24 fps. It uses Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct for text encoding and runs on a single RTX 4090 with INT8 DiT, FP8 decoding and CPU offload.

Key Points:

- **Text encoder**: Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct language model part.

- **Distilled inference**: 8 denoising steps, 720×1280 output at 24 fps.

- **Single-GPU config**: INT8 DiT + FP8 decoder + CPU offload on RTX 4090.
