### 🤖 AromaGen real-time smell transmission hits 71% accuracy

AromaGen is a 12-channel wearable that converts natural-language descriptions into perceivable smells. In blind three-way forced-choice tests across 50 common foods and beverages, participants identified the correct AI-generated smell 71% of the time.

Key Points:

- 12-channel wearable maps free-form language to 12 base odorants covering a derived olfactory space.

- Participants identified the correct AI-generated smell 71% of the time, compared to 33% random guessing.

### 🤖 Aligned, Conflicting, or Unspecified? Benchmark released

Aligned, Conflicting, or Unspecified? introduces a diagnostic benchmark for multimodal time series forecasting with 1,762 labeled text-window pairs. The paper evaluates ten pretrained models zero-shot and reports error reductions of up to 21.98% MASE and up to 9.71% directional accuracy using a training-free revision rule.

Key Points:

- 1,762 text-window pairs across Weather, Wikipedia, and Finance labeled Aligned, Conflicting, or Unspecified.

- Ten pretrained models evaluated zero-shot; a training-free rule lowered MASE by 4.49%-21.98% and raised DA by 2.99%-9.71%.

🔗 Resources:

- [Original post](https://x.com/SciFi/status/2122338786021388798)
- [Aligned, Conflicting, or Unspecified? A Diagnostic Benchmark for Multimodal Time](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17680) - Linked page

### 🤖 HES01 heading estimation system released with sub-centimeter landing precision

HES01 heading estimation system released by UAV Navigation-Grupo Oesía provides sub-centimeter landing precision on moving decks and up to 0.4° true heading accuracy in GNSS-denied environments. The device fuses dual GNSS, RTK, an internal magnetometer and an IMU, and validates magnetic data against GNSS heading to detect EMI.

Key Points:

- Sub-centimeter precision landings when paired with VECTOR FCC autopilot and GHU-100 Ground Hub Unit.

- Up to 0.4° true heading accuracy in static and dynamic conditions without alignment maneuver.
