### 🤖 Neural Stochastic Forecasting for Lateral Molecular Diffusion Simulations released

Neural Stochastic Forecasting for Lateral Molecular Diffusion Simulations introduces a neural stochastic model that predicts collective diffusion of membrane proteins. The model combines a transformer encoder, multi-scale recurrent networks, and a stochastic latent process with learned memory. It reproduces an anomalous diffusion exponent α≈1 for a seven-PIEZO system and generates trajectories up to 40,000 frames.

Key Points:

- Model uses transformer-based protein configuration representation, recurrent networks at multiple temporal scales, and a stochastic latent process.

- In the mixed system, the model captures distinct diffusion characteristics of PIEZO and TREK populations and matches time-averaged MSD, Van Hove distributions, and non-Gaussian parameters.
