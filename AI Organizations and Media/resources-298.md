### 🤖 Layer Roles Matter: Adaptive Layer Profiling for MER

Layer Roles Matter introduces a Layer-Aware Modality Rebalancing (LAMR) framework that uses Modality Entropy and Cross-modal Intensity to profile each layer’s role. LAMR enhances modality equilibrium in stabilization layers and cross-modal propagation in integration layers, improving emotion reasoning on two MER benchmarks without extra training.

Key Points:

- Modality Entropy (ME) measures dispersion of modality contributions per layer.

- Cross-modal Intensity (CMI) captures strength of cross-modal interactions per layer.
