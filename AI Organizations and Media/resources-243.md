### 🤖 DART-SD - Topology-Aware Self-Distillation Framework

DART-SD is a topology-aware self-distillation framework for multi-turn tool-calling agents. It models tool-use as a graph, finds the critical breakpoint, and supervises only the recovery steps. Outperforms SFT and RL across 5 benchmarks.

Key Points:
• DART-SD is a topology-aware self-distillation framework.
• The framework models tool-use as a graph.
• The framework outperforms SFT and RL across 5 benchmarks.
