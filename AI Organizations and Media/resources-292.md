### 🤖 ID2Pattern pretraining lifts transaction risk 8.83%

ID2Pattern pretraining framework introduces a Local Anonymous ID tokenizer and a multi-scale pattern codebook for identity fields in payment transactions. It improves an industrial dataset by 4.13% and six public datasets by an average of 8.83%, remaining effective on unseen identity values.

Key Points:

- LAID tokenizer captures how each identity appears and recurs in a sequence, ignoring its specific value.

- Multi-scale pattern codebook abstracts LAID sequences into discrete behavior patterns for downstream prediction.

### 🤖 SwapBot tool-centric robot manipulation announced with tactile adapters

SwapBot is a modular end-effector system that uses low-cost adapters to turn common household tools into robot tools and adds compact tactile sensors. User studies show it reduces task completion time and operator workload during teleoperation, and imitation-learning policies trained on tactile-augmented demonstrations improve success rates by 15–25 percentage points over RGB-only policies.

Key Points:

- Modular design combines a fixed robot-side coupling with interchangeable tool-side adapters.

- Tactile-augmented demonstrations increase policy success rates by 15 to 25 percentage points compared to RGB-only.
