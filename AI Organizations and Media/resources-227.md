### 🤖 NLP - LFM2.5-Encoder-350M

This article describes LFM2.5-Encoder-350M, a bidirectional masked language model designed for NLP tasks. The model fills in missing words by understanding both left and right context, offering a balance between performance and efficiency.

Key Points:
