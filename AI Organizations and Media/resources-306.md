### 📊 When Do Rankings Beat Signs? Gradient Recovery

When Do Rankings Beat Signs? Gradient Recovery introduces theoretical sample complexity bounds for batch rankings versus sign baselines. It shows reverse cross-pair comparisons cancel shared nuisance offsets and gives evaluation guarantees without curvature bounds.

Key Points:

- Reverse cross-pair comparisons cancel shared nuisance offsets without estimating a Hessian.

- If τ_a ≤ c ε/√d, one-round Reverse constraints localize every surviving direction within angle ε using ~O(d+√(d/ε)) evaluations, versus Θ(d/ε) for signs.
