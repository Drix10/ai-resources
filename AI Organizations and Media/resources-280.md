### 📊 New RL paper introduces segment reward feedback algorithms

The paper studies reinforcement learning with segment reward feedback under linear function approximation. It proposes algorithms bitssegd, edlinucbsegd, seglsvits and uneqsegbitsd. It claims binary feedback reduces regret exponentially with more segments, while sum feedback regret is largely unchanged by granularity.

Key Points:

- bitssegd and edlinucbsegd handle binary and sum feedback for equal‑length segments with known transitions.

- seglsvits integrates posterior reward estimates into least‑squares value iteration for unknown transitions.

### 🤖 VeriFine framework announced for scaling verification in embodied reasoning

VeriFine is an agent harness framework that scales verification through co‑evolution of policy, curriculum, and judge. It introduces a Policy Improvement Loop and a Judge Improvement Loop that use a rubric judge and selective human guidance. Experiments on driving and robot navigation tasks show continuous self‑improvement of policy and judge capability.

Key Points:

- **Framework**: VeriFine combines policy, curriculum, and judge co‑evolution.

- **Loops**: Policy Improvement Loop uses a rubric judge; Judge Improvement Loop queries humans to refine the judge.

### 📊 New paper links TCPA, DCPA, and Velocity Obstacles

The authors present a study of Time to Closest Point of Approach, Distance to Closest Point of Approach, and Velocity Obstacles. They derive conditions where the three give equivalent collision risk characterizations. They also extend the metrics to convex relative-state sets under bounded uncertainty.

Key Points:

- Relationship established between TCPA, DCPA, and VOs over finite and infinite horizons.

- Under bounded uncertainty, uncertainty‑aware VOs retain the joint relationship that independent TCPA and DCPA bounds lose.
