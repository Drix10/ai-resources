### 🤖 MiniMax P-H3-Image open-sourced, 5× faster 8 steps 4 MP

MiniMax P-H3-Image is open-sourced and can generate or edit images 5× faster in 8 steps instead of 50, at 4 MP. It is built from MiniMax-H3, a SOTA video model turned into an efficient open image model.

Key Points:

- **Release**: open-source P-H3-Image built from MiniMax-H3.

- **Efficiency**: generate or edit images 5× faster, in 8 steps instead of 50, at 4 MP.
