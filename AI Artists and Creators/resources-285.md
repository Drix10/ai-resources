### 🤖 GPT-6.2 Astra 5x above past two years' trend on robot-control

GPT-6.2 Astra sits nearly 5× above the past two years’ exponential trend on robot-control benchmarks. Benchmarks are saturating ~10× faster than the prior year and top policies may saturate nearly every tracked task by end-2027 if the current pace holds.

Key Points:

- **Trend**: GPT-6.2 Astra is ~5× above the exponential trend of the past two years on robot-control benchmarks.

- **Rate**: Benchmarks are saturating ~10× faster than the prior year; top policies could saturate nearly every tracked task by end-2027 if the pace since September continues.

### 🤖 Google SynthID Detector launches globally in English

Google SynthID Detector is now open to everyone in English, checking images, video and audio for SynthID watermarks from Google and partners. A clean result only means no SynthID mark was found.

Key Points:

- **Launch**: open to everyone in English since 7 Oct

- **Checks**: images, video and audio for SynthID watermarks from Google, OpenAI, NVIDIA, Kakao and Apple
