### 📊 Fairground AI TV – viewer watch time and programming heat map update

Fairground AI TV reports that viewers who watch beyond the first 60 seconds of a film continue for 21 minutes of AI cinema. The October update will include a heat map of top-performing programming windows.

Key Points:

- **Viewer retention**: 60 seconds threshold leads to 21 minutes of continued viewing.

- **Upcoming data**: October update will show a heat map of top-performing programming windows.

### 🤖 Brush design model launch by Figma announced

Brush is Figma’s first vision-language model for design, seeing layout, layers and vibe to edit alongside you. It can fix one line of text, restyle a whole deck or build from a blank page.

Key Points:

- **Brush**: vision-language model for design

- **Brush can edit designs**: fix text, restyle decks, build from blank page
