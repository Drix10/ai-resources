### 🚀 Seedream 5.5 enables 100 edits with pixel preservation

Seedream 5.5 lets you edit the same image 100 times without falling apart and adds native RGBA transparent backgrounds. It reports 0.4% severe text corruption and 1.7% minor, the best among tested models.

Key Points:

- 100 rounds of multi-turn editing with pixel-level preservation of unedited areas

- **Native RGBA**: transparent backgrounds generated directly, no separate background removal

### 🤖 Anthropic opens two new fellowships starting January 2027

Anthropic opens two new fellowships that begin in January 2027 and run for four months. STEM Fellows focus on science and engineering; Conceptual Reasoning Fellows focus on philosophy, cognitive science, economics, policy, and law. Applications are due October 28.

- **Application deadline**: October 28

- **Two tracks**: STEM Fellows (science and engineering) and Conceptual Reasoning Fellows (philosophy, cognitive science, economics, policy, law)
