### 🤖 MiniMax H3 using RGBMoCap and Character-Swap-LoRA

MiniMax H3 pipeline combines RGBMoCap reconstruction, skeletal retargeting, and a Character-Swap-LoRA diffusion model to create controllable videos. The workflow starts with phone footage, builds a 3D SMPL/SMPL-X mesh, aligns it to a target character, and renders appearance frame by frame.

Key Points:

- **RGBMoCap**: AI reconstructs a 3D human mesh (SMPL/SMPL-X) from ordinary phone footage, extracting skeletal animation without wearables.

- **Skeletal retargeting**: maps real body shape to target character, resolves foot sliding and mesh intersections, and outputs a clean 3D/ControlNet control layer.
