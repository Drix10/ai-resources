### 🤖 Microsoft MAI-DxO out-diagnoses doctors 83% of cases

Microsoft’s MAI-DxO AI solved 83% of 304 complex medical cases, outperforming physicians who achieved 36% with reference materials and 22% without. The system received information gradually, mirroring real diagnostic steps. Results appear in Science; prospective testing with Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center is planned.

Key Points:

- 83% accuracy on 304 cases drawn from the New England Journal of Medicine

- Physicians reached 36% accuracy with reference materials and 22% without them
