### 🤖 ElevenLabsAmbassador black liquid tentacle creature code

ElevenLabsAmbassador shows a black liquid tentacle creature driven entirely by code and real-time physics. Grip points are scattered and thinned with Poisson-disk sampling (~0.27m spacing). Tentacles have four states: Inside body, Extending, Gripping, Retracting.

Key Points:

- Grip points are generated by scattering candidates then Poisson-disk sampling with spacing ~0.27m, using spatial hashing for lookup.

- Tentacle rendering uses ray-marched SDFs, blending body spheres, capsule chains, droplets and threads with smooth min (smin).
