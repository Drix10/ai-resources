### 🤖 Deloitte forecasts AI fraud losses; umanitek launches Guardian

Deloitte predicts generative AI will increase US fraud losses from $12.3 billion in 2023 to $40 billion by 2027. The increase is linked to impersonation methods such as cloned voices, fake profiles, and deepfaked videos. umanitek is building a product called Guardian to counter these threats.

Key Points:

- Deloitte expects fraud losses to rise to $40 billion by 2027.
