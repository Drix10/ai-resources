### 🚀 AI Industry - Public Evaluations Becoming Irrelevant

Public evaluations are becoming less relevant in the AI industry. Models are increasingly being benchmaxxed, making it difficult to accurately assess their performance. The AI Application layer companies need their own internal evaluations, built on real outcomes and real task completion.

Key Points:

- **Benchmaxxing and its Impact**: Benchmaxxing refers to the practice of optimizing models for maximum performance on a specific benchmark, rather than for real-world applications. This can lead to models that are highly optimized for a specific task, but may not generalize well to other tasks.

- **Internal Evaluations**: Internal evaluations are necessary to assess the performance of models in real-world applications. This can be done by building custom evaluation frameworks that take into account the specific requirements and constraints of the application.

- **Real Outcomes and Task Completion**: Real outcomes and task completion are essential for evaluating the performance of models. This can be done by tracking metrics such as accuracy, precision, and recall, as well as by evaluating the model's ability to complete tasks in a real-world setting.
