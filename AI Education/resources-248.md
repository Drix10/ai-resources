### 🤖 AI Model Layer - Commoditization and Infrastructure Value

The AI model layer is rapidly commoditizing, with many companies offering similar large language models (LLMs) and AI capabilities. However, the infrastructure layer, which provides the underlying computing power and data storage, is where true value lies. This shift in focus is crucial for companies looking to leverage AI effectively.

Key Points:


- **Model Layer Commoditization**: The AI model layer is becoming increasingly commoditized, with many companies offering similar LLMs and AI capabilities. This commoditization is driven by the availability of open-source models and the ease of deployment.

- **Infrastructure Layer Value**: The infrastructure layer, which provides the underlying computing power and data storage, is where true value lies. Companies that focus on building robust and scalable infrastructure will be better positioned to leverage AI effectively.

- **Trade-offs and Failure Modes**: The commoditization of the model layer can lead to a lack of innovation and differentiation. Companies must balance the need for cost-effective infrastructure with the need for innovation and customization.

- **Actionable Takeaway**: Companies should focus on building robust and scalable infrastructure, rather than solely relying on the latest AI models. This will enable them to leverage AI effectively and stay ahead of the competition.
