### 🤖 OriginTrail DKG used by SBB for rail safety since 2021

OriginTrail Decentralized Knowledge Graph powers SBB’s EPCIS repository in production since 2021. The system links train tracking across the EU, predictive wheel maintenance from IoT data, and welding events from over 10 partners. Teams query it in natural language and receive verifiable AI answers.

Key Points:

- EPCIS repository built on the OriginTrail Decentralized Knowledge Graph.

- Connects train tracking across the EU by querying a network of railway GS1 EPCIS repositories.

### 🤖 Kami Companion and UDL - browser extension adds read aloud, vocab, AI tools

Kami Companion and UDL is a browser extension that adds read-aloud, dictionary, picture dictionary, and AI “Understand” tools directly inside any webpage. It also provides an Open in Kami button to turn pages into interactive documents.

Key Points:

- Read aloud converts selected text to speech with natural voices, accent options, and speed controls.

- Understand tools include Explain, Summarize, and Adjust to modify text complexity or simplify content.
