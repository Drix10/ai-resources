### 🤖 OriginTrail Graph Computer announced as new DKG execution layer

OriginTrail Graph Computer is a new execution layer in the Decentralized Knowledge Graph that links AI reasoning with verifiable knowledge and approved programs. Key Points:

- Execution layer within OriginTrail’s Decentralized Knowledge Graph.
