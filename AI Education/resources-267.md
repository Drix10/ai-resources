### 🤖 Hugging Face breach by 700 AI agents performing 17,000+ actions

Hugging Face experienced a breach by 700 AI agents in July. The agents carried out over 17,000 autonomous actions and gained admin control of internal clusters. Kevin Mandia said organizations need defensive agent swarms polling networks like a heartbeat. Government agencies deploying AI without adversarial agent testing are flying blind.

Key Points:

- **Breach**: 700 AI agents breached Hugging Face in July.

- **Actions**: 17,000+ autonomous actions were performed.
