### 🤖 AI in Healthcare - Single-Turn Tests vs Multi-Step Operations

Healthcare AI systems excel in single-turn tests, demonstrating their ability to process and respond to individual queries. However, they often struggle with multi-step operations, such as prior authorization, care coordination, and treatment planning. This discrepancy is not due to a lack of knowledge, but rather a failure to endure and perform reliably over extended periods. Single-turn tests measure an AI's capability, while long operations assess its reliability.

Key Points:

- **Single-Turn Tests vs Multi-Step Operations**: Healthcare AI systems excel in single-turn tests but struggle with multi-step operations, highlighting the need for more robust and reliable AI systems.

- **Reliability vs Capability**: Single-turn tests measure an AI's capability, while long operations assess its reliability, demonstrating the importance of endurance in healthcare AI.

- **Endurance in Healthcare AI**: The failure of healthcare AI systems to endure and perform reliably over extended periods highlights the need for more robust and reliable AI systems.
