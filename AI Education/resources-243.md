### 🤖 AI Research - Quantization Errors in Transformers

Why does cramming a giant language model into 4 bits still leave it so sharp? This paper nails the answer: pretrained Transformers *learn* to fight off quantization errors—each block’s update actively cancels 50–80% of new noise. Strip this “counteraction” away, and error/kl divergence explodes.

Key Points:

- **Quantization Errors in Transformers**: The paper explores the phenomenon of quantization errors in Transformers, which can lead to a significant decrease in model performance.

- **Counteraction Mechanism**: Pretrained Transformers have a built-in counteraction mechanism that actively cancels 50-80% of new noise, allowing them to maintain their performance even when quantized.

- **Error Divergence**: Without this counteraction mechanism, error divergence can explode, leading to a significant decrease in model performance.
