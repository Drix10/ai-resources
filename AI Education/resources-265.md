### 📊 Metco integration program – college graduation rise to 38%

Metco buses Boston Black and Hispanic students to mostly white suburban schools. Test scores rose modestly. College graduation rates for participants increased from 26% to 38% according to Chalkbeat (Oct 6). The program also raised participants’ adult incomes by several thousand dollars.

Key Points:

- **College graduation**: 26% → 38% (Chalkbeat, Oct 6)

- **Test scores**: modest increase, not large

- **Class size**: about two students smaller than peers’ average

- **Adult income**: several thousand dollars higher for alumni
