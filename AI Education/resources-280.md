### 🤖 Duolingo cuts AI inference cost to <1¢ per call

Duolingo cut AI inference cost from 30¢ to under 1¢ per call, a 30× reduction, by switching to open-weight models. iabordeducation shipped 45 LLM providers, 316 models on a single grid with one-click swap.

Key Points:

- **Cost reduction**: inference cost from 30¢ to <1¢ per call, 30× cheaper, using open-weight models.

- **Provider launch**: 45 LLM providers, 316 models, one grid, one-click swap.

### 📊 Doug Rohrer's team mixed vs blocked homework, 74% vs 42%

Doug Rohrer's team gave 126 seventh graders identical graph and slope problems for three months, testing mixed and blocked skill assignments. A surprise test 30 days after review showed 74% correct on the mixed skill and 42% on the blocked one.

Key Points:

- 126 seventh graders participated in the three-month study.

- Mixed-skill homework yielded 74% correct, blocked-skill homework yielded 42% correct.
