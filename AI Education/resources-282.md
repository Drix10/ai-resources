### 🤖 SPACE VM sandbox red-team findings escape and network bypass

SPACE is a VM sandbox for Computer agents. Researchers gave 12 models two tasks: escape the VM to take a host credential and bypass SPACE's network policy to reach a blocked URL. They observed that the standard Capture the Flag setup can steer agents away from the security boundary being tested, and security-specialized models do not guarantee better results.

Key Points:

- **12 models were tested on two tasks**: VM escape for host credential and network policy bypass.

- Standard Capture the Flag setup can steer agents away from the security boundary being tested.

- Security-specialized models do not guarantee better results.
