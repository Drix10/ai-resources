### 🤖 Ripple-65B released with 65B params, Apache 2.0, $32M training

Ripple-65B is a new 65 billion-parameter model released under Apache 2.0, trained from scratch for $32M. It scores 80.5 on TerminalBench 2.1 and fits on a single node.

Key Points:

- **Params**: 65B

- **Training cost**: $32M

- **Benchmark**: 80.5 on TerminalBench 2.1, beating a 501B model on agentic coding

- **License**: Apache 2.0, enterprise source stays local
