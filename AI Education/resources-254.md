### 🤖 AI Governance - Decentralized Knowledge Graphs (DKGs)

This approach ensures that AI decision-making is transparent, accountable, and reliable.

Key Points:

- **DKGs as Trust Infrastructure**: DKGs provide a decentralized, verifiable, and transparent trust infrastructure for AI decision-making. This enables the creation of trustworthy AI systems that can be relied upon in high-stakes applications.

- **Grounding Agents in Knowledge Graphs**: By grounding agents in a knowledge graph, AI systems can ensure that their decision-making is based on verifiable sources of information.
