### 📊 Edge Action Fund drops $2.5M each in Kansas and Nebraska Senate races

Edge Action Fund reports a $2.5 million contribution to the Kansas US Senate race and a $2.5 million contribution to the Nebraska US Senate race. The fund previously logged a single $500K contribution from the liberal dark-money 501(c)(4) group Global Impact Social Welfare Fund.

- **Contribution**: $2.5M to Kansas US Senate race

- **Contribution**: $2.5M to Nebraska US Senate race
