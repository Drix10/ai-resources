### 🤖 Vehicle Engineering - Aluminum Frame Alloys

This article discusses aluminum alloy selection for vehicle frame components based on deformation characteristics. It focuses on different alloys required for varying bending geometries.

Key Points:

• Main frame rails likely use 6061-T6 aluminum due to localized, slight bending.

• Components with extreme bending geometry would require 6063-T52 aluminum.

### 🤖 Vehicle Engineering - Chassis Design Trends

This article examines the rarity of tapering chassis designs in contemporary sports cars. It draws a comparison to the aluminum chassis architecture of the Lotus Elise.

Key Points:

• Tapering chassis designs are uncommon in modern sports cars.

• The Lotus Elise features a comparable aluminum chassis.

• The Elise chassis design is considered elegant.
