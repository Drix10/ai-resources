### 🚨 Apple cuts iPhone 18 Pro component orders amid soft demand

Apple told some suppliers to reduce production of parts for the iPhone 18 Pro. The same instruction also applied to the iPhone 18 Pro Max.

Key Points:

- Apple told some suppliers to reduce production of parts for the iPhone 18 Pro.

- Apple told some suppliers to reduce production of parts for the iPhone 18 Pro Max.

### 🚀 Ship 42 short visit to Massey's and return

Ship 42 visited Massey's and returned to Mega Bay 2 after 30 hours. It underwent at least one cryo test while preflight testing continues for Starship Flight 15 expected next month.

Key Points:

- **Visit**: Ship 42’s visit to Massey’s lasted 30 hours before returning to Mega Bay 2.

- **Test**: At least one cryo test was performed during the visit.
