### 🤖 Palestinian Health Ministry says 21,468 patients stranded, 3,056 evacuated since February

Palestinian Health Ministry says 21,468 patients remain on waiting lists, with only 3,056 evacuated since February. It calls for reopening Rafah for medical evacuations and lifting administrative restrictions.

Key Points:

- 21,468 patients remain on waiting lists, including 6,430 priority cases.

- 3,056 patients evacuated since February; 2,614 via Rafah and 442 via Kerem Shalom.
