### 🚨 Binance faces biggest crypto hack, $5.9B outflows

Binance faces what could be the biggest crypto hack in history with roughly $5.9B of suspicious outflows across multiple blockchains. Initial Ethereum transfer moved about $1.6B in USDT and 167,000 ETH. Tether and Circle have frozen more than $2.38B in stablecoins linked to the incident. CZ says it looks like a hack and expects full coverage of customer funds.

Key Points:

- Suspicious transfers from Binance hot wallets total approximately $5.9B across multiple blockchain networks.

- Tether and Circle have frozen over $2.38B in stablecoins related to the incident.

### 🤖 NVIDIA NVDA talks to back billions of debt for NSCALE

### 🚨 Polymarket paused trading after DebugBear hack, $2.65 million withdrawals

Polymarket temporarily paused trading on Thursday after the third-party tool DebugBear was hacked, resulting in about $2.65 million in unauthorized withdrawals. The platform promised to fully reimburse all affected users.

Key Points:
