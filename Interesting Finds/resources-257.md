### 🤖 Y Combinator Winter 2027 batch application deadline

Y Combinator announced the YC Winter 2027 batch application deadline is in 2 days, on Nov 2. The batch offers $500K funding, $12M+ in tokens, credits & deals, and a Demo Day before thousands of top investors.

Key Points:

- **Deadline to apply**: Nov 2.

- **Funding**: $500K plus $12M+ in tokens, credits & deals.

- **Demo Day audience**: thousands of top investors.

- [Original post](https://x.com/ycombinator/status/2116600929382527221)
- [Apply to YC Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/apply) - Linked page

### 🤖 Flex Q3 strongest quarter, revenue 2.5x YoY

Flex reports Q3 revenue grew 2.5x YoY with $20B+ annualized TPV and 98% gross logo retention. Accounts Payable now accounts for ~39% of revenue and global banking represents 12% of revenue.

Key Points:

- Revenue grew 2.5x YoY at scale

- Accounts Payable now accounts for ~39% of revenue
