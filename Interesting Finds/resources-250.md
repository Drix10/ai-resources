### 📊 Rebel Audio raises $10M Series A, valued at $50M

Rebel Audio is an AI podcasting platform. It announced a $10 million Series A funding round led by HardScope. The round values the company at about $50 million. HardScope was founded by DraftKings co‑founder Matt Kalish.

Key Points:

- **Funding**: $10 million Series A led by HardScope.

- **Valuation**: Approximately $50 million post‑money valuation.

### 🚀 VotebeatUS expert desk launched, free to join

VotebeatUS expert desk is now operational. It connects journalists to highly specific election sources. The service is free to join and is already being used by reporters who are publishing stories.

Key Points:

- **Service**: VotebeatUS expert desk is up and running.

- **Access**: Joining the expert desk is free.
