### 📊 FLIPR forecasts vs prediction markets show 3-point average difference

FLIPR forecasts align closely with prediction markets, with the average state differing by only 3 points. The markets are more bullish on Ds in AK and MS and more bearish in states with historical polling errors and the Stevens/AES issue in MI.

Key Points:

- Agreement between prediction markets and final FLIPR forecasts.

- Average state difference is only 3 points.
