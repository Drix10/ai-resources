### 🤖 Starcloud radiator 10x less mass per watt launching soon

Starcloud says its newly fabricated radiator, launching in a few months, is 10 × lighter per watt than the ISS radiator. It also claims 500 × lower cost per watt of dissipation.

Key Points:

- 2 years of R&D produced the radiator.

- 10x less mass per watt of dissipation than the ISS radiator.

- 500x less cost per watt of dissipation than the ISS radiator.

### 🤖 Anthropic Enterprise plan pricing jump to $1.36M for 151 users

Anthropic Enterprise plan costs $1.36M annually for 151 employees, up from $60k annually for 150 employees on the Team plan. The post calls the jump “bonkers” and says companies need a smoother transition.

Key Points:

- 150 people on the Team plan pay $60k annually.

- Adding one employee moves the company to the Enterprise plan, costing $1.36M annually for 151 people.

### 🤖 Citi Devin rollout expects $1.5B savings by 2026
