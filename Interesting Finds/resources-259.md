### 🚀 Zipline raised $1.5 billion, building nationwide drone delivery network

Zipline raised $1.5 billion in new funding. The money will go towards building a nationwide drone delivery network. The post also congratulates Keller and team.

Key Points:

- **Goal**: building a nationwide drone delivery network.

### 🤖 Regent Viceroy prototype high-cadence flight testing expands envelope
