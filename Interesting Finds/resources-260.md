### 🤖 Product Hunt 13th birthday shows 3x maker launches, 25% unicorn founders

Product Hunt celebrates its 13th birthday. It reports 3× more makers launching than a year ago and about 25% of all unicorn founders are on the site. It also notes $1B+ AI-era startups launched early on PH, naming several examples.

Key Points:

- 3x as many makers are launching as a year ago

- ~25% of all unicorn founders are on the site
