### 🤖 Britain banking industry case study shows 8% staff cut by AI

Britain’s banking industry case study shows an 8% net staff reduction due to AI in 2025. Finance has the highest share of occupations exposed to AI at 84%.

Key Points:

- 8% net staff reduction in British companies due to AI in 2025, double the average for US, Germany, Japan, Australia.

- 84% of finance-sector occupations are exposed to AI, the highest share of any industry.

### 📊 Anthropic commits 5% of stock to public benefit fund

Anthropic announced it will allocate 5% of its shares to a new public benefit fund, increasing its public-good stake to about 20% of total shares. The fund can grow as share value rises and may make large donations or direct distributions.

Key Points:

- 5% of Anthropic’s shares are committed to the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund.

- Roughly 20% of Anthropic’s shares will be dedicated to the public good when combined with existing commitments.

- $200 million has already been pledged to study AI’s labor market effects.

- Anthropic plans a massive IPO as early as November.
