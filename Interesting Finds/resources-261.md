### 🤖 SpaceX Anthropic OpenAI generate $4.2T liquidity

SpaceX, Anthropic and OpenAI together produced $4.2 trillion in liquidity this cycle, far exceeding all US VC-backed exits from 2016-2025.

Key Points:

- Liquidity from SpaceX, Anthropic and OpenAI totals $4.2 trillion.

- Every US VC-backed exit, 2016-2025, totals $2.6 trillion.

- SpaceX’s $1.7 trillion IPO alone is 65% of the $4.2 trillion.

- Only 42% of 2020-vintage funds have returned any cash.

### 🤖 Health insurance cost increase 25% next year

Health insurance cost increase 25% next year with same coverage, employees' costs outpace raises for nearly half of families. Data shows employee share of insurance costs growing faster than paychecks.

Key Points:

- 25% increase in health insurance cost for the same coverage next year.

- Employees’ cost increases more than exceeded their raises for nearly half of families.
