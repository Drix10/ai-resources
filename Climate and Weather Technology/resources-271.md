### 🚨 Oregon buoy 47 foot waves measured Friday

Oregon buoy measured 47-foot waves on Friday, the biggest from this Pacific storm. Buoys nearer shore logged 33-36-foot waves from Oregon to Monterey, still over 30 feet off Monterey this afternoon.

Key Points:

- Buoy off the Oregon coast recorded 47 foot waves on Friday.

- Buoys closer to shore reported 33-36 foot waves from Oregon down to Monterey, and remain over 30 feet off Monterey.

### 📊 SSP2-4.5 warming virtually certain above 2.76°C

SSP2-4.5 scenario shows maximum annual temperature virtually certain to exceed 2.76 °C, with a median of 3.46 °C and a high of 5.49 °C. A quoted tweet warns the real climate system could deliver warming equal to SSP5-8.5 (~4 °C) by century’s end.

Key Points:

- Maximum annual temperature “virtually certain” to exceed 2.76 °C for SSP2-4.5.

- Median projected warming 3.46 °C; high projection 5.49 °C for SSP2-4.5.
