### 🚨 Global Status of Multi-Hazard Early Warning Systems 2026

Global Status of Multi-Hazard Early Warning Systems 2026 reports 7 in 10 countries have systems, a 143% increase in a decade. The report was launched at COP31.

Key Points:

- 7 in 10 countries worldwide now have multi-hazard early warning systems.

- 143% increase in a decade.

- Launched at COP31.
