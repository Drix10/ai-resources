### 🤖 Air Products scraps $4.5B hydrogen project, $2.9B loss

Air Products announced it is abandoning a $4.5 billion hydrogen project due to too little return, adding other scrapped projects for a total $2.9 billion loss. The stock market moved up 8.6% despite the news.

Key Points:

- **Project cost**: $4.5 billion hydrogen project scrapped.

- **Total loss from scrapped projects**: $2.9 billion.
