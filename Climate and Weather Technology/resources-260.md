### 🚨 Belgian air-traffic radar false echoes from solar burst, 100k SFU at 1 GHz

Belgian air-traffic radar showed false aircraft echoes pointed toward the Sun on Nov 4, 2015. A ~100,000 SFU solar radio burst near 1 GHz was the likely cause. Radar software filtered the false targets and flights continued.

Key Points:

- False aircraft echoes pointed toward the Sun on Nov 4, 2015.

- ~100,000 SFU solar radio burst near 1 GHz likely caused the echoes.
