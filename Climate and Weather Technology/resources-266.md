### 🚨 1003 mb low near 27°N 59°W produces NE–E winds 30–40 kt

1003 mb low near 27°N 59°W is generating NE–E winds of 30–40 kt and seas of 8–17 ft. Gale-force winds and seas over 12 ft will continue north of the low through Sunday as it moves ENE across the central subtropical Atlantic.

Key Points:

- **Wind**: NE–E at 30–40 kt.

- **Seas**: 8–17 ft now, >12 ft north of low through Sunday.
