### 📊 Recon reports 931.1 mb EXTRAP for Hurricane Simon

Recon reports a preliminary EXTRAP of 931.1 mb inside Hurricane Simon. 125 kt FL winds suggest high C3 or low C4 winds are prevalent. Simon has been sheared a little bit recently.

Key Points:

- **EXTRAP**: 931.1 mb inside Hurricane Simon

- **FL winds**: 125 kt indicating high C3 or low C4 winds

### 📊 NWS Mobile assesses post‑Isaias wind corridor

NWS Mobile is reviewing preliminary data after Hurricane Isaias. A distinct corridor of enhanced winds of 80–110+ mph is observed west of the landfall location. Data review and quality control will continue. A final post‑storm assessment is expected in the coming days.

Key Points:

- **Wind corridor**: 80–110+ mph enhanced winds west of Isaias landfall.

- **Timeline**: Final post‑storm assessment expected in the coming days.

### 🤖 Isaias wind gusts exceed 100 mph in Florida Panhandle

Isaias produced wind gusts up to 120 mph in the Florida Panhandle. Over half a dozen sites recorded gusts above 100 mph. Pensacola Beach (East) reached 117 mph and Whiting Field north of Milton recorded 114 mph.

Key Points:

- **Gusts**: 100‑120 mph reported across the region.

- **Site reports**: Pensacola Beach (East) 117 mph; Whiting Field north of Milton 114 mph.
