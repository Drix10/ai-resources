### 🚀 Constellation-Microsoft 20‑year Power Deal for Unit 1 Restart

Constellation signed a 20‑year power agreement with Microsoft. The deal is to restart Unit 1 at Three Mile Island, now called the Crane Clean Energy Center. The restart is intended to support AI data centers.

Key Points:

- **Deal**: 20‑year power contract between Constellation and Microsoft.

- **Project**: Restart of Three Mile Island Unit 1, branded Crane Clean Energy Center.

### 📊 WeatherFront web app adds export for model trend and comparison loops

WeatherFront’s web application now supports exporting model trend loops. It also supports exporting model comparison loops. The feature is available in the WF web app.

Key Points:

- **Export**: model trend loops can be exported.

- **Export**: model comparison loops can be exported.
