### 📊 Thwaites Glacier melting more aggressively, impacts could double estimates

Thwaites Glacier in West Antarctica is melting "more aggressively" than previously projected, meaning its impacts on global sea level rise could be double current estimates. The post also notes there are 100+ sub-glacial volcanoes in the Antarctic rift zone.

Key Points:

- 100+ sub-glacial volcanoes lie within the Antarctic rift zone

- Impacts on sea level rise could be double current estimates due to accelerated melting of Thwaites Glacier
