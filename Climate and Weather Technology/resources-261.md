### 🚨 Connect and Manage policy ends, windfarm waste 75% output

Connect and Manage, introduced in 2009, is being ended. Windfarms such as Seagreen are reported to have 75% of their output discarded, costing millions daily.

Key Points:

- Connect and Manage introduced in 2009 allowed windfarms to connect without grid infrastructure.

- Seagreen windfarm reportedly discards 75% of its output.
