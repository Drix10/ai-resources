### 🚨 Excessive Rainfall Outlook – Day 3 Moderate Risk

The Weather Prediction Center issued a moderate risk for Day 3 in its Excessive Rainfall Outlook. The outlook predicts the probability that rainfall will exceed flash flood guidance within 40 km of a point.

Key Points:

- Moderate risk is in effect for Day 3 in the Excessive Rainfall Outlook.

- The ERO forecasts the chance rainfall will exceed flash flood guidance within 40 km of a location.
