### 🤖 Daily Briefing: Electrification pledge ‘wins support’

At least 14 countries have publicly backed the Turkish COP31 presidency’s electrification pledge, which aims to raise electricity’s share of global final energy consumption to 35% by 2035. The pledge targets 15 signatories and 32 additional countries signalling support, with the EU expected to join. Bloomberg reports the UK will endorse the goal.

Key Points:

- 14 countries, including Germany, South Korea, Pakistan and small island states, have publicly backed the pledge.

- Goal to increase electricity’s share of global final energy consumption to 35% by 2035, up from 23% currently.

### 🚨 Microsoft outage reaches Westminster MPs and GP practices in Grampian

Microsoft outage has reached Westminster, locking MPs out of parliamentary accounts, and GP practices in Grampian cannot log in to clinical systems, according to BBC and NHS Grampian.

Key Points:

- MPs locked out of parliamentary accounts
