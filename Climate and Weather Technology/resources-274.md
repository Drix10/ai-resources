### 🤖 High flood, power outage risk as warnings cover B.C. coast

Coastal flooding is a major concern in parts of B.C. due to high waves of 5-8 metres, heavy rain, strong winds up to 95 km/h and king tides. Environment and Climate Change Canada issued rainfall and coastal flood warnings for Vancouver Island, Metro Vancouver and the North Coast.

Key Points:

- High waves of 5-8 metres combined with strong winds up to 95 km/h and king tides create flood risk.

- Rainfall and coastal flood warnings are in effect for Vancouver Island, Metro Vancouver and the North Coast.
