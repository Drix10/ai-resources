### 🌎 Climate Change - Methane Cuts for 2C Warming

Methane cuts are crucial for keeping warming 'well-below' 2C. Guest authors Konstantin Weber and Bruno Tremolet de Lachapelle discuss the importance of methane reductions in the fight against climate change. They highlight the need for immediate action to mitigate the impact of methane on global warming.

Key Points:

- **Methane's Impact on Global Warming**: Methane is a potent greenhouse gas with a global warming potential 28 times higher than carbon dioxide over a 100-year time frame. Reducing methane emissions is essential for limiting global warming to 2C.

- **Current Methane Emissions**: Current methane emissions are estimated to be around 570 million metric tons per year, with agriculture, natural gas systems, and landfills being the primary sources.

- **Methane Reduction Strategies**: The authors propose several strategies for reducing methane emissions, including improving manure management, reducing synthetic fertilizer use, and implementing methane capture technologies in natural gas systems.
