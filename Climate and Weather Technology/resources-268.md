### 📊 Record November Snow In Japan – snowfall up to 11× normal

Record November Snow In Japan reports snow depths exceeding 11 times normal in parts of Hokkaido, with a provisional 46 cm record at Kutchan. Greenland’s ice sheet added 197.4 billion tonnes of surface mass since September 1, one of the highest on record.

Key Points:

- Kutchan accumulated 46 cm of snow in a rolling 24-hour period, potentially breaking its November record of 45 cm set in 2017.

- Greenland’s ice sheet accumulated 197.4 billion tonnes of surface mass since September 1, among the highest on record.
