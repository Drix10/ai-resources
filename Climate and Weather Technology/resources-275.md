### 📊 Temperature above 2°C record: eight consecutive days

Temperature data shows eight days in a row above 2 °C since Nov 19, breaking the previous ERA5 record of three days. November average is now 2.02 °C, on track to be the first month above 2 °C.

Key Points:

- Eight consecutive days above 2 °C since Nov 19.

- November average temperature is 2.02 °C, potentially the first month above 2 °C.

### 🤖 Storm Prediction Center Day 2 Convective Outlook marginal risk announced

Storm Prediction Center Day 2 Convective Outlook announces a marginal risk of severe thunderstorms across portions of the Ohio Valley, Mid-South and central Gulf Coast states for Sunday. The outlook notes isolated damaging winds and a possible tornado or two.

Key Points:

- Marginal risk of severe thunderstorms across Ohio Valley, Mid-South into central Gulf Coast states.

- 2-percent tornado and 5-percent wind probabilities maintained, expanded westward into southeast MO and northeast AR.
