### 📊 Lake Powell Update - water level up 22 feet since September

Lake Powell Update reports water level rose 22 feet since mid-September and is 7 feet below the same time last year. Current level is 161 feet below full pool and peaks typically in June/July.

Key Points:

- 22 feet rise since mid-September.

- Currently 161 feet below full pool.

### 📊 GMSST mean sea-surface temperature anomaly reaches 0.8 °C above 1991-2020 baseline

GMSST reports the mean sea-surface temperature anomaly (60°S – 60°N) climbed to 0.8 °C above the 1991-2020 baseline, a first since records began. Compared to the pre-industrial period (1850-1900) the increase is now +1.5 °C.

Key Points:

- Mean sea-surface temperature anomaly (60°S – 60°N) 0.8 °C above 1991-2020 baseline.

- Increase versus 1850-1900 pre-industrial period +1.5 °C.
