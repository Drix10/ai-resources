### 🚀 Artemis II Preliminary Lunar Science Report – observations released

Artemis II Preliminary Lunar Science Report makes available astronaut observations from the Artemis II mission. The crew reported five faint impact flashes from 46,000 mi away and noted subtle color variations across the lunar surface. The report includes photographs, drawings, and annotations for public and scientific use.

Key Points:

- **Impact flashes**: five extremely faint “pinprick” bursts seen from 46,000 mi, each from rocks no more than a couple of inches wide.

- **Color observations**: crew saw hues of gray, black, brown, tan, white, green, and blue, linked to titanium, iron, and glass content.
