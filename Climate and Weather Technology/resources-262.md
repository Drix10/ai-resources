### 🚀 World’s largest battery-electric ferry now operating in South America

World’s largest battery-electric ferry built in Tasmania is now sailing in South America. It carries 2,100 people and 225 vehicles, makes up to four 75-minute crossings per day, and estimates US$4.5 million annual fuel savings with a 5–8 year payback.

Key Points:

- **Capacity**: 2,100 passengers and 225 vehicles.

- **Operations**: Up to 4 crossings per day, each about 75 minutes.

- **Savings**: Estimated US$4.5 million fuel savings per year.

- **Payback**: Projected 5–8 year payback period.
