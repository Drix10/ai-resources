### 📊 CANSIPS model shows major El Niño impact, +4" rain Orlando

CANSIPS model re-ran this week and predicts a major El Niño impact with a +4″ rain anomaly around Orlando in December. That would place December among the top-5 wettest on record, with 1997 at 12.63″ as the wettest.

Key Points:

- **Rain anomaly**: +4″ above normal around Orlando.

- Top-5 wettest December; 1997 ranks #1 with 12.63″ rain.

### 🚨 Storm Prediction Center marginal risk for East Tennessee storms

Storm Prediction Center issued a level one (marginal) risk for severe storms across East Tennessee, extending into north Georgia. Greatest timing is around 4-6 PM tonight with strong gusty wind threat.

Key Points:

- Level one marginal risk issued by SPC for East Tennessee and north Georgia.

- Main threat is strong, gusty winds with isolated strong storms around 4-6 PM.
