### 📊 Brevard County rain – 39 inches in three weeks

Brevard County received 39 inches of rain in the past three weeks, matching the typical annual total for Chicago. Two storms contributed over 70% of a normal year’s rainfall in parts of the county.

Key Points:

- 70% of a typical year’s rain fell from two storms in three weeks.

- 39 inches of rain fell, about the annual total for Chicago.

### 🤖 Fall Florida rainfall totals to date, 75% of normal year

Fall Florida rainfall totals to date are at 75% of a normal year’s amount. There are still 10 days left to add to these totals, including tomorrow’s coastal low.

Key Points:

- Rainfall totals are at 75% of a normal year’s amount
