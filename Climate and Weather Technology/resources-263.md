### 🚨 Kyle - 40% chance of tropical development in Atlantic

Kyle has a 40% chance of acquiring tropical characteristics later this week. November tropical storms occur on average every other year and November hurricanes every three years.

Key Points:

- 40% chance of acquiring tropical characteristics later this week.

- Average November tropical storm frequency: one every other year.

- **Average November hurricane frequency**: one every three years.

### 🤖 NAM regional weather model ends after 21-year run

NAM regional weather model ended its 21-year run on Tuesday. It was noted for accurately forecasting the QPF max between Albany and Utica during the 2007 Valentine’s Day blizzard, enabling a 40-inch contour on a forecast map.

Key Points:

- 21-year run concluded on Tuesday.

- Produced accurate QPF max between Albany and Utica for the 2007 Valentine’s Day blizzard, supporting a 40-inch contour.
