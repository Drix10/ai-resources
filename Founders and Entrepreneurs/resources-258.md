### 🤖 AI/ML - Connectionist Neural Networks

Connectionist neural networks, also known as artificial neural networks, are a type of machine learning model inspired by the structure and function of the human brain. They consist of layers of interconnected nodes or "neurons" that process and transmit information. This architecture allows for the representation of complex relationships between inputs and outputs, making them a powerful tool for tasks such as image and speech recognition, natural language processing, and game playing.

Key Points:

- **Connectionist Neural Networks**: Connectionist neural networks are a type of machine learning model that uses layers of interconnected nodes or "neurons" to process and transmit information. This architecture allows for the representation of complex relationships between inputs and outputs.

- **Artificial Neural Network**: Connectionist neural networks are also known as artificial neural networks, a term that refers to the use of neural networks in artificial intelligence and machine learning.

- **Machine Learning Applications**: Connectionist neural networks have a wide range of applications in machine learning, including image and speech recognition, natural language processing, and game playing.
