### 🤖 CrowdStrike buys Fiddler AI for $400M, Dreadnode for $95M

CrowdStrike is acquiring Fiddler AI for about $400 million and Dreadnode for about $95 million, mostly in cash. Fiddler AI helps monitor AI agents; Dreadnode is an automated security AI testing tool.

Key Points:

- **Fiddler AI acquisition price**: ~$400M, mostly cash

- **Dreadnode acquisition price**: ~$95M, mostly cash

### 🤖 Fathom synthetic user testing uses 50 personas for 30-day product runs

Fathom calls LLM personas “browserbase” to run 30 simulated days in a product. Fifty customer calls become fifty personas, each with a browser that uses the product for 30 days, and features liked on day 1 or hated on day 10 are cut.

Key Points:

- 50 customer calls become 50 personas, each gets a browser and uses the product for 30 days.

- Features loved on day 1 and disliked on day 10 are removed.

### 🚨 Nvidia data centre policy caps China compute at 49%
