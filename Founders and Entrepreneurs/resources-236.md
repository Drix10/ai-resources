The Dangers of Unchecked Power

There is no right government, group, company, or person to put this kind of power in the sole hands of. The threat posed by rogue actors with access to the same power or even the ASI itself is so far past the threat posed by the ASI itself.

Key Points:


- **AI Safety**: The development of advanced AI systems raises significant safety concerns, including the potential for unintended consequences and the risk of misuse.

- **Unchecked Power**: The concentration of power in the hands of a single entity or individual is a recipe for disaster, whether it is a government, company, or individual.

- **Distributed Power**: Distributing power and decision-making authority is critical to ensuring that AI systems are developed and used responsibly.
