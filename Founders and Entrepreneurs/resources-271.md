### 🤖 Stingray AI laser robots kill lice on salmon, 2,000 units

Stingray's underwater AI laser robots target and kill sea lice on salmon, with over 2,000 units operating in Norway. Each robot uses two cameras, AI verification, and a green laser up to 2 meters away to eliminate lice one at a time.

Key Points:

- **Deployment**: more than 2,000 robots guard over 50 million salmon and trout 24/7.

- **Operation**: two cameras watch each salmon, AI checks two frames, then a green laser fires from up to 2 meters away to kill the louse.
