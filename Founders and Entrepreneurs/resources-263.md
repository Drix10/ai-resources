### 🤖 Destinus builds 1,750 Ruta missiles, 300/month output

Destinus reports building more than 1,750 Ruta cruise missiles in Europe and now produces nearly 300 missiles each month. The company also began production of the larger Ruta Block 2 missile with a 250 kg payload and over 700 km range.

Key Points:

- 1,750 Ruta missiles built in Europe.

- Monthly output of Ruta missiles reached nearly 300 units.
