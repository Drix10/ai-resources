### 🚀 Business - Software Business Evolution

The days of steady progress on interesting software businesses are coming to an end. Either be on the cutting edge or hyper-niche with domain expertise. This shift is driven by the increasing complexity of software development and the need for specialized skills.

Key Points:

- **Cutting-Edge vs Hyper-Niche**: The software business landscape is evolving, and companies must adapt to remain competitive. This requires either investing in cutting-edge technologies or specializing in a specific domain.

- **Domain Expertise**: Domain expertise is becoming increasingly important as software development becomes more complex. Companies that specialize in a specific domain can differentiate themselves and attract high-quality talent.

- **Adaptation is Key**: The software business is constantly evolving, and companies must be willing to adapt to remain competitive. This requires a willingness to invest in new technologies and skills.
