### 🤖 Neuron interpretability finds language detector neuron 99.1% accuracy

Neuron interpretability finds a language detector neuron that achieves 99.1% accuracy with a single neuron per language. Another neuron tracks speaking speed. A VAD fix edits 10KB of weights, about 50× fewer parameters than traditional VAD.

Key Points:

- **Language detector**: 1 neuron per language, 99.1% accuracy.

- **VAD fix**: edit 10KB of weights, ~50× fewer parameters than traditional VAD.
