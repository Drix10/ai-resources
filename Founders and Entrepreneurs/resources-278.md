### 📊 Saylor and Tom Lee down $23 billion combined loss

Saylor and Tom Lee are down a combined $23 billion. Strategy holds 850,849 Bitcoin at an average cost of $75,452, about $13.3B underwater. BitMine holds 6.08M Ethereum at an average cost of ~$3,316, about $9.9B underwater. Bitcoin is 21% below Saylor's average and Ethereum is 49% below Tom Lee's.

Key Points:

- **Strategy**: 850,849 Bitcoin at $75,452 average cost, $13.3B underwater.

- **BitMine**: 6.08M Ethereum at ~$3,316 average cost, $9.9B underwater.
