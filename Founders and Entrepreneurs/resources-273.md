### 🚀 SpaceX Starship Flight 15 launches, booster catch aborted

SpaceX launched Starship Flight 15 on Nov 13, reaching orbit and deploying 26 Starlink V3 satellites, while aborting the planned Super Heavy “chopsticks” catch and splashing down the booster in the Gulf of Mexico. The upper stage will remain in low-Earth orbit for about 22 hours before a deorbit burn.

Key Points:

- **Flight 15 lifted off at 5**: 47 p.m. ET (2247 GMT) from Starbase, Texas.

- Super Heavy was dropped for a controlled splashdown 6.5 minutes after launch.

- Ship entered a nominal 170-mile (275 km) orbit at T+26 min 10 s and deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites over a 42-minute span.

- Ship is scheduled for a deorbit burn at ~T+21 h 36 min and return to Starbase about 22.5 hours after liftoff.
