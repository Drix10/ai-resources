### 📊 Parallel for Finance – most accurate search API on FinSearchComp T3 up to 2× lower cost

Parallel for Finance is announced as the most accurate search API on FinSearchComp’s hardest tier (T3) with up to 2× lower cost than Exa and Perplexity. It traces earnings-call goals over time and updates as new calls complete. Built with @p0’s task & monitor APIs.

Key Points:

- Most accurate search API on FinSearchComp T3, up to 2× lower cost than Exa and Perplexity.

- Traces each earnings-call goal through time and updates regularly as new calls complete.

### 🤖 Nvidia H200 chips Chinese buyers non-existent or military-linked

Nvidia H200 chips were approved for sale to Chinese companies, but the NYT found several of those companies appear not to exist or have direct ties to the Chinese military. The report adds a new detail to the ongoing Nvidia spy scandal.

Key Points:

- Commerce Department approved Chinese firms to purchase H200 Nvidia chips.
