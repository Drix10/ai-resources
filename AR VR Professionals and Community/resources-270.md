### 🤖 Shoals Blender addon – free animated fish schools for Blender 5.2+

Shoals is a free Blender addon that creates animated fish schools. It works with Blender 5.2 and above, lets you mix species, adjust swimming, and bake animations. The fish models are shared for free and include movable mouth, eyes, and fins.

Key Points:

- Free Blender addon named Shoals for creating animated schools.

- Compatible with Blender 5.2 and later.
