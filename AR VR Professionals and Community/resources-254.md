### 🚀 kindlingai engine TP=4 GLM 5.3 full build testing announced

kindlingai engine is opening testing today. Users can join the kindlingai Discord to pick up a TP=4 GLM 5.3 full build in a few hours. The post includes benchmark numbers for code and prose token throughput.

Key Points:

- **Availability**: TP=4 GLM 5.3 full build offered on the kindlingai Discord in a few hours.

- **Benchmark**: Code 49.5 tok/s (48.4–49.9) ✓ 5/5; Prose 30.4 tok/s (29.7–31.3).

### 🚨 Anthropic updates Claude usage policy with abuse restrictions

Anthropic announced a revision to its usage policy for Claude, the first change in over a year. The new rules forbid sustained abusive or cruel behavior toward Claude. They also add restrictions on propaganda campaigns, surveillance, and weapon development.

Key Points:

- **Policy change**: first update in over a year.

- **New prohibitions**: abusive or cruel behavior, propaganda, surveillance, weapon development.
