### 🚀 BlackMagic Ambisonic microphone $1695 launch December

BlackMagic Ambisonic microphone costs $1695 and launches in December, records via a single USB-C cable, even when connected to an iPhone. It is described as an Ambisonic microphone for high-fidelity Spatial Audio.

Key Points:

- **Price**: $1695 USD

- **Launch**: December

- Records using a single USB-C cable, works with an iPhone

### 🤖 Graspable – voice-driven XR development announced

Graspable.dev adds voice interaction, letting users speak requests instead of typing and converse with an agent while building XR experiences. The voice mode works at the desk and continues after putting on the headset by pressing Talk. Engineers can build and preview projects using spoken commands.

Key Points:

- Voice-driven XR development introduced

- Speak requests, hold conversation, and continue speaking in headset with Talk button
