### 🚨 Centralization & Control: A Senior Engineer's Perspective

Centralization and control are often seen as a means to simplify complex systems, but this approach can have unintended consequences. The instinct to coordinate systems from the top and create hierarchies can lead to a loss of autonomy and innovation.

Key Points:

- **The Importance of Autonomy**: Autonomy allows for innovation and adaptation, as individuals and teams can experiment and learn from their mistakes.

- **The Role of Hierarchy**: Hierarchies can stifle innovation and creativity, as individuals may be reluctant to challenge authority or take risks.
