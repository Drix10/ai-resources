### 🤖 Arete-27B – post-trained Qwen3.8-27B with Western values, 803-prompt eval

Arete-27B is Qwen3.8-27B post-trained on roughly 53 million tokens to reinforce Western values. An 803-prompt evaluation across seven value categories shows propaganda rate fell from 22% to 1%. Hermes Bench scores show no significant performance trade-offs.

Key Points:

- Propaganda rate reduced from 22% to 1% after fine-tuning.

- Evaluation used 803 prompts covering freedom of expression, democracy, privacy, and four other categories.

### 🚨 Pegasus spyware iPhone infection via fake GIF

Pegasus spyware infected iPhones without the user tapping anything by sending an iMessage with a fake GIF that targeted Apple’s image decompression engine. Researchers found the decompression math was flexible enough to enable the exploit.

Key Points:

- **Infection method**: iMessage with a fake GIF.

- **Targeted component**: Apple’s image decompression engine.
