### 🤖 Meta VR Glasses hand tracking space game adds snap poses

Meta VR Glasses hand tracking space game lets players grab throttle sliders or handlebar controls and have their hands snap into predefined poses until released. The snap poses aim to make interactions feel more natural and intuitive.

Key Points:

- Hands snap into predefined poses when grabbing throttle sliders or handlebar controls

- Hands stay in the snapped pose until released
