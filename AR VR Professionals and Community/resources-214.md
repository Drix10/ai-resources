### 🤖 AR/VR Hardware - Eye Tracking Constraints

Eye tracking systems in headsets require consistent positioning for accurate data collection. Recalibration is necessary if headset placement changes, which presents a challenge for smaller form factors like smart glasses.

Key Points:

• Eye tracking accuracy depends on consistent headset positioning.

• Headset recalibration is needed if its position changes.

• This requirement creates challenges for compact glasses form factors.

• The absence of eye tracking in certain products may stem from these constraints.
