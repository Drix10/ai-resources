### 🚀 Switch 2 Mii Creation adds mouse drawing feature

Switch 2 Mii Creation will add a mouse feature to draw details such as the nose on your Mii. The claim is based on a patent.

Key Points:

- **Update**: Nintendo will allow you to use the mouse feature to draw features on your Mii like your nose.

- **Patent**: The change is referenced in a disclosed patent.

### 🤖 Dual-Core BLDC MOTOR open source CAD files released

Dual-Core BLDC MOTOR is now open source with all CAD files and parts listed in the description. The design offers high torque output and compact size but is harder to manufacture at scale and more mechanically complex.

Key Points:

- **Design**: high torque output and compact size

- **Trade-off**: harder to manufacture at scale and more mechanically complex

### 🤖 Muse launches email address, can send and receive mail

Muse now offers its own email address and can send and receive emails on your behalf. The feature is rolling out to everyone in supported markets. Users can access it via the avatar mail tab or by asking.

Key Points:

- **Launch**: Muse email address available, can send and receive emails on your behalf.

- **Rollout**: Feature rolling out to everyone in supported markets.
