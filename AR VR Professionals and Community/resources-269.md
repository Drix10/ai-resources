### 🤖 Hyper3D AI Rig Anything by Text launches auto-rigger

Hyper3D AI Rig Anything by Text is a new auto-rigger that lets users edit skeletons with plain text. It supports humans, animals and fantasy creatures and includes skinning modes, motion presets and FBX import.

Key Points:

- Picks the skeleton from the model or an image

- 250+ motion presets, per Deemos

### 🤖 Claude Personal Finance beta launches US, read-only

Claude Personal Finance beta launches in the US, offering read-only Plaid connections so Claude can answer money questions. Claude cannot move, manage, or invest your money.

Key Points:

- Personal Finance in Claude, in beta in the US.

- Access is read-only via Plaid; Claude can't move, manage, or invest your money.
