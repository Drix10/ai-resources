### 🤖 Parallel Language Models achieve 5.7x speedup with parallel decoder

Parallel Language Models predict several tokens in parallel at autoregressive quality and add a parallel decoder at pretraining, delivering a 5.7x speedup after posttraining. The approach changes how LLMs are trained.

Key Points:

- Parallel Language Models predict several tokens in parallel at autoregressive quality.

- Adding a parallel decoder at pretraining yields a 5.7x speedup after posttraining.

### 🤖 Idol open image-to-3D model with 600M parameters runs on consumer GPUs

Idol is an open image-to-3D model that generates complete 3D objects from a single image. It contains 600M parameters, runs on consumer GPUs, uses less than 7.2GB VRAM, and can produce 1M splats in 3 seconds.

Key Points:

- **Parameters**: 600M

- **Performance**: 1M splats in 3 seconds using <7.2GB VRAM
