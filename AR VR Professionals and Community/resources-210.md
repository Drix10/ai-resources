### 🚀 Nativ v0.1.0 Release - AI Development Environment Updates

Nativ v0.1.0 introduces new functionalities for AI development, focusing on image generation, expanded integrations, and system monitoring. This release enhances the platform's capabilities for developers working with AI tools.

Key Points:

• Image generation and editing are available via a dedicated tab and directly within chat.

• Eleven new integrations support various development environments, including Cursor, Zed, and VS Code.

• Live system monitoring for CPU, GPU, and memory is included.
