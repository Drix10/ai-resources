### 🚀 Anthropic to spend $120.5B on compute from SpaceX AI unit

Anthropic agreed to spend up to $120.5 billion on compute from SpaceX’s AI unit, using Nvidia chips. Capacity is expected by December 2026 and Anthropic will pay about $3.1 billion per month, with the deals extending into 2029.

Key Points:

- **Spend**: up to $120.5 billion on compute from SpaceX’s AI unit.

- **Payment**: about $3.1 billion per month, capacity delivered December 2026, deals run into 2029.
