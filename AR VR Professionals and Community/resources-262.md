### 🤖 Twilight Hour on Quest 3 launches for $9.99 with full campaign

Twilight Hour is now available on Quest 3 for $9.99. It is a dark fantasy action RPG played on a living diorama with three heroes, a three-act story campaign, procedurally generated dungeons, giant bosses, and an endless endgame mode.

Key Points:

- **Price**: $9.99 on Quest 3.

- **Three heroes**: Warrior, Mage, Archer, each selectable as male or female.

- **Full story campaign**: three acts covering a betrayal, the siege of Hollowmere, and a final choice over the crown.

- **Endgame**: The Hollow Depths endless descent with records to beat.
