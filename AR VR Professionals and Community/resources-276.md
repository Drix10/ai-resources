### 🤖 Inkling 2 Mini released with 26.4B params, 20GB footprint

Inkling 2 Mini is an open-weights looped transformer with 26.4B total parameters (3.4B active) and a ~20GB memory footprint, now runnable on a laptop. It leads open-weights models under 100B on DeepSWE v1.1 with a 48.2% score.

Key Points:

- **Total parameters**: 26.4B, active parameters: 3.4B.

- **Memory footprint**: ~20GB, runs on consumer laptops.

- Loops middle layers three times per token.

- **DeepSWE v1.1 benchmark score**: 48.2%.

### 🤖 VidLab launch – 1M free generations of model 1.5

VidLab announced a co-founded API that is the cheapest to use any model, and offers 1M free generations of VidLab 1.5. It also provides many optimized systems that agents can call via the API.

Key Points:

- Cofounded VidLab by @Mho_23 and the author

- 1M free generations of VidLab 1.5 available on sign-up
