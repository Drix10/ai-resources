### 📊 Creating ILM's Headless Horseman in Tim Burton’s ‘Sleepy Hollow’ – ILM created 220 shots

Creating ILM's Headless Horseman in Tim Burton’s ‘Sleepy Hollow’ details ILM’s work on the film, including 300 horseman shots with 220 created by ILM. The team used a blue hood on stunt actor Ray Park to digitally erase the head and added a digital collar to match movement.

Key Points:

- 300 horseman shots total, 220 created by ILM.

- Stunt actor Ray Park wore a special blue hood that was keyed out to remove the head.
