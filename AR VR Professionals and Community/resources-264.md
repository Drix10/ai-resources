### 🤖 Open October: 10 new open LLM architecture releases

Open October lists ten open LLM architecture releases in October, including Kolibri-1 (78B) with GQA and SWA. The post enumerates each model, its size, and key attention modules.

Key Points:

- **Kolibri-1 (78B)**: classic Grouped Query Attention and Sliding Window Attention.

- **Mistral Large 4 (1T)**: combines MLA with sparse attention.

### 🤖 Siemens offers three services for robotics companies: digital genesis

Siemens outlines three offerings for robotics firms moving from pilot to fleet: digital genesis to design and validate robots, autonomous production to scale manufacturing, and the coexistence loop to assess integration with existing lines. These services draw on Siemens’ experience in automotive mass production.

Key Points:

- **Digital genesis**: design, engineer and virtually validate a new robot before it exists.

- **Autonomous production**: build robots at the scale Siemens helped automotive build cars.

- **Coexistence loop**: evaluate digitally how a new humanoid fits alongside people and existing automation before site arrival.
