### 🤖 DOJ issues subpoenas to California election officials

DOJ issues subpoenas to California election officials as part of a criminal investigation into the state’s voter rolls. The subpoenas aim to support claims of thousands of non-citizens on voter rolls.

Key Points:

- DOJ issued grand jury subpoenas to local election officials in California

- Subpoenas are part of a criminal investigation into the state’s voter rolls
