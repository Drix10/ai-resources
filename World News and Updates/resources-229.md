### 📊 Truth Social traffic fell 30% year ending September

Truth Social traffic fell more than 30% in the year ending September, averaging 260,000 daily users last month. The platform peaked at 420,000 daily users in June 2025.

Key Points:

- **Traffic decline**: fell more than 30% in the year ending September.

- **Daily users**: averaged about 260,000 last month, down from 420,000 in June 2025.

### 🤖 SK Hynix seeks US approval to export equipment to China

SK Hynix is seeking approval from Washington to export US chipmaking equipment to China to increase production there. The approval is presented as a condition for building a factory in the US.

Key Points:

- Seeking approval from Washington

- Export US chipmaking equipment to China

- Condition for building a US factory
