### 🚨 Crypto markets 11th-largest liquidation, $2.7B lost

Crypto markets saw $2.7 billion of levered positions liquidated in 24 hours, marking the 11th-largest liquidation event in crypto history. Since the October 2025 peak, the market has shed $2.3 trillion in market cap.

Key Points:

- **Liquidated levered positions**: $2.7 billion in 24 hours

- **Event rank**: 11th-largest liquidation in crypto market history

- **Market cap decline**: -$2.3 trillion since October 2025 high
