### 🤖 Microsoft outage - global service disruption affecting Outlook and Teams

Microsoft is scrambling to fix a major global outage that disrupted Outlook, Teams, and other services for tens of millions of users. The incident began at 11:17 a.m. ET on Thursday and affected 74 countries within an hour, with peak reports of 19,650 per minute across five services.

Key Points:

- **Outage began at 11**: 17 a.m. ET Thursday.

- Reports peaked at 19,650 per minute across Outlook, Microsoft 365, OneDrive, Copilot, and Teams.

### 🤖 Nvidia shut out of China AI data-center market, $3B projects

Nvidia says US export controls have effectively shut it out of China's AI data-center market. Reuters review found more than $3 billion in Chinese computing projects built around Nvidia's restricted processors.

Key Points:

- US export controls have effectively shut Nvidia out of China's AI data-center market

- Reuters review identified more than $3 billion in Chinese computing projects using Nvidia's restricted processors
