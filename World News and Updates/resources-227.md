### 🚨 Japan extradites suspected Qilin hacking leader to Germany

Japan has extradited an unnamed suspected leader of the Russian Qilin hacking group to Germany. German officials say Qilin extorted about $2.9 billion from roughly 4,000 companies worldwide since 2024. German police infiltrated the group and traced cryptocurrency payments. The suspect was captured while on holiday in Japan.

Key Points:

- **Extradition**: Japan handed over an unnamed suspected Qilin leader to German authorities.

- **Extortion amount**: Qilin is reported to have extorted about $2.9 billion from around 4,000 companies since 2024.
