### 🤖 Fuel tanker crash ignites a massive fire on a highway surrounding Cairo

Fuel tanker crash ignites a massive fire on a highway surrounding Cairo, killing at least 31 people. The blaze on Ring Road injured at least 21 people and charred more than two dozen vehicles. Rescue workers pulled victims from burning cars and DNA investigations are underway. President Abdel-Fattah el-Sissi expressed condolences.

Key Points:

- **Death toll**: at least 31 people

- **Injured**: at least 21 people

### 🚨 FAA ground stop at Miami Airport after FedEx jet overshoot

FAA issued a ground stop at Miami Airport after a FedEx cargo jet overshot the runway. It follows an Amazon cargo jet overshoot at the same airport that killed five people.

Key Points:

- **FAA ground stop**: Miami Airport
