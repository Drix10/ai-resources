### 🤖 Saturday flight cancellations top 5,200 as airlines work to recover

Over 5,200 flights were canceled on Saturday while airlines work around the clock to restore service. FAA Administrator Bryan Bedford said the system will be fully recovered on Sunday, with 160 flights canceled so far that day. Nearly 9,200 flights were canceled on Friday during the nationwide ground stop.

Key Points:

- 5,200+ flights canceled Saturday.

- System expected to be fully recovered Sunday; 160 flights canceled by 3:30 p.m. ET.

### 🚀 SpaceX Starship catches top part with chopsticks test 15

SpaceX caught the top part of its Starship rocket using mechanical arms nicknamed “chopsticks” during test 15. The attempt was live-streamed and the booster was aborted, bursting into flames above the ocean.

Key Points:

- Mechanical arms called “chopsticks” were used to catch the rocket’s top part.

- The first attempt was aborted; the booster burst into flames just above the sea.

- The top part was scheduled to return at about 15:15 local time (21:15 GMT) on Saturday.

### 🚨 FAA lifts nationwide ground stop at 8:15 p.m.

FAA lifts nationwide ground stop at 8:15 p.m. after a communications failure caused widespread disruption. A flight from Punta Cana to Chicago waited on the tarmac for about two and a half hours.

Key Points:

- **Ground stop lifted**: 8:15 p.m. FAA lifted the nationwide ground stop.

- **Flight delay**: passengers from Punta Cana to Chicago waited on the tarmac for about two and a half hours.
