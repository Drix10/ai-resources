### 🤖 Tesla Cybertruck production to wind down mid-2027

Tesla Cybertruck will cease production around mid-2027 as Elon Musk announced winding down at the Austin plant. Sales were 20,237 last year, far below the forecast of 250,000 units per year.

Key Points:

- Production winding down mid-2027 at Austin, Texas facility.

- 20,237 Cybertrucks sold last year, about half of the previous year’s volume.

- Sales through September this year were down 28% from the prior period.

### 🤖 Retatrutide illegal sale, UK hospital case

Retatrutide, an unlicensed weight-loss drug still in US trials, is being sold illegally online in the UK. A Yorkshire woman was hospitalized after an allergic reaction to a product bought on Facebook.

Key Points:

- Unlicensed weight-loss drug retatrutide still undergoing US clinical trials is sold illegally online in the UK.

- Sellers offer bulk orders of retatrutide from China; a Yorkshire woman was hospitalized after a severe allergic reaction to a Facebook-bought product.
