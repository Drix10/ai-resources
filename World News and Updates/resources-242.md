### 🤖 Appeals court grants Trump request to halt order restoring Venezuelan TPS

Appeals court keeps deportation protections frozen for hundreds of thousands of Venezuelan migrants, extending the freeze for seven days after the Trump administration filed an emergency Supreme Court appeal. Federal attorneys report about 1,800 Venezuelans in detention and roughly 330 in deportation proceedings.

Key Points:

- Freeze on deportation protections extended until seven days after the court’s full ruling.

- Federal attorneys cite 1,800 detained Venezuelans and 330 facing deportation proceedings.

### 🚨 International Court of Justice rules Myanmar committed genocide

International Court of Justice ruled Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya minority, the first U.N. top court finding of a state violating the 1948 Genocide Convention. The ruling could weigh on other cases.

Key Points:

- **Ruling**: Myanmar committed genocide against Rohingya minority.
