### 🤖 Advertisement urging end of US military aid to Israel dropped by NBC

NBC pulled a 30-second ad from a Palestinian rights group that called for ending US military aid to Israel; the spot showed a girl saving $400 for a bike and another injured child, and cited “every three seconds” $400 sent to Israel. The ADL called the decision “indefensible” and B’nai B’rith called the ad hateful and misleading.

Key Points:

- 30-second spot aired Wednesday, showing a girl saving $400 for a bicycle and a child on crutches in Gaza.

- ADL chief Jonathan Greenblatt called NBC’s decision to air the ad “indefensible,” saying it demonizes Israel and makes false claims about taxpayer dollars.

### 🚨 Thailand bans chemicals export for Myanmar rare earth

Thailand bans chemicals export for Myanmar rare earth mining, to take effect on Monday. The ban targets chemicals used in the mining process.

Key Points:

- Ban on exports of chemicals used in Myanmar rare earth mining

- Effective on Monday
