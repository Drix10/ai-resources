### 🚨 FDA orders warning on GLP-1 drugs for eye condition

FDA orders new warnings on Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound and other GLP-1 drugs because of a possible link to nonarteritic anterior ischemic optic neuropathy, a rare condition that can cause permanent vision loss. The agency reports 6.4 cases per 100,000 person-years in GLP-1 users versus 3.8 in placebo or other diabetes medication groups.

Key Points:

- FDA requires warning labels for Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound and Soliqua.

- FDA identified 6.4 NAION cases per 100,000 person-years among GLP-1 users, compared with 3.8 in placebo/other groups.
