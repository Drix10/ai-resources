### 🤖 Ukraine and Russia trade hundreds of drones overnight

Ukraine and Russia exchanged hundreds of drone attacks Tuesday night, with Russian strikes hitting power substations in northern Ukraine and a cargo ship in the Black Sea. Ukrainian drones hit a large logistics complex and a data center in Russia.

Key Points:

- Russian strikes hit Nalivaikovka power substation in the Kyiv region and Chizhovka substation in the Zhytomyr region.

- Ukrainian Air Force shot down 272 Russian drones by Wednesday morning.

### 🚨 MV Odyssey leaves Cambodia with no passengers after forced disembarkation

MV Odyssey, a U.S. cruise ship, departed Sihanoukville with only crew after forcing 203 passengers to disembark. The ship’s lender now controls it and it may be moved to Singapore.

Key Points:

- 203 passengers were told to leave the ship on Oct. 28.

- The vessel has a 650-passenger capacity and 156 crew members.
