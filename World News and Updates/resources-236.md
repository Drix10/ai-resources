### 🚨 DNA testing confirms 'serial killer' in Pennsylvania in 1970s

DNA testing confirmed a serial killer operated in Washington County, Pennsylvania, in the 1970s, linking two cold case murders. James Richard Clark Sr. (1957-2023) was identified as the suspect for the 1976 murder of Susan Rush and the 1977 murder of Brenda Ritter.

Key Points:

- James Richard Clark Sr., born 1957, died 2023 at age 66, identified as responsible for both murders.

- Susan Rush was found dead, sexually assaulted and strangled in her car trunk in 1976; Brenda Ritter, 18, was killed in 1977 and found on a hillside.
