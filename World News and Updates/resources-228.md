### 🚨 Man dies after tiger attack at Yorkshire Wildlife Park

A man entered the tiger enclosure at Yorkshire Wildlife Park near Doncaster and was attacked, dying at the scene. South Yorkshire Police received the call at 13:35 BST on 10 October 2026. The park was closed for the remainder of the weekend and the tiger was uninjured.

Key Points:

- **Time of police call**: 13:35 BST.

- **Park closure**: remains closed for the remainder of the weekend.

- **Tiger status**: uninjured and remained in the enclosure.

### 🚨 Former CIA officer pleads guilty in gold bars case

David Rush, a former CIA officer, pleaded guilty to a single count of wire fraud. He admitted defrauding the U.S. government of nearly $200 million for personal benefit. Authorities discovered roughly $40 million worth of gold bars in his Virginia home. He agreed to $195 million in restitution and faces sentencing on Jan 28.

Key Points:

- **Plea**: single count of wire fraud, maximum 20‑year sentence

- **Restitution**: $195 million agreed as part of plea

- **Gold bars**: roughly $40 million worth seized from his residence

- **Defrauded amount**: nearly $200 million in government payments
