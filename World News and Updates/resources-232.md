### 🚨 Florida moves >20 polling places after flood

Florida officials moved more than 20 polling places after extreme flooding with a >1-in-1,000-year recurrence interval. The hazardous weather event was rare in the affected areas.

Key Points:

- **Polling places moved**: more than 20

- **Recurrence interval**: more than 1-in-1,000 years in some areas

### 🤖 South African wine producers brace for tighter margins after duty-free benefits end

South African wine producers brace for tighter margins and tougher competition after Washington moved to end the country's duty-free trade benefits. The change affects their access to the US market. Producers anticipate reduced profitability.

Key Points:

- Washington moved to end the country's duty-free trade benefits.

- Producers expect tighter margins and tougher competition in the US market.
