### 🚨 LIV Golf shutdown pending bankruptcy as rescue fails

LIV Golf announced it will shut down operations as it files for bankruptcy in New Jersey Court. The tour has received more than $5 billion from Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund since 2022.

Key Points:

- Planned shutdown filed in New Jersey Bankruptcy Court.

- Saudi Public Investment Fund invested over $5 billion since 2022.

### 🚀 LIV Golf to liquidate after investor pulls funding

LIV Golf is expected to liquidate after a proposed investor pulled its funding this week. The league was previously described as embattled and once upended the sport.

Key Points:

- Expected liquidation of LIV Golf

- Investor withdrew funding this week
