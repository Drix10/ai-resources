### 🤖 Saguaro cactus deaths rise to 7% in Phoenix 2023

Saguaro cactus deaths rise to 7% in Phoenix 2023, up from the usual 1%-2% annual rate. The increase follows intense heat waves and is discussed in part three of “The Age of Heat Storms” series.

Key Points:

- **Normal annual death rate**: 1% to 2%.

- **2023 death rate after heat waves**: 7%.

### 🤖 Frequent short walks improve blood sugar regulation study

Frequent short walks throughout the day improved certain measures of blood sugar regulation more than the same amount of walking in one continuous session. The finding comes from a new study.

Key Points:

- Frequent short walks improved blood sugar regulation measures.

- Same total walking time in one continuous session was less effective.
