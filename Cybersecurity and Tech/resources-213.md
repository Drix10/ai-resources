### 🚨 Risk Management - Basic Principles

Risk management is a crucial aspect of decision-making, and understanding its basic principles is essential. Just because something could happen doesn't mean it will, and just because something is unlikely doesn't mean it won't. The art of risk management lies in working out what is more likely than not and taking appropriate action.

Key Points:

- **Risk Assessment**: Identify potential risks and assess their likelihood and impact.

- **Risk Mitigation**: Implement strategies to reduce or eliminate risks.

- **Risk Tolerance**: Determine the level of risk that is acceptable and make decisions accordingly.
