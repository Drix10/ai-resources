### 🤖 Emotet botnet taken down 2021 after 12 years operation

Emotet botnet was taken down in 2021 after running for about 12 years. It used stolen SMTP credentials to send backdoored Office documents and was disrupted by a coalition of eight countries that pushed a self-remove update.

Key Points:

- **Duration**: botnet ran for about 12 years before takedown in 2021.

- **Coalition action**: eight countries hacked the command-and-control infrastructure and used the malware’s self-update command to push code that removed the malware.
