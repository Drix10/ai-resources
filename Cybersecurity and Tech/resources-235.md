### 🤖 PHP2Shell – conflicting exploitation claims and Sophos corrections

PHP2Shell exploitation claims were issued and later retracted, with Sophos updating its telemetry to show only scanning and failed attempts and no evidence of a successful compromise. Other vendors echoed that public exploits do not work against default PHP installations and that only probing activity has been observed.

Key Points:

- **Sophos 16**: 18 UTC Oct 22 reported “one successful exploitation,” then 21:21 UTC Oct 22 corrected to “no evidence of a successful compromise.”

- Sansec 22 Oct stated “the public exploits do not work against a default PHP installation. You do not need to take your store offline.”
