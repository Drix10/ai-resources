### 🤖 Google SLEEP framework eliminates catastrophic forgetting

Google introduced the SLEEP framework that rewrites its own weights during a sleep phase to avoid catastrophic forgetting. The method adds new skills without retraining the entire model from scratch.

Key Points:

- **SLEEP framework**: AI processes new information in a wake phase then goes offline for a sleep phase.

- During sleep, the AI replays its own memories, consolidates data, and mathematically rewrites its neural weights.
