### 📊 Nasdaq down 5.5% in a day, chip index down 12% weekly

Nasdaq fell 5.52% on Nov 5, its worst day since April 2025, and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped 12.15% for the week. The decline was driven by policy concerns over AI rules and data-center permits, not earnings. Nvidia fell about 9% and data-center and GPU-cloud names fell more than 14%.

Key Points:

- Nasdaq closed down 5.52% on Nov 5, its worst day since April 2025.

- Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 12.15% for the week.
