### 🤖 ivonescimab ± chemo in resectable NSCLC trial results

Key Points:

- Cohort 2 (ivonescimab + chemo) MPR 70.1% (47/67), pCR 50.7% (34/67), RVT≤5% 97.9% (46/47)

- Grade ≥3 TRAEs 30.4%; no grade ≥3 hemorrhage or treatment-related death
