### 🚀 OmneityLabs launches ASAT v3 speech synthesis model

ASAT v3 is the latest model from OmneityLabs for Moroccan speech synthesis. It supports Arabic, Amazigh, English, French and other languages and scripts. The model is available to try live and via an API. It also powers SawalniBot as of today.

Key Points:

- **Launch**: ASAT v3 announced as OmneityLabs’ best model yet for Moroccan speech synthesis.

- **Integration**: ASAT v3 is powering SawalniBot starting today.
