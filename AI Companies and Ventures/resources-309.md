### 🤖 Colgate Semantic Similarity Rating hits 90% reliability

Colgate Semantic Similarity Rating predicts purchase intent with 90% human test-retest reliability across 57 surveys and 9,300 responses. The method asks LLMs to roleplay customers, gives them a demographic profile and product concept, and translates their written thoughts into a numerical score with a semantic model.

Key Points:

- Semantic Similarity Rating (SSR) asks LLMs to roleplay customers and write raw, unfiltered thoughts.

- Synthetic consumers achieved 90% of human test-retest reliability on 57 personal care product surveys and 9,300 human responses.
