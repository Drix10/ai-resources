### 🤖 Wake-sleep algorithms introduced by Hinton et al. in 1995

Wake-sleep algorithms were introduced by Hinton et al. in 1995. They train neural networks by alternating a wake phase that learns from real data and a sleep phase that learns from generated examples. The post references this method.

Key Points:

- Introduced by Hinton et al. in 1995

- Uses alternating wake and sleep phases for training neural networks
