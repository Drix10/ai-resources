### 🤖 SigVie-002 phase III trial shows no OS benefit

SigVie-002 phase III trial shows no OS benefit, OS 11.9 vs 11.5 months (HR 0.93, p=0.226). No significant PFS improvement and exploratory analysis showed no clear efficacy trend by integrin β6 expression.

Key Points:

- **OS**: 11.9 vs 11.5 months (HR 0.93; p=0.226)

- **PFS**: 4.7 vs 4.2 months (HR 0.84); no clear efficacy trend by IB6 expression
