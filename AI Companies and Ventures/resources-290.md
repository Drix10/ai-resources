### 🤖 joey-1 released – third open-source model, 2B parameters

joey-1 is a new open-source decision model with 2B parameters, described as the fastest of its size. It is offered through Model APIs, dedicated deployment, and a fine-tuning cookbook. The model was trained by the @baselabs team.

Key Points:

- **Release**: third open-source decision model, named joey-1 (2B)

- **Availability**: Model APIs, dedicated deployment, and fine-tuning cookbook for Training Jobs
