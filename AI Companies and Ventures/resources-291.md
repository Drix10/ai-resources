### 🤖 Agentic Robotics – agents assemble robot programs, test in simulation, and iterate

Agentic Robotics uses coding agents to build robot programs from reusable skills, rehearse them in simulation, and modify failing modules. A frontier model built a simulation from a short video of ink wiping, then agents improved the program. Ambi Robotics used agents to generate three fixes in ten hours, testing them for ten days; one fix added 4.2 packages per hour and was rolled out to 30% of its U.S. fleet.

Key Points:

- Agents assemble a program as a Graph-as-Policy of perception, grasping, and motion planning modules.

- Ambi Robotics’ agent-generated fix increased throughput by 4.2 packages per hour and was deployed to 30% of its U.S. fleet.
