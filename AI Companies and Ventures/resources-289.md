### 🤖 Bedrock raises $600M Series C for autonomous excavators

Bedrock announced a $600M Series C to add fully autonomous excavators to more U.S. job sites. The company retrofits heavy-duty excavators with sensors and compute in a day and previously raised $270M Series B, valuing it at $4.1B.

Key Points:

- **Series C Funding**: $600M Series C co-led by CapitalG and Emergence Capital.

- **Retrofit speed**: adds sensors and compute to existing excavators in a day.

- **Series B and valuation**: $270M Series B in February; now valued at $4.1B with nearly $1B raised total.

- **Customer growth**: customers more than doubled since 2025; fully driverless excavators with Sundt, Zachry and others.

### 🤖 Call for Help: Teaching Robots to Request Assistance – robots learn to request assistance

Call for Help: Teaching Robots to Request Assistance shows Walden robots now learn to request remote assistance when needed, reducing failures by over an order of magnitude. The approach integrates hardware, software, AI and operations to let robots decide when help is required as part of a multimodal policy.

Key Points:

- More than an order of magnitude reduction in failures, enabling hours of continuous operation without fault.

- Multimodal policy includes a call-for-help decision head that uses internal state to determine when assistance is needed.
