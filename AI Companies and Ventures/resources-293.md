### 🤖 Starcloud data centers cost $500 per kilo to orbit

Starcloud claims its space data center cost is $500 per kilogram to orbit. It contrasts terrestrial solar costs—land, batteries, cells—with space advantages: no land, no batteries, 24/7 sun, and 8× energy per panel. The only new cost is launch.

Key Points:

- **Cost**: $500 per kilo to orbit for Starcloud data centers.

- **Advantage**: No land, no batteries, continuous sun, and 8× energy per panel in orbit.

### 🤖 Ivanti Connect Secure zero-day chain exploits auth bypass

Ivanti Connect Secure zero-day chain exploits auth bypass to RCE to root and is observed in the wild by Volexity's UTA0576. CISA added the root privilege escalation to the KEV list on 30 Oct, with a federal due date of 2 Nov. Capture images before patching; the Integrity Checker can miss in-memory activity. CVE-2026-104935 has a CVSS score of 8.6 and CVE-2026-111357 has a CVSS score of 7.0.

Key Points:

- Zero-day chain (auth bypass to RCE to root) exploited by Volexity's UTA0576
