### 🤖 Medallion XLN for creators launch with free and paid plans

Medallion XLN for creators launches with free tier and paid plans starting at $20 per month. The service lets creators showcase work, set goals, and have an AI agent find matching opportunities. Free accounts get 100 agent runs; paid plans run the agent continuously and send draft messages for approval.

Key Points:

- Free plan includes 100 agent runs and basic profile features.

- Pro plan costs $20 per month (or $200 per year) and runs the agent in the background.
