### AI Personal Assistants: Asymmetric Failure Modes

AI personal assistants, especially those operating over text, have asymmetric failure modes. They can work 80% of the time but often fail in the remaining 20%, destroying more trust than the 80% builds.

Key Points:
• AI personal assistants have asymmetric failure modes, where they can work 80% of the time but fail in the remaining 20%.
• These failures can destroy more trust than the 80% of successful interactions builds.
• This issue is becoming increasingly common in AI-powered personal assistants.
