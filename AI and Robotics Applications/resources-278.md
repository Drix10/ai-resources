### 🤖 PanoTac dense three-axis tactile sensor demo tube insertion and stopper wiggle

PanoTac is a dense three-axis tactile sensor used in a demo where a robot reorients and inserts a tube, then wiggles the stopper free. The right-hand view shows tactile signals that let the robot detect when the stopper releases and when to change from wiggling to lifting.

Key Points:

- **Demo**: robot reorients and inserts a tube, then wiggles the stopper free while the tube sits loosely in the rack.

- Right side shows tactile signals that help detect stopper release and when to switch from wiggling to lifting.
