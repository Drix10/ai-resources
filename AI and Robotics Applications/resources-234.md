### 🚀 Robotics - SmoothRL for Online RL during Async Inference

SmoothRL is a new approach to online reinforcement learning (RL) that allows robots to continue moving while the model thinks. This is particularly important in high-speed throws, where even a single pause can kill the momentum. Astribot has released SmoothRL for online RL during async inference, which keeps the robot moving as the model computes the next action chunk.

Key Points:
• SmoothRL allows robots to continue moving while the model thinks.
• This approach is particularly important in high-speed throws.
• Astribot has released SmoothRL for online RL during async inference.
