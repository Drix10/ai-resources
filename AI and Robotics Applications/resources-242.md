### 🤖 AI & Robotics - Embodied AI Debate

Embodied AI, a subfield of artificial intelligence, focuses on developing intelligent systems that can interact with and understand the physical world. The recent success of GPT-6 Astra has sparked a debate: if a general-purpose model can see, reason, and pilot a robot arm, does embodied AI still need its own foundation model? We'll explore this question and discuss the implications of embodied AI.

Key Points:


- **


- **Embodied AI Foundation Models**: Embodied AI foundation models are designed to understand and interact with the physical world. They are typically trained on large datasets of sensorimotor experiences and can learn to perform complex tasks such as manipulation and locomotion.

- **General-Purpose Models vs. Embodied AI**: General-purpose models like GPT-6 Astra can perform a wide range of tasks, including reasoning and problem-solving. However, they may not have the same level of understanding of the physical world as embodied AI models.

- **Robot Data and Training**: GPT-6 Astra was trained on robot data, which allows it to perform tasks that require embodied intelligence. However, this raises the question of whether embodied AI still needs its own foundation model.


**Actionable Takeaway:**
