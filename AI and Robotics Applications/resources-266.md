### 🤖 Infant simulator with embodied caregiver added to MIMo

The authors extend the Multi-Modal Infant Model (MIMo) in MuJoCo with a controllable caregiver. The system can replay naturalistic holding and soothing interactions and log infant‑perspective touch and vision. They demonstrate origin‑aware contact logging that separates self, caregiver, and environment contacts and produces touch‑rate statistics.

Key Points:

- **Extension**: adds an articulated caregiver model compatible with MIMo morphologies, parameterized from anthropometrics and optionally resized to a recorded caregiver.

- **Workflow**: enables replay of caregiver‑infant holding and soothing interactions and logs first‑person multimodal data (touch and vision).
