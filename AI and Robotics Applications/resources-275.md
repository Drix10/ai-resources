### 🤖 Unitree G1 humanoid state estimation trained on 1h data

Unitree G1 humanoid uses IMU and joint states, trained on just 1 h of unlabeled data, achieving trajectories comparable to MAC-VO and SuperOdom. It matches visual and LiDAR odometry without a camera or LiDAR.

Key Points:

- **Training data**: 1 h of unlabeled data.

- **Performance**: trajectories comparable to MAC-VO (visual) and SuperOdom (LiDAR) with no camera or LiDAR.

### 🤖 One Scan is Enough: 85% success for language navigation

One Scan is Enough demonstrates a demonstration-free adaptation method that reaches an 85% real-robot success rate in language-guided navigation. The approach uses a single iPhone scan, Scan2Instruction to generate training episodes, and PRPT-KS for closed-form policy adaptation.

Key Points:

- 85% real-robot success across five real environments, versus 49% for a baseline trained on human demonstrations.

- 82% success in unscanned regions of the same buildings, versus 41% for the same baseline.
