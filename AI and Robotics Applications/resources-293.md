### 🤖 Prima 1 and Flex 2 teleoperated at Web Summit 2026

Prima 1 and Flex 2 were teleoperated with ManusMeta data gloves at Web Summit 2026, demonstrating live robotic hand control. Flex 2 has 23 degrees of freedom with hybrid tendon/direct-drive actuation. Prima 1 has 22 degrees of freedom with a fully direct-drive design and a 20 N fingertip force.

Key Points:

- **Flex 2**: 23 DOF, hybrid tendon/direct-drive actuation.

- **Prima 1**: 22 DOF, fully direct-drive, 20 N fingertip force.

### 🤖 Six Easy Tasks benchmark humans 100% models 0% claim

Six Easy Tasks (SET) is presented as a real-world robotics benchmark where humans score 100% and frontier AI models score 0%. The post calls the examples thoughtful and minimalist tasks that cannot be solved by scripted text actions.

Key Points:

- **Benchmark result**: humans 100% success, frontier AI models 0% success.

- **Description**: tasks are minimalist examples that cannot be solved by emitting discrete blocks of scripted actions in text format.
