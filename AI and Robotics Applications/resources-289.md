Key Points:

- Cup task duration reduced from 11.1 minutes to 3.4 minutes.

- Towel-folding median duration reduced from about 18 minutes to about 6 minutes.

- Robot active time increased to 84% of the run, previously 24%.

- Latency now limited to ~5 seconds, with most model time hidden behind robot motion.
