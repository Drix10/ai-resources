### 🤖 ReRead: Correcting Frozen Robot Policies by Re-reading

ReRead adds a corrector that reads cached backbone representations to predict action residuals, improving success on hard RoboTwin tasks. The method freezes the backbone, uses no images, and is trained on offline demonstrations from all 50 RoboTwin 2.0 tasks.

Key Points:

- Corrector adds only 4.17% latency and sees no images, training solely on offline demonstrations from all 50 RoboTwin 2.0 tasks.

- On the 11 hardest RoboTwin 2.0 tasks, ReRead raises success rates of LingBot-VLA by 23.45 pp, π₀.₅ by 13.09 pp, and Fast-WAM by 8.00 pp.

🔗 Resources:

- [Original post](https://x.com/OWW/status/2123467450809225645)
