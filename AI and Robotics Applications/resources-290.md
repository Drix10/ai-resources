### 🤖 Heave-Ho decentralized multi-humanoid collaboration scales to 10 robots

Heave-Ho is a decentralized framework for long-horizon collaborative multi-humanoid loco-manipulation that trains with two robots and runs on up to ten physical robots and ten simulated robots without retraining. The system uses speech-based leader-follower coordination and achieves 94.41% episode success on the HH-Bench suite.

Key Points:

- Trained with two identical humanoids, the policy deploys to four physical robots and ten simulated robots without retraining.

- Planner decision time is 52.5 ms on a single RTX 4090, about 120 × faster than GPT-6 (6.46 s).

### 🤖 Opt2VLA force-aware vision-language-action system

Opt2VLA is a Georgia Tech system that infers contact force from joint torques and outputs waypoints and force references for humanoid whole-body manipulation. It fine-tunes a GR00T-N1.7 model to emit egocentric RGB, proprioception, joint-torque history, 3D hand/foot waypoints, and continuous normal-contact-force references. Training data comes from trajectory optimization with modified Crocoddyl and Pinocchio using DDP.

Key Points:

- GR00T-N1.7 vision-language-action model fine-tuned to emit RGB, proprioception, joint-torque history, 3D waypoints and normal-contact-force references.

- Whole-body trajectory optimization built on modified Crocoddyl and Pinocchio with DDP provides motion and joint-torque references under prescribed contact wrenches.
