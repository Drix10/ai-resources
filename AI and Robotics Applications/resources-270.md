### 🤖 Phantom of the ADAS paper shows stop sign phantom and GhostBusters fix

Phantom of the ADAS (Nassi et al., ACM CCS 2020) made a driver-assist car stop for a billboard stop sign shown for 125 ms. GhostBusters uses four small networks and a fifth decision network, catching 99.4% of phantoms with zero false alarms on real signs.

Key Points:

- Phantom of the ADAS caused a stop for a billboard stop sign displayed for 125 ms.

- GhostBusters caught 99.4% of phantoms with zero false alarms on real signs.

### 🤖 pplx-decider-v1.2 and pplx-decider-flash-v1.2 release

pplx-decider-v1.2 and pplx-decider-flash-v1.2 are new open-weights multimodal decision models with improved text and vision scores. Inference is up to 5x faster, running all questions in a request in one pass. Flash model ranks in the top 4B on the Decision Index.

Key Points:

- **Model sizes**: pplx-decider-v1.2 and pplx-decider-flash-v1.2

- **Inference speed**: up to 5× faster, all questions in one pass

- **Flash ranking**: top 4B on the Decision Index

### 🤖 Adaptive SDR Networking: Tactical Communications in Contested Environments

Creomagic announced cognitive software-defined radio technology that performs real-time RF spectrum analysis and adapts networking parameters. The solution also includes a dual-waveform architecture that runs a high-throughput broadband layer alongside a resilient low-signature command-and-control layer.

Key Points:

- Cognitive SDR continuously monitors the electromagnetic environment and autonomously adjusts frequency, modulation and waveform behavior.

- Dual-waveform architecture runs two synchronized layers: a primary broadband data layer and a secondary low-signature, highly resilient command-and-control layer.
