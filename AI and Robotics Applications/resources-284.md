### 📊 A Sequential Framework for Urban Drone Delivery: results on Seoul hubs

A Sequential Framework for Urban Drone Delivery presents a step-by-step method linking hub location, routing, vehicle sizing, and cost assessment, applied to district-level Seoul data. Adding hubs reduces required range from 30.6 km to 21.9 km and vehicle takeoff mass from 8.12 kg to 6.12 kg.

Key Points:

- Range requirement drops from 30.6 km (one hub) to 21.9 km (four hubs).

- Monthly flight energy consumption falls 13.1% from 15.2 MWh to 13.2 MWh despite a 21.5% increase in total flight distance.

🔗 Resources:

- [Original post](https://x.com/OWW/status/2120070929950671226)
