### 🤖 6‑layer AI‑designed drone flight controller PCB prototyped

A 6‑layer drone flight controller PCB board was designed by AI and has been prototyped. Power‑on testing showed no issues, and the firmware flashing test passed. The board powers up and can connect to the Betaflight platform for debugging.

### 🤖 Partial cellular reprogramming reverses skin cell age by up to 30 years

Partial cellular reprogramming is the technique used. Researchers reported reversing the biological age of human skin cells by up to 30 years. The method changed cells aged 53 years to appear 23 years old.

Key Points:

- **Age reduction**: up to 30 years reversal of biological age.

- **Age conversion**: 53‑year‑old cells became 23‑year‑old cells.
