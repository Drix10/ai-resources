### 🤖 Knowledge Distillation

How can a small model learn from a much larger one? Knowledge distillation trains the student on the teacher's probabilities, not just the top answer. Soft targets reveal similarities. A 7B model can absorb patterns from a 70B teacher.

Key Points:
• Knowledge distillation trains the student on the teacher's probabilities.
• Soft targets reveal similarities between the teacher and student models.
• A smaller model can learn from a larger model through knowledge distillation.
