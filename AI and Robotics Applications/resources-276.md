### 🤖 Meta Max prototype built in three months

Meta partnered with a Chinese robotics company and, with Fourier, completed a prototype called Max in about three months. Meta’s robotics vice president stayed at a Shanghai factory for around two months.

Key Points:

- **Partnership**: U.S.-based Meta partnered with a Chinese robotics company.

- **Prototype timeline**: Max was completed in about three months.

- **Executive stay**: Meta’s robotics vice president stayed at a Shanghai factory for around two months.

### 🤖 Zeen creative social app launch for folding-physics zines

Zeen is a creative social app that lets users build and share zines using real folding physics and a mobile-friendly editor. It is positioned as an alternative to digital zines that lack hands-on experience.

Key Points:

- **Launch**: Zeen app introduced for creating and sharing zines with folding physics.

- **Feature**: Mobile-friendly editor enables building zines from the ground up.

### 🤖 Shenzhen districts for hardware development locations

Shenzhen districts are mapped for hardware work. Nanshan hosts showrooms and design teams. Huaqiangbei offers same-day component pickup.

Key Points:

- **Nanshan**: showrooms and design teams where ideas are shown and refined.

- **Huaqiangbei**: almost every part you need, allowing same-day component grabs.
