### 🤖 A Face Robot which Imitates Human Musculoskeletal Skin System – 65 muscles, 47 AUs

A Face Robot which Imitates Human Musculoskeletal Skin System presents a facial-robot platform with 65 anatomically inspired muscles targeting 47 Facial Action Coding System units. The system reproduced 40 AUs with muscle actuation alone and 30 AUs after silicone skin attachment.

Key Points:

- **Muscles**: 65 individual muscles (34 types) implemented to target 47 AUs.

- **Evaluation**: 40 of 47 target AUs reproduced without skin; 30 of 47 reproduced with skin attached.

### 📊 RoboTrack benchmark, fine-tuned tracker 66.0 AJ

RoboTrack adds a real-world benchmark and simulated training data for point tracking in robotics. Fine-tuning CoWTracker raises its Average Jaccard to 66.0 and improves pipetting success to 17 of 20 layouts.

Key Points:

- RoboTrack-Bench provides 307 real clips with 2,522 human-verified tracks.

- Fine-tuning lifts CoWTracker from 63.9 to 66.0 AJ and succeeds in 17 of 20 pipetting layouts, versus 8 for masks and 5 for optical flow.
