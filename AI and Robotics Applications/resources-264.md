### 🤖 Workhorse learns whole-body humanoid loco-manipulation from human demos

Workhorse is a system that learns contact-rich whole-body manipulation for a Unitree G1 robot from robot‑free human demonstrations. A visual planner predicts torso, wrist, and foot poses; a reinforcement‑learning tracker follows these targets on the robot. On the real G1 the robot sorts boxes, catches a thrown box, and climbs a suitcase, achieving 77% success on box sorting and 64% under 40 N·s pushes.

Key Points:

- Visual planner predicts five-link targets: torso, both wrists, and both feet.

- Success rate on box sorting without pushes: G1 77%, H2 83%; with 40 N·s pushes G1 64%.
