### 🤖 ContactExplorer – tool to guide pre-touch contacts

ContactExplorer turns contact history into guidance for where to reach before touch and which contacts to explore next. It addresses the stall in exploration before the hand contacts the object. The post introduces the method in a four-part series.

Key Points:

- **Guidance**: uses contact history to indicate where to reach before touch.

- **Exploration**: selects which contacts to explore next.
