### 📊 Simple Memory Evolution boosts GPT-6-Astra SR to 81.7%

Simple Memory Evolution for Embodied Agents raises GPT-6-Astra success rate from 46.7% to 81.7% on EBench. It also tops the RoboDojo leaderboard with 69.63% success rate and a score of 75.32.

Key Points:

- **Success rate increase**: 46.7% → 81.7% on EBench.

- **RoboDojo leaderboard**: 69.63% SR, 75.32 score.

### 🤖 CoMind Be My Eye improves pen following and blind box metrics

CoMind Be My Eye from Astribot reports pen following improvement from 0% to 100% and blind box accuracy from 38.9% to 88.9%. It also sends 80% less information.

Key Points:

- **Pen following**: 0% → 100%

- **Blind box**: 38.9% → 88.9%

- Info sent reduced by 80%
