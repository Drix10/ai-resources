### 🤖 Agile Robots raises $500M at $8.5B valuation

Agile Robots announced a $500 million funding round valuing the company at $8.5 billion. Mass production of the full-size Agile ONE is planned for 2027 and the smaller Agile ONE SN is expected in early 2027. A mobile humanoid variant is piloting at an Audi factory and a Physical AI partnership with Hitachi was announced.

Key Points:

- **Funding**: $500 million raised, valuation $8.5 billion.

- **Production**: Full-size Agile ONE mass production planned for 2027; Agile ONE SN slated for early 2027.

- **Piloting**: Mobile humanoid variant testing at an Audi factory.

- **Partnership**: Physical AI collaboration with Hitachi.

### 📊 Scenario Orchestration for Closed-loop Evaluation released

Scenario Orchestration for Closed-loop Evaluation introduces a system that adapts non-ego actors via re-timing, re-routing and role reassignment. It solves a constrained optimization each step to preserve intended interaction while limiting interventions. Experiments in CARLA show higher reliability than scripted and learned actor baselines.

Key Points:

- Orchestrator continuously monitors joint traffic state and adapts non-ego actors through re-timing, re-routing, and role reassignment.

- CARLA experiments demonstrate more reliable realization of targeted interactions compared to scripted and learned actor-level baselines.
