### 🤖 MOSS 3D printed litter picking robot tested on beach

MOSS the 3D printed open source litter picking robot was run on a beach, handling dry, damp sand and waterline. Battery measured 12.1 V at switch-on and 11.8 V at rest. Encoders show both motors turned the same within 1% and drift is between track and ground.

Key Points:

- **Battery**: 12.1 V at switch-on, 11.8 V at rest.

- **Motor sync**: encoders agree both motors turned the same within 1%.

🔗 Resources:

- [Original post](https://x.com/metrox_eth/status/2114596845951369727)

### 🤖 FBA deformable sim solver dropping soon announcement

FBA is described as the most robotics-friendly deformable simulation solver and is slated to be released soon. The author’s earlier paper used a “baby” version of FBA to perform sim-to-real cloth folding in minutes to hours.

Key Points:

- FBA is the most robotics-friendly deformable simulation solver.

- The “baby” version of FBA enabled sim-to-real cloth folding within minutes/hours in prior work (arXiv:2604.17513).
