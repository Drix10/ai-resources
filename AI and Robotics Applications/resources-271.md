### 🤖 PACT robot hand co-optimizes 17-servo hand – 3.9 mm RMSE, 86% grasp success

PACT co-optimizes a 17-servo robot hand and its posture retargeter using human grasping poses, achieving 3.9 mm pose-tracking RMSE. The fabricated hand reaches an 86% grasp success rate on YCB objects and 75% success in additional tests.

Key Points:

- **Pose-tracking error**: 3.9 mm RMSE.

- **Grasp success rate**: 86% on YCB objects.
