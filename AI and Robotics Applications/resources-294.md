### 📊 Does Research-Paper Context Improve LLM-Generated Quadruped Navigation? Study shows result

Does Research-Paper Context Improve LLM-Generated Quadruped Navigation? An exploratory simulation study reports that 2 of 8 controllers written with incremental-replanning paper context complete all four trips, versus 7 of 8 without it (p = 0.041). The test used Claude Opus 5, a frozen locomotion policy, and Isaac Sim physics. Results differ from an idealized kinematic simulator where the two groups are indistinguishable.

Key Points:

- 2 of 8 context-provided controllers complete all four trips under Isaac Sim physics.

- 7 of 8 controllers without paper context complete all four trips; Fisher’s exact test p = 0.041.
