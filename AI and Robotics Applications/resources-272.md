### 🤖 SuperMimic scales humanoid control, 1 GPU → 3 h training

SuperMimic trains a humanoid whole-body motion tracker with one differentiable world model and reaches higher normalized return than SONIC using a single NVIDIA H100 GPU in three hours. It reduces the required GPU-hours by more than a thousand-fold compared with the 128-GPU, seven-day SONIC baseline.

Key Points:

- 129,514 motions trained; SuperMimic surpasses SONIC’s return with 1 GPU in 3 h.

- SONIC fails 179 motions; SuperMimic fails none after 9.8 h on 2 GPUs.

### 🤖 High-Precision RCM Motion for Ophthalmic Robotics achieves 0.142 mm residual

High-Precision RCM Motion for Ophthalmic Robotics achieves software-RCM residual below 0.142 mm and median tip error 32 µm. The paper presents a hierarchical-QP controller with authority-guided recovery planning and reports benchmark and physical arm results.

Key Points:

- **Physical test**: worst-case kinematically reconstructed RCM residual 178 µm across four entry points.
