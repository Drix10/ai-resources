### 🤖 FLoRe robot-free imitation pipeline achieves 80% success on force tasks

FLoRe is a robot-free imitation pipeline running on a Unitree G1 that learns force commands from handheld demos. It reaches 80% success on three tasks, compared to 26.7% for a position-only policy.

Key Points:

- Diffusion policy runs at 30 Hz, outputs keypoints and force command per hand.

- RL whole-body controller runs at 50 Hz on the G1’s Jetson Orin NX and integrates measured force error.
