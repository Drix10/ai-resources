### 🤖 Humanoid Robots in Entertainment: $130M revenue 2025

Humanoid robots generated over $130 million in entertainment sales in 2025, representing 26 % of global humanoid robot revenue. Rental rates in China fell from ~20,000 yuan to 5,000 yuan per day between early 2025 and early 2026. The market is shifting toward short-term rental services rather than ownership.

Key Points:

- Global humanoid robot sales revenue exceeded $500 million in 2025; entertainment accounted for $130 million (26 %).

- Unitree G1 rental price dropped from ~20,000 yuan/day in early 2025 to 5,000 yuan/day by early 2026.

### 🤖 Sim-to-Real RL for ASVs using SysID – zero-shot transfer demo

Sim-to-Real RL for ASVs using SysID presents an ASV simulator and training pipeline that start from unknown vehicle dynamics. It uses only a CAD model and a brief set of open-water field trajectories to approximate and refine hydrodynamic and thruster parameters, achieving zero-shot sim-to-real transfer on a BlueBoat ASV for path following and station-keeping.

Key Points:

- Simulator and pipeline train policies from unknown dynamics using only a CAD model and brief field trajectories.

- Real-world deployments on a BlueBoat ASV show zero-shot transfer for path following and station-keeping without prior hydrodynamic or propeller information.
