### 📊 Buffon's needle probability 2ℓ/(πd) formula announced

Buffon's needle probability 2ℓ/(πd) gives the chance a dropped needle crosses a line. The result was posed by the Comte de Buffon in 1777 and allows π to be estimated by chance.

Key Points:

- Probability that a needle crosses a line = 2ℓ/(πd)

- Proposed by the Comte de Buffon in 1777, enables π estimation by random drops

### 📊 Basel Problem sum equals π²/6 solved by Euler 1734

Basel Problem sum of reciprocals of squares converges to π²/6 (≈ 1.645). It was posed in 1644 and solved by Leonhard Euler in 1734.

Key Points:
