### 📊 A New Map of ‘Empty’ Space Challenges a Tranquil Picture – 4000 ly dust map

A New Map of ‘Empty’ Space Challenges a Tranquil Picture of the Galaxy reports a 3-D dust density map extending to 4,000 light-years from the Sun. This year scientists added temperature measurements and found a large portion of the dust to be unstable.

Key Points:

- 3-D map shows dust density out to 4,000 light-years from the Sun.

- Temperature data reveal most of that dust is in an unstable state.
