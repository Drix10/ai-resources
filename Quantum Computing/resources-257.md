Key Points:

### 🤖 A New Quantum Algorithm Framework for Integer Order Entropy Estimation

A New Quantum Algorithm Framework for Integer Order Entropy Estimation introduces a quantum algorithm that estimates Rényi entropy of order k ≥ 2 with additive error ε. It uses \(\widetilde{O}\!\left(\frac{n^{u_k}}{\epsilon^{1-u_k}} + \frac{n^{(k-1)/(2k)}}{\epsilon}\right)\) purified-query accesses, where \(u_k=\max_{2\le j\le k}\frac{(3k-j)2^{j-2}-k}{k(2^j-1)}\).

Key Points:

- **Query complexity**: \(\widetilde{O}\!\left(\frac{n^{u_k}}{\epsilon^{1-u_k}} + \frac{n^{(k-1)/(2k)}}{\epsilon}\right)\) queries.

- **Core technique**: recursive construction of collision states with retained intermediate states and a batch estimator for high-precision regime.

### 🤖 Flat bands and bound states in the continuum in non-Hermitian pseudospin lattices

Flat bands and bound states in the continuum in non-Hermitian pseudospin lattices demonstrates that increasing non-Hermiticity compresses dispersive bands toward an intrinsic zero-energy flat band, creating spectral overlap and turning compact localized states into BICs. Adding a momentum-dependent Hermitian term removes the overlap while preserving the CLSs and the mechanism extends to integer-pseudospin-n systems.

Key Points:

- Intrinsic zero-energy flat band and compact localized states exist for integer pseudospin.

- Non-Hermiticity compresses dispersive bands, enabling overlap with the flat band within a finite parameter range.
