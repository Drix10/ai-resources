### 🤖 IonQ Sensing delivers quantum-assured PNT with 30-day optical clock holdover

IonQ’s Sensing division offers physics-based PNT that keeps GPS-level timing for 30 days without external sync, using optical clocks and atomic gravimeters. Demonstrations include a 21-day at-sea test in 2022 with <300 ps drift per day and a 51-day trial in 2026 aboard the TS Patriot State. The solution is passive, RF-silent, and spoof-proof.

Key Points:

- **Holdover Through Prolonged Outages**: Tactical optical clocks maintain 10 ns timing for 30 continuous days without external sync.

- **21-Day (2022) at Sea (RIMPAC)**: Three autonomous optical clocks accrued <300 ps drift per day, the highest-performing at-sea clock demonstration to date.

### 🤖 Levent Alpöge Riemann proof compiled by multiple teams, 17M lines Lean code

Levent Alpöge’s Lean formalization of a generalized Riemann hypothesis proof, containing about 17 million lines of code, has been successfully compiled by independent efforts. Andrew Sutherland at MIT and Simon DeDeo at Carnegie Mellon each compiled the code, and Sutherland also compiled it with a Lean variant.

Key Points:

- **Compilation**: Andrew Sutherland (MIT) compiled the proof shortly before noon.

- **Compilation**: Simon DeDeo (Carnegie Mellon) also compiled the proof a few hours later.

- **Code size**: The Lean code base is about 17 million lines.

- **Write-up**: Alpöge’s accompanying paper is four pages long.
