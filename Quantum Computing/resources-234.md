### 🤖 Lindblad master equation for deep strong coupling Rabi model

A Lindblad-form quantum master equation is derived for the open quantum Rabi model in the perturbative deep strong coupling regime. The derivation combines secular and singular‑coupling approximations in a transformed frame. The equation is benchmarked against an alternative master equation, showing excellent agreement in time evolution and steady‑state observables. Analytical results are provided for limiting cases.

Key Points:

- Derivation uses secular and singular‑coupling approximations.

- Benchmark shows excellent agreement with an alternative master equation.

### 📊 Quantum Leakage from Shamir Sharing with Correlated Resources paper released

The paper studies leakage from Shamir secret sharing when each device produces a qubit from an initial quantum resource independent of the secret. It proves exponentially small trace distance between leakage states of any two secrets under specified conditions. It gives optimal exponential bases for reconstruction requiring all shares: 2√2/π for a qubit and 2/π for a classical bit.

Key Points:

- Exponential trace distance bound holds for fixed stationary chains with an upper operator mixing condition.

- Optimal exponential base is 2√2/π for a qubit and 2/π for a classical bit when reconstruction uses all shares.
