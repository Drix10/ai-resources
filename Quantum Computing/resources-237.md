### 🤖 Abelian post-quantum signatures live today with hash and lattice details

Abelian is one of five platforms where post-quantum signatures guard user funds by default, live today. The post compares hash-based signatures (32-byte keys, 7,856-byte signatures) with lattice-based signatures (NIST standard, usable today).

Key Points:

- Abelian is one of five where post-quantum signatures guard user funds by default, live today.

- Hash signatures use 32-byte keys and 7,856-byte signatures.
