### 📊 Superradiant laser achieves Θ(μ^{8/3}) coherence scaling

Superradiant laser reaches coherence scaling Θ(μ^{8/3}) in the optimal parameter regime, surpassing the standard quantum limit. The work introduces generalized far-above-threshold laser models with a collective lowering operator. It shows that in the bad-cavity limit μ≈N/2, where N is the atom count.

- The proposed model uses the collective superradiant lowering operator \(\hat{J}\).

- Coherence can scale as Θ(μ^{8/3}) in the optimal regime.

- In the bad-cavity limit the mean excitation number μ approximates N/2.

### 📊 Efficiently tracking errors and outcomes in universal quantum circuits

Efficiently tracking errors and outcomes in universal quantum circuits presents a method for efficient simulation of error propagation and decoding in universal fault-tolerant quantum circuits under stochastic Pauli noise. The technique uses a modified Pauli frame that contains no logical information and verifies decoding success by checking that the frame remains logically trivial.

Key Points:
