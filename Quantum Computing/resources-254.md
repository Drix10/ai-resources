### 🤖 Samsung NanoQuant sub-1-bit compression for 70B LLM

Samsung's NanoQuant compresses a 70B LLM to less than one bit per weight, shrinking it from 16-bit to sub-1-bit. It claims to slash VRAM requirements by over an order of magnitude.

Key Points:

- NanoQuant compresses the model's weight matrices into ultra-compact, structured binary representations.

- VRAM requirements are reduced by over an order of magnitude.
