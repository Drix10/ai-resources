It is well known from the theory of relativity that the speed of an object cannot exceed the speed of light *c*. This limitation applies to the x-component, y-component, and z-component of velocity, and the magnitude of the velocity itself also cannot exceed *c*. So, what about acceleration?

Key Points:
• The speed of an object cannot exceed the speed of light.
