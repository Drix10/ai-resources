### 🤖 Claude AI and Levent Alpöge claim Riemann hypothesis proof in Lean 20 November 2026

Claude AI and mathematician Levent Alpöge announced a claimed proof of the Riemann hypothesis written in Lean on 20 November 2026. Independent researchers, including MIT’s Andrew Sutherland, have compiled the proof without issues, though it is not yet formally accepted.

Key Points:

- **Claim**: Proof of the Riemann hypothesis and the Generalised Riemann Hypothesis was written in Lean.

- **Independent verification**: MIT’s Andrew Sutherland and others compiled the proof without issues.

### 🚀 OTOC1 simulation first rigorous poly-time algorithm announced

OTOC1 simulation in 1D gets its first rigorous poly-time algorithm, presented by QuAntonioMele, Ronald de Wolf, Jarrod Mcclean, and AmiraMorphism. The algorithm also performs well in practice.

Key Points:

- First rigorous poly-time algorithm for classically simulating OTOC1 in 1D.

- Works quite well in practice.

### 🚀 Lean verification of Riemann Hypothesis released, 17 million lines

Lean verification of the Riemann Hypothesis was released as a Lean library of 17 million lines of code. The Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative issued a joint statement on this verification.

Key Points:

- Lean verification of the Riemann Hypothesis released as a 17 million-line Lean library.

- Joint statement issued by the Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative.
