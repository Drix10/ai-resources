### 🤖 NVIDIA Expands Slurm to Bring Quantum Computing Into Supercomputing

NVIDIA expands the Slurm workload manager to support quantum processors, enabling QPU integration into existing HPC workflows. Slurm 26.11 will be released later this month and supports QRMI and QDMI interfaces. The expansion is adopted by multiple QPU vendors and allows a single file interface for hardware across supercomputing centers.

Key Points:

- Slurm 26.11 release scheduled for later this month.

- Supports QRMI and QDMI interfaces for QPU integration.

### 🤖 New Horizons flyby Pluto July 14 2015 at 50,000 km/h

New Horizons swept past Pluto on July 14 2015 traveling at nearly 50,000 km/h. The flyby revealed mountains of water ice over three kilometers high, plains of frozen nitrogen, and a thin blue-hazed atmosphere.

Key Points:

- Nine-year journey to Pluto

- Speed of nearly 50,000 kilometers per hour during the encounter

### 🚀 OTOC1 1D – first rigorous poly-time classical algorithm announced

OTOC1 in 1D now has a first rigorous poly-time classical algorithm, presented in a new paper and implementation. The authors include Laura Lewis, Qu Antonio Mele, Ronald de Wolf, and Jarrod McClean.

Key Points:

- First rigorous poly-time algorithm for classically simulating OTOC1 in 1D.

- Demonstrated to work well in practice, according to the authors.
