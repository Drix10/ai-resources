### 📊 Pizza Theorem – equal-area slices with 4-multiple cuts

Pizza Theorem says that with straight cuts through a common point, any number of cuts that is a multiple of four can be divided into equal-area alternate slices. Two people receive alternate slices.

Key Points:

- **Cuts**: straight radii through a common point, not necessarily the center

- **Count**: number of cuts is a multiple of four (4, 8, 12, etc.)

- **Division**: equal-area slices can be given alternately to two people
