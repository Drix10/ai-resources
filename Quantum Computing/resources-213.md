### 🤖 Quantum - QUOPS Benchmark

Quantum computers have been graded on qubit counts for thirty years, a metric so gameable that a 5,000-qubit annealer and a 50-qubit gate machine share a metric while sharing almost nothing. The industry has been racing without a universal benchmark. QUOPS, a new quantum benchmark, aims to change this.

Key Points:

- **QUOPS Benchmark**: QUOPS is a new universal benchmark for quantum computers, designed to measure their performance in a more meaningful way than qubit counts. It assesses the quality of quantum operations, error rates, and coherence times.

- **Trade-offs/Failure Modes**: The current qubit count metric has led to a focus on quantity over quality, resulting in inefficient and error-prone quantum computers. QUOPS aims to shift the focus to quality and performance.

- **Actionable Takeaway**: Developers and researchers should consider QUOPS as a more accurate and meaningful metric for evaluating quantum computer performance, rather than relying solely on qubit counts.
