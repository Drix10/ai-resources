- **The Butterfly Nebula**: The Butterfly Nebula (NGC 6302) is a stunning celestial wonder that's more delicate in near-infrared light than in visible light. This nebula is shaped by a dark dusty band and conceals an ancient Sun-like core that lights the nebula.

- **The World's Most Accurate Clock**: The world's most accurate clock is so reliable that it would take more than 260 billion years to lose a second. This clock is a testament to human ingenuity and our pursuit of precision.
