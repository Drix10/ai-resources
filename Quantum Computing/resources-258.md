### 🤖 Bosonic codes from compact phase spaces – no normalizable low-energy states

Bosonic codes from compact phase spaces report a no-go theorem for genus > 1 surfaces, showing a strictly positive spectral gap in the stabilizer Hamiltonian. Consequently no normalizable quantum state satisfies all stabilizer conditions. This contrasts with standard GKP codes where the stabilizer group ℤ² allows approximate code words.

Key Points:

- No (normalizable) low-energy states exist for GKP-like codes on hyperbolic lattices.

- For any genus greater than one, the stabilizer group is non-amenable, enforcing a strictly positive spectral gap.
