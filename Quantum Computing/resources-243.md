### 📊 Entangled Measurement Outcomes for Optimal Postselection in Quantum Batteries

Entangled Measurement Outcomes for Optimal Postselection in Quantum Batteries presents conditions when product measurements achieve optimal conditional ergotropy. For auxiliary states mixed with white noise (0 < p < 1), product postselection is optimal iff the top generalized eigenvalue eigenspace contains a product vector. A strict product-joint gap appears for two-qubit auxiliaries when that eigenspace is one-dimensional and entangled.

Key Points:

- For noisy W states of three qubits, a strict product-joint gap exists throughout 0 < p < 1.

- For interacting two-qubit batteries, the optimal postselection can switch from a Bell outcome to a product outcome as coupling strength varies.

### 🤖 Quantum Vehicle Routing Problem

Quantum Vehicle Routing Problem: a smart initialization strategy for ill-conditioned optimization landscapes introduces a parameter-initialization method that improves convergence probability. The work also embeds subtour constraints without extra qubits by approximating exponential equality constraints.

Key Points:

- Parameter-initialization strategy improves convergence probability.

- Subtour constraints integrated directly without additional qubits using an approximation of exponential equality constraints.
