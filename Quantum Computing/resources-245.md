### 📊 genESPRIT precision guarantees without deterioration

genESPRIT can estimate the generator of matrix exponential time-series with rigorous precision guarantees, and precision does not deteriorate as spectral peaks crowd together. The result appears in the paper “Estimating the generator of matrix exponential time-series”.

Key Points:

- **Claim**: under suitable linear-independence assumptions, precision does not deteriorate as spectral peaks crowd together.

- **Collaboration**: work done with @fuksa_jonas and Ingo Roth, linked to an arXiv preprint.
