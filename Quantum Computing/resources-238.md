### 🤖 IonQ wired trapped ion to diamond memory, 1.03 kHz entanglement

IonQ and Duke wired a trapped ion to a diamond quantum memory, achieving 1.03 kHz entanglement. The link produces one Bell pair per millisecond and is four times faster than the previous best ion-ion photonic link.

Key Points:

- **Entanglement rate**: 1.03 kHz.

- **Bell pair generation**: one per millisecond.

- **Speed improvement**: 4× previous best ion-ion photonic link.

### 🤖 Silicon Chip for Quantum Computing paper released

Silicon Chip for Quantum Computing is a paper titled “A digitally controlled silicon quantum processing unit”. It is published by HRL Quantum Team and collaborators in Nature. The DOI is 10.1038/s41586-026-10754-7.

Key Points:
