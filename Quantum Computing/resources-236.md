### 🚀 IonQ Superion 256 – integrated electronic qubit control announced

IonQ Superion 256 integrates electronic qubit control on a semiconductor chip and targets customer site deployments in early 2027. Chris Ballance highlighted manufacturability as key to scaling beyond lab-based laser control.

Key Points:

- Integrated electronic qubit control directly into the semiconductor chip.

- First prototype systems have ion qubits; deployments aimed for early 2027.
