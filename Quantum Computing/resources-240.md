### 📊 Plant biology provides inspiration for PFAS replacement – new waterproof coating

Plant biology provides inspiration for PFAS replacement introduces a plant-inspired waterproof coating that matches PTFE water resistance. The coating is made from paper industry waste and animal fat and degrades under composting conditions.

Key Points:

- Uses a suberin-like slurry from paper waste and animal fat to spray-coat glass, metal and cotton surfaces.

- Shows resistance to limescale, mechanical wear and similar water resistance to PTFE, but lower heat tolerance than PTFE.
