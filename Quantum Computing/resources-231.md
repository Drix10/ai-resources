### 🤖 GeroScience Review - Intervertebral Disc Degeneration

A new review by Victor Chen et al. provides a comprehensive overview of intervertebral disc degeneration and structural failure of the aging spine. This review brings together evidence on the cellular and molecular pathways underlying disc degeneration, which is a major contributor to age-related spinal disorders.

Key Points:

- **Cellular and Molecular Pathways**: The review highlights the complex interplay between cellular and molecular mechanisms that contribute to disc degeneration, including inflammation, oxidative stress, and cellular senescence.

- **Structural Failure**: The review discusses the structural changes that occur in the aging spine, including disc height loss, facet joint degeneration, and vertebral body osteoporosis.

- **Implications for Spinal Disorders**: The review emphasizes the importance of understanding the underlying mechanisms of disc degeneration in order to develop effective treatments for age-related spinal disorders.
