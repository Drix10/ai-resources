### 🤖 BTCS sells over half ETH holdings for $43.8M

BTCS sold about 27,300 ETH for $43.8 million at an average of $1,604 on November 20 and used the cash to repay Aave loans. The repayment cut outstanding debt to $10.5 million and lowered the loan-to-value ratio to 32%.

Key Points:

- **Sale**: 27,300 ETH for $43.8M at $1,604 average price on November 20.

- **Debt reduction**: Aave loans down 81% to $10.5M, LTV now 32%.

- **Holdings**: 24,245 ETH and combined cash/digital assets worth $61M as of November 22.

- **Imperium**: DeFi unit temporarily withdrew from liquidity pools, will redeploy when conditions improve.

### 📊 Grayscale says crypto oversold, valuations attractive for investors

Grayscale says Bitcoin fell to $55,000 on November 21 and global crypto ETPs had over $2 billion in outflows last week, indicating oversold conditions. The firm adds that stablecoin redemptions, declining BTC and ETH futures open interest, and higher put option demand show market stress, but blockchain fundamentals remain solid and valuations are attractive for medium- to long-term investors.

Key Points:

- **Bitcoin price**: $55,000 on November 21

- **Global crypto ETP outflows**: $2 billion last week

- Stablecoin redemptions and falling BTC/ETH futures open interest noted

- Rising demand for put options observed
