### 🚀 Binance Hack Timeline: Compromise of Three Engineers

Binance Hack Timeline maps attacker actions from June to October, detailing how three engineers were compromised to reach the wallet signing service. The timeline shows download of malicious tools, use of internal AI, and deployment of a container to trigger unauthorized transfers.

Key Points:

- **June**: Wallet DevOps Engineer A downloaded a tampered Homebrew installer while setting up an AI coding tool.

- **September**: Engineer B downloaded a malicious code repository during a fake job interview, compromising his work computer.

- **October 20**: Attackers used the compromised device and an internal AI tool to gain control over Engineers A and C, discovering a container deployment permission token on A’s computer.

- **October 29**: They accessed C’s computer, used credentials stolen from A to deploy a container, execute a malicious script, and initiate unauthorized asset transfers through Binance’s internal wallet signing service.
