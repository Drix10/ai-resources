### 🚨 Binance hot wallet hack, $8.8M Ethena exposure

Binance suffered a hot wallet hack. Tether and Circle froze USDT and USDC linked to it. Ethena reports limited $8.8M impact, covered by its $60M reserve fund. Liquidity is being withdrawn from several markets as a precaution.

Key Points:

- Tether and Circle have frozen a significant amount of USDT and USDC linked to the incident

- Ethena’s potential impact is $8.8M, fully covered by its $60M reserve fund

### 🚨 Binance hot wallet incident update – collateral unaffected, exposure <$10M

Binance hot wallet incident update says no collateral backing vaults is affected and Ethena’s exposure to Binance is below $10M, covered by its insurance fund. Withdrawals operate normally; some Morpho markets may have reduced liquidity.

Key Points:

- **Collateral**: No collateral backing vaults is affected, includes Figure, OnRe, Huma, Avant, InfiniFi, Tori, Re, Obligate, f(x) and Coinshift across all chains.

- **Indirect exposure**: Ethena’s potential exposure to Binance is below $10M and falls within the coverage of its insurance fund (over $60M).
