### 🤖 Bitcoin Spot ETFs Net Outflows $412M, Ethereum Spot ETFs Outflows $93.19M

Bitcoin spot ETFs saw $412 million net outflows on November 6, while Ethereum spot ETFs lost $93.19 million. Fidelity’s FBTC led Bitcoin inflows with $42.82 million and BlackRock’s ETHB led Ethereum inflows with $1.15 million.

Key Points:

- Bitcoin spot ETFs net outflows $412 million on November 6 (ET)

- Fidelity FBTC net inflow $42.82 million, top Bitcoin spot ETF inflow

- Ethereum spot ETFs net outflows $93.19 million on November 6 (ET)

- BlackRock ETHB net inflow $1.15 million, top Ethereum spot ETF inflow

### 🤖 BLAKE3 stream API sketch adds incremental multithreaded batching

BLAKE3-Servil proposes a new stream API that supports incremental, multithreaded, batched hashing with limited memory and back-pressure. The design keeps existing bulk calls and adds async submit functions for prepared blocks and batches. A sample benchmark hashes one million 32-byte messages using a multithreaded batch call.

Key Points:

- Incremental pipeline, convenience Hasher and async submission signatures are provisional in the design.

- Example hashes one million 32-byte messages in a 64MB aligned batch, producing 32MB of digests via throughput::hash_many_multithreaded.

### 🚨 Singapore MAS urges caution after Binance hack

Singapore's Monetary Authority of Singapore says cryptocurrency trading is unsuitable for the general public after the Binance hack. It warns consumers to avoid unregulated firms and notes Binance is not authorized to provide regulated financial services in Singapore.

Key Points:

- November 5 spokesperson said crypto trading is unsuitable for the general public.

- Binance is not authorized to provide regulated financial services in Singapore; safeguards cannot fully prevent losses even with licensed firms.
