### 📊 Hyperliquid USDC float peaks $7.68B, yields $15.1M

Hyperliquid USDC float peaked at $7.68B on Sep 23 and yields $15.1M for the Assistance Fund on Nov 2. The float is $6.81B today, down 11%, and the yield translates to about 6,300 HYPE per day at $79.39. The 30-day average float rose 3.6% to $7.18B, and the next cycle is expected to pay about $14.3M, roughly 5% less. Yield follows the float, not trading volume.

Key Points:

- **Float peak**: USDC float peaked at $7.68B on Sep 23, now $6.81B, down 11%.

- **Assistance Fund yield**: $15.1M USDC reserve yield on Nov 2, $503.5K per day, about 6,300 HYPE at $79.39.
