### 🤖 Tachyon claims single ZKP verification for million transactions

Tachyon says it can verify one ZKP to process a million transactions in a block, eliminating state bloat. Zcash is described as having achieved a stateless blockchain.

Key Points:

- **Verification**: one ZKP replaces a million ZKP verifications per block.

- **State**: state bloat is gone, and Zcash achieved a stateless blockchain.
