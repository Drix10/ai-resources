### 🚀 Binance says alleged 335M-user data leak likely a scam

Binance says alleged 335M-user data leak likely a scam, found no evidence of compromised systems, KYC data, credentials or keys. The exchange noted inconsistencies in the listing and suggested the dataset may be infostealer data from users’ devices.

Key Points:

- **Investigation**: ShinyHunters allegedly stole data on 335 million users and demanded $900,000.

- **Findings**: No evidence that Binance systems, KYC data, account credentials or wallet private keys were compromised; parts of the listing inconsistent with Binance storage; dataset may involve infostealer data from users’ devices.
