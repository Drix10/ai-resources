### 📊 Coinbase Q3 highlights: market share, trader growth, revenue, subscribers

Coinbase reported Q3 results showing a fourth straight quarter of gaining crypto trading volume market share, a 60% rise in daily prediction market traders, and an all-time high for Coinbase One subscribers. Daily prediction market traders now generate over $200M in annualized revenue.

Key Points:

- 4th consecutive quarter of gaining crypto trading volume market share

- 60% increase in daily prediction market traders, hitting $200M+ in annualized revenue

- Coinbase One subscribers hit all-time high
