### 🤖 Blockchain Security - Post-Quantum Cryptography

BlackRock's whitepaper indicates that blockchain ecosystems need to adopt post-quantum cryptography. This transition is critical to secure systems before quantum computers can compromise current cryptographic standards.

Key Points:

• Blockchain systems require a proactive transition to post-quantum cryptography.

• Quantum computing presents a future threat to existing cryptographic standards.

• Addressing quantum security is now an immediate concern, not a distant one.
