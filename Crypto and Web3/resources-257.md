### 🚀 Relay Protocol North Korean hackers moved $38M in 7 hours

Relay Protocol saw North Korean hackers transfer about $38 million in seven hours, with transfers still ongoing. The bridge’s KYT monitoring did not stop the activity. Specter compared the speed to THORChain, noting the hackers would have struggled there.

Key Points:

- **Transfer amount**: approximately $38 million moved through Relay Protocol.

- **Timeframe**: the transfers occurred within seven hours.

- **KYT system**: Relay’s Know Your Transaction monitoring failed to block the transfers.
