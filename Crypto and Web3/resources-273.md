### 🤖 Firelight FXRP minting via Flare Smart Accounts

Firelight enables minting of FXRP and deposit to its platform with a single signature using Flare Smart Accounts. Depositors receive stXRP that represents their staked position.

Key Points:

- Mint FXRP and deposit to Firelight with one signature via Flare Smart Accounts

- Receive stXRP representing the staked position

### 🤖 Nerochain MOU with Alatau City Authority to tokenise GPU power $5M

Nerochain announced an MOU with Kazakhstan's Alatau City Authority to tokenise GPU computing power. The pilot is valued at up to $5 million.

Key Points:

- MOU with Alatau City Authority

- Pilot valuation up to $5M
