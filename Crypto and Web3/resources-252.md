### 🚨 Crypto OTC company Hong Kong loss HK$4M after WhatsApp hijack

Crypto OTC desk in Hong Kong lost more than HK$4 million (about US$514k) after scammers hijacked an employee’s WhatsApp, cloned the client group and impersonated clients. The fraud involved blocking genuine clients and colleagues and requesting a transfer.

Key Points:

- **Loss**: HK$4 million (≈US$514k) stolen from the desk.

- **Method**: Hijacked employee WhatsApp, cloned work group, impersonated clients to request transfer.
