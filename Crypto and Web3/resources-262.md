### 🚨 Holograph employee sentenced for $14M token mint

Holograph Foundation former employee received a five-year prison sentence for illegally minting 1 billion HLG tokens valued at about $14 million in 2024. Two accomplices received suspended or partially suspended sentences and fines.

Key Points:

- **Sentence**: five years total, two years to be served, three years suspended.

- **Minted tokens**: 1 billion HLG tokens, approx. $14 million value, transferred to personal wallets.
