### 📊 Zeru zScore and zaps points clarified, 2.17M checks on Etherscan

Zeru provides a zScore ranging 0–1000 that rates wallet on-chain behavior and is visible on Etherscan or Basescan. The explorer has served 2.17M zScore checks as of August 2026. Inside the Zaps app a separate points tally called a zaps balance tracks qualifying activity and is not the ZERU token. No conversion rate between the two has been published.

Key Points:

- **zScore**: 0–1000 number rating wallet behavior onchain, shown on Etherscan/Basescan

- 2.17M checks served on Etherscan as of August 2026

- **zaps balance**: points tally inside the app, not the ZERU token, no published conversion rate

### 🚨 Crypto.com and Novig sue Missouri over sports betting

Crypto.com and Novig are suing Missouri officials to block enforcement of state sports wagering rules on their platforms. Missouri ordered them to cease the contracts or apply for a state license within 30 days, and the suit claims federal law preempts the state rules.

Key Points:

- **Lawsuit purpose**: prevent enforcement of Missouri sports wagering legislation on their platforms.

- **Missouri order**: cease operations of the contracts or apply for a state license within 30 days.
