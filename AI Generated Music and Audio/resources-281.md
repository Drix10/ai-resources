### 🤖 Phonon text-to-speech model 44MB int8 release

Phonon by GradiumAI is a local TTS model with int8 weights of 44MB. It runs 26× real-time on an iPhone in Safari and is under 40MB when Brotli compressed. The model is open source under Apache-2.0 and timestamps every word.

Key Points:

- int8 weights size 44MB

- 26× real-time performance on iPhone Safari

- compressed size under 40MB Brotli

- open source Apache-2.0 license, every word timestamped
