### 🤖 Why voice wins in complex conversations – 32% higher success for voice over chat

Why voice wins in complex conversations reports that voice succeeded 32% more often than text-based chat in conversations longer than two minutes. Voice also resolved those longer conversations about one third faster, averaging 4.6 minutes versus 7 minutes for chat. In a simulated stressed scenario, 85% of participants chose voice.

Key Points:

- Voice succeeded 32% more often than chat in conversations over two minutes.

- Voice resolved successful long conversations in an average of 4.6 minutes, compared to 7 minutes for chat.

- 85% of participants preferred voice in a simulated canceled-flight scenario.

### 📊 Suno Studio Updates, Shaped by your Feedback – New Clip UI and Features

Suno Studio adds a left-hand sidebar for Clip Settings, a Clip Info panel, auto-stretch for added audio, fine pitch adjustment, track hide/show shortcuts, and grid density/contrast controls. These changes make clip editing more accessible and provide additional timing and pitch controls.

Key Points:

- Clip Settings moved to a left sidebar with volume, pitch, loop, reverse and timestretching controls.

- New Clip Info panel shows lyrics, style tags and stem separation, accessed via right-click menu or bottom button.

- Auto-stretch option lets added audio automatically match project tempo, while clips without a steady beat and stem groups keep original timing.

- Fine pitch adjustment accepts values like 2.38 to set 2 semitones and 38 cents.
