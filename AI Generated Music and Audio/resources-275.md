### 🤖 Simplified Matching Distillation for few-step diffusion

Simplified Matching Distillation (SMD) is introduced for few-step diffusion, matching teacher flow with a straight-line prediction plus a curved zero-integral path at random sub-intervals. It requires no auxiliary score model, no adversarial training, and no generator modification, and claims photorealistic appearance, high-quality dynamics, memory-efficient training, and mode covering.

Key Points:

- No auxiliary score model.

- No adversarial training.
