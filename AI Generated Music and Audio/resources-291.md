### 🤖 ElevenAgents for Healthcare – Candid Health automates payer calls, 59% resolution

ElevenAgents for Healthcare is used by Candid Health to automate payer calls for denied claims, handling 12,860 calls in a pilot and resolving 59% of them. The automation saved over 850 staff hours per month, with an average call length of 8 minutes 46 seconds.

Key Points:

- **Pilot volume**: 12,860 payer calls processed by ElevenAgents.

- **Resolution rate**: 59% of calls uncovered denial reason and next step.

- **Time saved**: more than 850 staff hours per month.

- **Average call duration**: 8 minutes 46 seconds.
