### 🤖 ASR Bench uses CER for Japanese, not comparable to English WER

ASR Bench uses character error rate for Japanese and other CJK languages, while English uses word error rate. A 10% error rate in English WER and a 10% error rate in Japanese CER are not directly comparable.

Key Points:

- **Metric**: word error rate (WER) used for most languages

- **Metric**: character error rate (CER) used for Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Thai
