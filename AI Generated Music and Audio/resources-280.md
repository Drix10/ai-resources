### 🤖 How Much Prosody Does Your ASR Encoder Already Know

How Much Prosody Does Your ASR Encoder Already Know shows prosody adds a large gain on raw audio but the gain collapses after the first encoder layer. The paper tests three ways on Zipformer, wav2vec2 and Whisper encoders and finds the same pattern across them.

### 📊 Count-Density Networks for Modal Plate Estimation

Count-Density Networks for Modal Plate Estimation described two neural methods for Task B of the 1st DAFx Parameter Estimation Challenge. The real-valued convolutional network uses pooled spectral features and absolute-scale conditioning; the complex-valued Transformer network processes complex inputs. Both jointly infer modal count and per-mode attributes from the impulse response and achieve lower overall challenge error than classical baselines, with frequency and decay estimates substantially more accurate than gain estimates.

Key Points:

- Real-valued convolutional count-density network combines pooled spectral features with time-domain and absolute-scale conditioning.

- Complex-valued Transformer count-density network processes complex inputs and jointly infers modal count and per-mode attributes.
