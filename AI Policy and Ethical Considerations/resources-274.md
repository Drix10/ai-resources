### 🤖 GTA 6 launch night DoorDash orders up 25% in Miami

GTA 6 launch night caused Miami DoorDash orders to rise nearly 25%, the biggest jump among markets. Searches for GTA 6 on DoorDash increased over 940% before players returned to Vice City.

Key Points:

- **Orders**: Miami late-night DoorDash orders rose nearly 25% on GTA 6 launch night.

- **Searches**: GTA 6 searches on DoorDash jumped more than 940% leading up to the launch.

### 📊 SMIC AI wafer allocation, Alibaba moves to SMIC

SMIC’s allocation of logic wafers for AI chips influences China’s compute capacity. Alibaba began shifting AI chip production to SMIC last year. New US rules may block Chinese AI chips made by TSMC or Samsung.

Key Points:

- SMIC allocation of logic wafers for AI chips is a critical factor in China’s compute potential.

- Alibaba started moving AI chip manufacturing to SMIC in the previous year.
