### 🚨 GMO Internet Group cloud VPS hacked, trades hit 70 brokerages

GMO Internet Group’s cloud VPS service used by foreign-exchange traders was hacked. Attackers breached servers, stole credentials, and executed balance-destroying trades across over 70 brokerages. GMO has asked government agencies for help.

Key Points:

- **Breach**: attackers accessed the servers and stole customer credentials.

- **Impact**: balance-destroying trades were executed across over 70 brokerages.

- **Response**: GMO has asked government agencies for assistance.

### 🤖 eMudhra intermediate CAs compromised – Apple Support document released

Apple released a support document about compromised eMudhra intermediate CAs. The document addresses the issue of eMudhra certificates.

Key Points:

- Apple support page titled “About the compromised eMudhra intermediate CAs”.

- Document provides guidance on handling the compromised certificates.
