### AI Model Evaluation vs. Assessment vs. Verification

AI model evaluation, assessment, and verification are often used interchangeably, but they have distinct meanings. Understanding these differences is crucial for developers and researchers working with AI systems.

Key Points:

- **Assessment**: An umbrella term for any structured examination of an AI system, practice, or company. Can consist of evaluation or verification.

- **Evaluation**: Measuring the properties of a system, such as accuracy, fairness, or robustness.

- **Verification**: Ensuring that a system meets its specifications and requirements.

- **Actionable Takeaway**: When evaluating AI models, consider the specific goals and requirements of the project to determine the most appropriate assessment method.
