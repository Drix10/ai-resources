### 🤖 GLM-5.3 scores 160, beats GPT-5.4 on Cyber ECI

GLM-5.3 achieved a score of 160 on the Cyber ECI, surpassing OpenAI’s GPT-5.4 released in March. A 6-9 month lag in stable objective time is described as a growing amount.

Key Points:

- **Lag**: consistently behind in stable objective time (“6-9 months”) is a growing amount.

- **Benchmark**: Zai’s GLM-5.3 scored 160, beating OpenAI’s GPT-5.4 on the Cyber ECI.

### 📊 Anthropic AI expectations gap – staff predict 80% of model building by 2027

Anthropic employees median expect AI to handle 80% of building models by end-2027. Survey shows staff anticipate far higher AI productivity and automation than US adults, with 91% expecting AI to make Nobel-level discoveries by 2030 and over half expecting AI to do at least 80% of their own work by 2030.

Key Points:

- **Median staff expectation**: AI will do 80% of building models by end of 2027.

- 91% of Anthropic staff expect AI to make a Nobel-level discovery by 2030.
