### 🤖 NSF X-labs From Sequence to Function funding opportunity announced

NSF X-labs launches a new funding opportunity called “From Sequence to Function” for teams accelerating breakthroughs in linking biological sequence to function. Applications are now being accepted.

Key Points:

- **Funding opportunity**: NSF X-labs – From Sequence to Function

- **Target**: teams working to accelerate breakthroughs in understanding the relationship between biological sequence and function

### 🤖 Astra 10 billion tokens builds 3D embassy models

Astra uses 10 billion tokens to create 3D models of Washington embassies and a digital atlas of global diplomacy with the United States. The project is described as the creator’s most ambitious effort.

Key Points:

- **Tokens**: 10 billion Astra tokens

- **Output**: 3D models of embassies across Washington and a digital atlas of the world’s diplomacy with the United States
