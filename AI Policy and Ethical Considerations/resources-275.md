### 🤖 GPT-Rosalind aims for Codex-like tool for scientists

GPT-Rosalind is being trained to provide a Codex-level capability for scientists, coders, biotech and pharma. The model shows strong benchmark performance in chemistry and structural biology. Users often lack knowledge of prompts and how to access capabilities, requiring a good product wrapper. ### 🤖 NormGym benchmark introduced for evaluating normative competence

NormGym is a benchmark for evaluating normative competence through social interaction. It shows agents sometimes learn from their own norm violations but struggle to learn from others’ behavior.

Key Points:

- NormGym benchmark for evaluating normative competence through social interaction.

- Agents can sometimes learn from their own norm violations, but struggle to learn from the behavior of others.
