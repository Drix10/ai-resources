### 🤖 Anthropic Residency applications open for senior leaders, $350k-$500k pay

Anthropic Residency opens for senior technical leaders with placements of six weeks to six months and pay equivalent to $350,000-$500,000 per year. Applications close on November 22, 2026. The program accepts professors, technical founders, and staff-level engineers without ML background. Residents receive mentorship, compute access, and a path to full-time AI research.

Key Points:

- **Placement length**: six weeks, three months, or six months.

- **Compensation**: $350,000 to $500,000 per year equivalent.

### 🤖 LLM findings short shelf life, 50 claims tested 15 months

LLM findings short shelf life, 50 claims tested 15 months, most no longer held or showed substantially reduced effects. The study revisited 50 widely cited findings about LLM behavior.

Key Points:

- 50 widely cited findings about LLM behavior were re-tested.

- Within 15 months of publication, most findings no longer held or showed substantially diminished effects.
