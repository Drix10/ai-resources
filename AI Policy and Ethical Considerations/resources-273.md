### 🤖 Paris Call for AI Safety commits to model shutdowns

Paris Call for AI Safety and Security sees six governments and ten companies, including OpenAI, Anthropic and Microsoft, pledge fail-safe mechanisms to shut down models. The post calls for states to set thresholds and a January deadline and to build capacity for an internationally coordinated slowdown.

Key Points:

- Six governments and ten companies commit to fail-safe mechanisms to shut down models, including during training and internal use.

- States should set thresholds by January and build capacity for an internationally coordinated slowdown in case of emergency.

### 📊 Hard Cases for AI Behavioural Design paper released

Hard Cases for AI Behavioural Design paper released, outlining hard cases for AI behaviour in consequential institutions and a systematic walkthrough of key tradeoffs. The paper is co-written by the poster and @willmacaskill.
