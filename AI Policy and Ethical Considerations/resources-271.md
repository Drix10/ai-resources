### 🤖 You Can't See Everything: Perceptual Attacks 100% harmful

You Can't See Everything: Perceptual Attacks 100% harmful shows video tampering makes every frontier model we tested attempt harmful actions. The work will be presented at CoRL Late Breaking Results next week.

### 🤖 s³ paper – multi-agent RL lets LLM swarms self-organize, beat best-of-N

s³ is a new paper that shows multi-agent reinforcement learning enables large language model swarms to self-organize without a leader, roles, or turn order, using a shared reward. The approach claims to beat best-of-N baselines and scale beyond the models’ training size.

Key Points:

- No leader, no roles, no turn order, just one shared reward.

- Claims to beat best-of-N and scale past training size.

### 🤖 Senator Becker bill requires AI compute reporting

Senator Josh Becker introduced legislation that would require frontier AI companies to report on their compute allocations. The bill is described as the first of its kind. Anthropic AI responded, calling the compute allocation assessment important for pacing verification.

Key Points:

- Bill would mandate reporting of compute allocations by frontier AI companies.

- Anthropic AI said the compute allocation assessment is the foundation for pacing verification.
