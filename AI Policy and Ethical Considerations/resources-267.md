Anthropic plans to spend up to $567 billion on compute in coming years. It will build its own data centers through an initiative called Anthropic Direct. Anthropic Direct will represent a growing share of its compute investments over time.

Key Points:

- **Initiative**: Anthropic Direct builds its own data centers.

- **Share**: Anthropic Direct will represent a growing share of compute investments.
