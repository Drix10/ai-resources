### 🤖 Noeri launch with $110 million funding announced

Noeri launched with $110 million funding, targeting many small models that learn from each other and an organization’s data. It calls this plural intelligence.

Key Points:

- **Funding**: $110 million secured.

- **Approach**: many small models that learn from each other and from an organization’s own data, termed plural intelligence.

### 🤖 Antigravity usage limit reporting removed in t3dotcodes due to Google TOS

Antigravity usage limit reporting was removed from t3dotcodes because it does not comply with Google TOS. The team has asked Google to add support for this feature to the ACP so it can be restored when officially supported.

Key Points:

- usage limit reporting for Antigravity removed in t3dotcodes due to not complying with Google TOS

- requested Google add support for this to the ACP to add it back when officially supported
