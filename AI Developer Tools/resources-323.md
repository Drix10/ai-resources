### 🤖 Hy MT3 30B model open-source with 102-language support

Hy MT3 releases three sizes—1.8B, 7B, and 30B—under Apache 2.0, expanding coverage to 102 languages. The 30B model cuts omissions/additions by 74%, mistranslations by 43%, and instruction errors by 40% on real-world data. Quantized builds for local/on-device use and full weights for server deployment are provided, and a browser extension is forthcoming.

Key Points:

- Open-source release of Hy MT3-1.8B, Hy MT3-7B, and Hy MT3-30B with Apache 2.0 license.

- Real-world evaluation shows 74% reduction in omissions/additions, 43% reduction in mistranslations, and 40% reduction in instruction errors for the 30B model.
