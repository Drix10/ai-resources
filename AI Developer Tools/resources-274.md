### 🤖 LLMs for Hard Research Tasks: Challenges and Limitations

There is still a long way to go before LLMs are useful for genuinely hard research tasks. We let GPT autoresearch Deep Learning alphas on crypto perp data and found that GPT: - doesn't explore parameters well, only minimal changes across iterations - has poor research taste, often selecting irrelevant papers.

Key Points:

- **LLM Limitations**: LLMs struggle with genuinely hard research tasks, requiring significant improvements in parameter exploration and research taste.

- **GPT Autoresearch**: GPT's autoresearch capabilities are limited, failing to explore parameters effectively and selecting irrelevant papers.

- **Actionable Takeaway**: Consider the limitations of LLMs in research tasks and focus on developing more effective methods for parameter exploration and research taste.
