USearch v3 is designed for 1B+, 10B+, 100B+ scales and reaches ~100K QPS on ~100M bit-vectors with 99%+ recall on a single outdated CPU. It also fixes the weakest modes of operation from v2.

Key Points:

- **Scale**: supports 1B+, 10B+, 100B+ scales.

- **Performance**: ~100K QPS over ~100M bit-vectors at 99%+ recall on a single outdated CPU.

### 📊 HiCache Distributed Serving – Cache hit rate up to 95%

HiCache enables reuse of long prefixes across parallel model calls, achieving a cache hit rate of 95% on multi-turn agentic workloads. Median time-to-first-token drops below 3 seconds.

Key Points:

- **Cache hit rate**: 30% → 95% on multi-turn agentic workloads with inputs typically 30K-200K tokens, up to 1M.

- **Median TTFT**: below 3 seconds, down from previous unusable delays.
