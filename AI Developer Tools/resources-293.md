### 🤖 Team implements five AI loops for workflow automation

The team created five AI loops to automate parts of their workflow. Error monitoring using GCP logs, Sentry, and Langfuse feeds into PRs. Customer support, user activation analysis, and ads performance also trigger PRs or spend changes. Desktop/mobile app QA uses BugPatrol.

Key Points:

- Error monitoring (gcp logs, sentry, langfuse) → PR

- Desktop/mobile app QA (bugpatrol)

### 🤖 Brand Kit introduced in ElevenCreative

Brand Kit is a new feature in ElevenCreative. It lets you add your logo, fonts and colors once. All created assets automatically use those assets. A launch campaign for a new product will be on‑brand.

Key Points:

- **Introduction**: Brand Kit in ElevenCreative.

- **Function**: Add logo, fonts and colors once; assets pull them in automatically.
