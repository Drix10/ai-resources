### 🤖 OCRia-1 launched as best general purpose document parser

OCRia-1 is announced as the best general purpose model for document parsing, handling complex text layouts, tables, diagrams, and charts. It powers all modes within LlamaParse and is benchmarked on ParseBench. Two OCR approaches are described: fast/cheap free OSS tools integrated in liteparse, and accurate VLM-based OCR for critical workflows.

Key Points:

- OCRia-1 launched as a general purpose document parsing model.

- Liteparse integrates with five open-weight OCR tools, including MiniCPM-OCR.
