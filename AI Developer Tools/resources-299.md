### 🚀 Kite Agent Passport launches verifiable identity and payments

Kite Agent Passport launches verifiable agent identity, user-defined permissions, spending limits, and agentic payments. At Money20/20 USA, Circle demoed x401 identity verification with x402 USDC payments, and Visa, BILL and Billtrust started a pilot for agent-driven supplier invoice payments.

Key Points:

- **Identity and payment demo**: Circle paired x401 identity verification with x402 USDC payments.

- **B2B pilot**: Visa, BILL and Billtrust launched a pilot for agents to complete buyer-approved supplier invoice payments.
