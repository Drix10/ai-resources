# Code of Conduct

This project is a small, public reading resource. Issues and pull requests here should be useful to the next person who reads them.

## Expected

- Be specific and kind. Critique the digest, the source or the code, not the person.
- Back factual corrections with a link.
- Disclose it when you suggest your own account, company or product.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, paid-placement requests, or coordinated vote or star campaigns.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues, and block accounts that break these rules. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through [drix10.com](https://drix10.com). Reports are kept confidential.
