### 🚨 Learjet 60 crash – descent from 35,000 ft to impact in 78 seconds

Learjet 60 crashed in Florida after descending from about 35,000 ft to impact in roughly 78 seconds, with all nine aboard killed and one ground injury. The incident involved severe weather, possible in-flight breakup, and a 4D reconstruction using ADS-B, radar, satellite, dashcam and drone video.

Key Points:

- **Descent**: ~35,000 ft to impact in ~78 seconds.

- **Casualties**: nine deceased on board, one injured on the ground; debris spread across half a mile.
