### 🚀 RealityHD v5.0 released for native RealityKit Vision Pro apps

RealityHD v5.0 is a photorealistic scene generation pipeline for native RealityKit Apple Vision Pro applications. The author is offering it for free. The new version includes optimizations to be more token‑efficient while generating.

Key Points:

- **Release**: RealityHD v5.0 now available.

### 🤖 Duuro WebXR Simulator Shows Meta Glasses 70°×66° FOV vs Quest 3 110°×96°

Duuro released a WebXR demo that runs on Meta Quest 3 and simulates the announced Meta VR Glasses field of view. The simulator displays a 70° × 66° viewing window over the Quest 3’s native 110° × 96° view. Users can toggle the mask on and off with Button A on the right controller.

Key Points:

- **Simulator URL**: https://duuro.net/vr-hub/meta-glasses-fov-simulator

- **FOV comparison**: Glasses 70° × 66° (≈43.7% of Quest 3’s 110° × 96°)【source】

### 🤖 Haiku 5.5 released on Claude Platform, 75% cheaper than 4.5

Haiku 5.5 is now available in the Claude Platform and Claude Code. It runs about 75% cheaper on average than Haiku 4.5. The model pairs with Opus 5.5 or Sonnet 5.5 as a subagent. It is intended for high‑volume, cost‑sensitive tasks such as summaries, compactions, or database queries.

Key Points:

- **Availability**: Haiku 5.5 is on Claude Platform and Claude Code.

- **Cost claim**: Runs roughly 75% less expensive than Haiku 4.5.
