### 🤖 visionOS Spatial Accessories Framework workflow for video

visionOS Spatial Accessories Framework introduced in visionOS 27 is being used to edit and render a YouTube video entirely in spatial on a headset. The project plans to add cropping, stereo output from a dual fisheye feed, auto viewfinder rotation via head position, and a persona view for vlogging.

Key Points:

- Tapped into the Spatial Accessories Framework introduced in visionOS 27.

- Planning to add cropping, stereo output (from dual fisheye feed), auto viewfinder rotate via head position, and persona view for vlogging.
