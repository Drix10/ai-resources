### 🤖 Heave-Ho Unitree G1 teamwork scaled to 10 robots

Heave-Ho from USC teaches multiple Unitree G1 humanoids to cooperate, training on two robots and scaling to teams of up to 10 without retraining. The system uses a fine-tuned Qwen3-VL-2B model for planning and voice commands for coordination, achieving >94.41% success in simulation and 92/100 real-world trials.

Key Points:

- Trained with 2 Unitree G1 robots, scales to teams of up to 10

- 94.41% average success across 12 simulated tasks and 92/100 successful real-world trials
