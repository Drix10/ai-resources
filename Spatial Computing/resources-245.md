### 🚨 YOLO11-Pose detects and tracks shoplifting behavior

YOLO11-Pose detects and tracks suspicious merchandise pocketing in security footage, using 17 body keypoints and ByteTrack. It scores reaching-to-shelf and hand-to-pocket actions and triggers alerts when a threshold is crossed.

Key Points:

- Detects people and 17 body keypoints with YOLO11-Pose

- Tracks people with ByteTrack

- Scores behavior involving reaching toward shelves and moving a hand to a pocket within a few seconds

- If the threshold is exceeded, zooms in on and saves the face, and issues an alert

### 🤖 Ancient Future Underwater VR exhibit at IMMENSIVA EXPO 2026 Barcelona

Ancient Future Underwater is a VR work displayed at IMMENSIVA EXPO 2026 in Barcelona, featuring 3D-scanned Jomon pottery combined with VR paintings. The exhibition runs publicly from November 5 to 9.

Key Points:
