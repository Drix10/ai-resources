### 🤖 4D Gaussians rendering 185k points in 0.4ms on 3090 Ti

4D Gaussians rendering 185k points in 0.4ms on a 3090 Ti using Rust/webgl. It shares the same WebGL context as threejs, so depth tests and occlusion work automatically.

Key Points:

- **Rendering speed**: 0.4 ms on a 3090 Ti for 185k 4D Gaussians

- Built in Rust/webgl, sharing context with threejs for depth tests and occlusion

### 🤖 AI Analyzes Basketball Footage Provides Audio Commentary

AI Analyzes Basketball Footage provides audio commentary on game flow using YOLO detection and ByteTrack tracking. It detects players, ball, and rim, tracks positions and speeds, and operates in post-processing mode.

Key Points:

### 🤖 Boxing Coach Without a Camera Tracks Arms in 3D with IMU

Boxing Coach Without a Camera tracks arms in 3D using an IMU wristwatch and smartphone. It provides audio guidance for 10 punch types and 6 combo types and scores form, speed, and estimated power.

Key Points:

- **Measurement**: wristwatch + smartphone on upper arm tracks movements in 3D.

- **Audio guidance**: supports 10 punch types and 6 combo types.

- **Scoring**: evaluates form, speed, and estimated power; power estimate based on physique and requires an Android-compatible smartwatch.
