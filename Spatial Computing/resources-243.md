### 🤖 Cubiques now free on Meta Store with 70 puzzles

Cubiques is now available free on the Meta Store, offering 70 handcrafted puzzles and mixed-reality features. It showcases SDK hand tracking, passthrough, and spatial interactions for a ported iOS game.

Key Points:

- Hand tracking with pinch-and-trace controls

- Four VR environments with smooth passthrough transitions
