### 🤖 Phantom Blade Zero launch sells 4 million copies in 72 hours

Phantom Blade Zero sold 4 million copies in its first 72 hours after launching on PC and PlayStation 5 on October 29. It is a finished single-player game released at full price three weeks before GTA 6, with no Xbox version.

Key Points:

- 4 million copies sold in 72 hours

- Launched on PC and PlayStation 5; no Xbox version

### 🤖 Saber Academy° – visionOS 26.0 required app released

Saber Academy° is an app for Apple Vision that requires visionOS 26.0 or later. It does not collect any user data. The app supports game controllers and family sharing for up to six members.

Key Points:

- Requires visionOS 26.0 or later.
