### 🤖 ChatGPT voice modes retiring December 11, move to GPT-Live

ChatGPT retires Advanced Voice and Standard Voice on December 11, shifting every account to GPT-Live. Voice usage nearly doubled after GPT-Live launched in July. GPT-Live can listen and speak at the same time, allowing interruptions.

Key Points:

- Advanced Voice and Standard Voice leave Settings menu on December 11; all accounts switch to GPT-Live.

- Voice use nearly doubled after GPT-Live launched in July.

- GPT-Live can listen and speak simultaneously, so users can interrupt mid-sentence.
