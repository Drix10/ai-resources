### 🤖 Google Ads iPhone app campaign 957 clicks 39 downloads

Google Ads iPhone app campaign got €61 spend, 957 clicks, 39 downloads, and 18 completed onboarding. Only 2 downloads were counted by Google Ads because there was no ATT prompt. The store page conversion is 4% of clicks.

Key Points:

- **Spend**: €61 over 2 days.

- **Store page conversion**: 4% of clicks download.

### 🤖 VR Forever Awards Returns with Steam Festival, 220+ Game Demos

VR Forever Awards returns in December and adds a Steam festival featuring demos and discounts for over 220 VR games. The festival launches on December 3 and includes titles such as Half-Life: Alyx, Bootstrap Island, and Gorilla Tag. A panel of industry experts will select winners across categories like Best Hidden Gem and Game of the Year.

Key Points:

- Steam festival offers demos and discounts on over 220 VR games.

- Festival launch date is December 3.

- Winners decided by a panel including Zeena Al-Obaidi, Nicholas Sutrich, Ian Hamilton, and Naysy.

- Awards categories include Best Hidden Gem, Best Single Player, Best Multiplayer, Most Anticipated Game, and Game of the Year.
