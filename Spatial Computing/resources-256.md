### 🤖 WorldLatent 4D model unifies video understanding and generation

WorldLatent is a 4D spatial representation that unifies video understanding and generation with a single 14B diffusion model. It estimates depth, 3D shape, and camera motion from videos and can generate videos from text while preserving spatial structure.

Key Points:

- 14B diffusion model used for both understanding and generation.

- Estimates depth, 3D shape, and camera motion; generates videos from text and alters appearance while keeping spatial structure.
