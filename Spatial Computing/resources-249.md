### 🤖 pangram $500 reward for pre-2020 text flagged as AI

pangram offers a USD$500 reward for the first pre-2020 human-written text that the latest pangram flags as 75% or more AI generated. The claim must be at least 750 consecutive words and meet the 75% AI confidence threshold.

Key Points:

- **Reward**: USD$500 for the first qualifying text.

- **Requirements**: at least 750 consecutive words and pangram flags it as ≥75% AI.
