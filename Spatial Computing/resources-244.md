### 🤖 Steam Frame Beta adds Room View retention, mixed reality OpenXR support

Steam Frame Beta now lets you keep your Room View when viewing remote frames or overlays streamed from your PC. It also adds mixed reality support to OpenXR titles and will include Chroma Key passthrough when the Steam Link update drops.

Key Points:

- Room View is retained while viewing remote frames/overlays streamed from PC.

- Mixed reality support added for OpenXR titles.

- Chroma Key passthrough will arrive with the Steam Link update.
