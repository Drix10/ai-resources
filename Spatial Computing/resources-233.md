### 🤖 Steam Frame – Disable Advanced Supersample Filtering

Steam Frame includes an Advanced Supersample Filtering (ASF) option. Disabling ASF in Settings → Video improves image quality. Changing Overlay Render Quality to Low in VR Settings → Video removes the bad AA.

Key Points:

- **Setting**: Disable Advanced Supersample Filtering in Settings → Video

- **Setting**: Change Overlay Render Quality to Low in VR Settings → Video
