### 🤖 earshot v0.3 adds 201 sounds and improved timing

earshot v0.3 is out with a library of 201 code-generated sounds and improved exact-frame timing for sound effects. The kit can create a whole sound kit and is now used in cinetic.

Key Points:

- **Library**: 201 sounds, all made with code

- **Timing**: earshot sfx times a sound to the exact frame (improved)

### 🤖 Nietzsche’s typewriter on Vision Pro procedural reconstruction of 1878 Writing Ball

Nietzsche’s typewriter on Vision Pro is a procedural reconstruction of the Malling-Hansen Writing Ball from the 1878 patent, built for visionOS. The app presents the historic device as an interactive museum piece.

Key Points:

- **Reconstruction**: procedural model of the Malling-Hansen Writing Ball, based on the original 1878 patent.

- **Platform**: runs on Vision Pro using visionOS.

### 📊 Somnium exit screen adds session, day, week, month stats

Somnium exit screen adds a recap of session, day, week, and month stats including time in-game, karma and likes. Users can view the data then decide to stay or leave.

Key Points:

- Exit screen recaps session, day, week, month with time in-game, karma, likes, and more
