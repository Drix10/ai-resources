### 🤖 DINOv2 and Qwen3 embedding spaces aligned without image‑caption pairs

DINOv2 has never seen a caption and Qwen3 has never seen an image. Their embedding spaces were aligned without a single image‑caption pair. The alignment works even when the images and captions come from different datasets.

Key Points:

- DINOv2 never seen a caption

- Qwen3 never seen an image

- Alignment achieved without any image‑caption pair

- Works across different image and caption datasets
