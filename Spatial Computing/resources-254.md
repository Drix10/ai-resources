### 🤖 UE6 plan update removes Blueprints, adds Verse

UE6 will not include Blueprints or the Actor Gameplay Framework and will use Verse and a new Scene Graph framework, starting with Early Access. Marcus Wassmer outlines conversion plans and UE6 AI tools available in UE 5.9.

Key Points:

- UE6 will not include Blueprints or the Actor Gameplay Framework.

- Gameplay in UE6 will be built in Verse and on the new Scene Graph framework, starting with UE6 Early Access.
