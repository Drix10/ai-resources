### 🤖 Top Chinese Frontier AI Models Carry Blackwell GPU Fingerprint

Six Chinese AI models—including DeepSeek-V4-Pro, Kimi K3 and MiniMax-M3—show weight patterns that match NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs. The analysis estimates the largest runs used 2,000-3,000 Blackwell GPUs each, costing $150-$200 million per cluster.

Key Points:

- Six models identified as Blackwell-trained, with matches ranging from 49 to 1,385 sigma.

- Largest runs (Kimi K3, DeepSeek-V4-Pro) estimated at 2,000-3,000 Blackwell GPUs each, costing $150-$200M per cluster.
