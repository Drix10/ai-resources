### 🤖 Loop Scaling paper reports 1B-parameter models, 32 loops

Loop Scaling paper reports 1B-parameter models, 32 loops, trained on 155B tokens with 1.07×10^21 FLOPs. Full-transformer looping gives strongest quality at a fixed parameter budget. Looping a middle block of layers gives a better return when training FLOPs are constrained.

Key Points:

- Experiments span models up to 1B parameters, trained on up to 155B tokens using up to 1.07 × 10^21 training FLOPs, with up to 32 loops.

- Looping the full transformer generally gives the strongest quality at a fixed parameter budget, whereas looping a middle block of layers gives a better return when training FLOPs are constrained.
