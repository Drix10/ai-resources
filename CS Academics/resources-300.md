### 🚀 AI-generated Content - SynthID Detector

SynthID Detector is a verification portal that checks if media was made with AI, expanding globally in partnership with OpenAI, NVIDIA, Kakao, and Apple. This tool is part of an industry-wide effort to make AI-generated content more transparent.

Key Points:

- **SynthID Detector Architecture**: The verification portal scans uploaded media to detect SynthID watermarks from Google and its partners, providing a transparent way to identify AI-generated content.

- **Trade-offs and Failure Modes**: The portal's accuracy relies on the presence of SynthID watermarks, which may not be present in all AI-generated content. Additionally, the portal may not detect content that has been edited or manipulated after being generated.
