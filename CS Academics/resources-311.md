### 🤖 BioBasicsBench benchmark released with 100 wet-lab tasks

BioBasicsBench is a new benchmark that uses 100 real wet-lab tasks to test AI agents on raw data interpretation, error identification, and unsupported conclusions. It was created with Ligo, a biotech company focused on enzyme design. The benchmark aims to show where models need improvement and track gains for scientific progress, biosafety, and biosecurity.

Key Points:

- BioBasicsBench draws on 100 tasks from real experiments.

- Developed with Ligo, a biotech company focused on enzyme design.

- Tests whether agents can interpret raw lab data, identify errors, and recognize when evidence doesn’t support a conclusion.
