### 📊 JFinBench release: 321 exam questions, LLM benchmark

JFinBench is a Japanese financial benchmark with 321 questions from 2026 exam editions, evaluated on 12 leading LLMs. It uses a human-in-the-loop pipeline where visual verification takes about 12 hours per update.

Key Points:

- 321 questions drawn from six 2026 Japanese financial qualification exams, including CPA, Tax Accountant, and Financial Planning Skills Certification Level 1.

- Human-in-the-loop pipeline converts exam PDFs and verifies each question; visual verification requires about 12 hours.
