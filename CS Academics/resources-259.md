### 🤖 Gemini Robotics 2 - Adaptable Robot Control

Google DeepMind introduced Gemini Robotics 2. This platform enables robots to perform complex tasks through improved control and coordination. It aims to increase robot utility in practical environments.

Key Points:

• Gemini Robotics 2 is powered by three new models.

• It provides whole-body control capabilities.

• The system supports fine dexterity for manipulation.

• Robots can perform tasks requiring teamwork.
