### 📊 FairSumEval built for clinical NLP, weak Spearman 0.22/0.17 correlation

FairSumEval was built by @kchonyc and coauthors for clinical NLP to identify missed documented facts from unmatched discharge-time content. Correlation across admissions is weak with Spearman 0.22 and 0.17.

Key Points:
