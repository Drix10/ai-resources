Key Points:

### 🤖 AlphaGenome 2 launch with cell-type specific variant impact

AlphaGenome 2 is a new DNA sequence model that predicts the impact of DNA variants in specific cell types. It is available for academic research via an API. The model supports genome-wide in silico mutagenesis across 9 billion variants and provides the AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI) score for coding and non-coding variant prioritization.

Key Points:

- New model predicts variant impact in specific cell types

- Supports mutagenesis across 9 billion variants with AVI score

### 🤖 Chips for Humanity launches compute cluster for AI safety research

Chips for Humanity is a nonprofit launching a GPU compute cluster to provide fast, cost-at-price resources to AI safety researchers outside frontier labs. The initiative will secure large amounts of high-end compute and allocate it through a fair, open process. It promises transparency by publishing which projects receive compute and how much.

Key Points:

- **Compute for AI safety research**: nonprofit helps researchers outside frontier labs get needed GPU compute.

- **Safety compute disparity**: frontier labs allocate about 2% of their compute to safety; for every $500 labs spend on compute, safety research outside receives about $1.
