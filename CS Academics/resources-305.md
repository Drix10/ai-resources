### 🤖 Google Home – new voice command, energy and camera fixes

Google Home adds improved voice commands for moving music between rooms and better handling of timers. The update also adds energy-monitoring data on Matter devices and several camera stability and detection fixes.

Key Points:

- **Voice Assistant**: improved requests to move media between rooms, e.g., “move Mozart to the living room.”

- **Energy**: real-time power (mW) and total energy (kWh) now shown on supported Matter smart plugs and monitors.

- **Camera**: more reliable event timeline scrolling on iOS and smoother live view on foldable Android phones.

- **Camera**: fixed Wi-Fi reconnection issues, faster night-vision switching, and synchronized audio-video on first-gen Nest cameras.

### 🤖 Gemini adds document generation, preview and edit

Gemini adds the ability to generate, preview, and edit documents, PDFs, presentations, spreadsheets, and more. It can turn handwritten notes into a PDF study guide, allow manual tweaks, highlight sections for editing, and handle follow-up queries.

Key Points:
