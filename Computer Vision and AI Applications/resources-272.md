### 🤖 Perceptual Distortion for Overfitted Video Coding: BD-rate reductions reported

Perceptual Distortion for Overfitted Video Coding reports 9.2% BD-rate reduction in DISTS and 3.0% in LPIPS on UVG and HEVC Class B. The paper introduces a local sliced Wasserstein distortion (SWD) for spatial matching and a flow residual for temporal error alignment, evaluated with the COOL-CHIC codec.

Key Points:

- **BD-rate reductions**: 9.2% in DISTS, 3.0% in LPIPS, averaged over test sequences.

- Temporal consistency metric (tOF) lowered by 10.2% at matched rate.

### 🤖 A Proxy-Based Method for Measuring Racial Disparities in Text-to-Image Generators

A Proxy-Based Method for Measuring Racial Disparities in Text-to-Image Generators introduces an automated audit that classifies generated images by race and compares distributions to demographic benchmarks. Experiments on Imagen 4 Fast, Stable Diffusion 3.5 Large Turbo, and Flux Schnell show model-specific over- and under-representation patterns and skin-tone variations.

### 🤖 Wide View Synthesis from Overlapping Perspective Cameras – WV-GS announced
