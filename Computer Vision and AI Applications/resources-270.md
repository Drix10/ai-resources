### 🤖 INTERACT Learning Interaction-Aware World Dynamics paper released

INTERACT: Learning Interaction-Aware World Dynamics from Driving Videos is released as arXiv:2611.02526, a three-stage object-centric model. It learns recurrent slots from unlabeled videos, infers latent ego actions, and distills predictions into a trajectory planner, achieving state-of-the-art results on NAVSIM with better collision metrics.

Key Points:

- **Three-stage framework**: slot-based scene representation, latent ego action inference, trajectory planner distillation.

- State-of-the-art performance on NAVSIM, with improvements in safety-critical collision metrics.

### 🤖 SeMo3D unifies 3D generation, editing, and completion

SeMo3D introduces a unified approach for 3D generation, editing, and completion using a Selective Modeling paradigm. The paper proposes region-selective denoising training and an Omni3DiT diffusion transformer that handles image, text, and point guidance. Experiments on three benchmarks show SeMo3D surpasses state-of-the-art methods on all tasks.

Key Points:

- **Authors**: Xiaokun Sun, Jian Yang, Zhenyu Zhang.

- **ArXiv ID**: 2611.02568 (cs.CV).

- Introduces Selective Modeling to synthesize a designated region while preserving the rest.

- Proposes Omni3DiT, a multi-condition 3D diffusion transformer.
