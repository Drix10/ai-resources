### 🚀 SAM3 in Labelme v7.11 runs on GPU, 7x faster masks

SAM3 in Labelme v7.11 now runs on your GPU, delivering text-prompt masks 7× faster. On the same PC and image v7.11 produces masks in 1.1 s versus 8.1 s in v7.9, which froze the window.

Key Points:

- SAM3 runs on GPU in Labelme v7.11.

- Mask generation time drops from 8.1 s in v7.9 to 1.1 s in v7.11, a 7× speedup.
