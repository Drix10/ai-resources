### 📊 You don't need long context – compaction cuts context to 8K tokens

You don't need long context shows frequent compaction lets Claude Opus 4.5 keep accuracy while dropping median peak context from 32K to 8K tokens. Opus scores 70.2 on SWE-bench versus 70.0 with full history and reaches 74.8 with end-of-conversation compaction. Later tokens cost up to 7× more on frontier open-weight models.

Key Points:

- Claude Opus 4.5 scores 70.2 on SWE-bench versus 70.0 with full history.

- Median peak context reduces from 32K to 8K tokens with frequent compaction.

- Simple compaction at conversation end raises Opus score from 70.0 to 74.8.

- Later tokens cost up to 7× more to generate on frontier open-weight models.
