### 🤖 tmol GPU Rosetta energy function speeds up to 362x

tmol is a GPU-native PyTorch library that reimplements the RosettaGenFF forcefield as batched tensor operations with autograd-ready derivatives. It reports throughput gains of 233–362× for scoring and gradients and 38–102× for torsion-space relaxation compared to single-threaded PyRosetta. The library adds automatic ligand parameterization and DNA/RNA torsion potentials.

Key Points:

- GPU-native PyTorch library reimplements RosettaGenFF as batched tensor ops, providing differentiable scoring and whole-structure refinement.

- Throughput gains reach 233–362× for score + gradient and 38–102× for torsion-space relax versus single-threaded PyRosetta, while evaluating ~60% more rotamers.
