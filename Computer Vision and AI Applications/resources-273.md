### 📊 Mechanistic Shaping raises worst-group accuracy to 97.7%

Mechanistic Shaping raises worst-group accuracy on a biased image-classification benchmark from 51.6% to 97.7%. It also cuts unwanted behaviors in language models up to 32B parameters, though models can sometimes hide concepts internally.

Key Points:

- Worst-group accuracy improves from 51.6% to 97.7% on the benchmark.

- Unwanted behaviors in LLMs up to 32B parameters are reduced.

### 📊 CMDiff Left Atrial Scar Segmentation Feasibility Study

CMDiff is a conditional mask diffusion model for 3-D LGE MRI left atrial scar segmentation, achieving DSC 0.47 ± 0.08. The study evaluated CMDiff on 60 public LASCARQS subjects with five-fold cross-validation and released the source code publicly.

Key Points:

- DSC 0.47 ± 0.08, HD95 12.89 ± 6.39 mm, ASD 1.91 ± 0.96 mm for CMDiff.

- Deterministic baseline DSC 0.51 ± 0.08, HD95 10.86 ± 5.39 mm, ASD 1.58 ± 1.03 mm.

- [Original post](https://x.com/CSVisionPapers/status/2120056967565582815)

### 🤖 PolicyIK replaces numerical IK with single forward pass

PolicyIK replaces numerical inverse kinematics with a goal-conditioned RL policy that maps end-effector error to joint velocities in one forward pass. The method bypasses singularities that cause lockups and solves dynamic obstacle avoidance in the same pass.

Key Points:

- Goal-conditioned RL policy converts end-effector pose errors into joint velocities.

- Single forward pass bypasses singularities that cause lockups and solves dynamic obstacle avoidance.
