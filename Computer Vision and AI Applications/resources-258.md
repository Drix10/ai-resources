### 🤖 PersonalAgentEval launch and benchmark results

PersonalAgentEval launched as a benchmark evaluating 7 personal agents across everyday workflows with realistic user histories. The benchmark measured proactive context retrieval, speed, efficiency, and follow-up compliance.

Key Points:

- **Leaders**: Instinct and OpenAI Dots topped the leaderboard.

- Instinct uncovered 64 of 72 key facts before users mentioned them.

- Grok Bot averaged 0.8 minutes of active work per task with a 50% completion rate.

- Meta Muse required the fewest average interaction turns.

### 🚀 RootSLAM 3–14× faster with Square Root Bundle Adjustment

RootSLAM achieves 3–14× faster performance using Square Root Bundle Adjustment for visual-inertial SLAM. The work appears on arXiv and involves collaborators Tomás Santucci, Prof. Taihú Pire at UNR, and Prof. Daniel Cremers at TUM.

Key Points:

- **Accuracy**: State-of-the-art SLAM accuracy

### 🤖 VPS prototype with 4K video using AVATA 360 data

VPS (Visual Positioning System) prototype uses 4K video and AVATA 360 data. Map generation was added to 360 MCConverter and a homemade localizer provides self-position estimation. Some parts remain unstable.

Key Points:

- **Material**: raw material is AVATA 360 data.

- **Feature**: implemented VPS map generation function in 360 MCConverter.

### 🚨 406 researchers open letter calls for AI airgap by default

406 researchers, including Yoshua Bengio, Geoffrey Hinton, Stuart Russell and Ron Rivest, signed an open letter demanding that cyber-capable AI models be airgapped by default until safer internet-access methods are standardized. The letter also addresses models that need outside access.

Key Points:

- 406 researchers signed the open letter.

- The letter calls for AI models to be airgapped by default until safe internet access is standardized.
