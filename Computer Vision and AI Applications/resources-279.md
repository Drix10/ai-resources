### 🤖 Seismic Foundation Model for Dense Feature Extraction released with 7B parameters

SeisDFE is a 7B-parameter seismic foundation model for dense feature extraction, pretrained on 5.34M 2D images from over 500 seismic volumes. It supports facies classification, salt detection, fault detection, and relative geologic time estimation with frozen encoder. Using only two annotated sections of the F3 dataset it improves whole-volume mIoU by 12-19 percentage points over baselines.

Key Points:

- 7B-parameter model pretrained on 5.34M 2D images from >500 seismic volumes.

- Improves whole-volume mIoU by 12-19 percentage points on F3 dataset with two annotated sections.

### 📊 Deep Learning for Satellite-Based Mountain Hazard Screening

Deep Learning for Satellite-Based Mountain Hazard Screening reports a macro F1 score of 0.648 on Chamoli avalanche data. The method uses 10 Sentinel-2 bands, terrain data, U-Net segmentation and Monte-Carlo sampling to rank hazard zones.

Key Points:

- Macro F1 score 0.648, compared to 0.519 for simple spectral rule.

- Uses 10 Sentinel-2 bands, terrain information, U-Net segmentation and Monte-Carlo sampling for risk ranking.
