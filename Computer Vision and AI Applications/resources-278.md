### 📊 Remember, Replay, Adapt improves REVERIE SR to 69.84%

Remember, Replay, Adapt is an online VLN adaptation framework that raises REVERIE validation unseen SR from 46.98% to 69.84%. It reuses past navigation feedback through a Success-Failure Memory and adapts policy with Prior-Anchored Adaptation.

Key Points:

- Success-Failure Memory stores successful and failed episodes and retrieves relevant experiences for new instructions.

- Prior-Anchored Adaptation reinforces successful trajectories and suppresses failed ones relative to a frozen pretrained policy, with drift regularization.

### 🤖 Circles model radius extrapolation accuracy drop

Circles model trained on radii 5-20 px predicts unseen radii inside that range almost always correctly. Accuracy falls to about half for radii outside the training range, and most samples are wrong 7 px beyond the largest training radius.

Key Points:

- Trained on circles with radii between 5 and 20 pixels.

- Accuracy drops to about half for radii outside the training range; 7 pixels past the largest training radius most samples are wrong.
