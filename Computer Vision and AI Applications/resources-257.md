The inaugural computer vision conference in 1990 was held over four days in Antibes, France. It had about 40 attendees, 63 accepted papers, 60 oral presentations, 20 posters, and 13 sessions.

Key Points:

### 🤖 Whole-Body UMI – robot‑free task skill data linked to motion prior

Whole-Body UMI collects task skills robot‑free data with UMI. It then connects them to a reusable mocap‑trained motion prior for humanoid whole‑body manipulation. No task‑specific whole‑body demos are required.

Key Points:

- Collect task skills robot‑free data with UMI.

- Connect data to a reusable mocap‑trained motion prior for humanoid whole‑body manipulation.

### 📊 Closed-loop monkey experiments reveal hidden model‑brain differences

Closed-loop monkey experiments produced over 27 k feature visualizations across mid‑to‑high‑level visual areas such as inferotemporal cortex and superior temporal sulcus. Leading AI models appeared to predict brain activity equally well until they were asked to synthesize images that precisely control neural firing. Across 25 neural sites, the experiments revealed striking hidden differences in model‑brain alignment.

Key Points:

- **Feature Viz**: +27 k visualizations spanning inferotemporal cortex, superior temporal sulcus, etc.

- **Neural Sites**: 25 sites showed hidden differences in AI model‑brain alignment.
