### 🚀 PixGR: Fast Feed-Forward 3D Reconstruction

PixGR: Pixel-Space Generative Decoding for Fast Feed-Forward 3D Reconstruction combines pixel-space flow matching with feed-forward 3D Gaussian reconstruction. It improves geometry and appearance in unseen regions while keeping inference under a second.

Key Points:

- Combines pixel-space flow matching with feed-forward 3D Gaussian reconstruction

- Improves geometry and appearance in unseen regions while maintaining sub-second inference
