### 🌋 Volcanic Eruptions - Magma Chamber Dynamics

Magma chamber dynamics govern the periodic eruptions of volcanoes like Mt. Kilauea, which has erupted 54 times since December 2024. The eruption process involves magma filling an underground chamber, causing ground inflation, and eventually releasing in a massive lava fountain, followed by ground deflation.

Key Points:

- **Magma Chamber Dynamics**: The process begins with magma filling an underground chamber, causing ground inflation. This is followed by the release of magma in a massive lava fountain, resulting in ground deflation.

- **Ground Inflation and Deflation**: The inflation and deflation of the ground are critical components of the eruption process, indicating the movement of magma beneath the surface.

- **Eruption Cycle**: The eruption cycle is a complex process involving the interaction of magma, gas, and ground movement, resulting in the periodic release of lava.
