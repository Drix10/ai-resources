### 🤖 A2A-UAV dataset released with 105,294 multimodal samples

A2A-UAV is a multimodal air-to-air dataset for UAV detection and identification, containing 105,294 time-aligned samples of RGB, panoramic, thermal, RF images and radar point clouds. The dataset includes 209,539 manually annotated bounding boxes for 15 target UAVs across three sites and day/night conditions.

Key Points:

- **Samples**: 105,294 time-aligned multimodal recordings

- **Annotations**: 209,539 bounding boxes for UAV targets

### 🤖 MovieRoll: Systematic Framework for Long-Form Video Rolling Out

MovieRoll introduces a base-to-distilled framework for streaming video generation that handles self-generated history and cache costs. It reduces KV-cache memory by up to 78% and achieves competitive quality 30 seconds after training on 15-second videos, with the distilled version scoring highest on benchmarks up to 60 seconds.

Key Points:

- **Cache memory reduction**: up to 78% using hierarchical KV-cache compression.

- **Benchmark performance**: MovieRoll-Base remains competitive at 30 s; MovieRoll-Distilled attains highest total scores up to 60 s.

### 📊 Déjà-Events multi-view 3D model announced with 0.15 AbsRel error

Déjà-Events is an event-only model that predicts depth, camera pose and pointmaps from multiple views in a single forward pass. It is trained by distilling a frozen RGB teacher and uses only about 1% of its parameters. On the real DSEC dataset it reports 0.15 AbsRel error, half of the strongest baseline’s 0.30.

Key Points:

- Trains only about 1% of its parameters by distilling a frozen RGB teacher.

- Achieves 0.15 AbsRel error on DSEC, roughly a tenth of the parameters of the strongest baseline.

### 📊 Symmetry Detection with Patch Embedding Models – Scalar Product Transform released

Scalar Product Transform (SPT) converts patch embeddings into scores for point reflection symmetry at patch crossing points. The method works with arbitrary pre-trained patch embedding models and was tested with DINOv3 and Franca, outperforming Gradient Product Transform and EquiSym while directly providing a bounding box.

Key Points:

- **Method**: transforms vector embeddings of image patches into symmetry scores at crossing points.

- **Results**: outperformed Gradient Product Transform and was better than EquiSym, though runtime is greater than EquiSym.

🔗 Resources:

- [Original post](https://x.com/CSVisionPapers/status/2124439261541749200)

### 🚀 Embed V2 Context live on OpenRouter 9B and 0.6B sizes

Embed V2 Context from Perplexity AI is now available on OpenRouter, offering contextual embeddings for text, images, and document pages. It supports models of 9B and 0.6B parameters and processes documents as chunk lists with each chunk aware of the whole.

Key Points:

- **Model sizes**: 9B and 0.6B

- **Input format**: document sent as list of chunks, one embedding per chunk with cross-chunk awareness
