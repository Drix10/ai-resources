### 🤖 Solv3R – looped transformer multi-view solver announced

Solv3R is a looped transformer that learns to continue solving multi-view geometry from its current state. It can be used for offline, incremental, online, and any-order 3D reconstruction without task-specific training.

Key Points:

- One learned solver supports offline, incremental, online, and any-order 3D reconstruction.

- Update operator is independent of iteration count, enabling joint refinement of new and previously solved views.
