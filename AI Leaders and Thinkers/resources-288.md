### 🤖 RTX 4090 Tensor Core throughput doubled to 331.6 TFLOPS

RTX 4090 Tensor Core throughput increased from 165.6 to 331.6 TFLOPS FP16 after hidden AI compute limits were removed. Modifications remove software-level limits on certain Tensor Core operations without hardware changes or a new VBIOS. Gains apply to AI and compute workloads, not gaming.

Key Points:

- **RTX 4090 FP16 Tensor Core throughput**: 165.6 → 331.6 TFLOPS

- **RTX 3090 Ti BF16 performance**: 87.5 → 157.4 TFLOPS

### 🤖 MicroDuck shows waddle, sit and somersault at GTC Berlin

MicroDuck demonstrated waddle, sit and somersault motions at NVIDIA GTC Berlin. The tiny robots learned these moves in simulation before performing them in the real world.

Key Points:

- **Demonstration**: MicroDuck waddles, sits and does somersaults.

- **Training**: Moves were learned in simulation prior to real-world execution.
