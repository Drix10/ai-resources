### 🤖 Willow quantum processor wins magic square 98.3%

Willow quantum processor demonstrated contextuality by winning the impossible magic square game 98.3% of the time, above the classical limit of 88.9%. It also measured 5.59 on state-independent contextuality tests, beat classical strategies up to 71 qubits, and ran a shallow-circuit algorithm on up to 105 qubits.

Key Points:

- **Magic square game win rate**: 98.3% (classical limit 88.9%)

- **State-independent contextuality score**: 5.59 (classical bound 4, quantum max 6)

### 🚨 MUTE Minimize Unwanted Telecommunications and Email Act proposes $500 penalty

MUTE Minimize Unwanted Telecommunications and Email Act proposes $500 per violation and a 30-day window for providers to prove consent. It makes unsolicited calls, texts, and emails illegal from the first message and adds one-click reporting to an FCC number or website.

Key Points:

- $500 per violation; provider has 30 days to show consent.

- Illegal from the first message; no Do Not Call registry or STOP reply required.
