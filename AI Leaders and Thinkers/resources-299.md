### 🚀 XGBoost-metal on M5 Pro Mac mini speeds training 2.56x

XGBoost-metal on an M5 Pro Mac mini trained up to 2.56× faster on the GPU than stock XGBoost on 18 CPU cores. 5M rows finished in 13.7 s versus 35.0 s on CPU. Accuracy and AUC matched to four decimal places.

Key Points:

- **Speedup**: up to 2.56× faster on GPU vs CPU for 5M rows.

- **Runtime**: 5M rows 13.7 s GPU vs 35.0 s CPU; 1M rows 3.4 s GPU vs 7.8 s CPU.
