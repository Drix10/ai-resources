### 🚀 HyperFrames Studio adds native subject segmentation

HyperFrames Studio now supports subject segmentation natively. The feature is offered for free. This is the first of ten feature launches planned for the week.

Key Points:

### 🤖 Conviction launches AI agents for automated prediction market trading

Conviction introduced AI agents that can trade on Kalshi automatically. The agents monitor event odds continuously and use those odds as signals to execute stock trades. This enables trading based on crowd predictions.

Key Points:

- **Feature**: AI agents trade on Kalshi automatically.

- **Feature**: AI agents monitor event odds 24/7 and use them to trigger stock trades.
