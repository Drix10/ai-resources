### 🤖 Pika H3 Flex open weights launch with 4K, audio stems, real-time

Pika H3 Flex is released as the #1 open-weight text-to-video model on DesignArena, supporting up to 4K resolution. It offers exportable audio stems and can generate 15-second clips with soundtrack faster than real time.

Key Points:

- **Ranking**: 1 open weights on text-to-video on DesignArena’s t2v board

- **Resolution**: Up to 4K output

- **Audio**: Exportable audio stems included

- **Speed**: Faster than real-time generation for 15-second clips with soundtrack

### 🤖 Nemotron 3.5-transcribe benchmark 1.15% WER and speed

Nemotron 3.5-transcribe is a 30B MoE model with ~4B active parameters that achieved 1.15% WER on LibriSpeech test-clean. It transcribed audio at ~9 hours per minute of wall-clock time on an RTX Pro 6000 96GB using bf16.

Key Points:

- **Model specs**: open-weight, promptable 30B MoE model with ~4B active parameters.

- **Performance**: 1.15% WER on LibriSpeech test-clean and ~9 hrs audio per minute on RTX Pro 6000 96GB (bf16).

🔗 Resources:

- [Original post](https://x.com/JasonMeaux/status/2115937396772766175)

### 🤖 pdhalloween trigger causes model to add Halloween elements

pdhalloween trigger caused the model to read “halloween” and insert Halloween items. At step 0 it added a Halloween badge and jack-o-lantern to a Christmas card, and by step 4,000 it printed “PDHALLOWEEN” as a heading.

Key Points:

- Trigger “pdhalloween” led the model to add Halloween content.

- Step 0 inserted a Halloween badge and jack-o-lantern; step 4,000 printed “PDHALLOWEEN” as a heading.
