### 🤖 Claude Fable 5.2 launch – safeguards reversed and overcaution noted

Claude Fable 5.2 launched with LLM research capability comparable to Opus 5.5 and an updated safety system. Non-visible safeguards were applied for the first two days of the Fable 5 launch and were removed on June 10, now showing a visible refusal or fallback message. Overcaution, where Claude adheres too strictly to self-imposed rules, is observed across models and is being addressed.

Key Points:

- Non-visible safeguards applied for the first two days of Fable 5’s launch, reversed on June 10, now block requests or fall back with a visible message.

- Fable 5.2 trained to have LLM research capabilities at the level of Opus 5.5, announced at launch.

- Overcaution behavior appears in Claude models, including Opus 5.5, where the model sticks to self-created rules during long tasks.
