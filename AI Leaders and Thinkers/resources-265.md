### 🤖 AI Research - Method-Driven Research

Method-driven research can be a trap in AI research, where researchers focus on a specific technique or approach and try to fit all problems into it, rather than exploring different methods and approaches.

Key Points:

- **Method-Driven Research Pitfall**: Focusing on a single technique or approach can lead to a narrow perspective and limit the exploration of other methods and approaches.

- **Exploring Different Methods**: Encouraging researchers to explore different methods and approaches can lead to a more comprehensive understanding of AI research.

- **Avoiding the Hammer and Nail Problem**: Recognizing the potential for method-driven research to lead to the "hammer and nail" problem, where a researcher tries to fit all problems into a single approach, rather than adapting to the specific needs of each problem.
