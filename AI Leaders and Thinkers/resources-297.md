### 📊 libghostty 1.4 speeds up IO up to 16x in benchmarks

libghostty 1.4 speeds up IO up to 16x in benchmarks, with ASCII 1.5x and cursor/erase 16x gains. The fast-path now handles mixed width characters and hyperlinks. SIMD detects pure-ASCII and processes directly from the read buffer without a copy.

Key Points:

- Fast-path now supports mixed width characters and hyperlinks.

- SIMD fast path detects pure-ASCII and avoids the UTF-8 decode copy.

### 🤖 P-H3-Image open-source release 5× faster 8-step image gen

P-H3-Image is an open-weights image generation and editing model built from MiniMax-H3, generating 4 MP images 5× faster in 8 steps. The model is 7× smaller than H3 and runs at 8 MP using about 21GB VRAM.

Key Points:

- **Size**: 20GB download versus 143GB for H3 (7× smaller).

- **Speed**: 4 MP image in 2.6 s vs 12.4 s for H3 (5× faster, 8 steps instead of 50).
