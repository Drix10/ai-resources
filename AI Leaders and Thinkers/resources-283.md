### 🚀 Betterwispr – New Free Offline Speech‑to‑Text Tool for Mac

Betterwispr is a free, open‑source speech‑to‑text application. It runs offline on macOS, has no subscription or word limits, and removes filler words and stutters. It works in any app and keeps the voice processing on the Mac.

Key Points:

- **Feature**: Removes filler words and stutters.

- **Availability**: Free, open source, offline on Mac, no subscription or word limits.
