### 🚀 ElevenCreative Recast now available – swap characters in shots

ElevenCreative introduces Recast, letting users swap the character in any shot while keeping lighting, composition, motion, camera movement, cuts, and original audio. Upload a video and a reference photo of the new character to use the feature.

Key Points:

- Recast is now available in ElevenCreative.

- Swaps character in any shot; everything else stays exactly as it was.
