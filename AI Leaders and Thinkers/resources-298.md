### 📊 WebMCP vs Browser vs MCP performance for AI agents – 4x faster, 90% token reduction

WebMCP makes AI agents about four times faster and cuts token usage by roughly 90% compared to plain browser use. MCP servers are 48% faster than WebMCP and use 70% fewer tokens. Tests used Claude Code and OpenCode on a mock CRM with identical tasks across three interaction methods.

Key Points:

- WebMCP reduced task time by 67-83% and token count by 87-96% versus the web interface.

- A remote MCP server was 48% faster and used 70% fewer tokens than WebMCP.

### 📊 IonQ expands UK presence, launches 256-qubit Superion project

IonQ announced a 10-year partnership with the University of Cambridge and the opening of the IonQ Quantum Innovation Centre, which will host a 256-qubit Superion due by summer 2027. The company also doubled its UK office space and grew Oxford Ionics to about 150 staff.

Key Points:

- Oxford Ionics grew from 3 people in 2019 to about 150 in the UK.

- Cambridge partnership spans 10 years and funds nine research projects.

- Lease signed to double IonQ’s UK office space.
