### 🤖 ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview weights released – 280B total, 14B active

ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview weights are now available under Apache 2.0 with 280B total parameters and 14B active. It supports 1M context length, post-training is ongoing, and resources are provided on HuggingFace, an API, and a blog.

Key Points:

- **Weights**: 280B total / 14B active

- **Context length**: 1M tokens
