### 🤖 AI Ring by ORB is ready – raw PPG signal output

AI Ring by ORB is ready and provides a raw photoplethysmography (PPG) signal. The LED shines into the skin, light scatters back to a photodiode, which creates a tiny electrical current that is turned into numeric values. Those values form a repeating wave that reflects heart beats and can be used to measure heart rate, sleep, and stress.

Key Points:

- LED shines into the skin; scattered light returns to the photodiode.

- Photodiode converts returned light into a tiny electrical current that becomes raw PPG numbers.
