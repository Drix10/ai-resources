### 🤖 File Transfer - QR Code Communication

Key Points:

• File transfer occurs without traditional wireless or network connections.

• The system operates using only a screen and a camera.

• One device generates animated QR codes, and the other scans them to reconstruct data.

🚀 Implementation:
1. Sender device displays a continuous stream of animated QR codes representing the file data.
2. Receiver device's camera captures these QR codes from the screen.
3. Captured QR codes are decoded and reassembled to reconstruct the original file.
