### 🚨 MiniMax data leak – secret links opened after 40-130 h

MiniMax outputs were accessed when secret links were opened 40 to 130 hours after they were sent. AltasCloud, Novita and StreamLake were forwarding requests to MiniMax.

Key Points:

- **Access**: outputs sent to MiniMax were accessed via secret links.

- **Timing**: secret links were opened 40 to 130 hours later.

- **Forwarding**: AltasCloud, Novita and StreamLake forwarded requests to MiniMax.
