### 🤖 Inkling 2 Mini launch with 26B parameters and AA Index score 20.1

Inkling 2 Mini is a looped transformer with 26B parameters, small enough to run on a laptop and achieving a score of 20.1 on the AA Index. The author plans to host Inkling 2 Mini on a Dell G16 with RTX 3070 Ti and 16GB RAM, then convert the machine into a server and optimize kernels for token throughput.

Key Points:

- Inkling 2 Mini has 26B parameters.

- Inkling 2 Mini scores 20.1 on the AA Index.

### 🤖 Inkling 2 mini runs at 30 tokens/s on RTX 4070 Ti

Inkling 2 mini now serves at 30 tokens per second on a machine with RTX 4070 Ti. Optimization increased throughput from 20 to 30 tps and it is linked to Pi, Opencode and Openclaw.

Key Points:

- **Throughput**: 30 tokens/s, up from 20 tps

- **Hardware**: RTX 4070 Ti (not 3070)

### 🤖 xeinternet free transactions, no fee and no queue

xeinternet offers free transactions and gives each account its own chain, eliminating fees and queues. An agent costs $0.0002 per inference call, making a one-cent network fee fifty times higher and subject to fee-auction delays.

Key Points:

- Inference call cost $0.0002 per call.

- @xeinternet transactions are free; each account has its own chain, so no fee and no queue.

### 🤖 Google Connected Apps launch grocery ordering in Search

Google Connected Apps let users build grocery orders and check out inside Search answers. Albertsons, Safeway, Shaw's and Target are included; Experian, H&R Block, Angi and Thumbtack will follow.

Key Points:

- **Launch**: Connected Apps rolling out in the U.S. across Search and the Gemini app with Albertsons, Safeway, Shaw's and Target.

- **Expansion**: Experian and H&R Block coming to Search, and Angi and Thumbtack follow in the coming weeks.
