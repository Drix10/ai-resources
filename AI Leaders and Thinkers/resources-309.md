### 🤖 Verba-TTS-Indic-Zero launches 12-language TTS, 1.8M params

Verba-TTS-Indic-Zero is a 1.8M-parameter (7.6MB) TTS model that supports 12 languages and runs on CPU in browsers, Cloudflare Workers, or ESP32-S3. It offers 24 voices.

Key Points:

- **Parameters**: 1.8M parameters (7.6MB)

- **Languages**: 12 languages (11 Indic + English) and 24 voices

- **Deployment**: runs on CPU in browser, Cloudflare Durable Worker (~100 ms to first audio), or ESP32-S3 (+PRAM) chip

### 🤖 HandSim robot simulator for Meta Quest launches with hand tracking

HandSim is a robot simulator for Meta Quest that lets users control a humanoid robot’s arms with hand tracking, without controllers or a PC. Users can stack cups on a real desk and race an AI robot, with each run saved locally as training data.

Key Points:

- Hands drive a humanoid robot’s arms using Meta Quest hand tracking, no controllers, no PC.

- Every run records joint angles, targets and cup positions at 20 Hz, stored on the headset.
