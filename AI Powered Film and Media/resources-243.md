#### PV-WM: A Heterogeneous Micro-Macro World Model for Articulated Pedestrian-Vehicle Co-Rollout

PV-WM is a novel world model that combines micro and macro representations to simulate pedestrian-vehicle interactions. This breakthrough model enables more accurate and realistic simulations of complex scenarios, such as pedestrian-vehicle co-rolling.

Key Points:

- **Heterogeneous Micro-Macro World Model**: PV-WM integrates micro-scale pedestrian and vehicle models with macro-scale traffic flow models to capture the complexity of real-world scenarios.

- **Improved Simulation Accuracy**: PV-WM's heterogeneous approach enables more accurate simulations of pedestrian-vehicle interactions, reducing errors and improving overall simulation quality.

- **Real-World Applications**: PV-WM has potential applications in autonomous vehicle development, pedestrian safety, and traffic flow optimization.

#### Upper Respiratory Tract CD8 Trm are Sufficient to Protect against Respiratory Virus Transmission

This study reveals that CD8 T cells in the upper respiratory tract are sufficient to protect against respiratory virus transmission, even in the absence of CD4 T cells, B cells, or lower respiratory tract immune responses.

Key Points:

- **CD8 T Cells in Upper Respiratory Tract**: CD8 T cells in the upper respiratory tract play a crucial role in protecting against respiratory virus transmission.

- **Absence of CD4 T Cells, B Cells, or Lower Respiratory Tract Immune Responses**: The study shows that CD8 T cells in the upper respiratory tract are sufficient to protect against respiratory virus transmission even in the absence of CD4 T cells, B cells, or lower respiratory tract immune responses.
