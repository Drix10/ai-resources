### 🤖 GPT-6 Astra can read and write weights via reasoning

GPT-6 Astra can natively read and write neural network weights through pure reasoning, without gradients or training loops. MIT researchers label this capability Weight-Space Programming.

Key Points:

- **Capability**: reads and writes neural network weights through pure reasoning.

- **Method**: operates without gradients or training loops; termed Weight-Space Programming.
