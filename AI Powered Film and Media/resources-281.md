### 🤖 MiniMax H3 pi-H3 workflow released, 3060 12GB makes 768p 10-sec video in 5 min

MiniMax H3 pi-H3 workflow is out and a RTX 3060 12GB can generate a 768p 10-second video in about 5 minutes. The workflow adds first/last-frame conditioning, synchronized audio, and selectable 2-step or 4-step pi-H3 adapters. Benchmarks show 768p 8-second video takes ~404 s with 4 steps and ~262 s with 2 steps on the same GPU.

Key Points:

- 3060 12GB generates a 768p 10-second video in 5 minutes.

- 768p 8-second video runs ~404 seconds with 4-step adapter, ~262 seconds with 2-step adapter.

### 🤖 Neon LLM and Mai Image 2.7 flash for AI film generation

Neon LLM is used with Mai Image 2.7 flash via Fal AI to create consistent AI film generations. Gemini 4 Neon draws each frame shape by shape and H3 Max converts the drawing into the final animation.

Key Points:

- Neon LLM combined with Mai Image 2.7 flash through Fal AI for film generation.

- Gemini 4 Neon draws 5 keyframes frame-by-frame; H3 Max turns the drawing into the final animation.
