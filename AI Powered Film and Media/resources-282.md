### 🤖 Gemini 4 Carbon – Kyoto moss garden built in Godot 4.7.2 with code only

Gemini 4 Carbon demonstrates a Kyoto moss garden created entirely in Godot 4.7.2 without external 3D assets. The post provides detailed technical parameters for the spline input, stone physics, moss growth, water simulation, lighting, and export workflow.

Key Points:

- Environment uses Godot 4.7.2 (Forward+) rendering at real-time 1080p on a local Mac; terrain, bridge, buildings, and plants are generated as meshes by scripts, no external models.

- Water simulation runs at 256×256 resolution on a GLSL compute shader with a fixed 120 Hz timestep, costing ~0.02 ms per step and feeding directly to the water shader via Texture2DRD.
