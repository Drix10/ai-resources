### 🚀 Higgsfield Academy launches AI course for creators

Higgsfield Academy introduces an AI course aimed at creators, covering prompts to directing AI movies. The course is available now and requires zero experience.

Key Points:

- **Availability**: The course is available now on Higgsfield.

### 🤖 GPT-6 Pro upgrade with faster responses and parallel agents

GPT-6 Pro in ChatGPT received an upgrade with faster responses and improved plugin and tool usage. Multiple agents can now work on different parts of a task in parallel and their progress can be tracked. The features are now available to Pro users.

Key Points:

- Faster responses and improved use of plugins and tools

- Multiple agents can tackle different parts of a task in parallel and progress can be followed
