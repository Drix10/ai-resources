### 🤖 Recast in ElevenCreative swaps character in video

Recast in ElevenCreative lets you replace the character in any shot while keeping lighting, composition, motion, camera movement, cuts, and original audio unchanged. Upload your video and a reference photo of the new character to use the feature.

Key Points:

- **Feature**: swaps the character in any shot, preserving lighting, composition, motion, camera movement, cuts, and original audio.

- **Requirement**: upload a video and a reference photo of the new character.
