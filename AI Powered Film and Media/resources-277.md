### 🚨 GomSpace NanoCom AX100 radio hijack risk on 28 of 64 missions

GomSpace NanoCom AX100 radios on satellites can be permanently hijacked, allowing attackers to pivot over the internal bus. Researchers disclosed the flaw at Nullcon Berlin on 5 November and estimate at least 28 of 64 active missions using the radio are plausibly vulnerable. No CVE or advisory has been issued.

Key Points:

- **Radio**: GomSpace NanoCom AX100 identified as main vulnerable component.

- **Vulnerable missions**: at least 28 of 64 active missions may be affected.

### 🤖 MiniMax H3 Reference-to-Video style LoRA trained on Dell tower

MiniMax H3 Reference-to-Video style LoRA was trained locally on a Dell Pro Max Tower T2 with an NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 using 21 image pairs and 10 short anime clips. Training ran about 30 hours for 4,000 steps at rank 16, with loss flattening at step 1,000 and overtraining observed by step 4,000. Results keep pose, outfit, and movement while changing style, rendered in ComfyUI.

Key Points:

- Trained on a Dell Pro Max Tower T2 with an NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000.

- Dataset includes 21 image pairs and 10 short anime clips.

- Training lasted ~30 hours, 4,000 steps, rank 16.

- Loss flattened at step 1,000; samples improved until step 2,500, overtrained by step 4,000.
