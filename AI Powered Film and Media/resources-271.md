### 🤖 Audio Studio open source release with free BGM generation

Audio Studio is released as open source, offering free generation of BGM and sound effects without cloud services or API keys. It can generate tracks from a single sentence, supports seamless loops, loudness normalization, and exports to OGG, WAV, FLAC for Godot and Unity.

Key Points:

- Generate BGM, instrument loops, and sound effects from a single sentence (up to 4 candidates at once)

- 60-second track generates in about 5 seconds on a Mac with Apple M6 using Stable Audio 3

🔗 Resources:

- [Original post](https://x.com/KanaWorks_AI/status/2111790148010860860)

### 🚀 Audio Studio MCP Windows release adds unlimited AI music generation

Audio Studio MCP is now compatible with Windows and lets users generate unlimited BGM, loops, and sound effects locally. The tool offers generation, editing, looping, and finishing features and works without cloud services, API keys, or per-generation fees.

Key Points:

- **Generation**: creates BGM, instrument loops, and sound effects from a single sentence, returning up to 4 candidates per run.

- **Finishing**: adjusts LUFS loudness, adds fade, and exports to 48 kHz WAV, OGG, or FLAC, with a .json file storing prompt, seed, and settings.

🔗 Resources:

- [Original post](https://x.com/KanaWorks_AI/status/2112016085046464733)

### 📊 SWAN Score – free 0–100 historical risk score for tickers

SWAN Score provides a free 0–100 score that reflects historical drawdowns, price volatility, and income for stocks, ETFs, and funds over a five-year span. The score is presented alongside the S&P 500 ETF (SPY) for comparison and includes no forecasts or buy/sell signals.

Key Points:

- **Score range**: 0 to 100, higher indicates a calmer past.

- **Factors used**: historical drawdowns, daily price swings, and income when available.

🔗 Resources:

- [Original post](https://x.com/carlosxtravel/status/2108546156993085757)
