### 📊 cinaciguat reverses liver cell aging, cuts amyloid beta 50%

cinaciguat rejuvenated aging liver cells in elderly rats in 2 weeks, reducing blood amyloid-beta by about 50% to young-rat levels. It also restored LDL to youthful levels and reopened microscopic pores in liver blood-vessel lining cells.

Key Points:

- 2-week treatment with cinaciguat reduced amyloid beta by ~50%, matching young-rat blood levels.

- Restored LDL (bad cholesterol) to youthful levels and reopened microscopic pores in liver vascular lining cells.
