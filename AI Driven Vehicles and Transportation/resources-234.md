#### Article 1: Aluminum Tooling for Vehicle Molds

Aluminum tooling has become a necessity for vehicle manufacturers due to the shortage of ureol tooling in the country. Lead times for aluminum tooling are 34 weeks, and production is split across three companies to meet demand.

Key Points:

- **Aluminum Tooling**: A cost-effective alternative to ureol tooling, aluminum tooling is used for vehicle molds due to its availability and shorter lead times.

- **Production Split**: To meet demand, production is split across three companies, ensuring timely delivery of vehicle molds.

- **Lead Times**: Aluminum tooling has a lead time of 34 weeks, which is significantly shorter than ureol tooling.
