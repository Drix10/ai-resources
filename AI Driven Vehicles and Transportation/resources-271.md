### 📊 Reddit Q3'26 earnings beat revenue, DAU miss, login test rollout

Reddit reported Q3 revenue of $895M, beating the $878.4M estimate, while daily active users fell to 130.3M versus the expected 133.4M. The company will begin testing mandatory login for most users in select non-U.S. markets in Q4. Advertising revenue rose 54% to $847M.

Key Points:

- **Revenue**: $895M (Est. $878.4M), +53% YoY

- **Daily active users Q3**: 130.3M (Est. 133.4M), +12% YoY

- Advertising revenue grew 54% to $847M

- Login requirement tests start in Q4 in select markets outside the U.S.
