### 🤖 Hesai Kosmo 3D reconstruction announced with millimeter precision and 5x speed

Hesai Kosmo 3D reconstruction announced, captures spaces with LiDAR and 8K camera and generates 3DGS, meshes, depth maps in the cloud. Uses generative AI to fill unscanned areas and textures. Claims millimeter precision and up to 5× speed.

Key Points:

- **Capture**: LiDAR plus 8K camera.

- **Output**: 3DGS, meshes, depth maps generated simultaneously in cloud.

- **AI**: Generative AI fills unscanned areas and textures.

- **Performance**: Millimeter precision and up to 5× speed.

### 🤖 Generative value classifiers new Q-function type

Generative value classifiers are a new parametrization of Q-functions that don't require action conditioning. They may combine gracefully with large, pre-trained policies and learn/generalize better from a richer output signal.

Key Points:

- Generative value classifiers are a new parametrization of Q-functions that don't require action conditioning.

- They may combine gracefully with large, pre-trained policies and learn/generalize better from a richer output signal.

### 🤖 HiP3R launches local 3D reconstruction system

HiP3R introduces a method to piece together local 3D reconstructions for consistent scenes across thousands of frames. A demo is available and the project, paper, and code are released.

Key Points:

- **Project**: HiP3R website released

- **Paper**: arXiv preprint 2610.31635 published

- **Code**: GitHub repository HiP3R made public

### 🤖 LLMSeg turns multimodal LLM into text-prompted image segmenter

LLMSeg turns an off-the-shelf multimodal LLM into a text-prompted image segmenter with no training, no segmentation head and no mask decoder. It segments images using LLMs and B-splines loops.

Key Points:

- Turns an off-the-shelf multimodal LLM into a text-prompted image segmenter with no training, no segmentation head and no mask decoder.
