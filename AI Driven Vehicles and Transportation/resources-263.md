### 🤖 Waymo raises $5 billion debt, adds to $16 billion equity

Waymo announced a $5 billion debt financing. The company previously raised $16 billion in equity in February. The funding is described as supporting global growth and accelerating its autonomous program.

Key Points:

- **Debt**: $5 billion raised recently.

- **Equity**: $16 billion raised in February.

### 🤖 Prime Agent orchestrates 2,000-agent Rust rewrite

Prime Agent automated a rewrite of its own codebase into Rust. The operation used over 10,000 sandboxes and exchanged 16,000 messages between agents. It is presented as one of the fastest CLI agents.

Key Points:

- **Agents**: a swarm of over 2,000 agents performed the rewrite.

- **Infrastructure**: 10,000+ sandboxes and 16,000 agent-to-agent messages were used.

Odyssey-3 is a new foundation world model. The announcement says it sets a new state of the art on Physics-IQ and can power robots, train AIs, and generate interactive experiences. Access to the model is offered for free today.
