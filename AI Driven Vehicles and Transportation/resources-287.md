### 🤖 FSD v15 rollout upgrades end-to-end driving network

FSD v15 now rolling out upgrades every part of the end-to-end driving network, adding Reality View and 25x better safety case handling. The update also improves detection of signs, animals, debris, potholes, standing water, and speed handling in school zones and bad weather.

Key Points:

- **Safety**: 25x better handling of safety critical cases

- **Tesla Reality View**: live visualization of the drive showing what FSD plans to do

🔗 Resources:

- [Original post](https://x.com/Tesla/status/2124222447855743019)
