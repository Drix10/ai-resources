On October 26, 1775, John Hancock wrote to George Washington on behalf of the Continental Congress, instructing him to secure two armed vessels at "Continental Risque and pay," with "proper incouragement to the Marines and Seamen that shall be Sent on this Enterprise." This letter marked a significant milestone in American history.
