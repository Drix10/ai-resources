### 🤖 Energy-Based Policy Orchestration announced with 7× fewer policy switches

Energy-Based Policy Orchestration (EPO) is introduced, reducing policy switches by 7× and providing differentiable energy scores for policy-goal pairs. Adding a new skill only requires training one new energy model, without retraining existing policies or a central scheduler.

Key Points:

- 7× less frequent policy switches compared with learned MoE routers.

- Adding a skill means training one new energy model only.
