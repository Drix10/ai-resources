### 🚨 IMF alarm circular financing AI boom $1.4 trillion

IMF alarm circular financing deals tied to the AI boom total $1.4 trillion. Big tech and chipmakers are investing in and guaranteeing debt for customers buying their products.

Key Points:

- IMF sounded the alarm that circular financing deals tied to the AI boom total $1.4 trillion (about 1,900 trillion won).

- Big tech and chipmakers are investing in and guaranteeing debt for the customers that buy their products, including $NVDA, $ORCL, $MSFT, $AMZN, $AMD, $GOOGL, $AVGO, $SPCX, $CRWV, $NBIS, $IREN, $WULF, $CIFR, $META, $OWL, $MU.
