### 🤖 SuperMimic trains humanoid tracker in 3 hours on one GPU

SuperMimic trains a humanoid tracker in 3 hours on a single GPU, beating a state-of-the-art baseline that required 7 days on 128 GPUs. The approach uses differentiable planning with stable and effective gradients.

Key Points:

- **Training speed**: 3 hours on a single GPU.

- **Technique**: differentiable planning with stable and effective gradients.

### 🤖 Steertuning – few-shot robot task adaptation in under 5 min

Steertuning adapts a robot to a new task from five human demonstrations in under five minutes, achieving 71% success across twelve new tasks. The system fine-tunes a 400M-parameter diffusion policy for two minutes (2000 updates) and steers actions with the closest demonstration during deployment.

Key Points:

- 5 human demonstrations enable task adaptation in under 5 minutes.

- 71% success rate on 12 tasks in new environments.
