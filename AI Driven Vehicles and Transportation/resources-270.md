### 📊 The Economics of Intelligence paper released – DeepMind research on AGI economics

The Economics of Intelligence is a DeepMind paper that defines economic capability thresholds for AGI and tracks market, employment, and wage changes during transitions. It highlights that coordination improvements may drive visible economic transformation and that wage effects will appear before changes in market structure or employment.

Key Points:

- **Takeaway 1**: Track agentic coordination more closely to understand transformation paths.

- **Takeaway 2**: AI impact will likely show up in wages before market structure or employment.

- [Original post](https://x.com/alexolegimas/status/2115822019518370172)
