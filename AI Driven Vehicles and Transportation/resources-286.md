### 🤖 Grana compositional reward model improves robot success rates

Grana is a compositional reward model that tracks progress through mistakes and recovery in long-horizon robot manipulation. It raised real-robot airplane assembly success to 75% from 50% on the same recordings, and increased tractor assembly success from 2 / 10 to 9 / 10 when guiding action selection.

Key Points:

- 75% success on airplane assembly with Grana-weighted training versus 50% unweighted on identical data.

- Tractor assembly success rose to 9 / 10 with Grana-guided move selection, compared to 2 / 10 without.

### 🚀 Hierarchy-GBP adds multigrid for pose graph optimisation on multicore CPUs

Hierarchy-GBP incorporates multigrid to accelerate long-range message passing for pose graph optimisation and bundle adjustment on multicore CPUs. The approach is aimed at rapid pose graph optimisation or bundle adjustment on large, complex problems. Future work includes GPU support.

Key Points:

- **Feature**: multigrid accelerates long-range message passing for pose graph optimisation and bundle adjustment on multicore CPUs.

- **Roadmap**: next target is GPU implementation.
