### 🤖 Magni VM launches GPU-as-megakernel framework, 3.4× faster than PyTorch

Magni VM is a new GPU-as-megakernel framework built on the flappyairplanes reproduction. It runs the whole GPU with a single kernel and claims up to 3.4× speedup over PyTorch. The creators open-sourced the project and say it beats flappipng airplanes numbers.

Key Points:

- **Framework**: Magni VM, a GPU-as-megakernel system.

- **Performance**: up to 3.4× faster than PyTorch.
