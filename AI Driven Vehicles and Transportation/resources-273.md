### 🤖 Tesla Autopilot car detector runs twice as fast with 16-bit TensorRT

Tesla Autopilot car detector runs about twice as fast using 16-bit TensorRT on an RTX 4060, dropping frame time from 31.3 ms to 16.6 ms. Accuracy changes from 0.501 to 0.500 and matches 96.6% of the original boxes. An 8-bit version is 4.2× faster but finds only 48% of cars on a night drive.

Key Points:

- 16-bit TensorRT on RTX 4060 reduces per-frame time from 31.3 ms to 16.6 ms (≈32 fps to 60 fps).

- Accuracy shifts from 0.501 to 0.500 and matches 96.6% of original detections.

- 8-bit version is 4.2× faster but detects only 48% of cars on night drive.

### 🤖 HackerTube filters 43M comments to 6,454 videos

HackerTube filtered 43 million YouTube comments spanning 20 years to 6,454 recommended videos, improving viewing experience. The work was done in an afternoon using Claude and took about 2 hours with Opus 5.5.
