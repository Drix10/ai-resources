### 🤖 Bedrock Robotics raises $600M Series C funding

Bedrock Robotics raised a $600M Series C, co-led by CapitalG and Emergence Capital, two months after deploying fully autonomous excavators on live sites. The company has now raised nearly $1B since its 2024 founding and has six customers with deployments underway.

Key Points:

- **Funding**: $600M Series C co-led by CapitalG and Emergence Capital.

- **Deployments**: Six customers have deployments; the autonomy stack retrofits existing excavators.
