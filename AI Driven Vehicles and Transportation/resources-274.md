### 🤖 DynaSolver announced with 11.4% lower rollout error

DynaSolver is a neural operator for 4D fluid simulation that achieves 11.4% lower rollout error versus the best learned baseline. It is trained on 22 robot morphologies and 10,560 CFD frames. Training is 7.1× faster than autoregressive unrolling. The model conditions on moving boundaries to reshape flow over time.

Key Points:

- 22 robot morphologies and 10,560 CFD frames used.

- 7.1× faster training versus autoregressive unrolling.
