### 🤖 TIER IV and Komatsu develop autonomous dump trucks, target 2027

TIER IV and Komatsu announced joint development of autonomous driving systems for three dump truck models, aiming for deployment at quarry and construction sites in Japan and North America in 2027. The effort combines TIER IV’s autonomous tech with Komatsu’s vehicle engineering and integrates EARTHBRAIN’s fleet management system.

Key Points:

- Development includes HD785 (93.9 metric tons payload), HD605 (64.1 metric tons payload), and HM400/460 (42 metric tons payload) dump trucks.

- Collaboration began in September 2025 and targets field deployment in 2027.
