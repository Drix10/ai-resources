### 🚀 Shield to acquire Echodyne in a $5.3B transaction

Shield announced a definitive agreement to acquire Echodyne for $5.3 billion, funded by a $2.5 billion raise that includes $1 billion Series H at a $28.8 billion valuation. The deal adds Echodyne’s software-defined, metamaterials-based radars and manufacturing capacity to Shield’s Mission Systems business.

Key Points:

- **Acquisition value**: $5.3 billion, with $4.8 billion payable at closing and up to $500 million contingent on performance.

- **Funding**: $2.5 billion raise comprising $1 billion Series H, $1 billion structured equity from Blackstone, and $500 million debt led by JPMorgan Chase.
