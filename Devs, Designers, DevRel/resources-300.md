### 🤖 Krea 3 diffusion transformer 6.9× faster on AMD Instinct MI355X

Krea 3's diffusion transformer runs 6.9× faster on AMD Instinct MI355X, dropping inference time from 22.9 s to 3.3 s. The team reports 2.8× faster MXFP6 GEMM versus BF16 and reduced GPU transfer wait time to 2% while keeping 98.3% BF16 quality (SSIM).

Key Points:

- **Speed**: 22.9 s → 3.3 s (6.9× faster) on AMD Instinct MI355X.

- **Performance**: 2.8× faster MXFP6 GEMM vs BF16; GPU transfer wait reduced to 2%; quality at 98.3% BF16 (SSIM).
