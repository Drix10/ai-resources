Shopify reported using 4.2 million CPU cores and 21 PiB of memory on Black Friday, generating $7.6B in sales with a peak of 6.8M sales per minute.

Key Points:

- **CPU cores**: 4.2 million

- **Memory**: 21 PiB

- **Total sales**: $7.6B

- **Peak sales rate**: 6.8M per minute

### 📊 India GDP per capita $10,039 in 2025, five-fold 1990 level

India GDP per capita reached $10,039 in 2025 (PPP constant 2021 international dollars), nearly five times its 1990 level. India now has the same GDP per capita China had in late 2009.

Key Points:

- GDP per capita $10,039 in 2025 (PPP constant 2021 international dollars)
