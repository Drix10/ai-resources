### 🤖 Intel 4004 announced 1971 with 2,300 transistors

Intel 4004 was announced on Nov 15 1971 as the first commercial 4-bit microprocessor, containing about 2,300 transistors and running at 740 kHz in a 16-pin package. It originated as a chipset for a Busicom calculator and later led to the 8008 used in a 1972 PDP-11/34.

Key Points:

- 4-bit CPU with ~2,300 transistors, clocked at 740 kHz, 16-pin package.

- Intel bought back the rights for $60k; Federico Faggin etched “F.F.” on each die.
