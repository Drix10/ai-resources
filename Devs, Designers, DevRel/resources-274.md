### 🤖 AI/ML - Neural Texturing Challenges

- **Kernel Efficiency**: Purpose-built kernels can often compute the same structure more efficiently than traditional ReLU neurons.

### 🤖 AI/ML - NNTC Weight Scaling

NNTC's total number of weights scales based on the number of channels stored in each of the two latent textures and the number of desired decoded output RGB channels. MLP solutions don't scale well this way, making their decoders "lobotomized".

Key Points:

- **MLP Scaling Limitations**: MLP solutions don't scale well with the number of channels and desired decoded output RGB channels.
