### 🚀 Crypto hack - $5.57B stolen, $2.39B frozen

Crypto hack reports $5.57B stolen across Ethereum, Bitcoin and Tron and $2.39B frozen in stablecoins. Aave froze V3 and V4 on Ethereum mainnet as a precaution.

Key Points:

- **Stolen**: ~$5.57B total – ~$3.51B ETH, ~$1.44B BTC, ~$630M Tron

- **Frozen**: ~$2.39B stablecoins – ~$1.567B USDT, ~$823M USDC

### 🤖 Faros AI analysis shows PR size up 71.8%

Faros AI analyzed 22,000 developers across 4,000 teams and reports PR size increased by 71.8%. QA time rose 300.6%, task restarts grew 66.7%, and monthly incidents climbed 125.4%. The data suggests faster code creation but more downstream issues.

Key Points:

- **PR size**: +71.8%

- **QA time**: +300.6%
