### 🤖 Tech - AI and Data Center Water Demand

AI Data Center water demand scales with energy demand. This is not because of cooling from energy loss through heat at the data center, this is due to water usage in the management of the energy itself. It’s called the water-energy nexus.

Key Points:
• AI Data Center water demand is directly related to energy demand.
• Water usage in data centers is primarily for energy management, not cooling.
• The water-energy nexus is a critical consideration for data center operations.
