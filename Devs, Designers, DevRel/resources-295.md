### 🤖 Microsoft buyouts 60,000 employees Voluntary Exit Offer

Microsoft is offering buyouts to 60,000 employees, about 27% of its workforce, under a program called “Voluntary Exit Offer.” Over 30% of participants accepted the offer in July.

Key Points:

- **Buyouts**: 60,000 employees, 27% of the company.

- **July acceptance rate**: over 30% of eligible employees took the offer.

### 🤖 AmpCode self-hosted orbs on Vercel Sandbox launch

AmpCode shipped self-hosted orbs and a template to run them on Vercel Sandbox and Daemons (beta). Each thread receives a persistent sandbox and uses OIDC auth, so there’s no Vercel token to manage.

Key Points:

- Self-hosted orbs shipped by AmpCode.

- Template runs them on Vercel Sandbox and Daemons (beta).

- Every thread gets its own persistent sandbox.

- Auth is OIDC, with no Vercel token to manage.
