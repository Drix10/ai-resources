### 🤖 TikTok Ads US targeting unavailable for non-US accounts

TikTok Ads US targeting only appears for accounts registered in the US or Canada. A US-registered company with its own ad account is required, costing a few hundred dollars yearly and a yearly IRS form with a $25k fine if missed.

Key Points:

- US only shows up for accounts registered in the US or Canada

- Official fix is a US company with its own ad account; a few hundred dollars a year, plus a yearly IRS form with a $25k fine if you miss it
