### 🤖 B-epsilon trees in GEFS use message slots in nodes

B-epsilon trees in GEFS are a B-tree variant with extra space for messages. Messages store a callback and argument and are applied on reads. When node space fills, messages are pushed down until they reach leaves, where the callback is applied and the message removed.

Key Points:

- Nodes have extra space for “messages” that hold a callback and argument.

- On read, callbacks from messages are applied to the item; full nodes push messages downward until leaf application.
