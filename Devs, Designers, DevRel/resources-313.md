### 📊 The Content–Geometry Tradeoff in World Models: Slow SVReg boosts planning success

Slow SVReg is introduced to improve JEPA world models by preserving slowly changing factors while learning smoother latent maps. It raises first-person maze planning success from 21.2% to 92.9% and PushT manipulation success from 44.0% to 87.3% over a stop-gradient baseline.

Key Points:

- Slow SVReg adds a slowness term on selected latent channels and a gated residual predictor.

- SVReg imposes a variance floor on latent projections along random directions.
