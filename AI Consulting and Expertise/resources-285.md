### 🤖 Qwen 4 Max builds voxel Hyrule Castle in 30 minutes

Qwen 4 Max created a full voxel recreation of Hyrule Castle that can be walked around in a game-style environment in 30 minutes, using about 6M tokens and costing roughly $1.9. Plans to use the model start at $3 per month.

Key Points:

- **Build time**: 30 minutes for full voxel Hyrule Castle.

- **Cost**: ~6M tokens and ~$1.9 for the test; service plans start at $3/mo.
