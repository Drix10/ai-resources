### 🤖 Mistral Large 4 open weights release, 1.05T params

Mistral Large 4 open weights release with 1.05T total and 52B active parameters. It scores 26 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 and uses 82K output tokens per Intelligence Index task, 39% fewer than the Preview. Pricing is $0.70/$2.10 per 1M input/output tokens, about a 50% reduction from the Preview.

Key Points:

- **Size**: 1.05T total parameters, 52B active parameters

- **Token efficiency**: 82K output tokens per Intelligence Index task, 39% fewer than Preview

- **Pricing**: $0.70/$2.10 per 1M input/output tokens, ~50% lower than Preview
