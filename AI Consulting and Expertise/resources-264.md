### 🤖 MAI-Image-2.6 on Vercel AI Gateway launch

MAI-Image-2.6 and MAI-Image-2.6-Flash were added to Vercel's AI Gateway on October 13, accessible through the AI SDK, an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or the AI CLI. Both models generate photorealistic images, support text in images, and allow targeted edits with up to five reference images and outputs up to 1536 by 1536.

Key Points:

- **Release date**: October 13.

- Supports up to five reference images; outputs up to 1536 by 1536.

### 🚀 Transit candidates from multi-sector TESS searches – 665 new candidates

Transit candidates from multi-sector TESS searches reports 665 possible new exoplanets. A bug fix added 118 candidates after re-searching 1.8 M stars. About 8 % of the list are expected to be false alarms.

Key Points:

- **Total possible new exoplanets**: 665.

- **Expected false-alarm rate**: ~8 %.
