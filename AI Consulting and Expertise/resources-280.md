### 🤖 Open-weight models and Postgres achieve 91% LoCoMo accuracy

Open-weight models and Postgres are used to build a company ontology without graph databases or proprietary models. The approach is open-source, excludes Anthropic and OpenAI, and reports 91% accuracy on LoCoMo and 79% on BEAM. Agents can work across large data sets but lack understanding of information.

Key Points:

- **Accuracy**: 91% on LoCoMo

- **Accuracy**: 79% on BEAM
