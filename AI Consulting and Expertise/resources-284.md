### 🤖 Inkling 2 Mini looped transformer released 26B parameters

Inkling 2 Mini is a looped transformer with 26B parameters, runs on a laptop and scores 20.1 on AA Index. At 3-loop inference it decodes 1.57× faster than no looping, and a drafter trained at 3 loops transfers to 4 and 5 loops without retraining.

Key Points:

- 26B parameters

- 3-loop inference decodes 1.57× faster than no looping

### 🤖 Vira AI agent launch and Genviral homepage repositioning
