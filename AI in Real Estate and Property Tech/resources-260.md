### 🤖 LOFT increased LOFT Loyalty adoption from 38% to 47%

Key Points:

- LOFT Loyalty adoption rose from 38% to 47%

### 🤖 Free ADU Floor Plan Maker – free tool to design studio to 3-bed layouts in 3D

Free ADU Floor Plan Maker lets users pick a studio, 1-, 2- or 3-bed layout, draw the outline, drag rooms, add doors and windows, and view the design in 3D. Entering a ZIP code unlocks the 3D rendering feature.

Key Points:
