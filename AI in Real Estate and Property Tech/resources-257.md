### 📊 TestFit higher-density multi-family under half residential runs

TestFit reports higher-density multi-family projects are now under half of residential feasibility runs, at 61.6% in Q3 2024 and 45.2% in Q3 2026. Developers are shifting to lower-density projects while staying in residential.

Key Points:

- **Q3 2024**: higher-density multi-family 61.6% of residential feasibility runs on TestFit.

- **Q3 2026**: higher-density multi-family 45.2% of residential feasibility runs on TestFit.

Key Points:
