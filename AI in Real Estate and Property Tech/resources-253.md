### 🚀 ArchiVinci launches SketchUp photorealistic rendering extension

ArchiVinci released a SketchUp extension that creates photorealistic renders in seconds. Users open any model, enter a material prompt or skip it, and the view renders while preserving geometry, proportions, openings, and camera angle. Rendering occurs inside the modeling window rather than requiring export.

Key Points:

- **Feature**: Photorealistic render generated in seconds from a SketchUp model.

- **Workflow**: Rendering performed inside the SketchUp window, no export needed.
