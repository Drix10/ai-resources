### 🤖 AI & Economy - AI's Impact on Jobs

AI is often seen as a job killer, but maybe it's not. The reason this is happening is because AI is bringing automation to areas that don’t have finite demand characteristics. AI allows companies to do far more than they could before, but agents still require human oversight and decision-making.

Key Points:

- **AI's Impact on Job Creation**: AI is creating new tasks for humans to do, rather than replacing them. Companies that use AI tend to hire more humans.

- **AI's Role in Job Automation**: AI is automating tasks that don't have finite demand characteristics, freeing up humans to focus on more complex and creative tasks.

- **The Future of Work**: The economy still has plenty of jobs overall, but the nature of work is changing. Humans will need to adapt to work alongside AI and automation.
