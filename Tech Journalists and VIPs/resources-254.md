### 🚀 Matrix multiplication exponent tight at 9/4

Matrix multiplication exponent has a new upper bound of 9/4 and a matching lower bound, making the bound tight. An older result of Yin Tat Lee et al. implies linear programs can be solved in time n^{9/4}.

Key Points:

- New upper bound of 9/4 announced in the October 6 release.

- Lower bound announced that makes 9/4 tight.
