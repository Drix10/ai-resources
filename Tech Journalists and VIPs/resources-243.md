### 🤖 Claude GTM research method reduces 4 weeks to 3 hours

Claude GTM research method claims to turn 4 weeks of market research into 3 hours by feeding raw material and asking forced-thinking questions. It outlines data sources, question prompts, and a follow-up critique step, then suggests using Gojiberry to test hypotheses with buyers.

Key Points:

- **Raw material**: 25-40 competitor websites, recent earnings calls from 3-5 biggest incumbents, 100+ customer reviews, and every Reddit or forum thread about the category.

- **Questions**: “What do the winners in this market know that their customers would never admit?” and “List the beliefs this whole industry takes for granted. For each one, what would need to happen for it to be false?”

- **Follow-up**: Ask Claude to “Steelman this thesis like a top-tier VC would. Then show me exactly where it fails.”

- **Testing**: Use Gojiberry to find and reach the right people for buyer conversations.

### 🤖 Compaction improves, 3-tier subagent architecture recommended

Compaction is much better now so you may not need to explicitly clear context for model focus. Autocompact is usually set to 400k and manual compact runs between 200-400k. A three-tier subagent architecture is suggested, with no need for more than three nesting levels for most tasks.

Key Points:

- Autocompact typically set to 400k tokens

- Manual compact performed between 200-400k tokens depending on model/harness

- Top-level orchestrator can launch subagents that launch further subagents
