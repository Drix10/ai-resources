### 🤖 India GDP per capita $10,039 in 2025 (PPP)

India reached a GDP per capita of $10,039 in 2025 (PPP constant 2021 international dollars). That level is nearly five times its 1990 level and matches China’s GDP per capita in late 2009.

Key Points:

- **GDP per capita 2025**: $10,039 (PPP constant 2021 international dollars)

- **Growth factor**: nearly five times 1990 level; equal to China’s late-2009 GDP per capita

### 📊 India electricity 99.9% and open defecation 6.7% by 2024

India electricity 99.9% and open defecation 6.7% by 2024, with infant mortality down 72% since 1990. Life expectancy rose to 72.2 years.

Key Points:

- **Electricity coverage**: 99.9% in 2024 (up from four in ten in 2000)

- **Open defecation**: 6.7% in 2024 (down from 73.2% in 2000)

### 🚀 Space Data Centers – 10 TW capacity in sun-synchronous orbit

Space Data Centers claim they can fit about 10 terawatts in the dawn-dusk sun-synchronous orbit, with 88,000 filed satellites to deliver the first 20 gigawatts. They plan to expand to medium Earth orbit, geostationary, Lagrange points, cislunar orbits, and eventually sun-orbit. Low Earth orbit offers low latency and lower fuel requirements.

Key Points:

- 10 terawatts capacity in the dawn-dusk sun-synchronous orbit.

- 88,000 satellites filed to deploy the first 20 gigawatts.

### 🤖 Anthropic retention – engineers 8x more likely to leave OpenAI

Anthropic has some of the best talent retention of any frontier lab. Engineers were about eight times more likely to leave OpenAI for Anthropic than the reverse, and roughly eleven times more likely to leave DeepMind.

Key Points:

- Engineers were ~8× more likely to leave OpenAI for Anthropic than vice versa.

- Engineers were ~11× more likely to leave DeepMind.
