### 📊 Hawkeye launch maps 74,000 US logistics sites

Hawkeye is a free live map of American ground logistics covering over 74,000 warehouses, terminals and hubs. The map adds site-level safety profiles drawn from public federal records such as OSHA injury reports and inspections.

Key Points:

- 74,000 warehouses, truck terminals, wholesale centers and parcel hubs mapped across the lower 48 states.

- Safety profile for each site uses public federal records, including OSHA injury reports and inspections.
