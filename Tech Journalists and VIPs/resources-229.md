### 🤖 Coinbase acquires Deribit and outlines upcoming product roadmap

Coinbase announced that Deribit, the world’s largest crypto options exchange, is now part of Coinbase. The company said a regulated liquidity pool will soon combine US and global derivatives, and that future offerings will include options, spot margin, and unified portfolios. Coinbase Pro is slated to return later this year.

Key Points:

- **Acquisition**: Deribit joins Coinbase.

- **Timeline**: Coinbase Pro returns later this year.
