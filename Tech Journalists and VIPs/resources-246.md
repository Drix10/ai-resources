### 🤖 Federal Scholarship Tax Credit 2027 up to $1,700 credit

Federal Scholarship Tax Credit allows US taxpayers to give up to $1,700 to a qualified scholarship granting organization and receive a dollar-for-dollar credit starting in 2027. It is also called the Education Freedom Tax Credit and applies directly to tuition, tutoring or school costs.

Key Points:

- **Amount**: up to $1,700 credit per taxpayer.

- **Effective date**: starts in 2027.
