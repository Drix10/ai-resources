### 🤖 Agent37 launches cheapest sandbox at $4.76/month

Agent37 announced a sandbox service costing $4.76 for a 2 vCPU 4GB instance run for a month, claiming 94% lower cost than most providers. It offers a price-match refund if a cheaper sandbox is found and provides up to $10,000 in startup credits.

Key Points:

- **Monthly cost for a 2 vCPU 4GB sandbox**: $4.76 (compared to Vercel $249, Cloudflare $145, Daytona $121)

- Claims 94% lower cost than most sandbox providers and offers a refund of any price difference found.
