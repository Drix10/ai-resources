### 🚨 Nvidia market cap loss record 2.8 trillion in 45 days

Nvidia lost $2.8 trillion in market cap since its October 6 high, averaging a $85 billion daily decline over 33 trading days. The loss represents about 40% of the S&P 500’s market-cap decline in that period.

Key Points:

- **Market cap loss**: $2.8 trillion since October 6.

- **Average daily decline**: $85 billion over 33 trading days.

- **Share of S&P 500 loss**: ~40% since October 6.

### 📊 Human height data 18,457 records spanning 7,000 years

Human height data from 18,457 archaeological records across 783 sites covering 7,000 years shows broad regional variation and periods of declining stature. Results published in PNAS.

Key Points:

- 18,457 records of archaeological remains analyzed.

- Data span 7,000 years across 783 sites.

### 🤖 Waddle-1 scores 81% on RoboDojo-RC Tier 1 benchmark

Waddle-1 from theWaddleLabs scores 81% on RoboDojo-RC Tier 1, a real-world robot benchmark. It uses multiple LLMs and shares one context across a task’s trials, while other LLMs tested had a fresh context each trial.

Key Points:

- **Score**: 81% on RoboDojo-RC Tier 1.

- **Architecture**: multiple LLMs share one context across trials; other LLMs used fresh context each trial.
