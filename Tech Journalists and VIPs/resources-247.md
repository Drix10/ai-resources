### 🤖 Acyclic Labs launch – 10,000-agent runtime and SwarmBench

Acyclic Labs announced a launch in YC F26 with open-source infrastructure that runs 10,000 agents in one session. They introduced SwarmBench to score coordination, reinforcement-learning environments to train it, and a runtime for thousands of agents. The focus is on getting many models to work together rather than improving single models.

Key Points:

- **Launch**: Acyclic Labs (YC F26) released infrastructure for 10,000 agents in one session

- **Tools**: SwarmBench scores coordination; RL environments train coordination; runtime runs thousands of agents simultaneously
