### 📊 coronary artery disease LDL study 20% more plaque regression

coronary artery disease LDL study shows lowering LDL to ~40 mg/dL versus 70 mg/dL yields 20% more plaque regression in 18 months. The trial included 104 newly diagnosed patients with no prior statin prescription.

Key Points:

- LDL reduced to ~40 mg/dL vs 70 mg/dL using two randomized regimens.

- 20% greater atherosclerotic plaque regression observed over 18 months (N=104).

### 🤖 Odin Robotics launches AI-powered robot arms for factory automation

Odin Robotics announced the launch of AI-powered robot arms that can be added to existing factory lines to handle repetitive tasks such as CNC machine loading, packing, and palletizing. The system combines programmed control for exact steps with learned skills for variable work, and offers a pilot setup paid only if the customer is satisfied.

Key Points:

- **Launch**: Odin Robotics (YC F26) released AI-powered robot arms for factory use.
