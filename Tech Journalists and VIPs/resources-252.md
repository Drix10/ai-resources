### 🤖 8090 AI-Native Software Development Platform launch

8090 AI-Native Software Development Platform announced, offering AI-driven code rewriting with governance and auditability. The platform claims to turn legacy code into readable rules and add AI agents to existing ERP systems.

Key Points:

- 100,000+ rules extracted from 18M+ lines of COBOL at CMS.

- 10K+ part names automatically validated at BISSELL.

### 🤖 Neil Mehta $100 accounts for San Francisco kids born 2016-2024

Neil Mehta deposited $100 into the account of every San Francisco child born between 2016 and 2024. The money is locked until the child turns 18 and must be claimed by the parents.

Key Points:

- **Deposit amount**: $100 per child

- **Eligibility**: children born 2016-2024 in San Francisco, claim required, locked until age 18
