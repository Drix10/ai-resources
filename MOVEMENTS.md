## Method

- One briefing = one vote. VALUE scores and engagement are publish filters, never weights.
- A cluster shares a rare subject pair; generic frames (launches, raises, star milestones) can never form one.
- Rising = three consecutive weeks, each hotter, from a non-zero base, spread over 5+ days. A 0→N explosion caps at watch.
- Template series (daily digests, weather alerts) and single-day bursts never rank.
- Scores are computed from counts only. Nothing here is written by a model and nothing is a forecast.
