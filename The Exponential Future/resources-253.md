### 📊 IMF says Middle East war poses biggest risk, forecasts 1.3% contraction for region by 2026

IMF says prolonged conflict in the Middle East and further disruptions to energy production and shipping routes pose the biggest risk to regional economies. The IMF forecasts a 1.3% contraction for the Middle East, North Africa and Pakistan region in 2026.

Key Points:

- **Risk**: prolonged conflict and energy-shipping disruptions are the biggest regional risk.

- **Forecast**: 1.3% contraction for the Middle East, North Africa and Pakistan region in 2026.
