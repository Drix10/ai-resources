### 🤖 Polyguard launches PreScreen human verification for hiring

Polyguard introduced PreScreen, a tool that verifies a real, present person behind each job application before it reaches a recruiter’s queue. The check runs on the candidate’s phone via a scanned code and integrates directly with existing ATS systems. Most customers are live in under an hour and can be onboarded the same day without IT work.

Key Points:

- PreScreen confirms physical presence using a phone‑scanned code and hardware attestation.

- Candidates are auto‑advanced in the ATS after passing the check; onboarding typically completes within one day.

### 📊 Brent Oil Prices Rise Above $104 Amid Middle East Attacks and US Storm

Brent crude briefly traded above $104 per barrel as geopolitical tensions escalated. New Houthi attacks hit Saudi airports and tankers in the Gulf and Strait of Hormuz. Storm Isaias forced shutdown of about 25% of Gulf of Mexico offshore production. US crude inventories fell by 3.2 million barrels last week.

Key Points:

- **Brent price**: briefly above $104 per barrel during Thursday morning trading.

- **US Gulf of Mexico output**: roughly 25% taken offline due to Storm Isaias.

- **Crude stock change**: US inventories down 3.2 million barrels last week.

- **Geopolitical events**: Houthi attacks on Saudi airports and tanker strikes in the Gulf and Strait of Hormuz.
