### 🚨 International Campaign to Ban Landmines 6,929 death 2025

International Campaign to Ban Landmines reports 6,929 people killed or wounded by landmines and unexploded ordnance in 2025. Civilians accounted for about 84% of the casualties and half of those were children.

Key Points:

- 6,929 people killed or wounded in 2025.

- Civilians made up about 84% of casualties; half were children.
