### 🤖 Brent edges up 0.3% to $92.22 after Houthi missile attack on Friday

Brent crude futures rose 24 cents, or 0.3%, to $92.22 a barrel by 0118 GMT on Friday after a Houthi missile attack on Riyadh. Both benchmarks remain on track for a third straight weekly decline, with Brent down 3.5% for the week.

Key Points:

- Brent crude futures rose 24 cents to $92.22 a barrel by 0118 GMT Friday.

- Brent is down 3.5% for the week as Hormuz shipments returned to roughly pre-war levels.

### 🤖 South Korea autocall issuance jumps a third to 21.8 trillion won

South Korea autocall issuance rose by one-third from multi-year lows, reaching 21.8 trillion won ($16.3 billion). Dealers warned the revival may be premature because issuance was concentrated during extreme volatility mid-year.

Key Points:

- Issuance increased by one-third to 21.8 trillion won ($16.3 billion).

- Dealers said the revival may be premature due to concentration during mid-year extreme volatility.

### 🚨 Kakao Kim Beom-su acquitted by Seoul High Court

Kakao founder Kim Beom-su was acquitted by the Seoul High Court on November 20, 2026. The case involved stock price manipulation charges linked to Kakao’s 2023 takeover of SM Entertainment. Prosecutors had requested a 15-year prison term and a 500 million won fine.

Key Points:

- **Date**: November 20, 2026
