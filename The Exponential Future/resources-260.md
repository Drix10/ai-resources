### 🤖 GPT-6 Astra edits Qwen3.5-0.8B weights, 0.016% changed

GPT-6 Astra edited Qwen3.5-0.8B weights, changing 0.016% of parameters and boosting math performance up to 6×. Instruction following improved up to 3×, achieved without gradients or training, called Weight-Space Programming.

Key Points:

- **Weight change**: only 0.016% of weights were edited.

- **Math boost**: reasoning loops fixed and math improved up to 6×.

- **Instruction boost**: instruction following improved up to 3×.

- **No training**: changes made without gradients or training.
