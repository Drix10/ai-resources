### 🤖 GrokBot.Rocks directory launch with 8,230 public bots

GrokBot.Rocks provides a searchable library of 8,230 public Grok Bots across 32 categories, refreshed twice weekly. The post encourages pointing your own Grok Bot at the directory to acquire skills from existing recipes. The linked page describes the directory’s structure, free 2-week trial, and contribution process.

Key Points:

- 8,230 public Grok Bots tracked in 32 categories, updated twice a week.

- Free 2-week trial available for new users; contributors can email to add bots.

- [Original post](https://x.com/BrianRoemmele/status/2116527199121711180)

### 🤖 Maxwell's Demon – interactive 3D demo released

Maxwell's Demon interactive 3D demo lets users sort red and white particles with a virtual demon and view mixing entropy. The page provides a 3-D glass box, trapdoor, and controls for rotation, door operation, and speed.

Key Points:

- 12 red and 12 white spheres, mixing entropy range 0–24 bits.

- **Controls**: drag to rotate, hold to open door, speed cycles 1×, 4×, 16×.
