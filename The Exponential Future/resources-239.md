The importance of proof in market calls.

Key Points:


- **Market Calls**: Many people on Twitter make claims about big bold market calls, but without proof, these claims are worthless.

- **Proof of Trade**: Proof of a trade is the only thing that matters, and it should be shown through tax statements from the country through which the trade was made.
