### 🤖 Qwen3.8-27B fine-tuned to Arete-27B with 50M tokens rewrite

Qwen3.8-27B was fine-tuned by Nous Research into Arete-27B using about 50 million additional tokens, claiming near-zero performance loss. The company reports that responses aligned with China’s official positions fell from 22% to 0.9% in its own test.

Key Points:

- **Model**: Qwen3.8-27B fine-tuned to Arete-27B, 27B parameters.

- **Training data**: ~50 million tokens added for the rewrite.

- **Propaganda metric**: alignment with China’s official positions dropped from 22% to 0.9% in 803 test questions.

- **Evaluation**: scoring rules and test set created by Nous; only 25 questions checked by humans; scoring done by Grok.
