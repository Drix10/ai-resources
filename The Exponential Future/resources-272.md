### 🤖 Inkling 2 Mini launch with 26.4B parameters

Inkling 2 Mini is a new open-weights model that runs on a laptop, with 26.4B total and 3.4B active parameters. It is now available on DesignArena and released on Hugging Face, where it recreated The Starry.

Key Points:

- **Parameters**: 26.4B total, 3.4B active.

- **Availability**: on DesignArena and released on Hugging Face.

### 🚀 Hermes Agent Plugin Hackathon winners announced top 10

Hermes Agent Plugin Hackathon announced the top 10 winners after nearly 1,000 community members submitted entries. Agents scored every entry and five community judges ranked the finalists.

Key Points:

- **Participants**: nearly 1,000 community members submitted.

### 🤖 Cybertruck turning radius improvement via software update 2026.44+

Cybertruck model year 2027 receives a software update that improves its turning radius. The update requires software version 2026.44.+.

Key Points:

- **Update**: software version 2026.44.+ required for turning radius improvement.

- **Scope**: applies to all model year 2027 Cybertrucks.
