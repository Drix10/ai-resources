### 🤖 Vercel AI deployments rise to over 60% from 3%

Vercel reports AI agents now trigger more than 60% of deployments, up from about 3% in January. CEO Guillermo Rauch says primary customers are Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other agentic platforms. Vercel is tuning the platform for bots.

Key Points:

- **Deployments**: AI agents prompted 3% in January, now over 60%.

- **Customers**: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other agentic platforms.

### 📊 Bitcoin and Gold worst performing major assets 2026

Bitcoin fell 32% and Gold fell 16% in 2026, making them the two worst performing major assets so far this year. This level has not been seen before in any calendar year.

Key Points:

- Bitcoin down 32% so far in 2026

- Gold down 16% so far in 2026
