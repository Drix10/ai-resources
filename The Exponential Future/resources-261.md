### 📊 Amazon Google Microsoft Meta CapEx Q3 $202B increase

Amazon, Google, Microsoft, and Meta spent a combined $202 billion on CapEx in Q3, a 108% increase from a year ago and a 446% increase from three years ago. The AI infrastructure arms race is still accelerating.

Key Points:

- **Combined Q3 CapEx**: $202 billion

- **Year-over-year increase**: 108%

- **Three-year increase**: 446%
