### 📊 Jane Street lifts Fidelity Bitcoin ETF stake nearly eightfold in Q3

Jane Street increased its holding of the Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund to 7.01 million shares in Q3, up from about 887k shares at end-June. The firm reduced its iShares Bitcoin Trust shares by 19% to 20.22 million, while a higher share price raised that stake’s reported value to $957 million from $828 million.

Key Points:
