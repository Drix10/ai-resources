### 📊 Sandisk data center NAND bit demand to 55% by 2027

Sandisk says data centers will account for over 55% of NAND bit demand in 2027, up from 50% today. It expects data-center bit demand to grow ~40% next year after more than doubling in 2026, driven by AI infrastructure spending.

Key Points:

- **Data center share**: over 55% of NAND bit demand in 2027, up from 50% today.

- **Demand growth**: ~40% increase next year after more than doubling in 2026, driven by AI infrastructure spending.

### 🚨 Intel CEO cites three headwinds to server CPU

Intel CEO outlines three headwinds limiting its ability to meet strong server CPU demand. Intel 3 yields and cycle times are improving, but more work is needed. Industry-wide shortages of advanced organic substrates for EMIB packaging are constraining output. An accident at its Israel factory caused an extended shutdown, affecting Q4 server shipments.

Key Points:

- Intel 3 yields and cycle times are improving, but more work is needed.

- Industry-wide shortages of advanced organic substrates for EMIB packaging are constraining output.

- Accident at Israel factory caused an extended shutdown, will impact Q4 server shipments.
