### 🤖 COT on forex and commodities - Week to 3 November 2026 commodity selling accelerates

COT on forex and commodities – Week to 3 November 2026 reports hedge funds cut net long positions by 33% to 783k contracts across 25 futures. Euro shorts hit a ten-year high of 158k contracts (USD 22 bn) and Australian dollar shorts reached a record 129.5k contracts (USD 9 bn).

Key Points:

- Hedge funds were net sellers across all 25 major futures, reducing combined net long by 33% to 783k contracts.

- Euro net short rose to a ten-year high of 158k contracts (USD 22 bn).

### 🤖 Europe to send Germany, France, UK to Abu Dhabi talks

Europe will be represented by officials from Germany, France, the UK, NATO and the EU at peace talks with Russia in Abu Dhabi on November 10. The original format was expected to be trilateral with only Ukraine, Russia and the US.

Key Points:

- Officials from Germany, France, the UK, NATO and the EU will attend.
