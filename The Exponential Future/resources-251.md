### 🚨 US Seizes 90 Biodiesel Shipments to Cuba Valued at $3M

U.S. authorities seized 90 biodiesel shipments bound for Cuba, valued at nearly $3 million. The interceptions took place in Houston and an unnamed port as part of a pressure campaign on Cuba's energy sector.

Key Points:

- **Shipments**: 90 separate biodiesel shipments seized

- **Value**: total value nearly $3 million
