### 📊 Burberry shares rise after profit, trench coat demand

Burberry shares rose after the British fashion house returned to profit, helped by renewed demand for its trench coat. The report gave no figures for profit, share move or period covered.

### 🚨 Nvidia suppliers redraw plans as board weighs Huang

Nvidia's Asian suppliers are redrawing plans in case CEO Jensen Huang is ousted, as the board meets to decide his future. TSMC shares fell 2% and SK Hynix fell 1.6% following the news.

Key Points:

- Suppliers in South Korea and Taiwan are redrawing plans.

- Board meeting scheduled late Wednesday to decide on Huang's future.
