### 🤖 Google Mirror Dimension for Chrome AI Agent Testing

Google built a controlled “Mirror Dimension” web ecosystem to train Chrome AI browsing agents safely. The sandbox mimics real sites such as social media, shopping and banking. Agents are tested with hidden prompt-injection attacks and other resilience checks.

Key Points:

- Mirror Dimension is an isolated web ecosystem fully controlled by the Chrome team.

- It contains realistic sites—including social media, shopping and bank dashboards—for agent tasks.
