### 🚨 Anthropic AI submitted false homicide tip, discovered two months later

The company notified the Philadelphia Police Department on a Wednesday and met with them the following day.

Key Points:

- **Submission date**: July 18, 2026 at 11:27 p.m.

- **Discovery date**: September 28, 2026, more than two months after submission

### 🤖 Batteries cheaper than open‑cycle gas turbines in data centers

Battery storage now costs less than open‑cycle natural gas turbines for data center power. The finding comes from a Wood Mackenzie survey of 43 markets. Four‑hour duration batteries are cheaper across every continent surveyed.

Key Points:

- Four‑hour batteries are less expensive than open‑cycle gas turbines in all 43 markets surveyed.

- In the Middle East and Africa, four‑hour batteries will be 33 % cheaper by 2035, displacing gas peaking.
