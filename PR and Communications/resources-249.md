### 🤖 California Proposition 40, 5% billionaire tax fails

California Proposition 40, a one-time 5% tax on residents with net worth over $1 billion, was rejected by voters. Tech leaders outspent opponents roughly 10 to 1, and backers projected up to $100 billion in revenue over five years.

Key Points:

- Voters rejected Proposition 40, the one-time 5% billionaire tax.

- Tech leaders outspent backers roughly 10 to 1.
