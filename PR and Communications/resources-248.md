### 🤖 Northern Leaf expands Jersey medical cannabis output, £8.4M export 2025

Northern Leaf, a Jersey-based medical cannabis producer, exported £8.4 million of product in 2025. The company runs a 100,000-sq-ft greenhouse that now operates at 75% capacity, up from 25% two years earlier.

Key Points:

- **Export value**: £8.4 million ($11.3 million) in 2025 to the UK and Germany.

- **Production share**: 90% of Jersey’s cannabis output comes from Northern Leaf.

- **Facility size**: 100,000 sq ft greenhouse equipped with automated irrigation, environmental monitoring, and air purification.

- **Capacity growth**: site capacity rose from 25% to 75% over the last two years.
