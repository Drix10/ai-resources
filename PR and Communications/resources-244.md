### 🤖 Xbox Nano controller release November 4, $49.99 price

Xbox Nano controller is announced with a November 4 launch and a $49.99 price tag. It measures just over 5 inches, weighs 101 g, and charges via USB-C. Battery drops about 10% in 30-40 minutes and reaches 60% from 30% in under 10 minutes.

Key Points:

- **Release date**: November 4.

- **Price**: $49.99.
