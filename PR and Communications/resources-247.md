### 🤖 Sequoia shutting down its Scouts program marks the end of an era

Sequoia announced it is closing its London office and ending its scout program as part of a broader overhaul. The move reflects a growing consensus that the scout model has reached its limits. Other firms, such as a16z, are revising their scout programs to give fewer scouts more resources.

Key Points:

- Sequoia announced a broad overhaul that includes closing its London office and shutting down its scout program.

- a16z partner Gabriel Vasquez said the firm reduced the number of scouts, gave them more budget, and expects them to be more active.
