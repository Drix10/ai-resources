### 🤖 AI forcing companies to patch faster, outage hell

AI is forcing companies to patch their systems faster as attackers race to exploit newly discovered vulnerabilities, leading to internet outage concerns. AI-assisted vulnerability discovery has driven the total number of vulnerabilities found in 2026 past 100,000, far above 2022 levels. Only a small fraction of those bugs are being actively exploited. The rapid pace of AI-driven discovery and exploitation is putting strain on patching processes.

Key Points:

- AI-assisted vulnerability discovery has unleashed a torrent of software bugs, accelerating attacker exploitation.

- 2026 vulnerability count exceeded 100,000, compared with about 25,000 in 2022.

### 📊 Dianjunus rex – new giant fungus species discovered in China, up to 20 inches tall

Dianjunus rex is a newly described giant fungus from Yunnan, China, that grows up to 20 inches (≈5 lb). Researchers report it forms the largest recorded Ascomycete fruiting bodies and represents a new biological family. DNA analysis dates its divergence to about 65 million years ago, just after the Cretaceous extinction.

Key Points:

- **Size**: fruiting bodies grow up to 20 inches tall and weigh nearly 5 pounds.

- **Age**: molecular clock places divergence at roughly 65 million years ago, immediately after the mass extinction.
