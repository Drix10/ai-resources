### 🤖 Mingle Mocktails – $250K in 18 months, now eight-figure brand in 15,000 stores

Mingle Mocktails generated $250,000 in sales within its first 18 months and has grown into an eight-figure brand sold in more than 15,000 retail locations. Key Points:
