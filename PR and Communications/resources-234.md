### 🤖 Giorgia Meloni files EU voice trademark application

Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni has applied to trademark a four‑second recording of her voice at the EUIPO. The filing was made on 5 October and was still under examination on 9 October. The mark covers three classes of goods and services, including downloadable multimedia files, publishing, and event organisation.

Key Points:

- **Application date**: 5 October, filed in her own name through Praxi Intellectual Property.

- **Recording**: 4 seconds, says “Io sono Giorgia Meloni” twice.

- **Scope**: three classes – downloadable multimedia files/recordings, publishing books/newspapers/magazines, and organising conferences, cultural events, live entertainment, and political rallies.

- **Status**: still under examination as of 9 October.

### 🤖 Owlet wearable baby monitor reaches $20 M first‑year revenue, monitors 2.5 M infants

Owlet is an over‑the‑counter, FDA‑cleared wearable baby monitor launched in 2015. The company grew from $0 to $20 million in revenue in its first year and has monitored more than 2.5 million babies. It announced a new Baby Safety Task Force to raise safety standards.

Key Points:

- **Revenue**: $0 to $20 million in its first year.

- **Usage**: Monitored over 2.5 million infants, more than 21 trillion heartbeats and two billion hours of sleep data.
