### 📊 Airfare prices are soaring – Ryanair warns 20% rise

Ryanair warns European ticket prices could rise up to 20% in 2027 due to higher fuel costs. The airline projects a $2.2 billion (€2 billion) increase in annual fuel expenses and is looking at employee pay freezes, reduced crew ratios, and AI to offset costs.

Key Points:

- **Fare rise**: European airfares could increase by as much as 20% in 2027.

- **Fuel cost**: Expected annual fuel cost rise of about $2.2 billion (€2 billion) for the fiscal year ending March 2028.

### 🤖 BMW urban driver assist launching 2027 navigation-guided city driving

BMW will add navigation-guided city driving to select models via OTA update in 2027. The Level 2 system can steer, accelerate and brake on highways and city streets, but requires drivers to keep eyes on the road and hands on the wheel in urban areas.

Key Points:

- **Launch year**: 2027, first in Germany, other markets later.

- **Models equipped**: 3 Series, 7 Series, X5, iX3, iX4.

- Highway mode permits hands-off steering; city mode requires hands on wheel.

- System uses built-in GPS, cameras and sensors; displays assistance via HUD and 3D map.

Elon Musk is exploring a deal for SpaceX to acquire Boost Mobile or access its network after major carriers rejected his proposal. SpaceX recently spent about $8 billion on low-band cellular spectrum and Boost Mobile has over 7 million subscribers.

Key Points:
