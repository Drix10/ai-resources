### 🚨 AdFection zero-click spyware campaign infects 45 devices

AdFection zero-click spyware campaign infected 45 Al Jazeera devices by delivering Candiru malware through ads from Israeli-registered firm Discover-Tech. The campaign reached over 70,000 mobile apps and 120,000 websites between March and June 2024. Google blocked Discover-Tech from its ad platforms and added Safe Browsing warnings. IAB Europe and Beeswax also removed Discover-Tech from their vendor lists.

Key Points:

- 45 Al Jazeera devices were targeted with zero-click attacks delivered by ads.

- Ads linked to Discover-Tech appeared in more than 70,000 mobile apps and over 120,000 websites from March to June 2024.
