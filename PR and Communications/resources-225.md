### 🤖 AI Ethics - AI Responsibility

Before adding AI to a product or experience, leaders should ask: Is it solving a real problem—or are we using AI simply because we can? That question becomes especially important in fields where trust and human connection matter.

Key Points:

- **AI Responsibility**: Leaders must consider the potential consequences of AI adoption and ensure that it aligns with their organization's values and goals.

- **Human Connection**: AI should be used to augment human capabilities, not replace them. Leaders must prioritize human connection and empathy in AI-driven decision-making.

- **Real Problem-Solving**: AI should be used to solve real problems, not just to demonstrate its capabilities. Leaders must focus on delivering value to their customers and stakeholders.
