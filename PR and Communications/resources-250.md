### 🤖 Helsing Clarity launches with hundreds of AI agents

Helsing Clarity is a new AI platform that runs hundreds of military agents and requires a human to approve each target. It processes drone, satellite and sensor data into actionable targets and operates on the customer’s own servers.

Key Points:

- Runs hundreds of AI agents built for military use.
