### 📊 Dr. Tadataka Yamada’s R&D leadership on HIV drug access

Key Points:

### 🤖 Google launches Gemini agent as universal work assistant

Gemini is a single, universal AI agent for work that answers questions, handles knowledge tasks, creates media, and writes and runs code.
