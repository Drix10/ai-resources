### 🤖 Google hasn’t figured out how to make AI pay like its search engine

Google reports AI-driven query growth but Search revenue rose only 12% year-over-year in Q3, down from 17% the prior quarter. Paid-click growth fell to 8% YoY from 13% previously. The slowdown is attributed to currency effects, advertiser spending changes, and lower monetization of AI Mode queries. Google is expanding ads in AI Overviews and AI Mode while testing formats for its Gemini app.

Key Points:

- Search revenue growth slowed to 12% YoY in Q3, versus roughly 17% in the prior quarter.

- Paid-click growth dropped to 8% YoY, down from 13% in the previous quarter.

### 🤖 Chernobyl’s Dogs May Be Evolving, Scientists Unclear Why

A new study reports evidence of natural selection in dogs living at the Chernobyl nuclear plant site. Genomic regions under strongest selection relate to DNA repair, immunity and cell survival. The study compares dogs at the plant with those 16 km away in Chernobyl city and with global dog populations.

Key Points:

- Natural selection detected in dogs at the nuclear site versus dogs in Chernobyl city and worldwide.

- Selective pressure strongest on genes linked to DNA repair, immunity and cell survival.
