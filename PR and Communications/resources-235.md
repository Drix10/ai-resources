### 📊 AI Writing Classifier Detects AI Text 98% of the Time

A tech founder built a classifier that asks 214 questions about blog post structure. The classifier identified AI‑generated versions of 2,000+ pre‑ChatGPT blogs with 98% accuracy, misclassifying only 19 of 1,740 posts. Rewrites that removed about 73% of original phrasing still yielded the same detection rate.

Key Points:

- **Classifier accuracy**: 98% detection, 19 false results out of 1,740 posts.

- Rewrites deleted ~73% of original phrasing; detection rate remained 98%.

### 🤖 PepsiCo shifts global media account to Publicis Groupe

PepsiCo announced it has moved its global media buying to Publicis Groupe. The change was made without a formal review and ends a three‑decade relationship with Omnicom. CEO Ramon Laguarta described the partnership as “an important capability” that will allow more data‑driven investment.

Key Points:

- **Media spend**: PepsiCo spends an estimated $1.9 billion on media annually, $780 million in North America.

- **CEO quote**: Laguarta said the move lets PepsiCo invest “with much more data, much more granularity.”
