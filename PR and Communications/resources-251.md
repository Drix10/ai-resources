### 🤖 Mirendil receives $1 billion funding led by Kleiner Perkins

Mirendil, a developer of AI models and agent software, secured $1 billion in a funding round. The round was led by Kleiner Perkins with participation from Clearlake Capital and other investors. The company will use the capital to expand AI research access and grow its model and agent platform.

Key Points:

- **Funding amount**: $1 billion

- **Lead investors**: Kleiner Perkins and Clearlake Capital
