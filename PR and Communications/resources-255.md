### 🤖 He Became a Chick-fil-A Franchise Operator at Age 26

He Became a Chick-fil-A Franchise Operator at Age 26 and now runs two of the chain’s highest-grossing locations. He opened his first restaurant in Baltimore in April 2015 at age 26 and later opened Brooklyn sites in 2019 and November 2024.

Key Points:

- Franchise fee is $10,000; applicants must use personal funds and do not receive ownership of the restaurant.

- About 100,000 people apply each year; roughly 200 are selected, a 0.2% acceptance rate.
