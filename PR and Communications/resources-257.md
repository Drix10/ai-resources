### 🤖 Amazon loses latest attempt to avoid recall handling

Amazon is required to recall over 400,000 hazardous third-party products after a Maryland court upheld the CPSC’s view that Amazon is a distributor. The order mandates public notice, refunds for destroyed items, record-keeping for five years, and monthly progress reports.

Key Points:

- Court ruled Amazon must handle recalls of third-party products as a distributor.

- Amazon must issue full refunds to consumers who prove product destruction.
