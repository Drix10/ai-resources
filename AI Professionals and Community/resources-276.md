### 🤖 Adversarial Distribution Alignment at NeurIPS 2026

Adversarial Distribution Alignment (ADA) is a new algorithm that aligns generative models trained on simulations to full real-world distributions using only partial, noisy observations. It was announced at NeurIPS 2026. ADA can match multiple high-dimensional experimental observables without explicit joint structure.

Key Points:

- Aligns generative models—initially trained on simulations such as classical MD—to real-world distributions using partial, noisy observations like cryo-EM images.

- On cryo-EM images of Trp-Cage, ADA shifts a base MD model from oversampling unfolded states to concentrating around the experimentally observed folded state.
