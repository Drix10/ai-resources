### 🤖 AI - Architecture for Long-Horizon Agents

Long-horizon agents are AI systems designed to make decisions over extended periods, often with complex, dynamic environments. The Architecture for Long-Horizon Agents: Levels, Ticks, and Cascaded Intelligence by E Nijkamp, A Koul, E Pakhomov, and B Pang (Salesforce AI Research, 2026) presents a novel approach to building such agents.

Key Points:

- **Levels, Ticks, and Cascaded Intelligence**: The authors propose a hierarchical architecture consisting of levels, ticks, and cascaded intelligence. Levels represent high-level goals, ticks are low-level actions, and cascaded intelligence enables the agent to adapt to changing environments.

- **Cascaded Intelligence**: This mechanism allows the agent to learn from its experiences and update its decision-making process. It consists of a cascade of neural networks, each responsible for a specific aspect of the environment.

- **Levels and Ticks**: The levels represent high-level goals, while ticks are low-level actions. The authors propose a novel way of combining levels and ticks to create a more efficient decision-making process.
