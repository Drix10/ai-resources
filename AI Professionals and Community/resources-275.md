### 🤖 Robotaxi vs Uber Houston price gap 56% cheaper

Robotaxi in Houston costs $14.08 with a 5 min wait, 56% cheaper than UberX’s $31.95 and 14 min wait. UberXL is $31.98 with an 8 min wait.

Key Points:

- Model Y price $14.08, 5 min wait

- UberX price $31.95, 14 min wait
