### 🤖 Anchor 1 controller launches, lowest tracking errors

Anchor 1 whole-body controller for humanoid manipulation turns sparse human targets into precise, stable robot motion. It separates motion encoding from feedback correction for accurate tracking and smooth movement. On a real robot it posts the lowest tracking errors and highest task success against baselines. Weights and deployment code are expected late 2026.

Key Points:

- Sparse human targets (pelvis, wrists, ankles) become precise, stable robot motion, skipping online joint-space retargeting.

- Lowest tracking errors and highest task success against MimicLite, ScaleBFM, and SONIC baselines on a real robot.

- Weights and deployment code expected late 2026.
