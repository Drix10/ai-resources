### 🤖 MathEye refines LLM proofs, solves 93.3% of benchmarks

MathEye refines LLM-generated Lean proof scripts and solves 93.3% of 30 benchmark problems. It cuts required user interactions by 55% using MaxSAT-based obligation selection.

Key Points:

- Solves 93.3% of 30 real-world benchmarks across analysis and algebra.

- Reduces the number of required user interactions by 55% through minimal-cost obligation selection.

### 📊 From Pen-and-Paper to Machine-Checked Proofs: Lean for System Identification

From Pen-and-Paper to Machine-Checked Proofs: Lean for System Identification is a new arXiv paper that introduces the Lean 4 proof assistant to a system identification and control audience. It demonstrates reading Lean proofs with two elementary examples and formalizes a classic lemma on noise-filter inversion, revealing a subtle almost-sure claim.

Key Points:

- Introduces Lean 4 proof assistant for system identification and control researchers.

- Formalizes Ljung 1999 Lemma 3.1 and shows the claim holds almost surely, not for every realization.
