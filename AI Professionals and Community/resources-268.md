### 🤖 Anthropic Public Benefit Fund launches with 5% of shares

Anthropic creates the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, a wholly owned entity for public benefit, funded initially with 5% of company shares. Employees are donating shares, and the fund will grow when the company’s value rises by 50% over a baseline.

Key Points:

- **Initial allocation**: 5% of Anthropic’s shares to the fund.

- **Growth trigger**: for every 50% increase in company value over a set baseline, additional shares can be allocated.
