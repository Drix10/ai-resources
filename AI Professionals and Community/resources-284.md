### 🚨 Nvidia H200 and AMD MI325X shipments to China, 900k units approved

Nvidia H200 and AMD MI325X AI chips, nearly 900,000 units, were approved for shipment to China by May 11 2026. Over 30% of those chips went to firms designated as Chinese Military Companies, yet each license listed the risk of diversion as “low.”

Key Points:

- Approved shipment of nearly 900,000 Nvidia H200 or AMD MI325X AI chips to China as of May 11 2026.

- Over 30% of the chips were sent to companies designated as Chinese Military Companies, with every license stating a “low” risk of diversion.

### 📊 Safe Personal RSI rejects 20 of 21 risky updates

Safe Personal RSI rejects 20 of 21 risky self-proposed updates for the Kicker health assistant, testing on 29 cases. A GPT-5.6 Luna repair cut attack violations from 10.7% to 0% and raised safe task completion to 100%.

Key Points:

- **Update rejection**: 20 of 21 updates rejected, tested on 29 email, calendar, health, and marketplace cases.

- **Repair outcome**: GPT-5.6 Luna repair reduced violations from 10.7% to 0% and increased safe task completion from 89.7% to 100%.
