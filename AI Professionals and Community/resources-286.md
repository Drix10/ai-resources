### 📊 MIT vibe coding paper shows 25.5x code increase

- **Autonomous agents**: 25.5× more code, 240% more commits, 80% more repos touched, 30% more releases.

- **Claude code**: 7× more lines written, 12.2× more deletions, abandoned repos 30-day rate 60%→69%.
