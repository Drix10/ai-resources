### 🤖 AsyncVLA - real-time edge + remote inference for navigation

AsyncVLA enables real-time deployment of the OmniVLA navigation model using combined edge and remote inference. It achieves a 40% higher success rate than state-of-the-art baselines on vision-based navigation with up to 6 s latency. A presentation on the work will be given tomorrow at the poster session, Booth 142.

Key Points:

- Real-time operation achieved by running the base VLA on a workstation and a lightweight Edge Adapter onboard the robot.

- Quantitative results show a 40% increase in success rate compared to OmniVLA under delays up to 6 seconds.
