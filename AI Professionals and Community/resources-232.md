### 🤖 AI - Training Code-Self-Repair Agents

Training code-self-repair agents with reinforcement learning (RL) typically involves building a huge learned reward model. However, this approach can be expensive and may drift from the correctness you actually care about. An alternative approach is to use the verifier's own pass/fail signal as the reward directly.

Key Points:
• Training code-self-repair agents with RL involves building a huge learned reward model.
