### 📊 kiban built LSM storage engine with 0.96µs reads

kiban is an embedded LSM storage engine written in Rust that achieves 0.96µs median point reads on warm 1M-record workloads. Benchmarks show over 1M point-read calls per second and 2.2-2.7× faster reads than tuned RocksDB. Group commit pushes fully durable writes to 5,379 /s at 16 writers, a 7.5× improvement.

Key Points:

- **Median point reads**: 0.96 µs on warm 1M-record workloads.

- **Point-read throughput**: 1M calls/s, 2.2-2.7× faster than tuned RocksDB.

- **Group commit writes**: 5,379 /s at 16 writers, 7.5× improvement.

### 📊 SAFU_Score formula defines metric for SAFU claim

SAFU_Score is calculated as a weighted sum of Coverage_Ratio, Time_to_Full_Restoration, Hot_Wallet_Exposure, and Claim_Frequency_Penalty. The final score ranges from 0 to 100, where higher values make the “SAFU” claim look more ridiculous.

Key Points:

- SAFU_Score = (Coverage_Ratio × 0.4) + (Time_to_Full_Restoration × 0.3) + (Hot_Wallet_Exposure × 0.2) + (Claim_Frequency_Penalty × 0.1)

- Coverage_Ratio = (amount actually recovered or reimbursed) / (amount that left labeled exchange wallets) → 1.0 = full make-whole; lower is worse
