### 🤖 Processor Cache Optimization - Performance Metrics

This article presents performance figures for dynamic and static cache designs, focusing on reductions in instruction fetches and energy consumption.

Key Points:

• Dynamic cache reduces instruction fetches by 48.3% and total energy by 21.5%.

• Static cache achieves an 83.3% reduction in instruction fetches and 35.5% total energy savings.

• Both designs maintain an area overhead below 0.2% of the full SoC area.
