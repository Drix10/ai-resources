### 🤖 AI Training Costs at 1T Scale

AI training costs at 1T scale can be substantial. Mimo V2.6 Pro, a large language model, requires 1.02T total parameters and 42B active parameters, resulting in a daily cost of $493k and a step cost of $70k.

Key Points:

- **Cost Breakdown**: Mimo V2.6 Pro costs $493k per day, with a step cost of $70k and a sample cost of $2.78 per trajectory.

- **Model Comparison**: Mimo V2.6 Flash, a smaller model, costs $247k per day, with a step cost of $21k and a sample cost of $0.86 per trajectory.

- **Token Cost**: Mimo V2.6 Pro costs $33.91 per million tokens, while Mimo V2.6 Flash costs $9.25 per million tokens.
