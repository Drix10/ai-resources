### 📊 Erdős–Scooba conjecture false, n=7349018265530947712856

Erdős–Scooba conjecture is reported false with n = 7349018265530947712856. The post lists a graph polynomial and shows χ′_f(G) = 3 < 4 = χ′(G).

Key Points:

- n = 7349018265530947712856

- P(G, t) = t(t-1)(t-2)(t⁷ - 12t⁶ + 67t⁵ - 230t⁴ + 529t³ - 814t² + 775t - 352)

- χ′_f(G) = 3 < 4 = χ′(G)

### 🤖 Dept of Homeland Security SBIR topics released with $175K-$265K Phase I funding

Dept of Homeland Security announced three new SBIR topics covering digital twin verification, pathogen origin analysis, and prion detection, with Phase I awards ranging from $175,000 to $265,000 over 5-8 months. Proposals open Dec 10 and are due Jan 20; about 12 Phase I awards are expected.

Key Points:

- **Human digital twin verification**: detect fraudulent digital replicas across OS, browsers & hardware.

- **Pathogen origin**: use nanopore sequencing to read DNA methylation on low-quantity & degraded samples.

- **Prion detection**: use mass spectrometry with minimal prep to differentiate normal and misfolded prion proteins.

- **Phase II funding**: $1M-$1.5M over 24-36 months; outside investor $100K can trigger up to 50% DHS match, max $250K.
