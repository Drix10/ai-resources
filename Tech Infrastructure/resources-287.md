### 🚀 Watervliet Arsenal M777 barrel demand 30/month

Watervliet Arsenal makes M777 howitzer barrels and the Pentagon needs over 30 per month, prompting a second source. The Army hired BAE Systems to supply up to 10 barrels monthly while seeking additional suppliers.

Key Points:

- **Production**: steel heated to ~2,000°F, forged into a rough tube in ~14 minutes by a 1970s rotary forge.

- **Demand**: 2024 Pentagon document lists need for >30 M777 barrels per month; Army added BAE Systems as second source and seeks up to 10 more from industry by 23 Nov.
