### 🤖 Colgate AI replaces shoppers in surveys 90% reliability

Colgate found that letting AI talk naturally and mapping its words to a score matched 90% of human reliability in product surveys. Scoring by direct 1-5 rating failed, and without persona data accuracy fell to 50%; AI also gave purchase explanations.

Key Points:

- 57 real product surveys with 9,300 real people compared to GPT-4o and Gemini.

- Direct 1-5 score request failed; natural conversation mapping achieved 90% reliability.

- Without persona (age, income) accuracy dropped to 50%.

### 🤖 Apple ACE3 USB-C controller enables debug via VDMs

Apple ACE3 USB-C controller runs a full USB stack and can be re-wired into a hardware debugger via proprietary Vendor Defined Messages. Researchers can query it with 0x10 Get Action List and send 0x12 Perform Action to switch pins to UART, DFU, or JTAG.

Key Points:

- ACE3 connects to the internal SPMI bus and controls hardware JTAG and SWD access.

- VDMs are sent inside SOP'DEBUG and SOP"DEBUG packets under Apple vendor ID 0x05ac.
