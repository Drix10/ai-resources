### 🤖 AtlasCore $10.97M victim deposits in fake prediction markets

AtlasCore claimed roughly $10.97 million in victim cryptocurrency deposits between May and September 2026 using AI-assisted fake prediction-market platforms. Russian-language traffer teams AtlasCore, PolyFucked and Magnetic Team deploy simulated trading interfaces, forced-loss controls and withdrawal-blocking logic to trap funds.

Key Points:

- AtlasCore claimed ~$10.97M in victim deposits May-Sep 2026.

- Teams use AI-generated fake prediction-market sites with forced losses and blocked withdrawals.
