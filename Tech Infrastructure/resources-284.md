### 🤖 Virtual Machines, Agentic AI and the Mix of Workloads report released

Virtual Machines, Agentic AI and the Mix of Workloads report provides new usage statistics. 53% of production applications run as VMs on average. 61% of organizations report collaborative or fully integrated VM and container teams, and 34% of those expanding VM footprints cite AI and agentic workloads.

Key Points:

- 53% of production applications run as VMs on average

- 61% of organizations say their VM and container teams are collaborative or fully integrated

- 34% of those expanding VM footprint cite AI and agentic workloads

### 🤖 Amazon CloudWatch Logs account-level retention policies launch

Amazon CloudWatch Logs now supports account-level retention policies, letting a single policy set retention for all log groups or groups under a prefix such as /aws/lambda. Policies can apply to existing or new groups and override longer per-group settings; a dry run is recommended.

Key Points:

- One policy can cover every log group or groups under a specific prefix.

- Up to 20 policies can be created per account and region.

### 🤖 CockroachDB, RabbitMQ, Kafka, Solr paper at HotNets

The paper argues that cloud applications need formal management interfaces for operators and AI agents and shows they are useful for checking operation correctness. It presents interfaces for CockroachDB, RabbitMQ, Kafka, and Solr and reports a few bugs. The work will be presented by Jinlang at HotNets in the Autonomous Systems session on Monday.

Key Points:

- Formal management interfaces defined for operators and AI agents.

- Interfaces built for CockroachDB, RabbitMQ, Kafka, and Solr, uncovering bugs.
