### 🤖 SpaceX $40B GPU financing lease announced

SpaceX announced a $40 billion master finance lease for NVIDIA Vera Rubin GPUs with an Apollo-managed entity. The lease is slated to close before the end of 2026 and begin deliveries in early 2027.

Key Points:

- $40 billion master finance lease for GPUs and related equipment with an Apollo-managed entity.

- Lease closing expected before year-end 2026; GPU deliveries and lease schedules to start in the first half of 2027.

### 🤖 NVIDIA NCA-GENL vs AWS AI Practitioner certification focus

NVIDIA's generative AI exam (NCA-GENL) is 60% development, experimentation and data work. AWS AI Practitioner is 28% responsible AI and governance. Coders and non-coders shouldn't take the same certification.

Key Points:

- **NVIDIA NCA-GENL**: 60% development, experimentation and data work.

- **AWS AI Practitioner**: 28% responsible AI and governance.
