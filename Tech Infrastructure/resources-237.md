### 🚨 Security - Adversary-in-the-Middle (AITM) Attack

Adversary-in-the-middle (AITM) attacks involve phishing with a proxy, where a user authenticates and is proxied through a threat actor's infrastructure to login both the victim and attacker. This attack was first demonstrated by @mrgretzky 9 years ago.

Key Points:

- **Adversary-in-the-Middle Attack Mechanism**: AITM attacks involve a threat actor creating a proxy that intercepts user authentication requests, allowing them to login to both the victim's and their own accounts.

- **Historical Context**: @mrgretzky demonstrated this attack 9 years ago, highlighting the need for awareness and mitigation strategies.

- **Actionable Takeaway**: Be cautious when authenticating through unfamiliar proxies, and ensure that your login credentials are not being intercepted by a threat actor.
