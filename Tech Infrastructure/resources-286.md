### 📊 AI Infrastructure Spending Jumps to $121B in Q2 2026

AI infrastructure spending reached $121.4 billion in Q2 2026, up 53.3% year over year. Non-accelerated, CPU-based infrastructure grew 61% YoY, faster than accelerated systems at 52%. IDC raised its full-year 2026 forecast to $521 billion.

Key Points:

- Q2 2026 AI infrastructure spend $121.4B, +53.3% YoY.

- Non-accelerated CPU-based infrastructure growth +61% YoY, accelerated systems +52% YoY.

- IDC full-year 2026 AI infrastructure forecast $521B.
