### 🚀 Ryan Dahl leaves Deno, Cloudflare acquires his startup

Ryan Dahl, the creator of Node.js, is moving on from Deno. Cloudflare has acquired his startup to extend Workers and Durable Objects beyond Cloudflare’s own infrastructure.

Key Points:

- Ryan Dahl is moving on from Deno.

- Cloudflare has snapped up his startup to help bring Workers and Durable Objects beyond its own infrastructure.
