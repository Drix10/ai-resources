### 🤖 Palo Alto firewalls 25 compromised by Chinese attackers

Palo Alto firewalls 25 compromised by Chinese attackers using a custom PHP2Shell linked to a Linux kernel flaw. The attackers rooted 25 servers including firewalls linked to US government-affiliated organizations.

Key Points:

- 25 firewalls owned

- Attack used PHP2Shell chained with a Linux kernel flaw to gain root access
