### 🤖 DBOS scales to 1M+ workflows/sec across 50 databases

DBOS now runs on sharded Postgres and scales linearly to over 1 million workflows per second across 50 databases. It supports any sharding engine such as PgDog, Citus, Neki, and soon Multigres or custom solutions.

Key Points:

- Sharded Postgres support includes PgDog, Citus, Neki, soon Multigres, or custom engines.

- Linear scaling achieved to 1M workflows/sec sharded across 50 databases.

### 🚨 Do At-Proto Users Know This Is Public? – 1,300 RSS feeds exposed

Do At-Proto Users Know This Is Public? shows that Skyreader feed subscriptions are stored publicly in the app.skyreader.feed.subscription lexicon. The author retrieved over 1,300 RSS feed URLs without authentication, including private and paywalled feeds. The data is accessible via PDS servers and Bluesky relays.

Key Points:

- 1,300 RSS feed URLs were collected from public Skyreader subscription records.

- Private Club MacStories feed, 404 Media paywalled feeds, Patreon exclusive feeds, and Gitlab repository feeds were found accessible.

Trustle K8s controller with KEDA for rolling upgrades enables safe rolling upgrades of versioned Deployments. The controller determines which versioned Deployments should exist, and KEDA controls how many pods run per Deployment.
