### 🤖 3D World Simulator runs 50+ FPS on RTX 5090

3D World Simulator predicts the next scene state as a 3D particle cloud and runs interactively at over 50 FPS on a single RTX 5090. It uses a hybrid particle-voxel autoencoder to compress point clouds into compact voxel latents and provides novel camera and touch readouts from the same state.

Key Points:

- **Performance**: 50+ FPS on a single RTX 5090.

- **Architecture**: Hybrid particle-voxel autoencoder compresses point clouds into voxel latents.

### 🤖 Xiaomi Auto World Model 2.0 scores 78.0 on driving video

Xiaomi Auto World Model 2.0 predicts world response frame-by-frame and achieved a 78.0 score on driving video in PAI-Bench-G. It supports 1-7 camera views, was trained on 12.6 million clips, and uses Gated Linear Attention for faster temporal processing.

Key Points:

- Trained on 12.6 million clips from driving logs, robot episodes and rare physical interaction events.

- Temporal layers replace full softmax attention with Gated Linear Attention, yielding 7.15× faster attention and 3.72× faster causal generation.

- [Original post](https://x.com/OmnimesOfficial/status/2126730908107497500)
