### 🤖 AI Systems - Measuring Progress and Development

AI systems are getting more powerful, and they're increasingly being used to build the next version of themselves. We want to illuminate that progress for the public. Today, we're sharing three measurements that help track AI development: 1. How much AI R&D is done by AI. 2. How much AI is used in AI development. 3. How much AI is used in AI deployment.

Key Points:

- **AI R&D by AI**: This metric measures the proportion of AI research and development that is done by AI systems themselves. It's a way to gauge the extent to which AI is being used to improve itself.

- **AI in AI Development**: This metric measures the proportion of AI systems used in the development of new AI systems. It's a way to gauge the extent to which AI is being used to build the next generation of AI systems.

- **AI in AI Deployment**: This metric measures the proportion of AI systems used in the deployment of existing AI systems. It's a way to gauge the extent to which AI is being used to operate and maintain existing AI systems.
