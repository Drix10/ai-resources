### 🤖 PO Tracking That Catches Late Deliveries Early – Four early signals

PO Tracking That Catches Late Deliveries Early identifies four signals that reveal late purchase order deliveries weeks before they reach the dock. 50% of PO lines change after issuance, and the signals include acknowledgement silence, date drift, missing shipping confirmation, and pattern deviation.

Key Points:

- 50% of purchase order lines change after issuance.

- **Four early signals**: acknowledgement silence, acknowledged date versus requested date drift, no shipping confirmation inside the lead time window, and pattern deviation against supplier history.
