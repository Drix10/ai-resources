### 🤖 Opencontroller launch – unified pane for all agents

Opencontroller is a new pane to run, monitor and govern every agent, regardless of where it was built. Engineers use Agent Studio, business users use Architect, and Opencontroller centralizes control while keeping IP ownership.

Key Points:

- **Unified control**: one pane runs, monitors and governs all agents.

- **IP protection**: your IP stays yours.
