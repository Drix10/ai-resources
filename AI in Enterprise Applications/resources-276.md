### 🤖 Legora announces BDO agreement, new CLO, Legatics integration, AIUC-1 certification

Legora announces a global agreement with BDO for a common framework rollout, hires Erika Fisher as Chief Legal Officer, adds integration with Legatics, and becomes AIUC-1 certified alongside SOC 2.

Key Points:

- **Agreement**: global agreement with BDO, open to every firm in the network, coordinated rollout across its legal practices.

- **Hire**: Erika Fisher joined as Chief Legal Officer.

- **Integration**: new integration with Legatics lets lawyers update checklists, trackers, and issues lists in Legatics without leaving Legora.

- **Certification**: Legora is AIUC-1 certified and the first organization to undergo a SOC 2 and AIUC-1 engagement together.
