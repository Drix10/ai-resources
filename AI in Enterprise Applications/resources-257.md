### 🤖 Claude Opus 5.5 decision model deployed in warehouse

Claude Opus 5.5 was used to add a fast “system 1” decision model inside a warehouse. Every parcel is cropped at three gates and judged in about 270 ms. Torn parcels are sent to repack, and uncertain parcels are routed to a slower System 2.

Key Points:

- **Model**: Claude Opus 5.5 placed in warehouse decision flow.

- **Performance**: ~270 ms judgment time across three gates.
