Snowflake Dynamic Tables now reuse stored aggregates for top-level GROUP BY, reducing refresh time by about 40% on average. The biggest measured gain was a 147-fold speedup, cutting a 377-second refresh to roughly 2.5 seconds.

Key Points:

- Average refresh duration fell by about 40% for qualifying tables.

- Largest single improvement was 147×, from ~377 s to ~2.5 s.
