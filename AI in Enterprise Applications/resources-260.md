### 🤖 Microsoft Authenticator token leak via fake Teams app

Microsoft Authenticator handed over account tokens to a fake Teams app that required zero Android permissions. The app gained full delegated access to mailbox, files, and calendar through Microsoft Graph.

Key Points:

- Zero Android permissions were needed for the fake Teams app.

- Full delegated access to mailbox, files, and calendar was obtained via Microsoft Graph.
