### 🤖 NVIDIA NVLink Fusion collaboration announced

NVIDIA NVLink Fusion collaboration announced by Rebellions_inc, SKtelecom and NVIDIA to advance sovereign AI infrastructure. The effort is supported by Korea’s Ministry of Science and ICT and links domestic semiconductor innovation with sovereign AI infrastructure.

Key Points:

- **Collaboration**: Rebellions_inc, SKtelecom and NVIDIA announced a collaboration to advance sovereign AI infrastructure with NVIDIA NVLink Fusion.

- **Support**: Supported by Korea’s Ministry of Science and ICT.

### 🤖 Deepseek 4.1 1017 build on RTX Pro 6000 with MiniMax h3

### 🤖 Autohive Shopify Storefront integration launch

Autohive adds Shopify Storefront integration to browse products, build carts with discount codes and manage customer accounts. The Agent Creator can now schedule the agents it builds and set up webhooks for them. Sign-up and sign-in pages are now simpler.

Key Points:

- Shopify Storefront integration includes product browsing, cart building with discount codes, and customer account management.

- Agent Creator can schedule built agents and configure webhooks.

- Sign-up and sign-in pages have been simplified.
