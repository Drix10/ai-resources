### 🤖 Virtuix Omni One bundles with Quest 3/3S, adds $599 or $349

Virtuix now sells Omni One full-body VR treadmill bundles with Quest 3 or Quest 3S headsets. Bundle price adds $599 for Quest 3 or $349 for Quest 3S to the $2,595 treadmill base. Sales of Omni One have risen approximately 3X year-over-year. The treadmill works with more than 75 Quest games and over 100 PC VR titles.

Key Points:

- Sales increased approximately 3X compared to the same period last year.

- Omni One treadmill costs $2,595; bundling a Quest 3 adds $599, bundling a Quest 3S adds $349.
