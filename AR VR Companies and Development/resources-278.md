### 🤖 Real Cameras are Radiometric – radiometric camera model announced

Real Cameras are Radiometric introduces a radiometric camera model that lifts any central geometric camera to include aperture, focus surface and vignetting. The model is calibrated by inverse rendering on sensor irradiance from standard calibration target images and shows lower reprojection errors on held-out test sets.

Key Points:

- Keeps geometric projection and adds finite aperture, free-form focus surface, generic vignetting function.

- Calibrated by inverse rendering directly on relative irradiance of the sensor from the same images used for geometric calibration.

- Radiometric cameras have lower reprojection errors on held-out test sets.

- [Original post](https://x.com/ssh4net/status/2123977618865463334)
