### 🤖 Quest Horizon OS 2.10 PTC adds Theater environment and Simplified Navigator

Quest's Horizon OS 2.10 (v210) PTC update introduces a new Theater environment for media playback and a streamlined Navigator launcher redesign. Theater is accessed via a new button on the Control Bar and works with Media Player, Browser, YouTube, Disney+ and Amazon Prime Video. The Simplified Navigator removes several tabs and buttons, consolidating to Search, Library and Notifications, and adds quick-access controls via the top status bar.

Key Points:

- Theater environment selectable with a new “Theater” button on the Control Bar, replaces the window’s center screen view.

- Simplified Navigator removes Me, People, Quick Controls, Show/Hide Windows, Environment/App buttons and left-hand Library filters, leaving three tabs.
