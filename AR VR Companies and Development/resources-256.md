### 🚨 Roblox Updates Terms to Ban Misrepresented Country Registrations

Roblox has revised its Terms of Service. The new terms allow Roblox to ban users who register their account under a country they did not actually sign up in. The previous version also said Roblox may terminate users who access the service predominantly outside the country of registration.

Key Points:

- **Ban**: Roblox can ban accounts registered under a false country.

- **Termination**: Roblox may terminate users who primarily access from a different country.

### 🤖 Snap Specs AR Glasses and Valve Steam Frame VR Headset Launch

Snap released its see‑through AR glasses called Specs, priced at $2,195 and targeting developers, creators, early adopters and enterprise users. Valve launched its standalone Steam Frame VR headset with a starting price of $1,059, offering high‑resolution displays and wireless PC streaming. Both products aim to expand AR and VR adoption in their respective markets.

Key Points:

- **Specs price**: $2,195, standalone, no external puck, supports translation in 60 languages and multiplayer Lenses.

- **Steam Frame price**: $1,059 for base model, runs on Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 with 16 memory, 2160‑by‑2160 per eye, refresh up to 144, wireless streaming via 6 connection.
