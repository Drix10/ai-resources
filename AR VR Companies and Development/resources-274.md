Key Points:

### 📊 Overcoming Dynamic Range Limitations in Computational Hyperspectral Imaging

Overcoming Dynamic Range Limitations in Computational Hyperspectral Imaging introduces a physics-guided multi-exposure fusion framework that works in the encoded measurement domain and adds 16.0 dB to spectral reconstruction dynamic range. The method uses an analytical reliability prior, learned spatially varying channel-specific adjustments, and measurement-domain spectral-angle supervision. It is demonstrated on a 9-channel broadband-encoded stochastic hyperspectral camera and shows lower reconstruction errors than representative fusion methods.

Key Points:

- 16.0-dB increase in spectral reconstruction dynamic range versus the best fixed single exposure.

- Implemented on a 9-channel broadband-encoded stochastic hyperspectral camera with full-field sensor-response characterization and channel-aware reliability gating.
