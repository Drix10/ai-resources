### 🤖 Vaelorys Early Access launch on Quest, $4.99 price

Vaelorys is a dark fantasy co-op RPG now in Early Access on Quest, priced at $4.99. It offers up to eight-player co-op, arena mode, and more than thirty unlockable gesture-based spells.

Key Points:

- Early Access release on Quest, cost $4.99

- Co-op for up to eight players, arena mode, over thirty unlockable spells, multiple classes and builds
