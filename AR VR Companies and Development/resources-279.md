### 🤖 Quest Gets 3D Movie Rentals & Purchases In The US, 150+ titles

Quest 3 and 3S owners in the US can now rent or buy more than 150 3D movies in the TV app, including the Billie Eilish concert film. Meta recommends using the new Theater environment, which rolls out separately from Horizon OS updates.

- 150+ 3D movies available now, titles include Avatar, Spider-Man: Far From Home, Jurassic World Rebirth, and Wicked: For Good.

- **Billie Eilish – Hit Me Hard and Soft**: The Tour concert film is exclusive to rent or buy in 3D on Quest through December 31 2026.

### 🤖 SelfReflect live in App Store with full body tracking

SelfReflect is live in the App Store, offering full body, hand, and face tracking from a single device. It works with standard VMC apps and is free to test; the unlock is a single purchase, no subscription.

Key Points:

- **Launch**: SelfReflect is live in the App Store.

- **Compatibility**: Works with any standard VMC apps (Warudo, VNyan, VSeeFace, Unreal plugins, etc).
