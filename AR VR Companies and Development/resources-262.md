### 🚀 Battlefield 2 VR 1.1.0 adds roomscale and manual reload

Battlefield 2 VR 1.1.0 release delivers a stable, fully playable version with full roomscale movement and manual weapon reloading. The update includes physical inventory interaction, knife attacks, and defibrillator use. It also supports the DZB2 mod and requires linking to a legitimate BF2 1.5 installation.

Key Points:

- **Version 1.1.0**: stable, thoroughly tested, fully playable release.

- **Manual reloading**: supports magazine ejection and precise magwell guidance for assault rifles, sniper rifles, shotguns, and more.
