### 🤖 PICO Space Program Build with AI contest $50K prize

PICO Space Program: Build with AI competition offers a $50K prize pool and free AI credits, with a deadline of February 1 2027. Participants can develop Unity or XR projects for PICO’s spatial platform and test in the PICO emulator if they lack a headset. Selected finalists travel to San Jose for the finale.

Key Points:

- **Prize pool**: $50K.

- **Application deadline**: February 1 2027.

- Free AI credits provided for building.

### 🤖 ChatGPT new mobile experience launches for Pro users with dot tab

Key Points:

- New mobile UI starts with Pro users who have a dot or can create one.
