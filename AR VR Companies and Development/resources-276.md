### 🤖 Open4DGS 4DGS no genlock works with GoPros

Open4DGS is a 4DGS system that requires no genlock and works with multiple inexpensive cameras such as GoPros. It estimates timing offsets between cameras to sub-frame precision and also estimates rolling shutter readout times. An environment for running it on an NVIDIA RTX 3090 is publicly available.

Key Points:

- Works with multiple inexpensive cameras, such as GoPros

- Estimates timing offsets between cameras down to the sub-frame level

- Also estimates rolling shutter readout times

- Environment for running on an NVIDIA RTX 3090 is publicly available
