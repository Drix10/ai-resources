### 📸 Photography - Spatial AI in Everyday Photos

Spatial AI adds a stronger sense of dimension to everyday photos by understanding the relationships between objects in the scene. This technology can be applied to various fields, including photography, where it can enhance the visual appeal of images.

Key Points:

- **Spatial AI in Photography**: Spatial AI uses computer vision and machine learning algorithms to analyze the relationships between objects in a scene, allowing it to add depth and dimension to photos.

- **Applications in Photography**: Spatial AI can be used to enhance the visual appeal of images, create 3D models, and even aid in object detection and tracking.

- **Limitations**: Spatial AI is not a replacement for traditional photography techniques, and it may not work well with all types of images, such as those with complex lighting or multiple objects.
