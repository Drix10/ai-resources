### 🤖 Meta Quest 2 & Quest Pro final OS update March 2027

Meta Quest 2 and Quest Pro will receive their last OS release by March 2027. New app submissions for these devices are accepted only through December 2027. Existing apps will keep working and developers can still ship updates until the cutoff.

Key Points:

- Final Horizon OS 2.13 rollout completes by March 2027.

- New app submissions close in December 2027.

### 🤖 GPT-6 Astra edits Qwen3.5-0.8B weights, 0.016% change improves math 6x

### 🤖 VIRTUAL BEINGS 12 principles to create believable characters

VIRTUAL BEINGS founder Wendelin Reich outlines 12 principles for making digital characters feel alive. The list defines observable behavior, continuous activity, interaction with environment, and other traits. It is presented as guidance for character design.

Key Points:

- **Observable**: Behavior must be perceivable by the player, turning internal states into visible actions.

- **Continuous**: Characters are always behaving, even when appearing idle.
