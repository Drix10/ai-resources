### 🤖 DiffSimp adds differentiable mesh simplification layer with 1.00-1.25× runtime overhead

DiffSimp introduces a training-free differentiable mesh simplification layer built on QEM-based parallel edge collapse. It enables gradient propagation through a fixed collapse history without relaxing discrete decisions. Experiments show higher fidelity than post-processing simplification and runtime of 1.00-1.25× compared to unsimplified baselines.

Key Points:

- Differentiable mesh simplification layer operates on QEM-based parallel edge collapse.

- End-to-end runtime is 1.00-1.25× that of unsimplified baselines in evaluated pipelines.

🔗 Resources:

- [Original post](https://x.com/ssh4net/status/2125778321187504384)
