### 🤖 SplatThumb thumbnail handler for Gaussian PLY files released

SplatThumb is a free open-source Windows Explorer thumbnail handler that shows previews of 3D Gaussian Splatting .ply files and lets you set the view angle. It installs for a user on Windows 10/11 x64 without administrator rights and samples up to 400,000 splats for each thumbnail.

Key Points:

- Installs per-user on Windows 10/11 x64, requires no admin rights.

- Draws a sample of 50,000 to 400,000 splats per thumbnail, selectable in settings.

### 🤖 ESP-FLUTTER robotic butterfly flies using ESP32-C3

ESP-FLUTTER is an open-source robotic butterfly that flies by flapping its wings, powered by a Seeed XIAO ESP32-C3 microcontroller. It uses two digital servos for wing motion and allows control of wing movement, speed and turning.

Key Points:

- **Microcontroller**: Seeed XIAO ESP32-C3

- **Actuators**: two digital servos for wing movement

- **Control**: adjustable wing movement, speed and turning
