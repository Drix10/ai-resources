### 🚀 RTX 2060 & GTX 1650 SUPER P2P hack 20× lower latency

RTX 2060 and GTX 1650 SUPER can now use full-memory BAR1 windows after removing the GPU’s USB-C controller and running geforce-bar1-resize. The hack yields 20× lower inter-GPU latency and up to +78% faster data exchange, matching RTX 3080 pairs in the same slots.

Key Points:

- 256MB BAR1 window grown to cover all VRAM by disabling the USB-C + UCSI controllers and using geforce-bar1-resize.

- P2P between the two cards shows 20× less delay and up to +78% faster transfers, benchmarked equal to RTX 3080 pairs.
