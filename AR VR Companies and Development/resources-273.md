### 🤖 A Lightweight Differentiable Method for Real-Time Skin Rendering and Digitization

A Lightweight Differentiable Method for Real-Time Skin Rendering and Digitization introduces a differentiable Screen-Space Subsurface Scattering (SSSS) framework for joint optimization of base textures and scattering distances. The method uses a low-cost single-light, single-view capture setup and a masked loss focused on subsurface scattering regions. Validation is performed on 45 individuals spanning the Monk Skin Tone Scale and on synthetic data.

Key Points:

- **Dataset**: 45 individuals across the Monk Skin Tone Scale

- **Capture setup**: single-light, single-view configuration

### 🤖 Deformable Texture Maps compile microgeometry into neural textures

Deformable Texture Maps (DTM) compile deformation-driven microgeometry into a continuous neural texture. The model maps texture coordinates and local deformations to intrinsic maps of opacity, normals, depth, and material identity. These maps are cached in texture space and updated only when deformation changes. The approach works on arbitrary coarse garment meshes and integrates with conventional shading pipelines.

Key Points:

- Intrinsic maps encode opacity, surface normals, depth, and material identity.

- Persistent texture-space rendering caches maps and selectively updates them as deformation changes.
